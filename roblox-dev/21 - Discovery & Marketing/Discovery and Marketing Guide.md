# Discovery and Marketing Guide

## Summary

On Roblox, discovery runs through the **Home page recommendations**, search and sorts, plus paid **ads** (Ads Manager: sponsored and search ads) and off-platform marketing.

- **Official facts**, documented below: thumbnail specs, thumbnail personalization (QPTR-based), icon specs, video rules, ad products.
- **Community-measured practice** (CTR targets, testing volume, the algorithm's behaviour, ad budgets) is collected in **Hud's Guide**. It is labelled community observation, not platform documentation.

## What Roblox documents

| Topic | Official fact |
|---|---|
| Thumbnails | 16:9, ideally 1920×1080; under 3 MB for home uploads; up to 10 items on the detail page; keep key content off the bottom edge; Home Page and Experience Detail Page tabs are separate |
| Thumbnail personalization | Starts with **2+ active** home thumbnails (up to 5). Each user group gets the thumbnail with the best **qualified play-through rate**. Keep several active. Roblox reports an average +8.5% QPTR in testing. |
| Icon | Square, 512×512; check it at about 150×150 |
| Videos | Authentic gameplay only; no voice-over, lyrics or external footage; 3 uploads a month; not supported on Xbox, PlayStation or VR |
| Recommendations | See `production/recommendation` for how Roblox describes its home-recommendation signals |
| Ads | Ads Manager (`production/promotion/ads-manager`), search ads, rewarded video, standards (`comply-with-advertising-standards`) |
| Page and metadata | Descriptions, genres, content maturity, social links, experience events, notifications, share links |
