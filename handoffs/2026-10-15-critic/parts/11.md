## 11. World, first impression and performance (6/10)
The client scripts are better than most: the ~22 always-on per-frame callbacks nearly all early-return or throttle to 4-5 Hz, and UI particles are capped (250 burst / 60 ambient). The weak spot is the 3D world. It is 10,506 parts with no phone-specific scaling, the streaming radius is unset, there is no loading screen, and every kid spawns, waits, then gets teleported 15 studs.

### Problems, ranked
1. **The phone gets the same heavy 3D world as a desktop.** [Static] Only HideOthersPets is auto-on for touch (`BaseFxController` line 291). `ReducedFx` defaults to false (`DataService` line 100, `SettingsService` line 99) and is not set from `TouchEnabled`. A grep for `QualityLevel`, `SavedQualityLevel` and `Graphics` finds 0 hits. The world is 10,506 BaseParts. 3,812 (36%) have `CastShadow=true`, including 1,168 of the 1,648 wall bricks. 694 are translucent, 1,162 are Neon and 663 are Glass. There are 66 emitters, 39 SurfaceGuis and 24 BillboardGuis. Only UI effects are halved on phones (ModalFx, ShopController line 706); the 3D FX are not.
   - Fix: on first join, if `TouchEnabled and not KeyboardEnabled` and the saved ReducedFx is nil, set ReducedFx=true (one line in `DataService`/`SettingsService` defaults, resolved client-side like HideOthersPets). Run a one-off edit script that sets `CastShadow=false` on every decorative part, which keeps shadows for the lobby hero props only. Target at most 800 shadow casters (about -3,000). Effort S · Impact High.
2. **StreamingTargetRadius is not set, so a phone is asked to hold most of a 1,560-stud-long map.** [Computed] Parts lie from z=-1214.75 to 343.25. From Plot1 spawn, 3,408 parts lie within 300 studs, 4,759 within 500 and 7,776 (74%) within 1,024. No script assigns `StreamingMinRadius` or `StreamingTargetRadius` (grep, 0 hits; world.md). The property values are unreadable here, so I assume Roblox defaults. 1,965 instances never stream out because they are Persistent: Plots 1,876, `Workspace.Model` (Spikes, 56) and `WaterA` (33).
   - Fix: set `StreamingMinRadius` 96 and `StreamingTargetRadius` 350 in Studio properties. The lobby, all 5 plots and the Meadow nest (179-215 studs) fit inside 350. Test on a 2 GB phone. Effort XS · Impact High.
3. **There is no loading screen and no preload, and the join ends in a visible teleport.** [Static] `ReplicatedFirst` is empty. The engine spawns at `LobbySpawn`, then `spawnAtPlot` moves the character after a yield on `RequestStreamAroundAsync` (`PlotService` lines 190-208). That repeats on every respawn.
   - Fix: (a) make each plot's `Spawn` part a Neutral `SpawnLocation` and set `player.RespawnLocation` to it on assign, so the character spawns where it should and `RequestStreamAroundAsync` runs before `CharacterAdded`; (b) add a small ReplicatedFirst loader that holds a cover frame until `DataController.Ready` plus the first streamed Plot, with a 6 s cap; (c) `PreloadAsync` only the HUD icons. Effort M · Impact High.
4. **Only 5 plots exist, but Players.MaxPlayers reads 60.** [Static] Players 6 and up have no plot, no pen and no wheel. They stay at `LobbySpawn` and see nothing to do. Ad traffic fills servers fast. (The real server cap lives in Creator Hub and was not readable.)
   - Fix: set the server cap to 5 in Creator Hub, or add a per-server `PlotService` gate that sends overflow players to a reserved server through `TeleportService`. Effort S · Impact Very high for bounce if the cap is above 5.
5. **One waterfall and the sky corridor carry about 2,150 live particles, and the waterfall never streams out.** [Computed] Sum of rate x max lifetime over all Workspace emitters is about 2,157. `Workspace.WaterA` alone is about 1,370: the 500/s emitter (life 1) gives about 500, plus 125, 125 and others. It sits at (132.6, 21.7, -15.2), 286 studs from spawn, and is Persistent per world.md, so it replicates to every client at join. `Workspace.Atmosphere` holds 48 emitters and about 787 live particles. Whether off-screen emitters cost anything is [Opinion].
   - Fix: set `WaterA` to Default streaming. Cut the 500/s emitter to 80/s and the others by 60%. On touch, set `Enabled=false` on half of the Atmosphere emitters. Target under 600 live particles in any view. Effort S · Impact Med.
6. **The 1,648 wall bricks are individual Parts.** [Computed] `Workspace.World.Walls.BrickWalls` holds 1,667 instances, 1,168 of them with CastShadow, and 442 are Neon (266) or Glass (176). The Walls tree is 1,672 instances. They are decor kept as separate parts, so each adds to streaming, physics and draw cost.
   - Fix: replace each biome's walls with 4-6 long wall parts using a brick texture or one MeshPart per biome. Expected result: -1,600 parts (-15% of all parts). Effort M · Impact Med.
7. **Neon and Glass add cost with almost no visual gain.** [Static] No Bloom is active (world.md), so Neon does not glow. There are 1,162 Neon parts, 515 in Cosmic. There are 663 Glass parts. Only 694 parts are translucent in total, so most Glass is probably used as flat color.
   - Fix: switch Neon to Plastic or SmoothPlastic with the same color on every part not near the nest, the beam, the chevrons or the trail orbs. Keep Glass only where it must be see-through. Effort S · Impact Low-Med on low-end phones.
8. **Always-on per-frame work is mostly clean, but a few things run every frame for no reason.** [Static] RenderStepped connections: `HudController` (lines 1282 and 1295), `CarryController` 559, `GuardFxController` 456, `PlacementController` 226, `TutorialController` 777, `EggFxController` 35, `PetToolController` 151, `WheelFxController` 293, `PetIdleController` 594, `TrailStandController` 216 and `FirstWalkController` 431. That is 12. Heartbeat connections: `HudController` 1279 and 1308, `BaseFxController` 360, `TutorialController` 770, `SpeedFxController` 182, `WheelRunController` 97, `PetIdleController` 587 and `HugeEggController` 65. That is 8. Most early-return. The ones that always work: `renderLayout` (`HudController` line 712) recomputes the top stack every frame (`GetGuiInset`, `AbsoluteSize` reads, a new UDim2 per slot). `CarryController` line 485 reassigns `rayParams.FilterDescendantsInstances` with a new table, and raycasts, for every player carrying an egg every frame. `TutorialController.placeDistance` writes `pill.Position` every frame during step 1.
   - Fix: re-run `layoutTopStack` only when a Visible, AbsoluteSize or toast-count signal fires. Hoist the raycast filter table and rebuild it only when `folder` or `character` changes. Skip the `Position` write when the value is unchanged. Effort S · Impact Low (a cleanliness win).
9. **Dead weight ships to every client.** [Static] `StarterPlayerScripts.TEST` is a disabled 810-line decompiled script with 145 descendants. `CustomPrompts` (501 lines, 130 descendants) is third-party. `Workspace.TopbarPlus` is 26 instances whose only script is a disabled README; world.md says no script references it. The `Admin` ScreenGui (202 instances) is cloned for every player even though only admins see it. Menus is 791, Hud 290 and Events 77. About 370 instances (202 Admin + 145 TEST + 26 TopbarPlus) are avoidable.
   - Fix: delete `TEST` and `Workspace.TopbarPlus`. Build the Admin gui server-side only for admin UserIds. Effort XS · Impact Low.
10. **The first sight at spawn is a distraction, not the goal.** [Opinion] The gold egg prop, "HUGE EGG SOON" and "BIGGEST EGG TODAY" signs take the middle of the frame (screenshot 01) and the nest is 179 studs out of sight. At 667x375 the tutorial card also covers the Settings button by 67 x 34 px (ui.md). The beam and distance pill are supposed to carry the load, and they only exist at runtime, so I could not check them.
    - Fix: in the first 10 s, fade the BiggestEgg sign and "HUGE EGG SOON" board until the first egg is stolen, or turn the spawn so the first view contains the Meadow gate and its chevrons (the EntranceArch is at (188.25, 188.25)). Effort S · Impact Med.

### What's good
The client code is built for streaming and for cost: Streamed.Tagged hooks drop work on stream-out, `PetIdleController` and `TrailStandController` distance-cull, and `FirstWalkController` stops all work once step 1 ends. Particles are capped and halved on phones (UIFxParticles line 29, ModalFx phone halving), Rainbow eggs share one loop, and Spring/TextFx/Index disconnect when idle. HideOthersPets is auto-on for phones. Plot floors are blocky and consistently styled, so the world looks clear and fast to read (screenshots 01, 03). Only 5 point lights are enabled, all without shadows.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Turn ReducedFx on automatically for touch-only devices and set CastShadow=false on all decor parts (3,812 casters now, target 800).
2. Set StreamingMinRadius 96 and StreamingTargetRadius 350 (74% of parts are within 1,024 studs today), and make WaterA non-persistent with its 500/s emitter cut.
3. Cap the server at 5 players or add overflow handling (only 5 plots exist), and spawn at the plot through `RespawnLocation` instead of the visible 15-stud teleport.
