## 10. HUD, UI clarity and mobile (5/10)
The HUD is good-looking, consistent (one display font, bevelled buttons) and already has a real phone profile with a moved timer and a drop button that drops the keyboard hint. But it is a desktop-density HUD squeezed onto a phone. 14 elements are on screen at second 0, five buttons and the Shop close X are under 44 px, and the Tutorial card collides with Settings. There is also no persistent "what do I do next" chip once the tutorial ends.

Evidence base: `facts/ui.md` rects (engine-measured, no playtest), the Studio instances read for this review, and HudController (`StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts.Client.Controllers.HudController`). Nothing here is playtest-[Measured]. Screenshots 01 and 10 are Edit-mode BASE layout (not phone).

### Problems, ranked
1. **There is no persistent "next goal" chip once the tutorial ends.** [Static] No `NextGoal`-style element exists (grep "NextGoal" found 0 hits; Hud children are Stats, Left, Tabs, Timer, Carry, banners, Toasts, Tutorial, Settings, Boosts, FriendBoost, Shield, Spotlight, FirstWalk). `Hud.Tutorial` hides at step 17 (TutorialController showStep, nil step). After that the only guidance is `UpgradeHintController.evaluate()` (lines 81-101): one toast per item, `seen[key]` set so it never repeats, 5 s quiet period, muted while `Tutorial.InProgress`, toast life 3.2 s (HudController showToast line 159), `MAX_LIVE_TOASTS_COMPACT = 2` (line 137). If the kid is mid-roll when it fires, it is gone. The yardstick is "goal visible on screen at all times" and a "$x / $y" ticker; the only ticker is in tutorial step 6 and step 16 text.
   - Fix: reuse the existing Tutorial card slot (`HudController.layoutTopStack` already places it first in the top-centre stack) as a 220x40 "NEXT: Hamster Wheel  $60 / $100" chip with a thin progress bar. Priority order: next Wheel tier, then Pen level, then first Trail (the same candidates `UpgradeHintController.candidates()` already builds), then Rebirth. Tap the chip to point the beam at the target. Turns green and pulses when affordable. Effort S-M · Impact Very high.

2. **Tutorial card text is too small and too long for phones, and the card covers Settings.** [Computed] At 667x375 the compact card is 330x55 with body 11 px, and step 2 fits at about 8 px (27 words; yardstick is about 5 words per step and 14 pt minimum). The card's UISizeConstraint minimum width (330) overrides the 0.4W intent, so it spans x168-498 and covers Settings (x431-511) by 67x34 px (ui.md A). At 568x320 (small Android/old iPhone) it also overlaps the Timer by about 29 px ([Computed]: card 119-449, Timer 420-560).
   - Fix: make the compact card 340x76 with body at least 14 px. Cut every step body to 10 words or fewer (step 2 becomes "Roll it home. Cross the gold line!"). Move the long explanations into one-time hint pills. Move Settings out of the top-centre row: put it under the Tabs column or in the Left list as a 5th small button. Effort S · Impact High.

3. **Nothing is hidden during the tutorial: 8 buttons plus a Robux Shop at second 0.** [Static] ui.md: "no controller hides Shop/Daily/Quests/Rebirth for new players"; the 14-element count is above the yardstick of about 10. The Shop button is the largest, brightest blue button in the left stack and carries an R$ badge (`Hud.Left.Shop.RobuxBadge`). The yardstick says hide optional UI and Robux offers during the tutorial. Left plus Tabs take two full screen edges (Left y64-236, Tabs y108-294 of 375).
   - Fix: gate side buttons on `TutorialStep`. Steps 1-5 show only Stats, Timer and the card. Show Eggs at step 5, Shop and Wheel info at step 6, Index at step 8 and Daily at step 9 (those steps already spotlight them), and Quests and Rebirth after step 16. Slide them in with the existing `manage()/slotPos` mechanism. Effort S · Impact High.

4. **Touch targets and thumb zones fail the 44 pt rule on the core buttons.** [Computed] ui.md: Shop, Daily, Quests, Rebirth and Settings are 76x34 / 80x34 (34 < 44); the Shop modal Close X is 0.12 x 0.12 of the modal = 35x35 at 667x375 and 39x39 at 844x390 (Menus.Shop.Close and the 8 other modal Close buttons use the same 0.12 fraction); Eggs/Pets/Index are 45x54 (width just passes). Joystick zone (x<200, y>175): Stats (all), Rebirth (fully), Quests (bottom 14 px). Jump zone: Pets and Index. The Shop R$ button itself sits at y66-100, outside both thumb zones, which is correct.
   - Fix: compact side buttons 76x46 with 6 px gaps. That makes a 4-button column 214 px tall, so show only 3 at a time (see 3) or switch to a 2-column icon grid. Raise modal Close to at least 48x48 with UISizeConstraint MinSize 48x48 on every `Menus.*.Close`. Move Stats up and right of the joystick, to about x70, y above the stick area, or make it a single row at the top-left under the topbar. Effort S · Impact High.

5. **Several labels are below the 14 px floor.** [Computed] Quests and Rebirth labels 12 px (longer words force TextScaled down), R$ badge 9 px, Tutorial body 11 px (8 px on long steps), Timer caption 13-14 px. Shop-modal row descriptions fit at only 12 px (box 202x25: "Double hamster-wheel Speed gain for 15 minutes. One time only!"). That is the paid screen kids must read.
   - Fix: add `UITextSizeConstraint.MinTextSize = 14` to every Hud and Shop label. Shorten "Quests" to "Quest" and "Rebirth" to "Rebirth" with icon-only on compact if it still fails. Widen ShopOffer.Desc from 0.5 to 0.6 of the row and set row height 84 to 96 on compact so descriptions reach at least 14 px. Effort S · Impact Med-High.

6. **The Carry card is large and sits in the middle of the view during the most dangerous moment.** [Computed] `Hud.Carry` UISizeConstraint min 320x130; compact position {0.58,0},{0.965,-72}, so at 667x375 it is x227-547, y160-290 (about 17% of the screen). It carries 3 text lines plus Drop (147x52, fine size). On phones, `layoutTopStack` also clamps the toast and tutorial stack above it (`limit = carry top - 6`, HudController line 668-670). That leaves roughly y64-154 for the card, pill and toasts.
   - Fix: on compact, shrink Carry to 240x84 (title and one 14 px line, x-weight and max-kg dropped; they are in the Info row and could be tap-to-expand) and move it to the bottom-centre between joystick and jump button (x 250-420). Effort S · Impact Med.

7. **The same thing has three or four names, and some tutorial text is keyboard-only.** [Static]
   - Home base: "plot" (Tutorial steps 3, 4, 12), "pen" (step 6 Pre text, "Bigger Pen"), "bases" (step 2), "Porch".
   - Safe line: "gold line" (step 2), "LockLine" (target), "LOCKED IN!" (Hud.LockBanner), "SAFE ZONE" (floor text).
   - Wheel: "hamster wheel" (Tutorial.lua), "Wheel board" (step 6), "Egg Wheel" (HudController.buildShop fallback line 449), "treadmill" (Roblox listing description and `Multipliers.Treadmill`), "Treadmill" board in code.
   - Keys: step 13 "Hold F ... (or tap Roll)" and step 15 "hold E on it to hatch" (Config.Tutorial line 57) show keyboard letters to touch players; only `{Action}` is templated (Tap/Click). The step 1 pill does switch to "Hold the button on an egg!" on touch (TutorialController line 630), so the pattern exists.
   - Fix: one glossary ("Plot", "Gold line", "Wheel"), find-and-replace in Config.Tutorial, HudController, UpgradeHintController, Products descriptions and the Roblox listings. Add a `{Hold}` token resolved like `{Action}` ("Hold the button" on touch, "Hold E" otherwise). Effort S · Impact Med.

8. **Toasts are too short and too wordy for kids 7-12 on phones.** [Computed] Toast life is 3.2 s (HudController line 159) and phones cap at 2 live toasts (line 137). `UpgradeHintController` messages run 12-17 words ("You can afford the Green Trail (+50% ...)! Get it at the Trail Stand in the lobby."). A young reader needs 5-8 s for that. Toasts are also held while the hatch reveal runs (RevealGate), so they arrive late and in a burst.
   - Fix: lifetime = max(3.2, 0.45 s x word count), capped at 7 s. Cut hint messages to 8 words or fewer with an icon ("Wheel ready! $100 at your Wheel board"). Keep money-upgrade hints in the next-goal chip (problem 1) instead of toasts. Effort XS · Impact Med.

9. **The Stats block is unlabelled and sits where the left thumb lives.** [Static/Computed] `Hud.Stats.Speed` has only an Icon and a Value (no caption); the first number "10" has no word next to it, and Speed is also what boosts and trails modify. Stats (8,271 194x96) is fully inside the joystick zone; Roblox's dynamic thumbstick starts on a left-half touch that is not on a button, so a thumb landing on the numbers is wasted effort.
   - Fix: add a 12-14 px "SPEED" caption under the shoe icon (same Fredoka font) and move Stats to the top-left under the topbar (y64) with the buttons below, or shrink it to a 130x60 chip. Effort XS · Impact Low-Med.

10. **A Robux Skip prompt sits beside Hatch/Roll on the egg, which a phone shows as stacked tap buttons.** [Static, not verified in play] PenService creates Hatch (line 183), Roll (line 202) and Skip (`Skip R$ {PriceHint}`, lines 268-278, hold 0.25 s, Economy.Prompts.SkipHold) prompts on each incubating egg, all using the same 193x72 CustomPrompts billboard (`CustomPrompts.Default/Hatch/Roll/Skip`, ActionText 19 px). Near the egg, three round buttons stack in the action-thumb area. The Roblox purchase dialog still confirms, so this is a mistap and trust risk rather than a charge risk.
    - Fix: offer Skip only through the egg's timer billboard or the Eggs panel, or spawn the Skip prompt only when the timer is above 60 s and offset it 2 prompt-heights from Roll. Effort S · Impact Low-Med.

### What's good
The HUD already knows about phones: `isCompact()` (HudController line 520) switches layout on touch or height under 600, the Timer is moved out from under the jump button (lines 870-873), toasts drop to 2 on phones, and the Drop button label drops "[Q]" on touch (line 895). Every label uses one rounded font (FredokaOne) with bevelled buttons (screenshots 01 and 10), so it reads as one coherent kid-friendly style, and the Tutorial/FirstWalk pill gives an exact distance to the first objective. Reveal mode slides the HUD out of the way for hatch cards (lines 720-728), which keeps the payoff screen clean.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Add a persistent "NEXT: <thing> $x / $y" chip (reuse the Tutorial card slot) so the goal never leaves the screen after step 16.
2. Gate the side buttons by tutorial step (hide Shop/Daily/Quests/Rebirth/Pets/Index until needed) and make the remaining ones at least 44 px high with 14 px minimum text.
3. Rewrite the phone tutorial card (14 px body, at most 10 words, no Settings overlap) and replace keyboard letters with a `{Hold}` token.
