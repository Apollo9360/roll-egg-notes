# Skeptic pass on the 36 Top-3 items

Method: I re-read each claim's evidence against `critic-review-facts/*` and the live Edit DataModel (studio 1973f6fb). Everything was read-only: script_read/grep, inspect, and execute_luau reads only. Nothing was edited, no playtest was run, no server module was required. "Sim" means a critic's own simulation. I checked its inputs, not its outputs.

## Cross-cutting checks (the disputed points)

- **Mother Hen: catches a kid who pauses, never catches a runner.** Both are true. Critics 01 and 02 do not actually disagree. The numbers: hen WalkSpeed 13. Hop x1.6 for 0.5 s, then a 4.5 s cooldown, so the cycle is 5 s and the average is about 13.8. Wake 1.2 s. Catch reach = HitReach 4 + max(Body 4x4)/2 = 6 studs (GuardService:465; Body measured 4x5x4). The catch test runs every frame. A kid standing at a nest 11-22 studs from the post is caught about 1.6-2.4 s after the steal. A kid who runs straight at 16 x drag stays ahead until the egg passes size ~2.26, and starts ~30 studs clear. With the first-walk pickups (walk 21.6) the gap only grows.
- **EntranceSigns at Y=-84: bug or hidden on purpose?** They are invisible: all six are at Y=-84, ground is Y=-1, beam at -75.7. It is not a builder bug. Both backups (`WorldBackup_2026-10-12`, `Backup_PreBlocky_2026-10-12`) have them at Y=16. All six whole assemblies (sign, rails, beam, hangers) sit exactly 100 studs lower. BlockyMap:1320 would have hung the beam from post-top Y=18 if the sign had been at -84 when it ran, so the drop came after BlockyMap. No script moves them. No comment explains it. `Config.Biomes:56` still says RequiredSpeed is "shown on the entrance sign". So it looks like a deliberate manual move whose intent is unrecorded. One label is visible: each ZoneEdge has `FloorText` "Recommended Speed 300/8k/60k/400k/2.5M" at Y=0.1, on the floor just before the line.
- **First-purchase timing.** About 38-40 s (ideal) is right, so critics 01 and 04 hold. The starter Owl gets +0.5 size on top of ~190 studs of growth (size about 2.6-2.9), so it earns about $75-90/s, and $100 arrives 1-2 income ticks after the pet is placed (about 35-37 s). The Economy comment's "$24/s or more" is the no-growth floor. Critics 05 ($24-40/s, "1.5-3 min"), 08 and 09 ($24/s) under-count.
- **Night deletes the carried egg, and the toast text.** True. EggService:1026-1042 calls `discardEgg` (which is `destroyEgg`, clearing the nest slot), then shows "Night fell! Your egg rolled back to its nest." The nests are re-rolled 10 s later (DayStarted, then RefillNests).
- **Croc Lunge.** It is dead code. Body measured 6x4x12, so reach = 5 + 6 = 11. `behave()` starts a lunge only at dist <= Range 10, and the catch test (<= 11) runs first every frame.
- **Max Players 60 vs 5 plots.** Players.MaxPlayers = 60 and PreferredPlayers = 60 in Studio. The live value is not readable. There is no code cap and no overflow handling. `assign()` runs only on join, so a freed plot never goes to a waiting player.
- **Nest eggs refill only every 300 s.** True. `spawnNestEgg` is called only from `refillNests` (Start, DayStarted, and the Night Run event's `RefillNests("NightRun")`) and from SpawnRare/SpawnHuge, which replace an existing nest egg rather than add one. A delivered egg leaves its nest empty.
- **Daily D1 pays $250 if claimed early.** True. `DailyReward(NextDay, income(player))` uses placed-pet income at claim time, and the minimums are $250 and 60 Speed.
- **Offline double-pay after a crash.** True but rare. `LastSeen` is written only in PlayerRemoving (DataService:209), and `grantOffline` never updates it. Needs a real crash after an autosave.
- **Sell double-tap.** True. Sell is one tap. The Pets list re-sorts and rebuilds on every Pets change (HudController:1216-1221), so the next pet slides into the tapped row. The server limit is only 0.15 s.
