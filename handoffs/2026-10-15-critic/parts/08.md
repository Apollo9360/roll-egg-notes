## 8. Retention and return loops (5/10)
The plumbing is better than most: the Daily, quests, codes and offline pay are all server-authoritative and scale with the player. But nothing in the game tells a 7-12-year-old why to come back tomorrow, the streak is easy to lose by accident, and the biggest return reward (offline cash) arrives as a one-line toast. Day 1 is built; Day 2 is left to chance; Day 7 is reached by only a small minority.

### The kid's experience
Assumptions: new kid, US after-school player (UTC-7), Owl starter pet earning about $24/s ([Computed] 16 base x 1.3^1.6; `Economy.lua` comment says "$24/s or more"). No playtest was run, so nothing is [Measured].

| Time | What happens | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1, 0:00 | Join. The server makes 3 quests at load with income 0, so every reward is the minimum: $300-450 cash and 80-120 Speed each (`RetentionService.lua:185-196`, `Retention.lua` Quests.RewardCashMin 300 / RewardSpeedMin 80 x Difficulty). The Daily button shows a red dot from second 0 (`DailyController.lua:139-151`). [Static] | The dot is just a 34 pt button on a phone (`ui.md`). Fine for a tutorial; no hook yet. |

Claiming pays Day 1: about $2,900 + 60 Speed ([Computed] max(250, 24x60x2) and max(60, 5% of Speed)). | The kid claims Day 1 inside a chore, so the "come back" message lands at the moment of least interest. 60 Speed is 30 s on a tier-1 wheel (2 Speed/s, `Economy.Wheel`). |

### Problems, ranked
1. **The streak breaks on a single missed UTC day, with no grace, and the reset moment is 5 pm Pacific / 8 pm Eastern.** `Retention.DayNumber = t // 86400` (`Retention.lua:26`), `DailyStatus` requires `lastDay == today - 1` or resets to Day 1 (`:85-91`). A kid who plays Mon 4 pm and Tue 5:30 pm PDT (about 25 h apart) is "Broken". The same rule lets a kid claim Day 1 at 4:55 pm and Day 2 at 5:05 pm on one evening, burning two calendar days. [Static/Computed]
   - Fix: switch to a rolling clock: next claim opens 20 h after `LastClaim`; the streak survives until 44 h; add one free "grace day" per 7-day cycle (`Daily.Grace`) and show it ("Streak saved!"). Keep the server as the only payer. Effort S · Impact High.
2. **The 7-day rewards are small numbers, not things a kid wants.** Weekly cash is income x 60 x (2+3+4+5+6+8+15 = 43 min) = 24 x 60 x 43 = **$62K**, about 18% of one 8-hour away-day ($345.6K). Speed rewards are 60-400 (`Retention.lua:53-61`). The only non-number items are 1 Trap, 1 Bat tier and a Luck token. No pet, egg or cosmetic anywhere in the calendar. [Computed]
   - Fix: keep the numbers but make Day 3, 5 and 7 visible prizes: Day 3 a free Instant Hatch credit (`PaidTimers.HatchCredits` already exists), Day 5 a free Speckled/Spotted egg ready on the plot, Day 7 a guaranteed Rare+ pet with the full hatch reveal. Effort M · Impact High.
3. **Offline earnings are the strongest return hook and are presented as a toast.** `grantOffline` fires one `Notify` 3 s after load (`EconomyService.lua:70-81, 93`); no modal, count-up, cap message ("8 h max") or pointer to what to buy. `earned > 0` has no floor, so a 10-second rejoin shows a "$3" toast. `LastSeen` is only written in `PlayerRemoving` (`DataService.lua:209`), so after a server crash the next offline payment starts from the older stamp and re-pays the whole online period (capped at 8 h). [Static]
   - Fix: on join when away >= 60 s, show a "While you were away (8 h, FULL)" card: money counts up over 2 s with coin sounds, plus a "Spend it" button that opens the wheel/pen board; on leave, show "Your pets are full in 8 h". Write `LastSeen` every autosave (60 s). Do not nerf the amount; it is the carrot. Effort S · Impact High.
4. **Nothing is left for the kid to come back to at the end of Day 1.** Eggs hatch in 3-60 s at size 1 (`Pets.lua` HatchSeconds), and even a size-10 Moon Rabbit is 1,200 x 10 = 12,000 s = 3.3 h ([Computed], `MaxHatchMultiplier = 10`). The Daily is claimed inside the tutorial, so the next reward is invisible except as a modal countdown (`DailyController.lua:461`); there is no HUD chip. The only appointments are session-bound (5-minute egg reset, server events). [Static]
   - Fix: at tutorial complete, place a free "Sleepy Golden Egg" on the plot with `HatchSeconds = 8 h` (hatching already uses real time, `os.time() - PlacedAt + Bonus`, `tutorial.md` secondEgg) and say "Back tomorrow - it hatches while you sleep"; add an always-on HUD chip "Daily in 14:22:10 / READY". Move the first Daily claim to the next join (plan D3). Effort S · Impact Very high.
5. **Quest list wipes at UTC midnight mid-session and claiming silently fails.** `ensureQuests` regenerates on a 30 s tick (`RetentionService.lua:185-196, 462-465`); `claimQuest` just `return`s if the list rolled over (`:252-254`) with no toast, and the client shows no "new quests" message. A kid who finishes a quest at 4:59 pm PDT and taps Claim at 5:01 pm loses it. [Static]
   - Fix: at rollover auto-pay finished unclaimed quests (or keep `quests.Prev` claimable for 6 h) and send a "New quests!" toast. Effort S · Impact Medium.
6. **Day-2 shock: newbie protection burns in real time, including offline.** `ProtectedUntil = os.time() + 600` is set on first join (`PvPService.lua:63,194-198`) and tested with `os.time() < data.ProtectedUntil or next(data.Index) == nil` (`:191`). After the first hatch the Index is non-empty, so a kid who leaves at 4 min returns unprotected. Bats work in the wild corridor past the Meadow (`PvPService.lua:10-12`). [Static]
   - Fix: count only played seconds (store `ProtectedLeft`, decrement each second while online) and raise it to 900 s or until the first Riverbank delivery, whichever is later. Effort S · Impact Medium-High.
7. **A new account's quest list can contain quests it cannot finish on Day 1.** At load: income 0, PetCount 0, so allowed kinds are RollKg 3, HatchRare 2, KnockLoose 2, Escape 3, Deliver 3, Hatch 3 (`Retention.lua` Kinds + `kindAllowed`). Drawing 3 distinct kinds by weight, P(KnockLoose) = 40% and P(KnockLoose or HatchRare) = 69% ([Computed]). KnockLoose needs a bat hit on another player's egg, but protected players cannot bat (`PvPService.lua:6-9`). HatchRare needs Rare+, 14% per Meadow hatch (Fox 9 + Owl 4 + Goose 1, `PSY_FACTS.md`), about 7 hatches expected. One reroll per day. No bonus for finishing all 3. [Static/Computed]
   - Fix: until `TutorialStep >= Done` plus 3 days, draw only Deliver / Hatch / RollKg / Escape; add a "3 of 3 done" chest (a free egg). Effort S · Impact Medium.
8. **There is no off-platform or social return path.** No `UserNotification`, no `PromptGameInvite`, no favourite/like/group prompt anywhere (grep: no match). Only 3 hard-coded codes (LAUNCH, EGGROLL, THANKYOU), no `Expires`, and no place in the game that shows kids where codes come from (`Codes.lua:14-18`). [Static] The leader game (per `KEEP_DesignNotes.101_RESEARCH.html:141,294`) ties a Speed gift to like, favourite and group join; I could not verify Roblox policy on rewarding likes, so check before copying the reward.
   - Fix: one-time, non-rewarded card after the first Rare+ hatch: "Tap the star to Favourite so you can find us tomorrow" + "Join the group for codes"; add dated codes (use `Expires`) and show "New code!" in the lobby. I could not confirm whether Roblox experience notifications work for under-13 players; check Creator Hub before planning around them. Effort S · Impact Medium.
9. **Scheduled events are not appointments.** The scheduler runs from server start: first Capture at 8 min, Boss 14 min, Night Run 25 min, then every 20/30/45 min (`EventService.lua:41-48, 232-256`). `NextCaptureAt/NextBossAt/NextNightRunAt` are written (`:257-259`) but never read by any client (grep: only EventService). A kid cannot see "Boss in 12:40", cannot plan to return for it, and different servers run on different clocks. [Static]
   - Fix: read the attributes into a small HUD chip (XS); for return value, align one event to the UTC clock (for example the Boss at :00 and :30 of every hour on every server) and add a lobby sign "Today's event" so friends can agree a time. Effort S · Impact Medium.
10. **Return moments are noisy.** A finished quest fires two toasts: server `Notify` (`RetentionService.lua:212, 237`) and client `HudController.Toast` (`QuestsController.lua:819`). The "Welcome back" toast competes with hint pills and tutorial text. `claimDaily` toast ("Day N reward: +$2.9K, +60 Speed") is the only celebration outside the modal. [Static]
   - Fix: delete one of the two quest toasts, queue the Welcome-back card before other toasts for the first 5 s after load. Effort XS · Impact Low.

### What's good
All paying paths are server-side: `RequestClaimDaily`, `RequestClaimQuest`, `RequestRedeemCode` re-check state and are rate-limited (`RetentionService.lua:375-394`), and the reward is computed server-side from live income (`:157`). The Daily modal is well-built for kids: a flip-card calendar, a visible Day 7 jackpot tile and a live "Next reward in HH:MM:SS" countdown (`DailyController.lua:451-466`). Rewards scale with income and Speed so they stay relevant later (`Retention.DailyReward`), and offline pay needs no action (50%, 8 h cap, includes boosts via `Multipliers.Money`). The "Biggest egg today" board resets daily (`BiggestEggService.Core.lua:38`), a ready-made Day 2 reason to check back. Eggs hatch on real time (`os.time()`), so offline-hatch hooks are cheap to add.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Add a free 8-hour "sleepy" egg and an always-on "Daily in HH:MM:SS" HUD chip at tutorial complete, so Day 1 ends with a reason to return (S, Very high).
2. Replace the UTC calendar-day streak with a rolling 20 h / 44 h window plus one free grace day, and make Day 3/5/7 pay visible pets or eggs, not just numbers (S-M, High).
3. Turn the offline toast into a "While you were away: +$X (8 h, FULL)" count-up card with a "Spend it" button, and make newbie protection count played seconds so Day 2 isn't a bat hit (S, High).
