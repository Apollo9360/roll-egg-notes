## 6. Zones, guards and the world as a challenge (5/10)
Six zones exist and are all built (Meadow, Riverbank, Canyon, Snow Peaks, Volcano, Cosmic Rift; `Shared.Config.Biomes`, `Workspace.World.Biomes.*`), and the guard speed ladder plus chase feedback are well made. But the world never tells a kid where to go next (all six zone entrance signs are buried 84 studs underground), zones 2-6 are the same corridor with a longer walk, one guard move is dead code, and the 10-second night silently destroys the egg you are carrying while telling you it "rolled back to its nest".

Labels: [Measured] = read from a Studio instance or a screenshot in the facts folder, [Computed] = my arithmetic from config values (assumptions stated), [Static] = read from code, [Opinion] = judgement. No playtest was run. The "zone 1 is ~11 s from spawn" and "tutorial" facts come from `world.md`/`tutorial.md`.

### Problems, ranked
1. **The only place that says "go here next / this zone needs X Speed" is invisible.** [Measured] `Workspace.World.Biomes.<Zone>.EntranceSign.Position.Y = -84` for all six zones (the sign frame/beam model pivots at Y -82.8, so the whole assembly is underground). The text lives on that part ("Riverbank Zone | Needs 300 Speed", `SurfaceGui`). No script moves it (grep `EntranceSign`: only the DevTools builders). Nothing else surfaces `RequiredSpeed` to the player: the client only uses it for the Index gate list (`IndexController`:193). There is also no zone-entry banner, no HUD "next zone" goal, and the post-tutorial pointer is one toast (`TutorialService`:58).
   - Fix: raise each EntranceSign (and its frame) to about TopY+16 (the builder used y+16, `GrayboxBuilder`:618) so the gate shows from the previous zone; add a client "Entering <Zone>" banner on `BiomeAt` change with the guard name and "Needs N Speed (you: M)"; add a HUD goal pill "Next zone: Riverbank - Speed 36 / 300" after tutorial step 16. Effort S · Impact Very high.

2. **Zone 2 does not feel new, and zones 3-6 are the same level with a longer treadmill.** [Static]/[Measured] Every zone uses one mirrored layout: 150-wide flat corridor, 5-nest ring at x=226, guard post 14 studs past the nest at x=234, nest 40 studs before the far edge (`Config.Biomes` header; positions measured: Riverbank nest (226,-74) guard (234,-88); Canyon (226,-304)/(234,-318); Cosmic (226,-1174)/(234,-1188)). Props differ per zone (Riverbank 258 models: cattails, lily pads, pines, pond; Canyon mesas/cacti/bones; Snow igloos/ice; Volcano lava pools/vents; Cosmic crystals/planets; [Measured]) and that is real variety to the eye, but there are no route choices, no hazards on the path (`Workspace.Model.Spikes` has 0 scripts or touch parts and sits at (-3427, 512, 4408), outside the map), and no per-zone lighting, sky or music (the only Lighting writers are `EventController` night look and `ModalFx` blur). One-way nest distance from the safe line: Meadow 80, Riverbank 250, Canyon 480, Snow 740, Volcano 1,030, Cosmic 1,350 studs [Computed from nest z minus 176]. The kid's whole challenge is a straight run, then one guard at the end. Screenshot 03 also shows white star sparkles in the Meadow daytime sky (the shared `Workspace.Atmosphere` emitter box).
   - Fix: give Riverbank one mechanic Meadow lacks, for example a 14-stud stream the egg must cross with 2 bridges 50 studs apart (water = 0.6x walk speed, using `SpeedService.SetSlow`), and give each later zone one equivalent (Canyon: dust devil that pushes sideways; Snow: ice patches with low friction; Volcano: lava vents on a 3 s timer). Add a per-zone Lighting/Atmosphere/music tween on zone change (about 20 lines in a client controller). Effort M · Impact High.

3. **The 10-second night destroys your egg, and the message is false.** [Static] `EggService`:1026-1042: at night start every player inside the corridor loses the carried egg (`discardEgg`, not `returnToNest`) and is teleported home, with the toast "Night fell! Your egg rolled back to its nest." The egg is deleted (`destroyEgg` clears the nest slot), and 10 s later `DayStarted` → `RefillNests` (:909-917, :1022-1025) re-rolls all nests with new random variants and sizes. A Golden/Rainbow/Huge egg or a 40-second Snow run is gone for good. Exposure per run is about run time / 300 s [Computed, assumes the night start is uniform in time]: Meadow ~11 s = 4%; Riverbank ~18 s = 6%; Canyon ~24 s = 8%; Volcano ~37 s = 12%; Cosmic ~47 s = 16%. The warning is a corner timer captioned "NEW EGGS IN" (reads as good news), plus a hint shown once per account when the first night is under 25 s away (`HintController`:181-187, `Tutorial.Hints.Night`). No per-night toast, sound or screen pulse for carriers.
   - Fix: (a) when night starts, return the carried egg to its exact nest slot (`returnToNest`) and make `refillNests` top up only empty nests, so a kid never loses a rolled egg; (b) 15 s before night, if `CarryingEgg` is set and the player is in the corridor, flash "NIGHT IN 15 - GET HOME!" with a ticking sound and tint the timer red; (c) rename the caption "NEW EGGS IN" to "NIGHT IN" for carriers. Effort S · Impact High.

4. **River Croc's Lunge can never fire.** [Static]+[Measured] `GuardService`:465-471 catches the thief when flat distance <= `HitReach + max(Body.X, Body.Z)/2`. Croc Body is 6x4x12 [Measured], so reach = 5 + 6 = 11 studs. `behave()` only starts a Lunge when `dist <= Range` (:299-300), and `Config.Biomes` Riverbank `Behaviour.Range = 10`. The catch test runs first every frame, so no one is ever at 10 studs and uncaught: the "signature move" for zone 2 is dead and the 0.25 s windup flash never plays. Zone 2 is therefore "Hen with speed 26". (Scorpion TailStrike works: it starts between reach and reach+3.)
   - Fix: set Riverbank `Range = 18` (reach 11 + 7). It only triggers when a kid stumbles inside 18 studs, so it adds a readable danger without changing the escape maths for a kid who runs straight. Effort XS · Impact Med.

5. **Zone gates do not match the real difficulty: zone 2-3 are open far below the sign, zones 5-6 are caught even at the sign.** [Computed] Escape needs average player speed >= guard speed x L/(L+gap0), where L is the nest-to-safe-line distance, gap0 is ~14 studs plus wake time x player speed, and egg drag is `1/(1+0.12*(size-1))` (`Curves.DragMultiplier`, `Config.Economy` Egg.DragPerSize). Using the mean egg (soft cap ~0.83 x MaxSize, `Economy.Egg.Cap`) and walk speed `16+10*log10(S/10)`: Riverbank is passable from Speed ~80-100 (sign says 300); Canyon ~2.6K (sign 8K); Snow ~56K (sign 60K, correct); Volcano ~1.0M (sign 400K); Cosmic ~30M (sign 2.5M, ~12x short, because the Star Whale averages ~63 studs/s with its dash: 60 x (4.5+0.4x0.3+0.6x2)/5.5, vs a 70 walk x ~0.75 drag). Egg size varies a lot (cap factor 0.45-1.75), so treat these as +-30%. Effect: kids who reach Volcano/Cosmic on paper get caught, and kids at Speed 100 could already be farming Riverbank.
   - Fix: pick one source of truth. Either set `RequiredSpeed` to Riverbank 100, Canyon 3K, Snow 60K, Volcano 1M, Cosmic 30M (and update the sign text), or ease the guards: Volcano `WalkSpeed` 53→46, Cosmic 60→50 and Dash `SpeedMult` 2→1.6. Effort XS · Impact Med.

6. **Guard threat is real only at the far end of each zone, and the first guard is harmless.** [Computed]/[Static] Mother Hen 13 (+ hop 1.6x for 0.5 s every 4.5 s = ~13.9 avg) vs a Speed-10 kid at 16 walk and drag ~0.9 after growth: the hen never closes the ~33-stud head start (14 studs + 1.2 s x 16). So the kid learns "guards are scary-looking but I always win". Zones 2-4 then jump to "barely escapable" (margins computed in #5). Other personalities: Yeti pound makes the yeti stop dead for 0.5 s (`WINDUP_SPEED.GroundPound = 0`) and only slows players within 14 studs while its catch reach is 10, so it mostly helps the thief; Magma Golem lava patches are dropped at the golem's own feet behind the fleeing thief (`dropLava`, :229-236) so they rarely touch the thief; Star Whale dash catches anyone within ~84 studs every 5.5 s. Failure cost is low (egg returns to nest; no loss), which is kind for kids but removes stakes. A second thief during a chase "gets away clean" (`wake`, :398-400), which could be a fun decoy mechanic but is invisible.
   - Fix: give the hen one real moment: a 2.2x hop-lunge when the thief is within 12 studs (Hop `SpeedMult` 1.6→2.2 for 0.6 s) so a kid who zigzags or stops gets caught once. Give Volcano lava a forward effect (drop patches 12 studs AHEAD of the thief's path, or fire the pool at the thief's feet). Tell friends: show "You distracted the guard!" credit when a second player steals during someone else's chase. Effort S · Impact Med.

7. **The tutorial promises a mechanic that no longer exists.** [Static] Step 2 text: "Kite the Mother Hen for more" (`Config.Tutorial`:38) and `GuardService`:9 says "EggService uses ChasedBy for kiting growth", but `EggService` never reads `ChasedBy` (grep: only HudController, GuardFxController, GuardService, RetentionService). Growth is per stud walked anywhere (`EggService`:855-890), so luring the hen around does nothing. The text is also 27 words against the ~5-word yardstick.
   - Fix: replace with "Run to the gold line!" (5 words) and delete the "kiting" comment, or implement a real +25% growth while `ChasedBy` is set. Effort XS · Impact Low-Med.

8. **Nest depletion and the "new eggs" reset have no feedback, and the tutorial can dead-end on an empty nest.** [Static] Each zone has 5 nest eggs, never respawned until the 300 s refill (`EggService`:313-328, :1022-1025); nothing client-side shows an empty nest or a refill (the `NestBeacon` pillar has no client reference; `Refilled` has only server listeners). Tutorial step 1/11 beam points at the Meadow nest centre even when no Meadow egg exists (`TutorialController`:121-126), so with other players draining the Meadow a new kid can run 11 s to an empty nest and wait up to ~5 min (tutorial steps 1-4 and 11 all need a nest egg). Only 5 plots exist (`world.md`), so 3-5 players per server is the realistic case, enough to drain 5 eggs in a few runs.
   - Fix: toast "New eggs! 5/5 ready" on refill; show eggs left per nest zone on the HUD timer ("Meadow 2/5"); while TutorialStep is 1 or 11 and the Meadow has 0 eggs, spawn a personal tutorial egg (client-visible only to that player) in a free nest slot. Effort M · Impact Med.

9. **The night look and the night wall are crude.** [Static]/[Measured] Day is a fixed noon (`ClockTime 11.6`, `world.md`) for 290 s and night lasts 10 s (`Economy.Cycle`), so ~97% of play has one look and the 3 s tween in + 3 s tween out (`EventController` NIGHT_TWEEN, :71, :525-534) leave only ~4 s of full dark. `NightWall` (Workspace.World.Walls.NightWall: pos (188,15,132), rotated, 150 x 30 x 102 studs, dark blue, transparency 0.35 at night, `WorldService`:33-36) turns collidable BEFORE `NightStarted` teleports players, so anyone inside the Meadow front half (z 81-183) is briefly embedded in a solid 30-stud block. Night also silently gives every unfinished incubating egg +19 s per second (`NightGrowthMultiplier = 20`, `PenService`:945-947, ~190 s per night), which is a lovely reward that is never mentioned.
   - Fix: say it: "Night! Your eggs hatch 20x faster for 10 s" as a toast and timer caption; lengthen night to 20 s with a visible moon/sky; fire `NightStarted` (teleport) before `CanCollide = true` on the wall. Effort S · Impact Med.

10. **Mid-session PvP begins at zone 2 with no boundary, and the shield is clock time.** [Static] `PvPService`:62, 143-149: bats and traps work everywhere in the corridor except Meadow; protection is `ProtectedUntil = os.time() + 600` or "empty Index" (:191), so a kid who plays 8 min on Day 1 and comes back Day 2 has no shield, and zone 2 (a "new" zone for them) is where other players can knock their egg loose. No signage tells them the Meadow/Riverbank line is also a PvP line.
   - Fix: count shield time while in-game only (accumulate per-second while a character exists), and add a one-line banner on first Riverbank entry: "Players can bat you here." Effort S · Impact Low-Med.

### What's good
The chase feedback is genuinely strong for kids: Zzz over sleeping guards visible to 140 studs, a red "!" with a pop, a yellow/red flash on each move telegraph, chase music, an RUN banner with a red pulse vignette, a pulsing ground arrow home, a camera shake and knockback on catch (`GuardFxController`:88-311, `FeelController`). The speed ladder (Config.Biomes guard speeds are ~85-87% of each gate's walk speed, and gates step ~7-27x) gives a clear "get faster to go deeper" reason to use the wheel, and zone 4 (Snow) is correctly tuned [Computed]. Each zone has its own 220-260 props and its own nest beacon, so the far-off coloured pillars (screenshot 04) work as visible goals from the lobby, and a catch is gentle (egg returns to the nest, no currency loss).

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Raise all six EntranceSigns from Y=-84 to ~Y+16 and add an "Entering <Zone> - Needs N Speed (you: M)" banner plus a HUD "Next zone" goal pill after the tutorial.
2. Stop night from deleting carried eggs (return to the same nest slot, refill only empty nests) and add a 15-second "NIGHT IN 15 - GET HOME!" warning for carriers; fix the false "rolled back to its nest" text.
3. Make zone 2 mechanically new (stream crossing with bridges + slow water) and fix the dead Croc Lunge (`Range` 10→18) so each zone teaches one new thing instead of just being longer.
