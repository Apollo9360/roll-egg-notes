## 4. Economy and pacing (6/10)
The early and mid economy moves fast and has a good event cadence (something to buy or hatch about every 46 s in minutes 2-15), but the first five minutes are a flat $92-$180/s plateau fed mostly by gifts, and the whole upgrade ladder is exhausted by about minute 69 with Rebirth too cheap to act as a sink. Hour 2 has nothing to buy. Server-wide egg supply (5 nest eggs per zone per 300 s, shared by everyone) is the hidden cap on every player's income.

No playtest was run. Every number below is [Computed] from the configs and service code unless tagged otherwise. Simulation script (Python, 300 seeded runs per scenario): `/private/tmp/claude-501/critic4_econ/sim.py` (+ `run.py`).

### Assumptions and formulas ([Computed])

Player model and constants:
- Solo server, joined exactly at a Day start (phase 0 of the 290 s day + 10 s night cycle, `Economy.Cycle`). Never AFK, no codes, no friends, no passes, no quests, no events, no Fuse, no selling, no Rebirth.
- Tutorial followed exactly. Steps 1-7: first walk collects 13 pickups x +2 Speed = +26 (`FirstWalkService:18`), so Speed 36 and walk 21.6. Steal at the Meadow nest, roll home, place, 5 s starter hatch (`Economy.Egg.FirstEggHatchSeconds`), first egg is always an Owl with +0.5 size (`PenService:510-521`). Wheel bought when $100 is reached. Step 8 Index claim, step 9 Daily claim, step 10 9 s timer. Steps 11-15: a second Meadow egg with hatch at least 40 s, rolled in the wheel at 3x timer. Step 16: Green Trail at the lobby stand.
- Distances (all x1.10 for path wobble). Nest positions measured from the Edit datamodel via Luau, 5 `Nest#` parts in every zone [Static]: nest centre z = 96.2 / -73.8 / -303.8 / -563.8 / -853.8 / -1173.8 for Meadow / Riverbank / Canyon / Snow / Volcano / Cosmic. Own plot floor front edge z = 284 (`world.md`). One-way run nest to plot = 207 / 394 / 647 / 933 / 1252 / 1604 studs. Safe line z = 176.25.
- Egg supply: nests refill only on `DayStarted` (`EggService:1022-1025`); `spawnNestEgg` is only called from `refillNests` (:325) and `SpawnRare` (:949). A stolen nest stays empty until the next day. Solo case: 5 eggs per zone per 300 s.
- Walk speed: `WalkSpeed = 16 + 10*log10(Speed/10)`, capped 160 (`Curves.WalkSpeedFromSpeed`). Carry drag `1/(1+0.12*(size-1))` (`Curves.DragMultiplier`).
- Egg size after x studs rolled: `s = s0 + (span/3) * ln(1 + 3*g*x/span)` where `span = cap - s0`, g = Biome.GrowthPerStud. This is the closed form of `ds/dx = g*exp(-3(s-s0)/span)` (`EggService:889`, `Curves.GrowthFactor`, `Economy.Egg.GrowthDecay = 3`). Start size = U(0.8,1.3) + variant bonus. Cap = Biome.MaxSize x (0.45 + 1.3*u^2.4) x variant cap multiplier. Variant weights 44/22/14/10/6/3/1 (`EggVariants`).
- Pet pick: weight x min(max(relLuck,1),5)^min(tier-1,4), relLuck = (1 + 0.75*(size-1)) x (1 + variant luck) (`Curves.LuckAdjustedTable`). Pet income = Base x size^1.6 x mutation (`Curves.PetIncome`; Shiny 1.25, Golden 2, Rainbow 3). Only placed pets earn (`PenService:822-834`), slots 4/6/8/10/12/14 (`Economy.Pen`). "BEST" is used so the pen always holds the top earners.
- Hatch seconds = ceil(HatchSeconds x min(max(size,1)^0.8, 10)) (`PenService:521`). Incubating eggs gain +1 s per second, plus +19 s per second during the 10 s night (`PenService:945-946`), so an egg makes 490 s of progress per 300 s cycle (x1.63). Max 6 incubating eggs (`Economy.MaxIncubatingEggs`). After the tutorial the player does not carry eggs in the wheel (that 3x trick is taught once, then ignored).
- Cash gifts: Index claim = Pets.IndexReward Speed and $ per newly discovered pet (`IndexService`), capped by `SpeedRoom` (stays below the second gate above current Speed) and by the next Rebirth cost. The claim is done 8 s after each hatch with a new pet. Daily D1 = max(250, income x 60 x 2) at the moment of claim (`RetentionService:157`, `Retention.DailyReward`, `Days[1]`).
- Spending policy: always the cheapest affordable of {next wheel, next pen only if unplaced pets exist, next trail}. Time cost 3 s at the plot boards, 14 s round trip to the Trail Stand. The player stands in the wheel whenever nothing else is possible. Wheel Speed per second = 2 x tierMultiplier x trailMult x (1 + 0.5 x rebirths) (`SpeedService:92-95`, `Multipliers.Treadmill`).
- Zone choice: deepest zone whose `RequiredSpeed` is met, with enough day left to finish before night.
- Guard chase (scenarios D, E, F): guard starts 16 studs from the nest, waits `WakeSeconds`, then closes at (guardSpeed - walk x drag(size along the path)); caught if gap <= reach (Croc 10 for the lunge, Scorpion 6+3, others `HitReach`; Whale dash averaged to 68 studs/s). The chase runs to the safe line. A caught player loses the egg and spends ~8 s plus half the distance walking back. After 2 consecutive catches in a zone the player avoids it until Speed is at least 2x the gate. This model is my own simplification of `GuardService`, not a replay of it.
- Not modeled: Friend boost (+10% per friend), quests, codes, daily D2-D7, Boss / Capture / Night Run events, rare and Huge eggs, offline income, other players stealing from nests (only in E), and runs that straddle the 10 s night (the sim always finishes a run before dusk; a real kid sometimes loses the egg and is teleported home).

### Problems, ranked
1. **Egg supply is 5 eggs per zone per 5 minutes for the whole server, and the tutorial's first step needs one of them.** [Static] `EggService:1022-1025` refills nests only on `DayStarted` (every 300 s, `WorldService:90-99`); `spawnNestEgg` has no other caller except `SpawnRare` (:949); a stolen nest stays empty (an egg only returns to a nest if dropped, `returnToNest`:335). There are 5 plots, so up to 5 players share 5 Meadow eggs. [Computed] In scenario E (2 eggs/zone/cycle) income is 36% lower at min 15 ($5.4K vs $8.4K) and 45% lower at hour 1 ($1.41M vs $2.55M). Players 6+ have no plot and no pen at all (`world.md`: 5 plots, `Players.MaxPlayers` 60 in Studio).
   - Fix: respawn each nest 20-30 s after it is emptied, and give tutorial steps 1-5 a private first egg (client-visible only to that player, never taken by others). Keep the day refill for variety. Effort S-M · Impact Very high.
2. **Income is flat for the first 4-5 minutes and the early cash is gifts, not play.** [Computed] Income goes $92 (0:35), $97 (2:00), $122 (3:00), $187 (5:00), then x6.9 by 7:00 ($1.3K). By min 5 pets have earned $35K total but gifts paid $13.8K (Daily D1 $11.1K + Index $2.7K). The tutorial's second egg is always from the Meadow nest (`TutorialController` NestEgg beam, `Tutorial` step Steal2) and is worth a median $26/s (p25-p75 $8-$80) while the average Riverbank egg is $1,155/s (median $512/s) and the gate for it was already passed at 0:53. First Riverbank hatch is at ~5:00 (median hatch 244 s).
   - Fix: point Steal2 at Riverbank when Speed >= 300 (it already is at step 11), and cap the first three Riverbank egg timers at 90 s (e.g. Heron 120 to 60, Beaver 75 to 40, Swan Queen 240 to 90 in `Config.Pets`) so the first big income jump lands by about 3:30 (estimate) instead of 5:00. Effort S · Impact High.
3. **Money is $0 for the first ~35 s and the first purchase cannot happen before 0:38; a slow kid (scenario F) is at 1:08.** [Computed] `Economy.StartingMoney = 0` (Economy line ~22); first pet placed at 0:35 (F: 1:03); wheel bought 3 s later. Yardstick: first purchase within 5-10 s and a number going up from second 1 (Speed is the only number moving, and only on the first-walk pickups).
   - Fix: `StartingMoney = 100` and make tutorial step "BuyWheel" the first step, so the wheel drops from the sky and Speed ticks at ~0:05, before the egg hunt; the Owl then pays for Shell Wheel. Effort XS · Impact High.
4. **Jackpot pets take 20-97 minutes to hatch, and 14-21% of eggs from Canyon onward are Mythic.** [Computed] Median egg size per zone (3.3 / 4.2 / 5.2 / 6.1 / 7.2 for Riverbank to Cosmic) at hatch = HatchSeconds x size^0.8: Thunderbird 31 min raw (19 min with night bonus), Ice Dragon 45 (28), Phoenix 59 (36), Nova Unicorn 77 (47), Moon Rabbit 97 (59). Pet odds at those sizes (relLuck 2-5, exponent capped at 4): Canyon Thunderbird 14%, Snow Ice Dragon 18%, Cosmic Nova Unicorn 20%, Secret 0.6-2.3% (so "Mythic" is a common drop, not a rare one). Only Robux Instant Hatch (R$ 49 for 10 min, R$ 149 for 30 min) shortens it. Max 6 incubating eggs; 4-5 of the 6 slots are busy at every checkpoint from min 15 on, so the player waits.
   - Fix: `HatchExponent` 0.8 to 0.55 and `MaxHatchMultiplier` 10 to 3, and halve Mythic base times (Thunderbird 600 to 300, Ice Dragon 720 to 360, Phoenix 840 to 420): Thunderbird at size 4.2 goes from 31 min to ~11 min raw (4.17^0.55 = 2.19; 2.19 x 300 s = 657 s; ~6.7 min with the night bonus). Effort S · Impact High (average session is 15-20 min; the first Mythic currently pays off after it).
5. **Speed gates fall silently and Speed comes mostly from Index claims, so the zone signs and wheel purchases feel meaningless.** [Computed] Speed at min 15: 12.5K from Index claims vs 10K from the wheel; at min 5: 570 claims vs 335 wheel (the kid is running eggs, not standing in the wheel). Gate 300 is crossed at 0:53 by the Owl claim (+320). No gate announcement exists: grep for "zone unlocked" returns 0 hits in live scripts. `UpgradeHintController` (lines 41-77) only toasts affordability of Wheel/Pen/Trail, once per item, muted until the tutorial ends (:83). There is no "$ x / $ y" progress ticker toward the next buy except tutorial step 16 (`SaveFor`) [Static].
   - Fix: on crossing a `RequiredSpeed`, fire a full-screen "RIVERBANK UNLOCKED" toast plus a beam to that zone's gate; add a one-line HUD goal "Next: Blue Trail $12,400 / $75,000" under the Money stat. Effort S · Impact High.
6. **From about minute 69 there is nothing to buy, and Rebirth is far too cheap to act as the next goal.** [Computed] Wheel L6 at min 32, Pen L6 at min 19.5, Galaxy Trail at 43, Rainbow Trail $4B at 69. Hour 2 income $39M/s with ~$71B unspent (range $17B-$695B across scenarios). `Rebirth.Cost = 250,000 x 3.5^n`: the first three rebirths ($250K, $875K, $3.06M) cost about 1 s of hour-1 income ($2.55M/s); n=8 ($5.6B) is ~37 min of hour-1 income but only ~2.4 min at hour-2 income. Each rebirth adds only +0.5 (additive) and resets Speed, wheel, pen but not income (placed pets back to 4 slots, then re-bought in seconds). Income grows only x15 from hour 1 to hour 2 (min 60 $2.5M to min 120 $39M). It is first affordable at min 10 when a kid is mid-grind.
   - Fix: add Wheel T7-T9 ($500M / $5B / $50B), Pen L7-L9 (16/18/20 slots, $30M / $300M / $3B) and rescale Rebirth to `BaseCost 50M x 8^n`. Also require Speed >= 400,000 (Volcano gate) to rebirth, so the $250K button at min 10 is not a trap. Effort M · Impact High (hour-2 retention).
7. **At gate-level Speed the zone guards outrun a carrier with a grown egg from Riverbank onward.** [Computed] End-of-run carrier speed at exactly the gate Speed and the median final egg size vs guard speed: Riverbank 30.8 x 0.786 = 24.2 vs 26; Canyon 45.0 x 0.725 = 32.6 vs 38; Snow 53.8 x 0.663 = 35.7 vs 46; Volcano 62.0 x 0.621 = 38.5 vs 53; Cosmic 70.0 x 0.574 = 40.2 vs 60 (68 with dashes). The `Biomes` comment computes the guard at 85-87% of gate walk speed but ignores drag (`Economy.Egg.DragPerSize`). In scenario D (guards on) hour-1 income is 4.2x lower than scenario A (guards off): $2.55M vs $10.7M; median 15 catches per player in the first hour, almost all in Volcano and Cosmic. Rough model: treat as direction, not exact. See also the guards critic.
   - Fix: lower guard speeds to ~65% of gate walk (Snow 46 to 35, Volcano 53 to 40, Cosmic 60 to 45) or show "Safe at N Speed" (about 2x the sign) on each entrance sign. Effort XS · Impact Med.
8. **Several purchases change nothing the player can see or feel.** [Computed]
   - Shell and Speckled wheels ($1.5K at 2:11, $20K at 3:14): wheel Speed totals 335 by min 5 because the kid is running eggs, so the counter barely moves; only the model re-skin changes.
   - Pen L2 ($300) at min 5 with 2-3 pets does nothing: it needs a 5th pet, which arrives at min ~5-6 (`PenService:755`, `setPlaced` only blocks above slots).
   - Rainbow Trail ($4B) arrives at 69 min, 4 min after the last Speed gate (Cosmic, 65 min): purely cosmetic. The Galaxy Trail ($250M, min 43) comes while Speed is still 984K, below the last gate, so it still matters.
   - Every wheel tier is a Speed/s number: walk speed is logarithmic (x10 Speed = +10 walk), so wheel T6 over T5 (x3.3 gain) adds about +5 walk speed.
   - Fix: pop a "+6 Speed/s" and "+3 slots" floater plus camera shake on wheel/pen purchase, show the hatch-rate bonus on the wheel (x3, +0.25/tier), and give Galaxy and Rainbow trails a visible gameplay effect (e.g. +egg growth rate). Effort S · Impact Med.
9. **Daily D1 pays $250 or ~$11K depending on when the kid presses the button.** [Static] `RetentionService:157` calls `Retention.DailyReward(NextDay, income(player), data.Speed)` at claim time; `Days[1] = { CashMinutes = 2, CashMin = 250 }`. A kid who taps Daily at 0:20 (before the Owl is placed) burns the Day 1 reward for $250 instead of ~$8-14K; the HUD Daily button exists from spawn (`ui.md`). [Opinion] Day-1 reward should be the best moment of the first 2 minutes.
   - Fix: `CashMin` for day 1 = 5,000 (and 2,500 for D2), or hide the Daily button until tutorial step 9. Effort XS · Impact Low-Med.

### What's good
Early cadence is strong: from min 2 to 15 the base case has 16 purchase or hatch events (about one every 46 s), and each upgrade price step is a consistent x10-x17 over the previous tier (wheel 100/1.5K/20K/250K/3M/50M, trail 5K/75K/1M/15M/250M/4B), which keeps the next goal 1.5-9 minutes away until about min 30. The starter Owl (`PenService:510-521`) guarantees a ~$92/s first pet, the Index claim (+320 Speed, +$1,600) gives a real 2x walk-speed jump at 0:52, and Daily D1 scales with income (`Retention.DailyReward`). Night's +19 s/s hatch bonus (`PenService:945-946`) and os.time-based eggs give a free comeback reward. The closed-form growth curve (`GrowthDecay`) keeps eggs growing without a hard cap. Between min 1 and min 30 income grows x1,100 with no softlock and each next price is reachable within 99-526 s of income at the Min 5 to Min 30 checkpoints.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Give new players a personal starter nest egg and respawn emptied nests every 20-30 s (not once per 300 s), because 5 eggs per zone per 5 minutes is shared by the whole server. Effort S-M · Impact Very high.
2. Start with $100, put "buy the wheel" first, and make the second tutorial egg a Riverbank egg with a 60-90 s timer to remove the flat $92-$187/s stretch from 0:35 to 5:00. Effort XS-S · Impact High.
3. Rescale the late game: shorter jackpot hatch timers (HatchExponent 0.8 to 0.55, Mythic base time halved), more wheel/pen tiers (T7-T9, L7-L9), and Rebirth at 50M x 8^n, so something is still worth buying after minute 69. Effort M · Impact High.
