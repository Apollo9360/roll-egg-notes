## 2. Core loop: steal, roll, escape (6/10)
The moment-to-moment feel is the strongest part of the game: grab, roar, chase music, a weight number that jumps, and a big gold "SAFE" payoff all land inside about 25 seconds. But the guard cannot actually catch anyone in the Meadow, the egg supply behind the loop is five shared eggs per five minutes, and the tutorial text tells kids to do the only thing that can make them fail.

Assumptions for all [Computed] numbers: new player Speed 10, WalkSpeed 16 (Curves.WalkSpeedFromSpeed, Economy.SpeedCurve); egg start size 0.8-1.3 (Economy.Egg.StartSize*); a median soft cap of ~2.8 (Meadow MaxSize 4 x Cap factor ~0.70); drag = 1/(1+0.12*(size-1)) (Economy.Egg.DragPerSize). No playtest was run, so nothing here is [Measured].

### Problems, ranked
1. **Nest eggs are a tiny shared supply with no feedback, so the first and second steals can dead-end.** [Static] `refillNests()` runs only at server start and at each day start (EggService l.313-328, l.1018-1025; WorldService l.88-99: 290 s day + 10 s night). A stolen egg never respawns, so each biome has 5 eggs per 300 s for the whole server. Meadow has 5 nests (Studio: Nest1-5). The tutorial needs two Meadow eggs per new player (steps 1 and 11, tutorial.md). Three ad-driven newcomers in one server can empty the ring, and the fourth sees an empty nest ring for up to ~5 min. The step-1 beam falls back to `NestCenter` when no egg exists, and the pill says "Hold E on an egg!" within 15 studs even when nothing is there (TutorialController resolveTarget; tutorial.md section 6). Solo play is also capped: 5 eggs, then a wait. Nothing tells the kid why.
   - Fix: respawn each nest egg 20 s after it is taken (Meadow only, `spawnNestEgg(biome, index)` on a timer). Reserve Nest1 as a "starter nest" that refills in 5 s while the player's TutorialStep is 1 or 11. Put a world billboard on an empty nest: "Next egg in 0:18". Effort S-M · Impact Very high.

2. **The Mother Hen cannot catch a player who runs straight, and nothing about the approach is risky.** [Computed] Hen: WalkSpeed 13, hop x1.6 for 0.5 s every 4.5 s, so average 13 x (1 + 0.6 x 0.5/4.5) = 13.9 studs/s (Biomes l.203, l.209; GuardService l.294-298). Catch reach = HitReach 4 + Body X/2 (Body is 4x5x4, measured) = 6 studs (GuardService l.465). Chase starts with a gap of ~19 studs - 6 reach + 1.2 s x 16 = ~32 studs. A new player runs at 16 x 0.916 = 14.7 at size 1.8 (without pickups) or 19.8 (with pickups), so the gap only grows. The hen outruns a rolled egg only above size ~2.28 (16/(1+0.12(s-1)) = 13.9). Even the Huge egg (start 4.6, speed 11.2) escapes with ~13 studs to spare. EggService l.120 says the designer tuned exactly that. The steal is also not a stealth moment: "Sneak up" (Tutorial step 1), but the guard only wakes when a grab happens (GuardService l.570-580), and there is no noise or proximity check. The visual drama is all theatre. A kid who runs gets a "dangerous" chase that cannot go wrong, so there is nothing to learn or master.
   - Fix: keep the first egg safe, then make the Meadow hen real. After `Stats.EggsDelivered >= 1`, raise Meadow Guard.WalkSpeed 13 to 17. Then a no-pickup runner (14.7) is caught if they stall for about 2 s, and a clean run arrives with ~10 studs spare. Add one near-miss beat: the hen's first hop lunges so it ends ~12 studs behind at the line. Effort XS · Impact High.

3. **The tutorial tells kids to "Kite the Mother Hen for more", which is false and is the only way to get caught.** [Static] Tutorial step 2 text: "Kite the Mother Hen for more" and "Your egg grows while you roll it in its zone" (Config.Tutorial l.38). EggService has no kiting bonus: growth is `credited * GrowthPerStud * growth` anywhere (EggService l.887-891), and `ChasedBy` only drives UI (grep: HudController l.1094, GuardFxController l.451, RetentionService). The Biomes header comment (l.123) is also stale. A kid who obeys circles the hen with an egg above size ~2.3 and loses it. The card is also 25 words against a 5-word yardstick.
   - Fix: replace step 2 with "Run to the gold line!" (5 words). Delete the kiting claim and fix the Biomes l.123 comment. Effort XS · Impact Med-High.

4. **Failure explains nothing, and the retry is quietly harder.** [Static] The only message is "Caught! Your egg went flying." (GuardFxController l.384). After a catch inside the zone the egg returns to the nest with its grown size, because `returnToNest` keeps `egg.Size` (EggService l.347-353). The hen is in Return (walking at 9.1 studs/s, `RETURN_SPEED_FACTOR` 0.7), so a re-grab wakes it in 0.35 s instead of 1.2 s (GuardService l.64, l.403). A kid who wanders into Riverbank is caught every time and is told nothing about Speed. The tutorial step also regresses silently at 1 Hz (TutorialService l.199).
   - Fix: make the toast cause-specific: "Hen was faster! Run in a straight line to the gold line." In Riverbank and deeper: "River Croc is too fast! Need Speed 300 (you have 36)." Reset the egg to its start size on catch, or give the hen a 1.5 s fresh wake after a catch. Effort XS · Impact Med.

5. **The danger curve is a cliff, and the zone gate is only a sign.** [Computed] Meadow catch chance is ~0 (Problem 2). Riverbank (croc 26 studs/s, lunge x2.4 for 0.35 s, 0.25 s windup) catches any player below ~Speed 300 (Biomes l.226-232). At Speed 36, walk 21.6 x 0.87 drag = 18.8, so the croc closes 7 studs/s and the kid is caught in ~4 s. RequiredSpeed is "shown on the entrance sign (soft gate)" (Biomes l.176) and nothing blocks entry. At exactly Speed 300 and size 2.5 the margin is only ~0.8 studs/s, and above size ~3.3 it is negative, so the sign number is break-even. The first Index claim (Owl +320 Speed, Pets l.21; IndexService cap allows it at Speed ~46) pushes a new player to ~366, so the tutorial happens to hand over the gate speed around steps 8-9. It is accidental and unexplained.
   - Fix: a warning toast and a pulsing red line when a player with Speed < 0.5 x RequiredSpeed crosses a zone edge ("River Croc zone: need Speed 300"). Raise the sign text to "Speed 400+ recommended". Effort S · Impact Med.

6. **Rolling feels like growth for ~100 studs, then plateaus, and size is not tied to risk.** [Computed] Size = s0 + (span/3) ln(1 + 3 x 0.018 x x/span), with span ~1.7 for a median egg. Weight (100 x size^2.2, Curves.EggWeight): ~113 kg at the nest, 346 kg at the line (77 studs), 568 kg at the plot (208 studs). The label pops, the egg diameter goes 2.6 to ~4.3 studs, "Grow" plinks. That reads well. But the same egg reaches its soft cap after ~612 studs of walking anywhere, and the guard adds no bonus, so the best play is to walk circles on the plot (EggService l.887; Economy.Egg l.381). The carry card shows `x5.0 · -8% speed · growth 62%` (HudController l.1037) but never the payoff in pet income ($/s). So the kid sees the number rise without learning what it buys.
   - Fix: show a second line on the carry card: "Pet would earn ~$X/s" using `Curves.PetIncome` with the egg's size. Give kited growth a real bonus: x1.5 growth while `ChasedBy` is set (this makes Problem 3's text true and risk pays). Effort S · Impact Med.

7. **Sound is thin and partly reused.** [Static] `Gameplay.Grow` and `Gameplay.SpeedGain` are the same asset (rbxassetid://15675046931), so "egg grew" and "Speed gained" sound identical. The SAFE moment plays LockIn (CarryController l.231) and HatchRare (HudController l.1127) together, and HatchRare is also the legendary-hatch sting (HudController l.780), which dilutes the reveal sound. `StealAlarm` is only an alias entry (SoundController l.22) and is never played. There is no rolling or footstep loop and no sound on the approach. 28 sound assets exist in total (18 Gameplay, 8 UI, 2 Music). Stock IDs could not be verified for loading offline.
   - Fix: add a distinct "egg growth" plink (pitch up with size), give SAFE its own sting, and add a soft rolling loop whose volume tracks speed. Effort S · Impact Med.

8. **Stealing at night fails silently.** [Static] `grabEgg` returns with no message when `WorldService.IsNight()` (EggService l.580). Night is 10 s of every 300 s (~3.3%), and the prompt stays visible. A kid who holds on the egg and sees nothing happen will assume the game is broken.
   - Fix: disable nest prompts at night and send `Notify` "Night! The nests sleep for 8 s." at night start. Effort XS · Impact Low-Med.

9. **Camera and phone feel details.** [Static/Opinion] Shake is random `CameraOffset` jitter each frame (GuardFxController l.392-395), and it also fires for other players' hen wakes within 120 studs (l.305-308), which will be constant in a busy Meadow. The rolled egg sits `d/2 + 1.6` ahead of you (CarryController l.480); it reaches 4.3 studs at size 2.2 and up to MaxDiameter 12 later, so on a phone it will block more of the view as it grows. Jump is disabled while rolling (EggService l.415-432) but the mobile jump button stays. The drop button is a bottom-centre card, close to where the thumb travels.
   - Fix: only shake for your own wakes (0.25 to 0), use a smooth noise shake, and fade the egg label/mesh to 70% when it blocks the screen centre. Effort S · Impact Low.

### What's good
The "juice" at each beat is genuinely above average for a kid game: wake telegraph (alert, roar, 1.2 s warning), HUD fade, a ground arrow pointing home, chase music, then a multi-layer SAFE payoff (GuardFxController; CarryController; HudController l.1119-1138). The first reward lands at ~30-35 s with a guaranteed Owl, a $100 wheel seconds later, and 13 pickups give a number going up from second 3 (Economy l.405; FirstWalkService). The failed-run design is kind: no cash loss, a 1.5 s egg return, and the player is knocked toward home (GuardService l.415-431). The weight inflation plus the honest "-N% speed" drag readout on the carry card makes growth readable (Curves.FormatWeight; HudController l.1014). The second thief gets away clean when the hen is busy (GuardService l.398-400), which creates a natural friend-decoy tactic.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Respawn nest eggs every ~20 s with a visible "next egg" timer (and a fast starter nest for tutorial steps 1 and 11), so the loop cannot dead-end for ad-driven newcomers.
2. Make the Meadow hen a fair threat after the first delivery (WalkSpeed 13 to 17) and fix the false "Kite the Mother Hen" text with "Run to the gold line!".
3. Add cause-specific failure and gate messages ("Croc is too fast! Need Speed 300") plus a carry-card $/s preview, so every failed or successful run teaches the next step.
