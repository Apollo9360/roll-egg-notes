## 7. Monetisation (5/10)
The plumbing is better than most kids' games: idempotent receipts, an in-modal "Buy X for R$ N?" confirm, live price lookup, and no fake "was" prices or fake "limited" tags. But the Luck boosts are sold with no odds shown and no PolicyService gate, which breaks Roblox's paid-random-items rule. The first purchase path is also thin (one 3 R$ item buried in the Shop), Robux prompts sit inside the tutorial, and paid timers burn while the player is offline.

One-off spend ceiling [Computed]: 2 passes (866) + 6 trails (494) + starter (3) = **1,363 R$**. Everything else is repeatable with no cap in code. The biggest single tap is 349 R$ (Speed Pack L). Offered in four places: Shop, egg Skip prompt, Trail Stand pedestals, and the Trails menu.

### Problems, ranked
1. **Paid Luck is a "probability modifier" sold with no odds and no PolicyService gate.** [Static, plus Computed] Luck15/30/60 (49/99/199 R$) multiply egg luck at placement (`EggService` line 822, `acceptDelivery` picks the pet at `PenService` line 519). Roblox's paid-random-items policy (see PSY_FACTS) requires the effect to be explained numerically before purchase and the boosted odds to be shown live while it is active.
   - Today the only text is "Eggs you bring home roll twice the luck." (Products 83, 88, 93) and a `Luck x2` pill (`BoostStripController` line 189).
   - `Curves.Odds` (Curves line 150) is defined and **never called**. The grep for `PolicyService` returns 0 hits.
   - The copy is also wrong. Because the effect is `weight x relative^(tier-1)` with relative = luck / LuckBase, "2x" at relative 1 to 2 is not twice the odds. Meadow, size-1 egg: Chick 40.0 to 21.2%, Fox 9.0 to 19.1%, Owl 4.0 to 17.0%, **Golden Goose 1.0 to 8.5%**, Eggbert 0.05 to 0.42%.
   - `LUCK_RELATIVE_CAP` 5 also wastes part of the boost. A size-4 Meadow egg has relative 3.25, so 2x gives 6.5, clamped to 5 (23% unused), with no warning.
   - Fix:
     - In each Luck row and in the confirm card, add the line "Golden Goose 1.0% to 8.5%" plus an "All odds" list from `Curves.Odds` for the player's current zone, at size 1.
     - Make the active-boost pill tappable to show the live table.
     - At `onLoaded`, `pcall(PolicyService:GetPolicyInfoForPlayerAsync)` and set a player attribute.
     - When `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` is true, hide the Luck rows and make `promptProduct` refuse `item.Timer == "Luck"`.
     - Keep the free Daily Day-7 luck, since free needs no disclosure.
   - Effort M · Impact Very high. This is a moderation and takedown risk on the products that earn most from this audience.
2. **A transient failure on join kills a paid pass for the whole session, and the Shop says "Owned".** [Static] `loadPasses` (MS 469-481) makes one `UserOwnsGamePassAsync` call per pass, with no retry. The client's own check (SC 249-260) only changes the label to "Owned" and hides the Buy pill (SC 148-150, 462), so the kid cannot re-trigger the grant either. Offline income is granted `task.delay(3)` after load (`EconomyService` line 93) using `Multipliers.Money`, so a slow check also pays offline income without the x2.
   - Fix: retry with backoff (0 / 2 / 6 / 15 s, then every 60 s while unowned), re-check on Shop open, and have the offline grant wait for `loadPasses` to finish (cap 8 s). Add a Notify "Your 2x Money pass is active" on first apply. Effort S · Impact High (399 and 467 R$ items).
3. **Paid timers run on the wall clock, including while offline.** [Static] Speed and Money use `data.PaidTimers = os.time() expiry` (MS 289), and Luck uses `daily.LuckUntil` (MS 279). A kid who buys Luck60 (199 R$) and logs off keeps only the part that was still running. `onLoaded` zeroes expired timers silently (MS 485-489), and nothing in the Shop row ("Active · m:ss left", SC 215) or the product text warns about it. Offline income is also computed after the Money30 timer has been zeroed.
   - Fix: store `remaining` on `PlayerRemoving` and rebuild the expiry on join, so timers only run while playing. If that is too big a change, add the line "Runs even when you're away" to every timed product and to the confirm card. Effort S for text, M for pause · Impact High (refund and rating risk for kids' pocket money).
4. **Robux prompts appear inside the tutorial.** [Static] The Skip prompt is enabled for any egg with time left (PenService 268-278), so it shows on the 5 s first egg and the 40 s second egg. The Trail Stand R$ 19 prompt on the tutorial's target pedestal is always enabled (TSC 85-88). YARDSTICKS: hide Robux offers during the tutorial.
   - Fix: add `and not Tutorial.InProgress(data.TutorialStep)` to `skipPrompt.Enabled` (PenService 271), and set the `RobuxPrompt` `Enabled` to `not owns and tutorialDone` (TSC 87). Effort XS · Impact Med to High (trust and tutorial completion).
5. **No Starter Pack and no first-purchase moment.** [Static] The only starter item is the 3 R$ "2x Speed (15 min)", reachable only by opening the Shop. The `UpgradeHintController` header says it never badges the Shop. After the 3 R$ item, the next rungs are 9 (a hatch worth about a minute), 19 (a trail) and 49, with no bundle. Nothing is offered after the tutorial or on Day 2. Grep for "Starter Pack" returns 0 hits.
   - Fix: add a one-time dev product, e.g. **Starter Pack 49 R$**: Green Trail (x1.5, normally 19) + Speed Pack S (49) + Luck15 or 30 min of 2x Speed, with a "Value 117 R$" note that is literally true. Show it once, when the tutorial finishes, and once on return day 2. Keep the 3 R$ item as the first rung. Effort M · Impact High.
6. **The catalogue is mis-ordered, and the power items are mispriced.** [Computed and Opinion]
   - Speed Packs are the worst value in the game: Meadow gives +75 / +225 / +600 Speed, which is 37 s / 112 s / 300 s of a tier-1 wheel. In Riverbank (span 7,700) at wheel tier 3 (16 Speed/s), S / M / L is 120 s / 6 min / 16 min. The tier-6 wheel multiplier is 200, so late-game packs are close to zero. Speed Pack L at Speed 10 gives about 610, which is over the 300 Riverbank gate, so a kid can skip the tutorial wheel loop.
   - Trails are the opposite: x8 permanent wheel gain for 199 R$ (Rainbow, cash price $4B) against x2 for 1 hour at 129 R$ (Speed60). They are listed last of 7 sections.
   - Fix: reorder to Starter, Trails, Passes, Boosts, Hatch, then packs. Rescale the packs to "minutes of your wheel gain" (S 10 / M 30 / L 90 min of current wheel rate) and show "= 30 min of wheel time" on the row. Reprice Rainbow to about 349 and Galaxy to about 199, or ship it knowingly as the whale hook. Add a 10 to 15% volume discount to Luck60 and Money30. Effort S · Impact High.
7. **Missing passes in the core loop.** [Static, with Opinion] The only passes are 2x Money and 2x Egg Growth. There is no permanent 2x Speed pass, although Speed is the central stat (gates, walk speed, and the stat every quest pays out), and Speed is sold only as timed boosts. There are no extra egg slots (`MaxIncubatingEggs = 6` is fixed, Economy line 126), no auto-hatch or auto-place, no Premium perk (grep `MembershipType` returns 0 hits), no gifting, and no VIP. Pass2xGrowth (467) is priced 17% above Pass2xMoney (399) for a stat with exponential decay (`GrowthDecay` 3), so "twice as fast" does not give twice the size.
   - Fix: add **2x Speed pass 399 R$** (permanent `Multipliers.PAID_SPEED`). Add **+3 egg slots 199 R$**. Reprice Growth to 299. Add a Premium +10% income perk on join. Effort M · Impact High.
8. **The Skip prompt invites accidental Robux taps.** [Static] The hold is 0.25 s (`Economy.Prompts.SkipHold`), key R, next to Hatch (E, 0.5 s) and Roll (F, 0.4 s) (PenService 190-233). `OnSkip` goes straight to `PromptProductPurchase` (MS 576-578), skipping the in-modal confirm that the Shop uses (SC 264-273). The tier follows the egg's remaining time, so the price can be 9, 49, 149 or 299 R$, and it shows on a 5 s egg.
   - Fix: raise `SkipHold` to 0.8 s, hide it when the egg has under 20 s left, show only HatchT1 to T3 on the prompt (T4 at 299 stays Shop-only), and route it through the same "Buy X for R$ N?" confirm. Effort XS · Impact Med.
9. **Copy mismatches and a dead listing.** [Static]
   - Traps5 (R$ 25, id 0) shows in the Shop (`Products.List(section)` without `configuredOnly`, SC 568, 600) and passes the in-modal confirm, then answers "Coming soon!" (MS 545-548).
   - The Roblox listing for Speed Packs says "+25% / +75% / +200% of your Speed", but the real formula is `max(Base, ZoneSpan x Pct)` capped by the second gate (products.md section 1).
   - The listings say "treadmill", the game says "hamster wheel". Hatch and Trail listings have an empty description. HatchT4 is listed as "Hatch All" but is "Instant Hatch" in game.
   - The "no egg ready, pay anyway" case (MS 326-330) takes the Robux and gives no credit.
   - Fix: filter the Shop with `Products.List(section, true)` so Traps5 is hidden until it has an id. Rewrite the three listing descriptions to match the game. Block Hatch purchases when no egg has a timer, instead of banking a credit. Effort XS · Impact Low to Med.
10. **There is no monetisation analytics.** [Static] Grep for `LogEconomyEvent` returns 0 hits. `ProcessReceipt` (MS 447-459), the Shop open, and `RequestPromptProduct` log nothing, so there is no data on which items are viewed, confirmed, cancelled or bought. The `OnboardingService` funnel stops at Tut17_Done.
    - Fix: `AnalyticsService:LogEconomyEvent` for each granted receipt (item key, R$), and `LogCustomEvent` for Shop open, confirm shown and confirm cancelled. Effort S · Impact Med (it decides every other tuning change).

### What's good
Receipts are correct: grant first, then store the PurchaseId, then save immediately (MS 400-444). Boosts persist across rejoin, and `max()` stops a longer token being shortened (MS 279-284). Server-side refusals stop double-buying passes, the one-time offer, trails already owned, and Money30 under the pass (MS 505-519). Every Robux button is the same blue pill with a dark stroke, at least 44 px, so it cannot be mistaken for a cash button (SC 298-307), and the Shop asks for a confirm before every purchase. All 21 live prices match the configured hints (products.md section 1), and I found no fake "was" prices or fake "limited" tags.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Add odds disclosure and a `PolicyService` gate to the three Luck products (Curves.Odds is unused; Golden Goose 1.0 to 8.5% is the number to show), because it is a policy breach on a product that sells.
2. Hide the Skip and Trail Robux prompts until `TutorialStep == 17`, and add a one-time 49 R$ Starter Pack shown at tutorial end.
3. Make paid timers pause while offline (or say clearly that they don't), and retry the pass check so a 399 or 467 R$ pass can never silently fail.
