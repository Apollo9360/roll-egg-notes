## 3. Egg growth, luck and the hatch (6/10)
The egg economy is mechanically generous and satisfying: a plain Meadow egg rolled home is Epic-or-better about 31% of the time, and the count-up and drop-in moments feel good. But the player is never told what the clover number does, the first hatch is a scripted Epic with the traits stripped, the hatch reveal barely changes between a Chick and a Golden Goose, and the size tiers are tuned so loosely that "HUGE" is the norm in Meadow.

Method note: odds below are [Computed] from `Config.Biomes`, `Config.Pets`, `Config.EggVariants`, `Config.Economy.Egg` and `Curves.LuckAdjustedTable` (Curves line 118-129) with a Monte Carlo of the growth loop in `EggService.carryTick` (lines 887-891), 60k-200k eggs per run, plain walking 190 studs (nest to plot: Plot1 spawn to nest is 179 studs, world.md). No playtest was run, so nothing here is [Measured].

### Problems, ranked
1. **The clover/luck number is never explained and never turned into odds, so the main reason to grow an egg is invisible.** [Static] `Config.Tutorial` contains no "luck", "clover" or "rare" string (searched the module source); the six hints (`Tutorial.Hints`) never mention luck; the only egg-size hint, HeavyEgg ("bigger egg, slower roll", HintController line 166-171), is negative. `Curves.Odds` (Curves line 150) is defined but called from nowhere (script grep: only the definition). The cost of a big egg is shown as a plain "-14% speed", the benefit as an unexplained "x10.2". Impact on spending: the Luck 2x boosts (R$49/99/199, `Products` lines 84-94) take a typical Meadow egg from Legendary 9% to 33% [Computed], but the kid cannot see that.
   - Fix: on the carry card and the placed-egg tag, replace or add to the number with "Epic+ 27%" computed via `Curves.Odds` (`Curves.Odds(biome.DropTable, luck, biome.LuckBase)`, sum Epic and above). Add one line to tutorial step 3: "Bigger egg = luckier pet!" (6 words). Show the token as "Epic+ 27% to 64%" on the boost pill. Effort S · Impact Very high.

2. **The displayed luck is inflated by the zone base, so it means nothing across zones and looks 2-12x bigger than it is.** [Computed] Shown luck = `LuckBase x (1 + 0.75 x (size-1)) x (1 + variantBonus)` (`Curves.EggLuck`, lines 49-53) but the drop table uses only `luck / LuckBase`, clamped to 1-5 (lines 118-129). A size-1 plain egg shows x5.0 in Meadow, x25.0 in Riverbank and x125 in Canyon; a typical size-2.4 Meadow egg shows x10.2 while its real boost is x2.05 (Legendary 1% to 9%). The 2x token doubles the shown number (x10 to x20) and the real relative luck from 2.05 to 4.1.
   - Fix: show relative luck (1.0 to 5.0, "x2.05") everywhere the clover appears, with "MAX x5" as the cap, so a kid can see that x2 means about 9% Legendary. Keep the large base only for the leaderboard flex if wanted. Effort XS · Impact Med.

3. **The hatch reveal does not escalate with rarity and the card does not show the pet.** [Static] `PlotEggController.playHatch` (lines 325-428) runs the same timeline for every pet: 3 shakes (0.84 s), 8 shards, pop (0.46 s), hover (0.25 s), fly (0.7 s). The only rarity differences are the sound name (`"HatchRare"` for Epic or better, line 389), the card stroke/sunburst colour and the arrival sparkle colour. No camera or FOV move, screen flash, longer suspense or aura exists in that file or in `HudController.hatchReveal` (lines 746-814; only `FeelController` has a camera punch, and it is catch-only). The `HatchCard` template has three text labels (Rarity, PetName, Stats) and a sunburst, no pet image (inspected instance). The card is created after `HATCH_FX_SECONDS` 2.4 s, when the 3D pet has already flown into the player. No "NEW!" line even though a first discovery pays Speed and cash in the Index (Chick: +150 Speed, +$500, `Config.Pets` IndexReward). On a 667x375 phone the card is 252x140 px (`UISizeConstraint` min).
   - Fix: add a rarity table: Common 3 shakes and 1.0 s hold; Rare 4 shakes plus a 0.15 s white flash; Epic 5 shakes, FOV +8 for 0.3 s and rarity-colour ring; Legendary 6 shakes, ground beam, slow-motion 0.4x for 0.5 s and a 4 s card hold. Put a ViewportFrame or the pet icon in the card, a "NEW! +150 Speed, +$500 in Index" strip on first discovery, and keep the tap-to-skip at 0.6 s. Effort M · Impact High.

4. **Egg 1 is a scripted, trait-stripped Epic and egg 2 is usually a downgrade.** [Static+Computed] `acceptDelivery` forces an Owl and drops the egg's mutation and size tier for the starter (`PenService` lines 510-520, 586-587). The starter hatches at about size 2.9, about $88/s (`16 x 2.9^1.6`). Egg 2 is rolled normally: 33% Common, mean income about $70/s, 18% beat the starter, 31% earn under $10/s [Computed, 100k eggs]. A Shiny, Golden or Rainbow egg is grabbed by about 10% of new players (6+3+1% weights) and it hatches as a plain Owl, which teaches "special egg = nothing". The tutorial then forces a 40 s timer on a Chick that is 20% likely.
   - Fix: (a) give the starter `Tier = "Huge"` (1.6x model scale and ring, presentation only) and keep its variant mutation if it has one; (b) pity for the first three rolled eggs: raise the relative luck floor to 3 (Common 17%, Epic+ 49%) or guarantee egg 2 is Rare or better; (c) remove the 40 s minimum when the pet is Common. Effort S · Impact High.

5. **Size tiers saturate in Meadow and almost vanish deeper, and the cap is invisible while you roll.** [Computed] Tier is the egg's size divided by its own rolled cap (`SizeTiers`, `PenService` line 508). After one 190-stud Meadow run: 28.7% no tier, 36.3% BIG, 34.5% HUGE, 0.5% COLOSSAL. At 260 studs COLOSSAL is 6%, at 400 studs 28% (walk laps anywhere; `Announce = true` for Colossal). Riverbank at 250 studs: HUGE 0.6%; Canyon at 300 studs: 0%. So the flex is common on day 1 and rare later. The carry card shows "growth 62%" (the decay factor) but never the cap or the tier thresholds.
   - Fix: change `SizeTiers` MinRatio to 0.80 / 0.93 / 0.99 (about 56% none, 29% BIG, 13% HUGE, 1.4% COLOSSAL in Meadow at 190 studs); for Riverbank and later, raise `GrowthPerStud` about 1.4x so a 250-stud run yields about 10% HUGE; add a thin "cap" bar to the carry card with ticks at BIG and HUGE. Effort S · Impact Med.

6. **Growth rules are mistaught.** [Static] Tutorial step 2 text: "Your egg grows while you roll it in its zone. Kite the Mother Hen for more" (`Config.Tutorial` line 38, 26 words). The code grows the egg anywhere you walk (`EggService.carryTick` lines 854-856, 887-891; `Economy.Egg` comment) and nothing rewards kiting the hen. A kid who obeys will waste time near the hen and lose the egg. Also the wheel is a weak growth tool in Meadow: +30 s on a tier-1 wheel adds about +0.19 size for a median egg (2.29 to 2.48), +8% income [Computed], so "grow it in your wheel" (HudController line 1048) over-promises.
   - Fix: step 2 text: "Walk to grow your egg. Cross the gold line to be safe!" (11 words). Cut the wheel-grow hint to the Speed benefit, or double `WheelGrowthFactor` from 0.15 to 0.3 if the wheel is meant to compete with walking. Effort XS · Impact Med.

7. **Bigger egg = better pet is readable only as a number, and the picture undersells it.** [Computed] Diameter is logarithmic (`DiameterLogScale 0.9`, `Curves.EggDiameter`): size 2.4 to 3.5 is +46% size, +83% income, +35% hatch time, Legendary odds 9% to 19%, but the egg only grows 4.5 to 5.3 studs (+19%). There is no visual threshold (ring, glow) as the egg passes BIG or HUGE, even though those tiers exist at hatch. The hatch card shows "686 kg - $65/s" with no link back to the egg.
   - Fix: show a BIG/HUGE chip and an egg ring in the tier colour (`SizeTiers.Color`) on the carried egg when it crosses the new thresholds, with a pop and sound; on the hatch card add "From a big egg: x4.1 income". Effort S · Impact Med.

8. **Variants: nearly half of eggs look special but are not.** [Static] Speckled, Spotted and Marbled (46% of eggs) give only +5/8/12% luck and +0.1 to +0.25 start size; they have no glow and no pet mutation (only Shiny to Silver 1.25x, Golden 2x, Rainbow 3x, `Mutations.List`). The nest gives no size or luck read before you grab. A kid picks blindly unless the prompt text is read.
   - Fix: give Marbled a faint glow; add a variant-coloured billboard label over nest eggs ("SHINY", "GOLDEN"); in step 1 add "Glowing eggs are better!" Effort S · Impact Med.

9. **Legendary is common enough to cheapen the server-wide announcements, and the config comments are out of date.** [Computed] A random Meadow egg is Legendary 11.2% and Epic-plus 30.7%; `Pets` base weights say 1%. `Rarities.Legendary.Announce = true` and `PenService` lines 614-617 announce every Legendary hatch. With 5 plots and an assumed 1 egg per 90 s per player, a Legendary toast appears about every 2.6 minutes [Opinion, assumption labelled]. The `Config.Biomes` Meadow comment (above `DropTable`) says luck scales tier-7 weight by `(luck/base)^6` and gives 0.2%; the real exponent is capped at 4 (`Curves.LUCK_EXPONENT_CAP`) and Secret is 0.56% (1 in 180 eggs).
   - Fix: keep early generosity, but announce only Mythic, Secret and Colossal server-wide (Legendary goes to the owner and friends only); lower `LuckPerSize` from 0.75 to 0.5 once the player passes 10 minutes or 5 eggs if you want Legendary closer to 6%. Fix the stale comment. Effort XS · Impact Low.

10. **The luck readout is tiny on phones and the pet identity is hidden even though the timer leaks it.** [Computed] Compact carry card is 250x112 px (`Hud.Carry` `CompactMinSize`); the luck row is 0.26 x (0.52 x 112) = about 15 px tall at 667x375, with the whole "x10.2 - -14% speed - growth 62%" in one scaled label. Placed eggs show luck only; the hatch timer length tells the rarity (Chick 6 s vs Goose 242 s) but nothing in the world hints at it. A 4-minute Legendary egg gets the purple "Skip R$" prompt tier by remaining time (`PenService` lines 270-275).
   - Fix: make the luck row 22 px with the odds text at 16 px minimum; tint the placed egg's rim by rarity once at least 50% of its timer has passed (no spoiler of the exact pet) so friends can watch and the owner stays near to see it hatch. Effort S · Impact Med.

### What's good
Growth is satisfying and cheap to understand: weight number, pop sound every 0.25 size and a clover that breathes while walking (`CarryController` lines 43, 362-370; `HudController.renderClover` line 1066). The drop-in with squash and a 1.3 s luck count-up (`PlotEggController`) is a proper reveal-before-the-reveal. Luck mechanics are sound underneath: relative luck is clamped 1-5 with an exponent cap, so stacked boosts cannot make Secrets a coin flip (`Curves` lines 108-129), and the tier burst (`TierBurst:Emit(30)`) is already wired for BIG/HUGE/COLOSSAL pets. First-time hatches are guaranteed exciting (Epic Owl plus "Mama!" confetti), and the lobby "HUGE EGG SOON" podium (screenshot 01) plants a chase goal.

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Turn the clover into odds ("Epic+ 27%") on the carry card and egg tag, and teach "bigger egg = luckier pet" in tutorial step 3. XS-S.
2. Make the hatch reveal scale with rarity (longer shake, flash, FOV punch, pet icon on the card, NEW! reward line). M.
3. Fix the early pet pipeline: starter gets a HUGE tier with its mutation kept, and the first three random eggs get an Epic-leaning luck floor; retune `SizeTiers` to 0.80/0.93/0.99. S.
