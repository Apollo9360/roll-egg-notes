## 1. First 60 seconds and tutorial (7/10)
The onboarding is unusually well engineered for this genre: a Speed number rises at about second 1.3, the first egg is stolen at about 11 s, a guaranteed Epic Owl hatches at about 31 s, and the whole 16-step loop can finish in roughly 1:45 (ideal) to 2:15 (phone kid) [Computed]. What drags it down is that step 1 depends on a shared 5-egg nest pool and 5 plots, that the pet-placement step and the card text are hard for a 7-year-old on a phone, and that after step 16 the screen has no goal even though Riverbank was already "unlocked" at about 0:47 [Computed].

### First 10 minutes for the kid who always does the obvious next thing
Inputs: spawn Plot1 (188.25, 271.25); Meadow nest centre (226.25, 96.25); nests 3/4 at z=101.1; safe line z=176.25; plot floor starts z=284.25 (world.md). Speed to walk: `walk = 16 + 10*log10(Speed/10)` (Curves.WalkSpeedFromSpeed). Egg drag `1/(1+0.12*(size-1))` (Curves.DragMultiplier). Egg growth `ds/dx = 0.018 * exp(-3*(s-s0)/(cap-s0))` (Biomes.Meadow.GrowthPerStud, Economy.Egg.GrowthDecay). I simulated these in Python over 20,000 random nest eggs (variant, start size 0.8-1.3, soft cap `4*(0.45+1.3*u^2.4)*variant`).

### Problems, ranked
1. **Step 1 can be impossible: the five Meadow eggs are a shared pool that refills only once per 5-minute day, and only 5 plots exist.** [Static] `spawnNestEgg` is called only from `refillNests` (EggService:313-328), which runs at server start and on `DayStarted` (:1020-1025). A taken egg leaves its nest empty for up to 290 s; `grabEgg` fails silently at night (:580). The tutorial beam falls back to `NestCenter` when no egg is streamed (TutorialController:124-126) and nothing tells the kid "no eggs, wait 2:10". Separately, `assign` gives plots 1-5 only and just `warn`s "No free plot" (PlotService:212-226); a 6th player has no plot, so `Plot`, placement and the pen do not work. Players.MaxPlayers reads 60 in Studio (world.md); the live cap is in Creator Hub and I could not read it. [Computed] With N new players in the Meadow each stealing once per cycle, N >= 5 means the next joiner waits; N = 5 is exactly the plot count.
   - Fix: (a) confirm Creator Hub max players is 5 or add plots to match; (b) add a "starter nest" egg that respawns every 10 s and only pays out when `Stats.EggsDelivered == 0` (the first delivered Meadow egg is always an Owl, so the egg's identity does not matter), (c) when TutorialStep is 1 and all Meadow nests are empty, show "Eggs come back in m:ss" on the pill. Effort M · Impact Very high.
2. **Step 6 asks a 7-year-old to equip a pet from the hotbar and tap the pen, with no pointer to the hotbar, while the whole economy is idle until they do.** [Static] After the hatch, the pet arrives as a hotbar Tool in slot 3 behind Bat and Trap (PvPService:13-18; PenService:631-634 toast "Equip it and click inside your pen"). The card waits for the 6 s reveal (TutorialController:700-719), then needs two actions (select tool, tap inside the pen; PetToolController:61-80 uses the last touch point as the aim point). $ stays at 0 until placed. The starter pet is a one-off event, yet the game makes it a UI exercise. `PetRecord.PenX = nil` already means "random spot" (DataService:20).
   - Fix: when `egg.Starter` is true, hatch the pet already `Placed = true` in the pen (PenService.hatch :580-588), and change the card to "Your Owl earns cash! Buy the wheel". This removes 2 actions and about 4-6 s, moves first purchase from 40 s to about 34 s, and removes the highest-risk step for the first minute. Teach hotbar placement with the second pet. Effort S · Impact Very high.
3. **The card text is too long and too small for phones, and the longest text lands at the moment the kid must move.** [Computed from ui.md] Body text is 11 px on both 667x375 and 844x390 phones (Hud.Tutorial.Body is TextScaled, base 8); at 667 px the card overlaps Settings by 67x34 px. Step 2 body is 130 characters / about 25 words (Cfg:38) against a 5-words-per-step benchmark; it appears the instant the hen wakes (kid must run within 1.7-2.5 s, Computed above). Steps 13 and 15 say "Hold F" and "hold E" (Cfg:55, :57) although {Action} only covers Tap/Click; phone kids have no E or F key. The "1/16" counter tells a 7-year-old the task has 16 parts.
   - Fix: cap every body at 6 words and 16 px minimum (step 2: "Run! Cross the gold line."), swap "E/F" for the prompt button wording, drop the n/16 counter, add a one-time "Run!" flash on the HUD when the guard wakes. Effort S · Impact High.
4. **After step 16 the screen has no goal, and Riverbank (open since about 0:47) is never pointed at.** [Static] When `Tutorial.Steps[currentStep]` is nil the card fades and hides (TutorialController:432-448); the only message is "Tutorial complete! Get faster to reach deeper zones." (TS:58). Riverbank's `RequiredSpeed = 300` is only a sign, not a gate (Biomes:97; grep shows no check anywhere). The Speed jump that opens it (+320 Speed, 36 to 363) happens inside the Index menu as a toast (IndexService:324-328), with no world feedback and no "Riverbank unlocked" moment. The next real gate (Canyon, 8,000) is about 7:30 away at best [Computed].
   - Fix: add step 17 "Riverbank!" with a beam to the Riverbank nest and a "$x / $y"-style ticker for the next wheel; fire a full-screen "+320 SPEED" whoosh (reuse FirstWalkController.whoosh, :334-344) and a banner "Riverbank unlocked" on the Index claim. Effort S · Impact High.
5. **Steps 8-10 put 3 menu steps right after the first big win, with a near-black spotlight and a forced 9 s wait.** [Static] The spotlight is four gradients at 0.03-0.08 transparency (StarterGui.Hud.Spotlight) so everything except a button-sized hole is almost black (TutorialController:226-368); it triggers while the kid is running in the wheel and lasts until the modal closes, up to AckSeconds 120 + 2 s (TS:223) for Index and again for Daily. Step 10 "Protection" is a pure 9 s read with no action (Cfg:50, TS:218-226) and blocks the step 11 card. The Index tip also says "Claim your reward" only when the Claim button is active (TC:596).
   - Fix: lighten the spotlight to 0.55 edge transparency, cap each info step at 12 s, merge Index claim into an automatic reveal after the first hatch ("Owl discovered: +320 Speed" shown with the hatch card), and move Daily and Protection to after the second hatch. Effort S · Impact High.
6. **The first screen carries 12 always-on HUD elements, Robux and Daily included, and a lit Daily badge invites the kid to burn Day 1.** [Static] Nothing hides Shop (R$ badge), Daily, Quests, Rebirth or Settings during the tutorial (ui.md: 8 buttons + 4 readouts + 2 tutorial widgets = 14 elements before Roblox's own UI); five buttons are 34 px high (< 44) and Quests/Rebirth text is 12 px at 667 px; Stats sit entirely in the joystick zone. `Retention.DailyStatus` returns `CanClaim = true` when `LastClaim <= 0` (Retention :83), so the badge is lit at second 0; the Day 1 reward is `max(250, income*60*2)` and `max(60, Speed*5%)` (Retention.DailyReward; RetentionService:157): $250 if claimed at second 5, about $10,300 if claimed after the Owl is placed. [Computed]
   - Fix: hide Shop, Quests, Rebirth, Settings and the Daily badge until step 8 (keep Eggs, Pets, Index hidden until needed too), and make `claimDaily` return "Come back after your first hatch" while TutorialStep < 9. Effort S · Impact High.
7. **The spawn view shows a decoy: a giant, untouchable egg with a "HUGE EGG SOON" sign is the first thing the eye lands on.** [Measured, screenshot 01 edit-mode] The goal (nest) is 179 studs away behind the arch and off screen; the beam is 1.6 studs wide on the ground (TutorialController:386) and the distance pill only says a number. The lobby also has a statue, a leaderboard board and the Trail Stand within 47-88 studs, all clickable-looking.
   - Fix: for TutorialStep <= 2 hide the podium egg and its sign for that client (LocalTransparencyModifier) and put the tutorial "Meadow nest" billboard (FirstWalk NestMarker already exists) on-screen at spawn by turning the spawn heading 15 degrees toward the nest. Effort S · Impact Medium.
8. **The first hint a new player sees is untrue for them, and the hotbar shows two tools they cannot use.** [Static] HintController shows "Trap" as soon as a PvP tool exists and `Tools.Trap > 0` (HC:190-195); a new player is protected for 10 minutes (PvPService:191, 194-199) and the Trap hotbar tool answers "You can't use traps while protected" (:573-576); the Meadow is also a no-trap zone (:143-149). Bat and Trap occupy slots 1-2 from load (PvPService:13-18), pushing the first pet to slot 3 (see Problem 2).
   - Fix: gate hints on `TutorialStep >= 17` and Trap on `Protected ~= true`; do not give the Bat and Trap tools until protection ends. Effort XS · Impact Medium.
9. **First purchase happens at about 40-56 s against a 5-10 s benchmark, with $0 on screen until the pet is placed.** [Computed] The wheel label beside the spawn says "Buy on the board: $100" (SpeedService:110) from second 0, but the $100 only exists after hatch, reveal, equip and place (36.8 s ideal). Quick wins exist: the first walk already pays +2 Speed per pickup.
   - Fix: make each first-walk pickup also pay $8 (13 x $8 = $104, FirstWalkService.Grant) so the green BUY lights up on the board on the way home, and drop the "Save $100" wording. Combined with Problem 2 the first purchase can come at about 25 s. Effort S · Impact High.
10. **Rejoin and clock edge cases.** (a) [Static] Onboarding is saved on every step (`DataService.Set` TutorialStep, replicated, saved with the ProfileStore profile, done = step 17; the autosave interval lives in the third-party ProfileStore module I did not read). (b) Steps 2-4 with no carried egg regress to step 1 or 5 within 1 s of load (TS:199-200), so a returning kid sees a stale card for up to 1 s and the first-walk pickups reappear (server `collected` is memory-only, remotes flag 3: +26 Speed per join while on step 1). (c) The protection clock is wall-clock: `ProtectedUntil = os.time()+600` is stamped at first load (PvPService:194-199), so a 4-minute first session leaves 6 minutes of shield that expire while the kid is away; on Day 2 the kid also has a non-empty Index, so they return unprotected and meet the "Your protection ended!" danger toast (:266). (d) Night is random at join: 10 s of every 300 s; [Computed] 3.3% of joins start at night (grab fails silently, EggService:580) and about 5% of first runs have the 10 s night start inside the 9-14 s corridor window, which discards the egg and teleports the kid home (EggService:1026-1041).
    - Fix: count protection in online seconds (decrement `ProtectedUntil` only while in game), extend the day while a TutorialStep 1-4 player is in the corridor (WorldService.ExtendDay already exists), toast "Night! Eggs come back in n s" when a grab is refused. Effort S · Impact Medium.

### What's good
The first walk is carefully built: 13 Speed pickups beside the beam, chevrons, a distance pill that switches to "Hold E on an egg!" within 15 studs, and a one-time whoosh when entering the corridor (FirstWalkController:26-451, TutorialController:614-650). The first egg is guaranteed to hatch an Epic Owl in 5 s with a "Mama!" bubble and confetti, so the first reveal is a Rare+ moment at about 31 s (Economy FirstEggGuaranteedPet/FirstEggHatchSeconds; TutorialController:490-576). No popup or modal opens at join; progress is saved server-side per step with repair rules for lost eggs (TS:199-204) and info steps can never softlock because every one auto-advances (TS:218-226).

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Auto-place the starter Owl in the pen at hatch (PenService.hatch, `egg.Starter`), so no hotbar step and first purchase about 6 s sooner. Effort S.
2. Guarantee step 1 can be completed: a starter nest that respawns every 10 s for `EggsDelivered == 0` players, and match plots to the server cap. Effort M.
3. Rewrite the card for phones (6 words, 16 px, no E/F letters, no n/16), hide Shop/Daily/Quests/Rebirth until step 8, and end the tutorial with a Riverbank beam plus a "+320 SPEED / Riverbank unlocked" moment. Effort S.
