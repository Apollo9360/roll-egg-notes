## 5. Progression systems (6/10)
The numbers behind progression are well built. Every zone pays about 10x the last (Meadow commons $1-2/s, Riverbank $12-18/s, Canyon $140-200/s, Snow $1.5-2.2K/s), and each zone's Index pays roughly the next zone's Speed gate. But a kid is never told what to do next after the tutorial. Zone gates are invisible and silent, there is no persistent "$x / $y" goal, and cash upgrades can only be bought at the plot or the lobby, so the game relies on the player's own curiosity to keep the next goal in view.

### The kid's experience
Durations are [Computed] from config. No playtest was run.

### Problems, ranked
1. **After the tutorial there is no persistent "next goal" anywhere on the HUD.** [Static] ui.md lists the always-on HUD as Speed, Money, Income, Timer plus 8 buttons, with no goal element. The only next-price displays are:
   - the plot fence boards, about 23 studs from spawn and invisible once the kid is in the Meadow or Riverbank;
   - a one-time "You can afford X" toast per item (UpgradeHintController:89-99), which is muted all through the tutorial (:83);
   - the Rebirth modal's cost bar, visible only when the modal is open (RebirthController:298-314).

   The yardsticks ask for a "$x / $y" ticker and a goal visible at all times. After step 16 a kid at $400 with the next wheel at $1,500 sees nothing about it.
   - Fix: add one pill under the Stats block (about 190x28 px), "Next: Wheel 2  $320 / $1,500" with a thin bar. It picks the cheapest of next wheel, next pen and next cash trail, and tapping it fires the tutorial beam to that board. When all cash items are bought it becomes "Riverbank: Speed 356 / 300 READY!", then "Canyon 3.2K / 8K", then "Rebirth $x / $250K". Effort S/M · Impact Very high.

2. **Zone unlocks are invisible and silent.** Zone gates are soft: `RequiredSpeed` is only shown on a sign (Biomes.lua:56), and nothing enforces it. [Measured, Studio read] Every `EntranceSign` sits about 83 studs underground. Riverbank's sign is at y = -84 while the ground is at y = -1, and its hanging beam is at y = -75.7, so "Riverbank Zone / Needs 300 Speed" cannot be seen. The only visible hint is `ZoneEdge.FloorText`, "Recommended Speed 300", on the floor 4 studs before the line (an 8 pt TextScaled label on a 6x40 stud part). Searching the scripts for "unlocked" and "ZoneName" found no "you can now enter Riverbank" moment. The 300 gate is passed silently at tutorial step 8. A kid at Speed 7,900 gets no "almost Canyon" cue, and one at 8,000 gets no fanfare.
   - Fix: raise each `EntranceSign` to y = +16 (the GrayboxBuilder default at line 618 was `y + 16`). Add a client check on the Speed attribute against `Biomes[*].RequiredSpeed`. When a gate is crossed, show a full-screen "RIVERBANK UNLOCKED  x10 income" banner with confetti, and keep a "Zone pill" showing Speed against the next gate. Effort S · Impact Very high.

3. **Cash upgrades cannot be bought from anywhere except in-world boards and the lobby, and phone kids are the audience.** [Static] Wheel and Pen are bought only on the two fence boards (PlotBoardController:122-129). Trails are bought only at the Trail Stand, which is 4-9 s from spawn (world.md), and the Trails menu opens only from the stand's catalog prompt. The HUD "Shop" is the Robux shop (UpgradeHintController header: "the Shop is the Robux shop now"). A kid with $20K in the Riverbank, far from the plot, has to run home just to tap a small SurfaceGui board (about 6 studs wide in screenshot 07). Rebirth, by contrast, has a HUD button and a badge.
   - Fix: add an "Upgrades" HUD button (or a tab in the Rebirth modal) listing Wheel, Pen, Trail and Rebirth with $ / $ bars and Buy buttons. It reuses `RequestBuy` and `RequestBuyTrail`. Show a badge when anything is affordable. Effort M · Impact High.

4. **The tutorial's last step sells the worst-value upgrade first.** [Computed] A Green Trail ($5,000) takes the tier-1 wheel from 2 to 3 Speed/s, so +1/s costs $5,000. Wheel 2 ($1,500) takes it from 2 to 6 Speed/s, so +4/s costs $375 per Speed/s, 13x better value. The Index claim at step 8 pays $1,600, enough to buy Wheel 2 on the spot. Yet "you can afford" toasts are muted until step 17 (UpgradeHintController:83), and the tutorial only finishes when the Trail is owned (TutorialService:214-217). A kid who spends on Wheel 2 or Pen 2 ($300) first extends the tutorial by 2-3 min of waiting at Owl-level income of about $24-40/s.
   - Fix: end the tutorial on "Buy Wheel 2" ($1,500) and make the Trail an optional hint once the player is richer (about $15K). Effort S · Impact Med/High.

5. **The Index's best moments are capped, and a single claim can forfeit most of the reward.** [Static] `IndexService.core.ClaimPet` pays `min(reward, room)` and marks the pet claimed (:163-168). The cash room equals the next rebirth cost, $250K at 0 rebirths (:77-79). Examples:
   - Snow Ice Dragon is worth $6.5M and would pay $250K.
   - The Canyon batch totals about $1.24M against the $250K cap.
   - "Claim all" stops at the first misfit, saves the rest, and starts a 90 s lockout (CAP_COOLDOWN, :47, :351-353).

   The kid reads "(capped at your stage - the Index can't skip zones)" at the best moment of the loop. Cash is trivial at that stage: a Snow income of tens of K/s earns $250K in under 10 s, so the cap protects nothing and only deflates the reward.
   - Fix: drop the cash cap entirely (keep the Speed cap, which does gate zones). Never mark a pet claimed when it would pay less than 100%; leave it claimable instead. Effort S · Impact Med.

6. **Completing a zone's Index only earns a cosmetic bat skin.** [Static] BatSkins.lua header: "Skins never change gameplay." The capstone of the collection loop (all non-Secret pets, which needs a Mythic at about 0.5% base odds, Biomes DropTable) gives nothing that feeds eggs, Speed, or money. Per-pet claims already paid out. Luck from egg size does help (relative luck 3.25 gives about 11-12% Mythic in Canyon [Computed]), so completion is reachable, but the payoff is thin for the effort.
   - Fix: each completed zone grants a permanent small perk tied to the egg loop, for example +10% egg growth or +5% luck, shown in the Index zone header ("Meadow complete: +10% growth"). Effort S · Impact Med.

7. **The Speed number moves much faster than the player's actual run speed, so later wheel upgrades feel like nothing.** [Computed] Walk speed = 16 + 10*log10(Speed/10) (Curves.WalkSpeedFromSpeed), so every 10x Speed is only +10 walk speed. The jump from step 8 (+320 Speed, 16 to 31.5) is the strongest one. After that, a 60 s run on Wheel 4 (40/s) at Speed 20,000 adds +2,400 Speed, or +0.5 walk speed. The HUD shows only the Speed number. The wheel tier costs run from $1,500 to $50M (1.5K, 20K, 250K, 3M, 50M), but it is rarely obvious what a tier changes.
   - Fix: show "Run speed 45" next to Speed and pop "+3 run speed!" whenever walk speed crosses a whole number. Add a "your next zone needs about 45 run speed" line to the zone pill from problem 2. Effort S · Impact Med.

8. **Rebirth works as a money and wheel multiplier but does nothing for eggs, and it fades quickly.** [Computed] The multiplier is 1 + 0.5n, so each rebirth adds +50%, +33%, +25%, +20%, +17% over the previous one while the cost multiplies by 3.5: $250K, $875K, $3.06M, $10.7M, $37.5M, $131M. It affects only money and wheel gain (Multipliers.Money and Treadmill), not egg growth or luck, which are what the kid plays. It resets Speed to 10, the wheel and the pen, which is the same shopping list that Wheel 4 ($250K) and Pen 5 ($300K) sit on at the same price (Economy.lua). The confirm card states the loss clearly (RebirthController:164), which is good. The endgame also ends at the Cosmic gate (2.5M): past it Speed has no job, and with rebirth 4 (x3) plus Gold Trail (x4.5) and Wheel 6 (400/s), Cosmic is regained in about 8 min [Computed].
   - Fix: add +5% egg growth and +3% luck per rebirth (cap at +50%/+30%) and show them in the Rebirth modal's "next" column. Add endgame sinks priced above the current ceiling, for example a Rebirth 10+ aura set and Secret-pet goals on the HUD pill. Effort S/M · Impact Med.

9. **Trails are a poor speed purchase after Green, and only matter at the wheel.** [Computed] Trail multipliers relative to the previous tier are x1.33, x1.5, x1.5, x1.33, x1.33 for 15x, 13x, 15x, 17x, 16x the cost (Trails.lua: $5K, $75K, $1M, $15M, $250M, $4B). A wheel tier gives x2.5-x3.3 for 12-17x. Trails also do nothing for eggs or money (Trails.lua header). Trail multipliers do carry over a rebirth, which is the best thing about them. The Robux prices (R$19-199 for x1.5-x8) read as pay-to-skip, not as a goal.
   - Fix: let trails also give +5% egg growth per tier, or make them show the walk-speed gain next to the Speed gain ("x2 wheel gain, about +1 run speed per 10 min"), so the purchase has a visible reason. Effort S · Impact Low/Med.

10. **The wheel is the only Speed source that needs no core-loop action, and it pays nothing when the player is away.** [Static] SpeedService:215-219 pays Speed whenever the character stands on the pad (there is no movement check), and EggService:880-885 lets that standing also grow a carried egg. Index (the other big source) comes from hatching, which is good for the loop. The Speed gain from steal/deliver/escape actions themselves is zero; only quests give 4% (RewardSpeedPct 0.04, min 80). An idle kid learns to stand in the wheel, and a kid who leaves gets only cash.
    - Fix: pay a small Speed bonus per delivered egg (for example 1% of the next-gate gap) so stealing visibly raises Speed too. Effort S · Impact Low/Med.

### What's good
- The Index design couples collecting to zone progress. The six-pet Speed sums are Riverbank 12.2K, Canyon 77.5K, Snow 467K, Volcano 2.42M and Cosmic 11.9M, against gates of 8K, 60K, 400K, 2.5M. Each completed zone pays about 1.0-1.5x the next gate. Silhouette cells and found/required counters are built in (IndexController:258-270).
- Pricing ratios keep a purchase due every 1-3 min at the matching zone's income (Wheel 3 $20K is about 20-100 s of Riverbank income, Wheel 4 $250K about 1-2 min at Canyon, and so on) [Computed], which matches the "meaningful every 5-10 min later" yardstick. The plot boards show "+Speed/s" before and after and a "!" badge when affordable (PlotBoardController:92-105).
- Rebirth is honest and well presented: its modal shows Money / cost with a bar, the exact Speed that will be lost, a server-wide announce, a colored aura and a "Rebirth N" title (RebirthService:148-149, 38-93).
- Offline earnings (50%, 8 h cap) and the daily calendar scale with current income and Speed, so they stay relevant late (Retention.DailyReward).

### Top 3 for the summary
1. Add a persistent "Next:" HUD pill ($ / $ bar, tap to get a beam) that cycles wheel, pen, trail, then next zone gate, then rebirth (Very high, S/M).
2. Make zone gates visible: move the EntranceSigns above ground (currently y = -84) and add an "UNLOCKED" banner when Speed crosses a gate (Very high, S).
3. Add an "Upgrades" panel with Buy buttons so cash upgrades do not require a run home; end the tutorial on Wheel 2 instead of a $5,000 trail (High, M).
