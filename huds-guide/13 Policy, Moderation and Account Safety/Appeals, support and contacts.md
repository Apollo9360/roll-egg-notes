# Appeals, support and contacts

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Asset, description and account moderation]] · Next: [[Age gates (summary)]]

1. Appeal every warning you believe is false, because warnings stack quickly.
2. Use [Report Appeals](https://www.roblox.com/report-appeals#/), an AI chatbot. False bans are often lifted within 10 minutes.
3. If denied, appeal again and ask for human review. Second appeals are often granted within about 2 hours.
4. For a suspended account, reactivate it first, then appeal.
5. Keep appeals short and human, stating clearly that it is a false ban.
6. For terminations, send about 3 tickets with a clear explanation.

For unexplained game removals:

1. Check email, including spam, for a DMCA notice from the copyright department.
2. Email copyright_agent@roblox.com to ask about DMCA strikes.
3. Check the [Safety Dashboard](https://www.roblox.com/safety-dashboard#/) and Violations and Appeals.
4. Post on the DevForum if support stays silent.

Support realities:

- General support is at [roblox.com/support](https://www.roblox.com/support). It is slow, so file multiple requests; holidays are slower.
- Removal appeals on large games have gone 10+ days, and some over a month, with no human reply.
- Stolen-game cases often get one reply, then silence.
- An all-ages game suddenly showing a different maturity label may be a bug; contact support.
- "Charts" in a Roblox notice means the Charts (games) page.
