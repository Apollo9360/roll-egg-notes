# Account structure alts and groups

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Account security and scams]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Policy)]]

- Never develop on your main account. Keep a separate account that owns the group and the Robux.
- Set up account safety before launching a game, to avoid DevEx problems later.
- Store assets locally so a ban doesn't take your work.
- Upload images and risky assets from an alt with a separate Gmail.
- If your main has old bans, use an alt for development and DevEx.
- Give each game at 2–4k+ CCU its own group. A banned group takes every game with it, and a sale transfers the whole group.
- Card payments for ad credits require an 18+ account. Under-18 creators must pay in Robux, at about 3x the cost.
- One ID can verify only a limited number of accounts. Under-18 creators can verify with facial age estimation.
- Developers under 16 can't add social links to games or groups.
- Exploiters appear in growing games; build anti-cheat.
- Enabling console is fine if the GUI works with a controller.
- Verified badge: needs 1M+ playtime hours in the last 90 days and at least a 10% revenue share in that game.
    - Badges are granted in waves, so dropping below the share before a wave can miss it.
    - Holding the share for about 3 months is expected; badges were removed from those who sold out early.
