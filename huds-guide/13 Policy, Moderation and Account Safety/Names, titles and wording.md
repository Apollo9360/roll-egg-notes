# Names, titles and wording

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Content maturity, chat and discoverability]] · Next: [[Unexplained removals, AI moderation and ban waves]]

- Roblox's Name and Logo Community Usage Guidelines ban Roblox-derived names such as "Blox" in game names.
- Violent verbs aimed at a group of people (such as "Kill cops") may be flagged as discriminatory. Use "Destroy" or "Down" instead (owner-recommended).
- Titles like "anticheat testing spot" drew 7-day bans for Misusing Roblox Systems (low confidence).
- A bio saying "this is not a beam link" drew a warning under the off-platform links rule.
- Brand logos (such as sportswear logos) may trigger automated strikes (low confidence).
- Usernames can trigger bans even years later; one 8-year-old name drew a 3-day ban, and a name containing "YouTube" was reset.
- Names close to a recently removed game may be caught by similarity checks (low confidence).
