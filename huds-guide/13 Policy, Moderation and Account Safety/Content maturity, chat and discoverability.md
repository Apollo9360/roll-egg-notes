# Content maturity, chat and discoverability

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Banned monetization and reward mechanics]] · Next: [[Names, titles and wording]]

- A content-maturity strike can arrive the day after a new feature ships, even on 10k+ concurrent-user (CCU) games.
- Strikes usually allow about 3 days to fix the issue before takedown.
- Answering the maturity questionnaire inaccurately risks takedown, privating or an age restriction.
- Small details count as content. Reported examples:
    - A poo emoji in emotes counted as Crude Humor (Mild).
    - Red effects not disclosed as blood led to a 7-day ban.
    - Visible dead bodies got a game privated after it reached 1k CCU; see this [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/experience-moderatedinaccessible-for-inaccurate-maturity-rating/4815099).
- Questionnaire flags often come right after growth: at all-ages unlock, at 1k–4k CCU, or a couple of weeks into monetizing.
- A flagged questionnaire must be retaken before the deadline, or the game becomes unplayable.
- Moderators can request questionnaire changes after the game reaches the highly engaged player (HEP) threshold, delaying all-ages review.
- Player-built bases and drawings can display profanity or banned imagery, which is a moderation risk (owner-recommended caution).
- Games showing user-made content (such as drawings) to under-16 accounts despite a 16+ setting are expected to face crackdowns.
- Custom or global chat must use Roblox text filtering and age-group tags. A game banned for TextChatService misuse was later restored.
- Chat restrictions reportedly hurt social guessing games (low confidence).
- Troll games covered in fake keycaps have been silently restricted: ads stopped and the game marked "undiscoverable", with no notice.
- Threats to mass-dislike your game unless given admin rarely have any effect. Ignore them.
- Shadowban claims are mostly unverified:
    - A like ratio near 10% is said to act like a shadowban, with no effect above it (low confidence).
    - Mass dislikes from Discord testers are suspected of hurting reach (low confidence).
    - Whole genres (such as brainrot) are not shadowbanned; poor retention explains most cases.
    - Missing the HEP threshold across several games usually means an oversaturated genre, not an account ban.
