# DevEx, payouts and taxes - Rates and eligibility

Up: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Next: [[Taxes from November 1, 2026]]

- The DevEx rate is about $3.80 per 1,000 Robux, up from about $3.50 a year earlier. Viral revenue screenshots using $10 per 1,000 are inflated.
- The US 18+ DevEx rate pays 42% more, but only on Robux from buyers verified as 18+ in the US.
- Only earned Robux can be cashed out; purchased Robux don't qualify. One DevEx was declined as "not earned" despite coming from the developer's own game (low confidence).
- Sign-up needs about 30,000 earned Robux. Monthly cash-outs are reportedly capped near 20M Robux (low confidence).
- DevEx requires a W-9 (US) or W-8 (non-US) IRS tax form, set up through Tipalti and moving into Creator Hub.
- Bank transfers through Tipalti cost about $5 (low confidence).
- Old account bans can block DevEx: one developer couldn't cash out 1.5M Robux over a years-old ban.
- Russia has no DevEx and no Robux purchases (low confidence).
- Giving Roblox your ID adds no privacy exposure if you already give a tax ID for DevEx.
