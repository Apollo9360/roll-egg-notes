# Black-market and off-platform Robux

Up: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Previous: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes - Timing and holds]]

- Black-market rates (about $3.50–8 per 1,000 Robux) sit near the DevEx rate and break the ToS.
- Gamepass or group-payout transfers flagged as Robux selling have banned both parties.
- Large Robux transfers can get flagged even when legitimate.
