# DevEx, payouts and taxes - Timing and holds

Up: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Previous: [[Roblox Wallet and Card]] · Next: [[Black-market and off-platform Robux]]

- Flow: move Robux from the group to your account, then cash out.
- Payment usually arrives within a week, sometimes in a couple of days. First cash-outs have taken 4 days to 1.5 months.
- A cooldown of about 4 weeks between cash-outs was reported (low confidence).
- Pending Robux took weeks in mid-2026 reports; by October it cleared in about 5 days. Shirt and limited sales have sat pending for 1–2 months.
- Group payout holds:
    - Verified groups wait about 3 days to pay a new member; unverified groups about a week (low confidence).
    - Large recent earnings can be held 1–2 extra weeks.
    - Group-to-self transfers can be held up to 10 days.
- New group payouts are locked for about 31 days, likely anti-fraud checks for low organic activity or sudden fund spikes:
    - Complete phone, email and 2-step verification on the owner account.
    - Locks usually lift around day 30. If still locked on day 32–33, file a Support ticket or DevForum bug report.
- Reported problems: pending for up to a month with only bot replies, funds unsent up to 2 months after acceptance, and no reachable human support.
- Repeated "Not Eligible", even on 500k Robux, means an eligibility requirement is unmet. Check that the Tipalti account and tax setup are complete.
- DevEx was repeatedly rejected for one developer as "from a moderated game" he wasn't affiliated with (low confidence).
