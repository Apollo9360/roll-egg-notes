# Taxes from November 1, 2026

Up: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Previous: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes - Rates and eligibility]] · Next: [[Roblox Wallet and Card]]

- DevEx payouts become royalties for US federal tax purposes. Until then they are reported as 1099-NEC service payments.
- Without valid tax info in Creator Hub by October 31, withholding of up to 24% may apply.
- With tax info, revenue from US players may face up to 30% withholding, reducible to 0% under a tax treaty.
- Revenue from non-US players is not subject to US withholding.
- Example: $50k from US players at 30% becomes about $35k before local taxes. See [Tax information](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information).
- Country notes: UK 0% (treaty); Philippines has a treaty, so file correctly; Georgia about 1% local with 0% on US income; Malta about 5%; Dubai 0%.
- A tax-migration email advised cashing out between October 20 and 31 (low confidence).
- DevEx is a taxable event, while Robux kept on-platform (for example spent on ads) is said not to add taxable income (disputed; not tax advice).
