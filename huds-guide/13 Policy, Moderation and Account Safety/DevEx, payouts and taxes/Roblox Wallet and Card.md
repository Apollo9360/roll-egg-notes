# Roblox Wallet and Card

Up: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Previous: [[Taxes from November 1, 2026]] · Next: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes - Timing and holds]]

- Announced at RDC in September 2026. Earnings convert to a USD wallet balance you can spend with a card.
- You can buy Robux back at the DevEx rate, far below retail purchase prices.
- Roblox plans to transition away from DevEx in the US in mid-2027; other regions keep it longer.
