# Age gates (summary)

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Appeals, support and contacts]] · Next: [[Platform changes timeline]]

- New games are 16+ until they reach 250 HEP. The normal route then needs the 1,000 Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus.
- All ages also needs ID verification, two-factor authentication (2FA) and an accepted maturity questionnaire.
- A 50,000 Robux expedited review skips HEP; it cost 100,000 until about mid-September. It is refundable after roughly 90 days, with reported conditions varying.
- A permanently removed game forfeits the refund.
- A drop to 100 HEP in November is announced but not yet in effect. A 25k skip fee is an unverified rumor.
- See the [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players|Age Gates & Highly Engaged Players]] tab for tactics and costs.
