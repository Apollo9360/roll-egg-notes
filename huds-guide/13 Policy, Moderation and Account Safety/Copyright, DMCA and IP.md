# Copyright, DMCA and IP

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Unexplained removals, AI moderation and ban waves]] · Next: [[Asset, description and account moderation]]

| Content | Risk | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Cartoon images (such as SpongeBob) | High: Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) takedowns happen | High |
| Exact copy of another creator's model or reused mesh IDs | High: DMCA-able | High |
| Model merely similar to another | Low: not DMCA-able | Medium |
| Copied idea or mechanic only | Low: no DMCA for ideas, though some owners have filed anyway (disputed) | Medium |
| Another game's characters or designs in your thumbnail or game | High: copyright claims reported | High |
| Similar thumbnail or icon | Medium: competitors have filed DMCAs | Medium |
| Self-uploaded simple meme sound effects | Low | Medium |
| Toolbox sounds | Generally safe; beware DMCA abusers | Medium |
| Background music you don't own | High: can lead to bans; one sound took a game down | High |
| Someone else's animations | Not allowed unless you own them | Medium |
| Toolbox brainrot and meme models, free or paid | Medium: uploaders often don't own the intellectual property (IP); paid models confer no rights | High |
| "Guess the Meme" style games | Low: not usually taken down | Low |

- Big-brand IP (Disney, Nintendo, One Piece, Marvel): Roblox rarely moderates it proactively; owners must file DMCAs and often don't.
- Risk rises with visibility. Games around 20 CCU are rarely hit; claims tend to arrive once a game reaches home recommendations.
- Most experienced developers still advise against big-brand IP as risky and unethical. IP-ripped games get deleted, especially at scale.
- Viral copies are often bought out by the original studio rather than DMCA'd (low confidence).
- Roblox takes games down on potential claims and treats most claims as valid. Big studios have spammed abusive DMCAs at whole genres.
- Copying another game's metadata is disputed: some games were flagged for it, while others copy thumbnails without issue.
- Cloning a hit game too closely, including with AI, carries the same similarity risk.
- Asset theft is common; some games are built from stolen assets or decompiled games. A decompiled copy once reached 15k CCU.
- If your map or game is stolen, you must file the DMCA yourself. You can only DMCA a copy that uses your actual assets.
- Counter an invalid DMCA as soon as possible.
- DMCA tickets often sit "under review" for 4–7+ days.
- Outcomes can be severe: a 136M-visit game died after a DMCA, and about 30M Robux was reported locked by one.
- Too many DMCAs can cost DevEx eligibility.
- In one false-claim case the thief was banned, but the original developer never got the game back (low confidence).
- Safer free asset sources shared by developers: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets), [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/), [Mixkit sound effects](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/), [Lucide icons](https://lucide.dev/icons), [UI Resources](https://ui-resources.com/) and the [Creator Store](https://create.roblox.com/store).
