# Asset, description and account moderation

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Copyright, DMCA and IP]] · Next: [[Appeals, support and contacts]]

- Removed audio shows the reason in its moderation notice.
- Gore assets that keep getting moderated can be whitelisted by emailing Roblox.
- False positives usually cost the asset or a 1-day suspension. Commonly flagged:
    - Cartoon images read as gory.
    - Shapes resembling a swastika; shirtless athletes read as sexual.
    - White smoke clouds and model icon renders.
    - Real faces flagged as personal information (PII).
    - Game icons, usually unbanned quickly on appeal.
    - A fake decorative QR code, which drew an instant 1-day ban.
    - A badge reading "XXXV" (Roman numeral 35) read as "XXX", which drew a week-long game ban (low confidence).
    - Nude-looking monster models and thumbnails read as sexual.
- No tool exists to pre-check an asset before upload.
- Descriptions can be rejected with no reason. Word swaps may not help; phrases like "get rich quick" may trigger it.
- An upgrade-tree asset was removed for Misusing Roblox Systems (low confidence).
- A game switched to "free" was banned as a scam (low confidence).
- Right-to-erasure requests are common, even for tiny games. Delete the listed player IDs from DataStores; see [Right to be forgotten](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/right-to-be-forgotten).
