# Estimates and projections (Policy)

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Account structure alts and groups]] · Next: [[Open questions]]

- Estimate: 100k Robux ≈ $380 at DevEx and 50k ≈ $190 (basis: about $3.80 per 1,000 Robux).
- Estimate: the 50k skip fee costs about $500 if bought with cash (basis: 100k Robux reported at about $1,000 retail). Pay it only from earned Robux.
- Estimate: 1,000 Robux from verified 18+ US buyers DevExes for about $5.40 (basis: $3.80 rate plus the 42% uplift).
- Estimate: $1,000 of US-player DevEx nets about $700 at 30% withholding, $760 at 24%, or $1,000 under a 0% treaty (basis: published withholding rates).
- Estimate: the monthly DevEx cap near 20M Robux equals about $76k gross (basis: reported cap times $3.80 per 1,000).
- Estimate: 100M Robux ≈ $380k gross, so the reported ~$250k after tax implies a combined tax rate near 34%.
- Estimate: buying Robux back through Roblox Wallet at the DevEx rate costs roughly 60% less than retail (basis: about $3.80 versus about $10 per 1,000).
- Estimate: a new group needs about a month of cash runway before its first payout (basis: 31-day new-group lock plus up to 10-day transfer holds).
- Estimate: a verified badge needs about 460 CCU held continuously for 90 days (basis: 1M hours divided by 2,160 hours).
