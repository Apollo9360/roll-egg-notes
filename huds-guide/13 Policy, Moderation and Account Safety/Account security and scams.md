# Account security and scams

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[DevEx, payouts and taxes]] · Next: [[Account structure alts and groups]]

| Risk pattern | Worst losses seen | Prevention | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fake Discord "verify" servers pressuring you to screenshare or change 2FA as "proof" | Whole games at thousands of CCU | Never screenshare account settings or change 2FA for anyone | High |
| Fake "dev job" offers with upfront pay, then a call | Hacked or banned accounts | Never install a "Rojo" or VS Code plugin or accept a Team Create invite on request | High |
| Fake Bloxlink or RoVer bots and sites stealing cookies | Accounts and DevEx Robux | Never click scout links; hand-type or search URLs | High |
| Malicious VS Code extensions or "upload gamepasses" tools installed under pressure | Session cookies and accounts | Install nothing on request; create gamepasses in Creator Dashboard | High |
| Cookie theft and email-verification bypass | Up to 400k Robux | Authenticator-app 2FA; log out all sessions after any doubt | Medium |
| Groups stolen and rebranded | Whole groups and games | Audit roles; see the [stolen groups thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/urgent-roblox-groups-are-being-stolen-and-rebranded-as-pixel-studios/4861826/3) | Medium |
| Group-permission bypass bugs | Owner removal and role escalation | Watch the [exploit report](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/critical-group-permission-bypass-allowed-owner-removal-role-escalation-member-removal-and-asset-changes/4723159) | Low |
| Hacked co-developer uploading banned content | Owner banned and group locked on a 17k CCU game | Give collaborators minimal permissions | High |
| Fake escrow supplied by a game buyer | A game earning about $100k/month; support won't return it | Use only a verified third-party escrow | Medium |
| Impersonators selling fake "game shares" in your name | Reputation | Warn your community and report them | Medium |
| Unsolicited offers from new accounts | Varies | Treat as scams | High |
| Hacked Discord account spreading scam images | Contacts and reputation | Factory reset the PC, reset passwords, enable 2FA | Medium |

Selling a game safely:

1. Take payment first, and never use an escrow the buyer provides.
2. Use a verified third-party escrow: buyer pays escrow, you hand over the game, escrow pays you.
3. Accept that no transfer is 100% safe.

If you were hacked:

1. Regain access and log out all sessions.
2. Change passwords.
3. Set up 2FA with an authenticator app.
4. Contact support. A claimed stolen-Robux hotline on investor relations pages is unverified.
