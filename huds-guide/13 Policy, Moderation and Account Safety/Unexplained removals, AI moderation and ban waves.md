# Unexplained removals, AI moderation and ban waves

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Previous: [[Names, titles and wording]] · Next: [[Copyright, DMCA and IP]]

- Large games (up to 30M visits and 10k CCU) have been removed with no notice, sometimes with DevEx cash-outs rejected.
- An outdated maturity questionnaire is one suspected cause (low confidence).
- A reported AI system removes games similar to, or named like, a previously removed game (low confidence). No notice is sent.
- A "[Content Deleted]" label means a serious violation.
- Platform glitches can make many games "unavailable" at once with no moderation message, costing hundreds of players until fixed.
- Age restrictions can arrive unexplained while support is unreachable. A DevForum post is a fallback; see this [example appeal thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/friends-game-got-age-restricted-urgent/4902748).
- Groups have been banned on false AI flags. Whether earlier creator rewards still pay out is unclear, possibly after 30 days.
- Appeals over poorly moderated decals are sometimes lost. No public Developer Relations (DevRel) contact exists for these cases.
- Repeated texture flags escalate fast: 7 auto-moderation hits in 48 hours led to a 7-day ban, Studio included, and a 180-day DevEx ban.
- Strikes are said to be permanent, with 3 strikes meaning a permanent ban (low confidence).

### Ban waves in 2026

| Period | Wave | Effect | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early August | AI ban wave on accounts and assets | Scattered false bans | Low |
| Mid–late August | Bot account ban wave | CCU fell across many games | High |
| Late August | Hangout-game false bans | A 500 CCU game earning 10k+ Robux per day was terminated | Low |
| September 7–14 | Anti-fraud bans on Robux commission payments (group payouts, gamepass or shirt payments, trades) | Senders and receivers banned as black-market sellers; a 3.4M-member group lost; appeals slow and bot-answered | High |
| September | Mass cleanup of old assets and accounts | One old shirt cost 3 accounts, 4 clothing groups and 10k Robux | Medium |

- During payment ban waves, freeze Robux commission payments until things settle.
- DevForum threads on the September wave: [false ban thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/false-ban-automated-moderation-system-roblox/4856601) and [repost](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/false-ban-automated-moderation-system-repost/4858902).

### Impact of moderation on reach

- Takedowns rarely fully recover. Reported cases:
    - 17k to 7k CCU after a first takedown.
    - 90% of players gone after restoration from a single-sound DMCA takedown.
    - 40k to 10k CCU after an AI action on the owner account, with updates blocked.
    - A 10-day ban permanently cost one game its algorithm position.
- A questionnaire change cut one game from 3k to 300 CCU. Losing a maturity label cost another about 300 CCU.
- Games have reverted from all ages to 16+ and re-entered final review after questionnaire flags.
