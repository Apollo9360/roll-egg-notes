# Banned monetization and reward mechanics

Up: [[Policy, Moderation and Account Safety]] · Next: [[Content maturity, chat and discoverability]]

| Mechanic | Status and typical outcome | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Robux given to players for in-game tasks | Against the Terms of Service (ToS); game removed | High |
| Robux-for-Robux gambling (spins or credits bought with Robux, Robux prizes) | Banned; never reaches the front page | High |
| Plinko and other casino-style games | High risk; bans for "illegal activity" reported | Medium |
| Robux-funded gambling hangout, even 18+ only | Seen as a likely ToS violation | Low |
| Robux logo in a thumbnail | Reportedly led to a ban | Low |
| Player-to-player donations (PLS Donate style) | Allowed; buying gamepasses from other games was removed in mid-2026, which hurts this model | High |
| Like-for-reward | Technically against ToS; widely used, rarely enforced | Medium |
| Paid random items (lucky blocks, eggs) | Answer "yes" on the questionnaire and block under-18 purchases in the UK and Australia (owner-recommended); governed by the `ArePaidRandomItemsAllowed` policy flag | Medium |
| Reward-driven media feeds (in-game scroll feeds) | Restricted for Roblox Kids and Select accounts since August 24, 2026 | High |
| Selling CCU, followers or likes; botted player counts | Likely against ToS; a botted-followers game was deleted within a week | Medium |
| Selling Robux off-platform (black market) | ToS breach; bans common, especially at DevEx | High |
| Placeholder games used only to route commission payments | Robux earned from empty games is reportedly flagged | Low |

- A casino simulator earning only Premium payouts, with no monetization, may be allowed (low confidence). Such games were historically banned.
- Lucky-block bans were rumored but never confirmed (low confidence).
- Spam bans can be reversed; one restoration took about a week.
- Banned games relaunched with large ad budgets were banned again within a week (low confidence).
