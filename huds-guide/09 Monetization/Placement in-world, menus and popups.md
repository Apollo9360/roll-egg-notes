# Placement in-world, menus and popups

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[High-end items, bundles and upsells]] · Next: [[Product design sell solutions to real problems]]

### In-world

- Physical purchase prompts often beat menus. One developer saw about 10x the sales from in-world points versus a UI shop.
- Example: a ProximityPrompt on a starter-pack model at spawn.
- Place triggers where the need appears: 2x wins near win collection, 2x power where players return to grind, luck passes beside roll pads.
- A tycoon auto-collect button beside the collection area is a proven seller.
- Place stands where players must pass (on the way to upgrades, in front of bases) without blocking movement.
- Moving a product closer to the entrance converts more even if it inconveniences free players (owner-recommended).
- Bigger Robux buttons signal "overpowered" to kids. Use bigger text for 10x than for 1x offers.
- Reserve physical displays for high-Robux items with visible effects and stats. Put 49-Robux items in shop UI or a starter-pack popup.
- Do not put developer-product prompts where players walk over them by accident.
- What sells most is what players see without opening a UI. Lay out the plot to show your 1–2 best sellers.

### Menus and on-screen UI

- Show each product in several places, not only the shop. Include purchase options in every frame the player opens.
- Making products more visible and accessible raised PCR, and in one case retention by about 10%.
- Put starter packs and offers on the right side. Show how cheap the starter pack is.
- Enlarge shop and VIP buttons, put 2x cash on the main screen, and add satisfying buy sounds, confetti and tweens.
- Keep the Robux shop separate from coin crates, and make it obvious what costs Robux versus coins.
- Link "+" buttons (e.g. on a capacity bar) straight to the related pass.
- In incrementals, put strength and boost passes under the main progress bar. Make 2x stand out with a rainbow gradient.
- Rotate gamepasses on screen every few seconds. Make buy buttons bigger with looping motion.
- Do not clutter the HUD with too many buy buttons.
- Show prices on buttons; younger players may click thinking items are free.
- Show boost values and luck values directly, not on hover, because mobile cannot hover.
- Show starter and pro packs as on-screen limited-time offers with bigger icons.
- Rebirth UI must show the reward, or there is no incentive.

### Popups

- Most developers accept contextual prompts at the moment of need and reject random interruptions.
- Good contextual prompts: revive-for-Robux on death, and a prompt when a player repeatedly lacks cash.
- "Not enough cash" should prompt the developer product covering the deficit, not just show a message.
- Auto-opening the cash shop on an unaffordable purchase raised PCR in the owner's farming game. Whether it hurts D1 is untested.
- Many prefer showing the specific item with a "buy with Robux" button rather than opening the whole shop.
- Being too passive also costs money: a game with no pay-to-win and no popups earned about 6k Robux at 800 CCU.
- Animated side popups (common in brainrot games) work for some genres. A minority reject popups entirely.
- Every reward popup needs a close button.
- Egg-opening reward screens should appear only for rare drops (legendary, secret, or rarer than 1 in 1,000).
