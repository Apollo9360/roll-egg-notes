# Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - DevEx

Up: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx]] · Previous: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - Pending times]]

DevEx (Developer Exchange) converts earned Robux into real money.

| Rate pool | USD per Robux | Per 100k Robux | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $0.0038 | About $380 | High |
| Robux earned before the last rate change | $0.0035 | About $350 | Medium |
| Spent by verified US 18+ players | $0.0054 | About $540 | High |

- The US 18+ rate pays about 42% more on that spend; see the [DevForum post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-us-18-devex-rate-earn-42-more-on-spend-from-18-us-players/4607091) and [docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/18-plus-devex-rate).
    - The game must use R15. Any chance to spawn or swap into R6 during play makes it ineligible.
    - R6-style animations on R15 avatars are fine. A 16+ game at 100k CCU missed it for lacking R15.
    - It suits mature-audience games; roleplay games benefit most.
    - Decent US audiences see about 15–20% more revenue overall.
- Payouts split automatically between rate pools. Which pool is spent first (e.g. on ads) is unclear, so some pay ads in USD.
- Buying Robux costs about $1,000 per 100k, against $380–540 from DevEx.
- Requirements: 30,000 earned Robux minimum, age 13+, verified email, a DevEx portal account, and good standing.
- You can DevEx once every 30 days. The owner calls this bad for cash flow; the Roblox card pays faster.
- The first DevEx takes about a month; later ones usually 1–2 weeks.
- Donations and resale items do not count as earned Robux and can trigger rejection. Proof sent to support fixed repeat rejections.

1. Move Robux from the group to your personal account.
2. Submit the DevEx request.
3. If it stays pending 1.5 months or more, send repeated support requests.

- Tax withholding: from 1 November 2026, US withholding of up to 30% applies without a tax ID or US tax treaty.
    - Example: $50k becomes about $35k before local taxes.
    - Treaty countries can be 0%, and the DevEx page warns you.
    - See [Roblox tax docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/tax-information).
- Summer delays are common due to volume. Large balances (around 12M Robux) have been stuck.
- Minors: a "name discrepancy" rejection with guardian info is fixed by a ticket or devex@roblox.com.
- Keep most money in Robux, live cheaply and expense what you can (owner-recommended).
