# Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - Fees and splits

Up: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx]] · Next: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - Pending times]]

- Roblox takes 30% once at each sale, e.g. 99 Robux nets about 69. Tiny items can net 1–3 Robux.
- Group payouts to members carry no second 30% fee.
- Some sales show less than expected (e.g. 42 instead of 69); regional pricing or affiliate splits are suspected.
- Revenue splits (owner-recommended): always take your percentage at the group level.
    - Percentages at the experience level still earn Creator Rewards. Leftover Robux from uneven splits go to the group owner.
    - Commissions from avatar items bought in your game are not in automatic payouts.
    - The verification checkmark needs a percentage in the experience.
- The group funds dashboard requires 18+. You cannot choose which DevEx rate pool pays members.
- To pay from a group without funds, sell a gamepass and absorb the 30% fee.
