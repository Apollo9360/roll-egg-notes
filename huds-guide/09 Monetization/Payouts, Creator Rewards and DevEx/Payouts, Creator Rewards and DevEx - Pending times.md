# Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - Pending times

Up: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx]] · Previous: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - Fees and splits]] · Next: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx - DevEx]]

| Source | Time to available | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Gamepass and developer product sales | About 5 days typical; a couple of days to about 2 weeks; up to about 1 month called normal by Support | High |
| Creator Rewards | 60 days, e.g. earned 29 September, available 29 November | High |
| Group-owned Creator Rewards | Do not show as pending; about 2 months | Medium |
| UGC sales | Can pend longer than a month | Medium |
| Group payout to personal account | Shows pending for about a week | Medium |
| New group's first payout | About 31 days after group creation | Medium |

- The new-group wait is Roblox verifying the group is not used for black-market trading.
- Creator Rewards occasionally fail to appear after 60+ days. A group-percentage payout bug has been reported.
- Check official documentation on whether in-game Roblox Plus prompts earn rewards.
