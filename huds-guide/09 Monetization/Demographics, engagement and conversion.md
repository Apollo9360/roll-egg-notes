# Demographics, engagement and conversion

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Regional and managed pricing]] · Next: [[Revenue expectations by CCU]]

- Monetization only works on engaged players. Heavily monetized games with weak engagement can earn 15–20 Robux per day.
- Five-minute playtime cannot earn money even with excellent monetization.
- New games monetize poorly until Roblox learns the audience. ARPPU around 30 Robux at 15 CCU before reaching the HEP (highly engaged players) threshold is typical.
- Demographics drive revenue more than CCU. A 10k-CCU game with a poor audience can earn like a 1k-CCU game.
- Top markets are likely the US, Australia and the UK. A 25% US share visibly limits revenue.
- Lower-income audiences (Indonesia, Brazil, Philippines, India, Southeast Asia) usually kill monetization. Only Tier-1 countries reliably spend.
- Russian audiences spend little. At about 40 CCU, a handful of new-country players can swing demographics.
- Older audiences spend more. A 2k-CCU older game can out-earn a 50k-CCU kids game.
    - A 16+ social game ranked top-earning at 20–30k CCU, above 400k-CCU kids games.
    - Legacy prison games at 100–800 CCU out-earn many 5k-CCU games.
- The anime audience spends heavily (owner-recommended). Korean, Japanese, Saudi and superhero audiences also earn well.
- Avatar and outfit games earn well even near 100 CCU, with up to 20% PCR.
- Whales matter early: single players have spent 1k–5k Robux within hours, then left.
- Ad traffic monetizes poorly, with PCR and ARPPU swinging daily. Ad players often buy only cheap items like self-revives.
- Organic home-recommendation players spend about 3x more than ad players.
- Purchases are low during the 16+-only phase. All-ages reach needs 250 HEP plus the 1,000-Robux fee or Roblox Plus, or the 50,000-Robux expedited review.
- Unlocking all ages can raise CCU sharply while purchases fall; one game saw 6x CCU and its lowest sales.
- Young-kid ad audiences (default avatars) can yield near zero, e.g. about 5 Robux in 24 hours at 100 CCU.
- Improve D1 before raising prices. Add reasons to return first.

### Algorithm effects

- Roblox eventually drops a game that makes no money, so launch with monetization even if early sales are small.
- Early numbers understate potential until the algorithm learns the audience.
- Revenue usually improves a lot once traffic shifts from ads to organic.
- Having friends buy gamepasses to trick the algorithm is not endorsed.
- How pass and product categories affect monetization is unknown.

### Options for a poor-spending demographic

1. Shift the demographic (hard, usually only possible for long-lived games).
2. Improve monetization, including very cheap items that exploit sunk cost.
3. Sell the game.
4. Find an investor.

- Price cuts give mixed results. Cuts of 25–50% have helped some games.
- Very deep cuts usually earn less, because the underlying demographic problem remains.
