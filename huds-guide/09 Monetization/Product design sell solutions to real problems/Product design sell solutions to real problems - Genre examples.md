# Product design sell solutions to real problems - Genre examples

Up: [[Product design sell solutions to real problems]] · Next: [[Like and group rewards]]

| Genre | Common products | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Obby | Revive popup, playtime rewards, jumpscare buttons, troll panel, slap hand, magic carpet, prompt on leave, working admin | Medium |
| RNG | Keep auto-roll free or as an upgrade; sell luck boosts, rolls and roll-to-buy | Medium |
| Asymmetric horror | Skins, VIP, 2x EXP or gold, loot crates, "play as killer" pass | Medium |
| PvP | Become-the-special-role product, role-chance pass, 39–800+ Robux pack sizes, currency, revive | Medium |
| Survival or raid | Tools, gear speed, player speed, health, pets, 2x money, 2x kills | Medium |
| Shooter or FPS | Robux crates for skins, effects and knife skins with animations | Low |
| Casual (Spin The Gun style) | Ramping difficulty, revives, skips, "fly 500m further" boosts | Medium |
| Incremental | Keyboard Clicker-style cheap products, end-game skips | Medium |
| +1 games | Starter pack, temporary boosts, cheap 2x speed, skins, autoclicker, roll-multiplier titles | Medium |
| Brainrot | Sell packs of brainrots instead of putting them in the base game | Medium |
| Anime or card | Characters, limited cards, starter, pro and OP packs | Medium |

- Niche or hardcore audiences (hard towers, puzzles): pay-to-skip can kill the game. Use donations or cosmetics like chat tags.
- A minority argue that power-ups, level skips and hints for kids make money even in hard games.
- Launch timing: launch with monetization in place. It is free revenue, helps stats, and Roblox eventually drops games that earn nothing.
- Players from early-session funnels differ from veterans: show starter packs and revives first, then 200–800 Robux skins and characters later.
- How to balance paid 2x against free progression remains an open question.
