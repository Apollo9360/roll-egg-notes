# Other income Creator Rewards and ads

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Revenue expectations by CCU]] · Next: [[Terms of Service limits]]

- Creator Rewards (engagement payouts) replaced Premium payouts and are no longer based purely on Premium playtime.
- Reports say Creator Rewards pay about 10x less than old Premium payouts (low confidence).
- They matter for low-monetization games and give consistent income; AFK and playtime games can live on them.
- Payouts are negligible around 100 CCU. Estimates of about 3 Robux per 100 plays are unverified.
- Rewards need Premium or Roblox Plus players; a game with no products and none of those players earns nothing.
- Playtime rewards keep players in-session, which is how many MM2-style copies earn.
- Premium buyers get a discount, but you still receive the full share. Do not inflate prices for them.
- Rewarded video ads earn about 0.1–1 Robux per rewarded view and require the content-maturity questionnaire.
    - They suit young audiences and free players, not whales.
    - Design rewards so free players watch at least 3 per session.
    - Mobile games earn about 20% of revenue from in-game ads.
- Immersive ad payouts are CPM-based (cost per thousand impressions), around $6 typical (low confidence).
- AFK or background-play games suit ad monetization.
