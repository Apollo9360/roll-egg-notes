# Core metrics payer conversion vs ARPPU

Up: [[Monetization]] · Next: [[Product types]]

- Payer conversion rate (PCR) is the share of daily players who buy anything.
- Average revenue per paying user (ARPPU) is Robux spent per buyer.
- Average revenue per daily active user (ARPDAU) combines both: ARPDAU = PCR × ARPPU.
- Read "10% conversion, 250 ARPPU" as 10% of DAU (daily active users) spending about 250 Robux that day.
- Cheap products raise PCR but lower ARPPU. Getting high ARPPU is easy; high PCR with balanced ARPPU is the hard part.

| Signal | Likely meaning | Action | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| High PCR, low ARPPU | Prices too low or no whale items | Raise prices and add whale sinks (owner-recommended) | High |
| PCR below about 0.5% | Low conversion | Good ARPPU is unlikely; fix conversion before raising prices | High |
| PCR rises while ARPPU falls | You added cheap items | Normal; check ARPDAU, not ARPPU alone | Medium |
| Low PCR with a cheap 2x product | Players do not need the currency | Ask why cash does not matter in your loop | Medium |
| Low PCR or ARPDAU overall | Products do not solve an in-game problem | Redesign products around real friction | High |
| Products not selling | Low perceived value or poor visibility | Add value or improve visibility | High |

- Most advice favors fixing PCR first, then ARPPU.
- PCR probably matters more than ARPPU for the algorithm. Many repeat this, but no one has proven it.
- Mismatches are common, e.g. PCR at the 97th percentile with ARPPU at the 36th.
- In incrementals, low ARPPU usually means players progress fine without buying.
- One big spender skipping a day can swing ARPDAU sharply in small games.
- Researching typical genre prices with an AI assistant raised both PCR and ARPPU in one report.
