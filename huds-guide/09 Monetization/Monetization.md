# Monetization

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Revenue comes from engaged, well-targeted players meeting useful products at the moment of need, not from purchases placed everywhere. Pair a very cheap first purchase (1–9 Robux) for payer conversion with 1,000–9,999 Robux whale items, and expect audience and genre to move earnings more than CCU (concurrent users).
