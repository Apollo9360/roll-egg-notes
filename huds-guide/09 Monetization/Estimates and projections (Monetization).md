# Estimates and projections (Monetization)

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Payouts, Creator Rewards and DevEx]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported numbers, not reported facts.

- Estimate: DAU ≈ CCU × 1,440 ÷ daily minutes per player. Basis: the plays-per-day formula above.
    - 100 CCU at 20 minutes ≈ 7,200 DAU; 250 CCU ≈ 18,000; 1k CCU ≈ 72,000.

| CCU (20 min per DAU) | ARPDAU 0.2 (weak) | ARPDAU 1 (average) | ARPDAU 3 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | About 1,400 Robux per day | About 7,200 | About 21,600 |
| 250 (interpolated) | About 3,600 | About 18,000 | About 54,000 |
| 1,000 | About 14,400 | About 72,000 | About 216,000 |

- Estimate: these ranges match reports of 2–3k (weak) and 20k–100k+ (strong) Robux per day at 100–300 CCU. Basis: benchmark table.
- Estimate: a US or 16+ heavy audience sits near the right column; a low-income audience near or below the left. Basis: demographic reports.
- Estimate: 100k Robux per day is about $380 per day at standard DevEx, or $11k per month. Basis: $0.0038 per Robux.
- Estimate: if 35–50% of spend comes from US 18+ players, total payout rises about 15–21%. Basis: 42% uplift times that share.
- Estimate: net per sale is about 70% of list price; a 499 pack nets about 349, a 9 item about 6. Basis: 30% fee.
- Estimate: regional pricing cuts list price about 40–60% in lower-income countries. Basis: 299 shown as 119–169.
- Estimate: regional pricing breaks even only if those buyers increase about 1.7–2.5x. Basis: inverse of the 40–60% cut.
- Estimate: ladder midpoints of about 15, 150, 1,500 and 5,000 Robux. Basis: 1–49 entry, 99–300 core, 499–8,000 high tiers.
- Estimate: time-skip pricing runs about 1–1.5 Robux per minute saved. Basis: about 20 per 15 minutes and 79 per hour.
- Estimate: detecting a 20% sales change needs a few hundred purchases per price, about 2–3 days at 100 CCU. Basis: 2% PCR gives about 140 buyers per day; counting noise is about 1 ÷ √purchases.
- Estimate: at 250 HEP the 1,000-Robux reach fee returns in under a day at about 1 ARPDAU. Basis: 250+ DAU × 1 ARPDAU.
- Estimate: the 50,000-Robux expedited review (refundable after about 90 days) pays back in about 3–25 days at 100 CCU. Basis: 2k–20k Robux per day.
- Estimate: when the threshold drops to 100 HEP (announced for November), small games reach all-ages sooner but expect falling ARPDAU. Basis: unlocking all ages raised CCU while purchases fell.
