# Regional and managed pricing

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Product design sell solutions to real problems]] · Next: [[Demographics, engagement and conversion]]

Managed pricing is Roblox's setting that combines regional (per-country) pricing with automatic price optimization. See the [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managed-pricing-one-system-for-better-pricing-and-earnings-growth/4684738).

| Period (2026) | Prevailing advice | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| June to early July | Recommended; lower-income countries will not pay full price | Medium |
| Late July to August | Debated; most said keep it on, some said disable it for products under about 100 Robux | Medium |
| September | On for normal and expensive items, off for very cheap ones | Medium |
| October | Mostly on, but disputed after one large revenue drop | Low |

- Roblox and the owner cite a 4–10% revenue lift (owner-recommended).
- Example local prices: a 299 item costs 119 in Indonesia and 169 in Argentina; a 400 pass shows about 170 in the Philippines.
- For: it turns zero spenders into small spenders. It mainly raises PCR, not ARPPU.
- Against: you receive fewer Robux per sale. One 600-CCU game fell from 7k to under 1k Robux per day after enabling it (low confidence).
- A 10k+ CCU game saw no noticeable change when toggling it.
- Some fear the algorithm compares your monetization with your genre, so lower prices hurt placement (low confidence).
- Turn it off for very cheap items (under about 10 Robux); no one needs a discount on 9 Robux.
- Pair it with expensive options to protect ARPPU. Some disable it and add appealing high-price passes instead.
- Use it for normal gamepasses but not gifting gamepasses.
- Skip it if your demographic already spends well or monetization targets whales.
- Test by toggling it for a couple of days and reverting if revenue drops. Forcing an optimization test is not possible.
