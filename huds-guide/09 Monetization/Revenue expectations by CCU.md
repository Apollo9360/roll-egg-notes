# Revenue expectations by CCU

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Demographics, engagement and conversion]] · Next: [[Other income Creator Rewards and ads]]

- Plays per day ≈ 1,440 ÷ average playtime in minutes × CCU.
- Example: 15 minutes at 100 CCU gives 9,600 plays per day.
- 0.4–0.9 Robux per play is decent to good, depending on genre. That example then earns about 3,840–8,640 Robux per day.
- Reported lifetime revenue per visit ranges from about 0.6 to 3 Robux; one target is 4–7.
- At equal CCU, a game with twice the playtime has half the DAU, so lower revenue.
- Revenue does not scale linearly with CCU. Games at 10k and 300k CCU can sit side by side on top-earning charts.
- A well-monetized 100-CCU game can earn about 30x a poorly monetized one; under-100-CCU games can out-earn 4k-CCU games.

| Benchmark | Typical value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Floor for a new idea, US audience | About 0.2 ARPDAU | Low |
| 90th-percentile cleaning game | About 0.5 ARPDAU | Low |
| Simulator median ARPPU | About 77 Robux | Low |
| Mid-quality monetization at 7k CCU | About 1 ARPDAU | Medium |
| US-heavy tower games | 2–3 ARPDAU | Medium |
| Good brainrot game | About 3 ARPDAU | Medium |
| Top RNG game | 10–20 ARPDAU | Low |
| Monetization overhaul | ARPDAU from 0.05 to 0.6 | Medium |

| Situation | Typical result | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 700 CCU, average game | About 1,500 Robux per day, considered low | Medium |
| 100 CCU with many cheap products | About 20k Robux per day | Medium |
| 100 CCU, 2% PCR, poor ladder | 2–3k Robux per day | Medium |
| 300 CCU, 10% PCR, 3 ARPDAU | 100k+ Robux per day | Medium |
| 1k peak CCU, 1.5M visits, weak monetization | About $5k USD lifetime | Low |
| 1k CCU niche game vs 6k CCU playtime-farm game | 300k vs 150k Robux per day | Medium |
| Group Creator Rewards | About 600 Robux per day in one report | Low |

| Genre | Monetization | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Incremental, tower defense, anime, RNG | Highest revenue per CCU; a 100-CCU incremental can out-earn a 1k-CCU tower | High |
| Tycoon, simulator, avatar or social | Strong; a good tycoon earns about 16x a good obby | Medium |
| Brainrot | Weak below about 5k CCU | Medium |
| +1 games | Strong organically for some; low during ads and often weak at 1k CCU | Medium (disputed) |
| Obby, tower, minigame, survival | Low ARPDAU; 20k-CCU games can earn little | High |
| Car and driving games | Very weak despite high CCU | Medium |
| Co-op horror | Low; a 2k-CCU game earned less than a 100-CCU one | Low |
| "___ or die" trend | Barely monetizes, even with cheap ads | Medium |
| RPG, fishing | Hard to monetize; weak fishing monetization dies fast | Medium |

- For incrementals, many say balancing matters more than gameplay; others insist gameplay is everything (disputed).
- Survival games tend to have low ARPDAU.
- Some games make serious money at 100 CCU (owner-recommended).

### Game valuation

- Evaluate revenue per 72 hours and lifetime.
- Rough monthly estimate: (72-hour revenue ÷ 3) × 30.
- A 1.5k-CCU game making 150k Robux per day was estimated at about $45k (low confidence).
