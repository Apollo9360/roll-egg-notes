# High-end items, bundles and upsells

Up: [[Monetization]] · Previous: [[Cheap entry items and starter packs]] · Next: [[Placement in-world, menus and popups]]

- Raise ARPPU with whale items and bundles at 1,000–5,000 Robux. An "All Gamepasses" bundle helps.
- Make whale sinks when PCR is high but ARPPU is low (owner-recommended).
- Expensive products do sell: developer products at 2,000+ Robux sold over 20,000 times in one game.
- An anime game at 1k CCU made about 115k Robux per month selling characters at about 3,000 Robux.
- Limited anime characters at 1,499–4,799 Robux sold out in batches of about 250–300. Make only about 2 items limited per update.
- 600+ Robux items are hard for most players; mainly clothing, anime, simulator and older-audience games sell them.
- One big, high-quality shop item can outperform many cheap ones.
- With a good demographic, price boldly: make the item worth it, then push the price up.
- Make 800+ Robux whale items very visible. After a purchase, upsell a bigger "pro pack".
- Tiered items: sell a weaker version for currency or Robux and a much stronger Robux-only version, so players see the gap.
- Stack offers: buying 2x reveals a 3x offer, then 4x, up to a cap; later purchases add time.
- Global server boosts (e.g. +1 luck for 15 minutes) sell very well, especially on update days.
- Upsell, but do not plaster purchases everywhere.
- Incrementals: sell end-game items directly for players who do not want to progress.
- Egg games: sell overpowered pets and eggs for Robux, plus bundles.
- Skins sold via crates with drop rates earn more than fixed-price shop items. Crates must follow the paid random items rules.
- Crates at 49 Robux with a 0.5% top-tier chance deter buyers. Lower prices, add a pity system, and offer a single crate before a 10-pack.
- Cheap rerolls (3–5 Robux) for rare cosmetics work in social flex games; some players spend 10k–600k Robux on rare traits.
- Status items sell on scarcity and exclusivity, far above material value (owner-recommended).
- Cosmetics must be visible to others so players can show them off. Making cosmetics also earnable for free can raise their sales (low confidence).
- 2x currency passes only sell if there are meaningful things to buy.
- Admin or troll products (kill all, fling all, admin roles at 499–999 Robux) earn well; one took a 700-CCU game from about 1k to 30k Robux per day (low confidence).
