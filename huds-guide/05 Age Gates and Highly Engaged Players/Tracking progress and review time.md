# Tracking progress and review time

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Typical costs and timelines]] · Next: [[What happens to CCU at unlock]]

### Where to see HEP progress

- July: Overview, under "Audience reach".
- August onward: the Reach tab in Creator Hub.
- From ~October 12: the new "Manage audience" page. Several developers could not find the counter after the rework.

### Counter lag

- Expect a 1–2 day delay. The panel shows a "last updated" time (owner: be patient, data is slow).
- The counter updates about once a day in UTC, so far-off time zones see it up to ~2 days late.
- Counters sometimes freeze for days, then jump for everyone at once.
- HEP can wobble during ads, for example 330, 320, 335, 330.
- Zero HEP after one day of ads is probably just lag.

### Review time after hitting the threshold

| Period (2026) | Typical wait | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 500 era, June – July | 24–48 hours at "pending final review" | High |
| 500 era, August | 1–3 days; occasional 5-day waits | Medium |
| 250 era, August | Near-instant after paying the 1,000 Robux fee | Medium |
| 250 era, September | 2–3 days; longer if a moderator requests questionnaire changes | Medium |
| 250 era, October | Instant for some, up to 72 hours for others | Medium |
| Expedite fee | ~3 hours to ~2 days; payment verification up to a week | Medium |

- Some games unlocked at 210 HEP or before the counter showed 250. Counter lag likely explains this.
- Redoing the questionnaire once triggered an immediate unlock.
- Check the review status and questionnaire before contacting [Roblox support](https://www.roblox.com/support).
