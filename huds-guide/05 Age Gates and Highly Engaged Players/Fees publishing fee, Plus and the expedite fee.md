# Fees publishing fee, Plus and the expedite fee

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Checklist to unlock all ages]] · Next: [[Pay the fee or run ads]]

### The 1,000 Robux publishing fee

- It is a publishing or reach fee, not a way to skip HEP.
- On the normal route you pay it in the Reach tab after reaching 250, to submit for review.
- It is waived if the owner has held Roblox Plus (or earlier Premium) for 2+ consecutive months.
- The waiver applies even if the UI still shows the fee.
- It is refunded after about 3 months if the game has no takedown or warning and keeps about 100 HEP.
- Paying it alone does not unlock all ages and does not change ad delivery.
- It is not needed to publish to 16+. This confusion is very common.

### The 50,000 Robux expedite fee

- It is optional and skips only the HEP threshold. It was 100,000 Robux until about mid-September.
- It is worth about $175–190 at the Developer Exchange (DevEx) rate, but about $500–600 if bought.
- Unlock after paying took from ~3 hours to ~48 hours. Payment verification sometimes took 3 days to a week.
- It does not put the game in the algorithm. You still need ads and good stats.
- Refund: after ~90 days if the game stays in good standing. Some wording also requires reaching the organic threshold or keeping 100 HEP.
- It is not refunded if the game is rated above all ages or permanently removed.
- A claim that the refund comes even at 0 CCU is disputed.
- Pay only with earned Robux, never with bought Robux.
