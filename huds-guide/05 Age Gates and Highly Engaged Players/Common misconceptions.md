# Common misconceptions

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Age Gates)]]

| Claim | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Pay 1,000 Robux to unlock under-16s. | False. It is paid after 250 HEP, or waived with Plus. It does not skip HEP. | High |
| You must pay 50,000 Robux to publish. | False. The fee is an optional HEP skip; 16+ publishing is free. | High |
| The expedite fee is still 100,000 Robux. | False since about mid-September; it is 50,000. | High |
| The threshold is already 100. | False. 100 is announced for November, not in effect. | High |
| Paying the fee puts the game in the algorithm. | False. It only lifts the gate; stats still decide. | High |
| 250 HEP means 250 CCU. | False. It counts qualifying players over a window. | High |
| Highly engaged players are just 16+ users. | False. They must also play and have spent Robux recently. | High |
| Ad players are what fill the bar. | Mostly false. Home-rec and search players count far more. | High |
| Meeting HEP on one game covers the whole group. | False. It is per game. | High |
| HEP drops are bot removals. | False. Players age out of the rolling window. | High |
| You need the threshold before any home recs. | False. Home recs can start while 16+. | High |
| Engagement ads are the only way to reach HEP. | Disputed. Plays often gets there; results conflict. | Medium |
| Unlocking all ages always spikes CCU. | Usually a spike, often temporary, with many counterexamples. | High |
| Bad 16+ stats will be fixed by all ages. | Usually false. | Medium |
