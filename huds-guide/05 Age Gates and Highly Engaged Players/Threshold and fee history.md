# Threshold and fee history

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Current rules at a glance (October 2026)]] · Next: [[How the 16+ default lock works]]

| When (2026) | Change | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| ~May 19 | 16+ default lock launches; 500 HEP unlocks all ages if the questionnaire passes | High |
| Before mid-June | The 500 rule reportedly applied in only 4 countries | Low |
| Early June | Roblox announces a paid "fast-track" review, refundable for games in good standing ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/alternate-publishing-requirements-for-roblox-kids-and-select/4630944)) | High |
| ~June 16 | Global Kids and Select launch; 100,000 Robux expedited review offered ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)) | High |
| June – July | ~90-day window to reach 500, otherwise reach stays 16+ | High |
| ~July 2 | Refund wording adds "maintains 100 highly engaged players" | Medium |
| ~July 6 | HEP definition loosened; Roblox estimated 33% more games entering Kids and Select ([DevForum recap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/weekly-recap-july-6-10-33-more-games-entering-kids-select/4730771/1)) | High |
| Launch to mid-July | Rule changed from "Plus and 500 HEP" to "1,000 Robux fee or 2 months of Plus" | High |
| Mid-July | "Needs attention" banner asked previously exempt games for the 1,000 Robux fee | Medium |
| ~August 19 | Threshold halved from 500 to 250 for everyone ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164)) | High |
| August 19–25 | 1,000 Robux fee in the Reach tab becomes the post-threshold submit step | High |
| ~September 11–12 | Drop to 100 HEP in November announced around the Roblox Developers Conference (RDC) | High |
| ~Mid-September | Expedite fee cut from 100,000 to 50,000 Robux; developers were paying 50,000 by about September 21 | High |
| Early October | Refund terms: 90 days after eligibility, or 90 days after payment if never eligible; forfeited on permanent moderation removal | Medium |
| Early October | A rumored October 1 drop to 100 did not happen | High |
| ~October 9 | Rumor that the fee will fall to 25,000 Robux | Low |
| ~October 12 | Creator Hub rework adds a "Manage audience" page; a DevForum post titled "A simpler path to publish plus invite early testers" circulated | Medium |
| Mid-October | Suspicion that HEP became harder to earn | Low |
| November (pending) | Threshold expected to drop to 100 | Medium |

- A social-media claim that Roblox removed the HEP requirement was wrong. It was halved.
- A claim that 1,000 HEP was mentioned at RDC was unconfirmed and likely a joke.
- Opinions on the 250 drop differed. Some saw the 500 bar as a useful filter against cash-grab and condo games; others welcomed the cut.
