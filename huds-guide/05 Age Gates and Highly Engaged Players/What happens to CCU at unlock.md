# What happens to CCU at unlock

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Tracking progress and review time]] · Next: [[Common problems and fixes]]

- Common view: CCU spikes at the switch to all ages, often 2–4x. The data is mixed.
- Spikes often revert within minutes to a day. Roblox is likely testing the game with a wider audience.
- Unlocking without home recs is close to useless.
- A bug at the moment of unlock wasted one spike (1.4k down to 10 CCU). Test before the switch.

| Situation | Typical outcome | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good stats, kid-appealing game | CCU doubles or more and holds | 35 to 500+; 150 to 520; 60 to 200; 100 to 3k | High |
| Ad-driven spike | Brief peak, then falls back | 90 to 6 within minutes; 1k back to 3–20 | High |
| Spike that partly holds | Settles above the old level | 20 to 400–500, then 240–300 | Medium |
| Weak playtime or retention | Little or no change | +30%; stuck ~20; 50 before and after | High |
| Drop after unlock | CCU falls for hours or longer | 130 to 40–60; 10–15 to 3–4 | Medium |
| Ads stopped at unlock | CCU collapses | 1–10 CCU | Medium |

- Unlocking raises purchases and player count when the audience is under 16.
- Average playtime and play-through rate (PTR) often rise after unlock, but D1 rarely doubles.
- CTR often jumps after unlock, for example 5% to 10%.
- Purchases per player can fall if the new audience is younger.
