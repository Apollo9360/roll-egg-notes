# Checklist to unlock all ages

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[What counts as a highly engaged player]] · Next: [[Fees publishing fee, Plus and the expedite fee]]

1. ID-verify the owner account. Paying any fee without ID verification is pointless.
2. Enable 2FA on the owner account.
3. Complete the Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire honestly.
4. Reach 250 HEP for that game, or pay the 50,000 Robux expedite fee.
5. On the normal route, pay the 1,000 Robux fee in the Reach tab, or have the owner hold Plus for 2+ consecutive months.
6. Wait for the final review. No button is needed after the bar fills.
7. Confirm the "16+" label has gone from the game page.

- Check ads eligibility first. One developer wasted $32 because the account was not eligible for HEP tracking.
- "This game is not yet eligible for ads" usually means the questionnaire or moderation step is incomplete.
- HEP keeps accumulating while the questionnaire is under review.
- If a moderator rates the game 16+ (fear content, blood, a "moderate" rating), all ages is rejected.
- For group games, the group owner needs Plus for the fee waiver.
- If Plus lapses, new updates drop the game back to 16+.
- One developer sat a day at 250 before realizing the 1,000 Robux fee was unpaid.
- Features gated behind all ages or verification reportedly include immersive ads, selling marketplace items and Team Create collaborators (low confidence).

### Questionnaire risks

- Lying or outdated answers risk takedown. A 30M-visit, 10k-CCU game was removed, with an outdated questionnaire suspected (low confidence).
- Small details count. Red effects not declared as blood led to a 7-day ban; a poo emoji counted as mild crude humor.
- Moderators can flag a questionnaire weeks after unlock, with a deadline to retake it.
- A questionnaire change cut one game from 3k to 300 CCU.
- Paid random items (eggs, lucky blocks, rolls) must be declared "yes" (owner-recommended).
- Under-18 players in the UK and Australia cannot buy paid random items. Gate those purchases and offer a free route.
- Rewarded video ads also need an approved questionnaire.
