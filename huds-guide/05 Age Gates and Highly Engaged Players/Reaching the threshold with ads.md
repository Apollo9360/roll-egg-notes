# Reaching the threshold with ads

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Pay the fee or run ads]] · Next: [[Typical costs and timelines]]

### Plays vs Engagement objectives

- The Engagement objective targets players likely to count as HEP, such as recent spenders.
- Engagement costs far more per play, gets about 10–20x fewer impressions, and brings little CCU.
- Engagement stats also report late, which led to overspending in some cases.
- Roblox's UI says Engagement is "best used to reach eligibility requirements" and auto-suggests it.
- Engagement is no longer offered once the game reaches all ages.
- The owner calls Engagement not worth it (owner-recommended: Plays ads).
- Results genuinely conflict (disputed). Engagement fans report 50–110 HEP per day; Plays fans report 25–100 HEP per day.
- Several weak games still reached 250 on Engagement alone, while good games on Plays sometimes stalled.

### How the advice changed

| Period (2026) | Prevailing advice | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Late May – June | Plays at ~16 credits/day; HEP follows home recs | High |
| Early July | Engagement until the threshold, or mixed 1:2 with Plays | Medium |
| Mid-July – August | Consensus flipped to Plays; Engagement often cost more for less | High |
| September – October | Most common: two campaigns, ~16 Plays + 8 Engagement per day | Medium |
| October variants | 12 + 8, 10 + 6, or Engagement only for 2–3 days | Medium |

### Practical rules

1. Start with Plays to get data and fix the funnel.
2. Add a smaller Engagement campaign (about half the Plays budget) if stats are good but HEP lags.
3. Keep Plays running alongside so HEP-targeted players do not land in empty servers.
4. Kill Engagement once you reach 250 HEP.
5. Taper Plays once home recs arrive. Stop when home-rec impressions or plays exceed ad ones (owner-recommended).
6. Make no big updates during ads, only funnel fixes.

### Targeting and budget

- Target all ages and let Roblox optimize. Ineligible younger users are not served the ad.
- Some target 18+ while gated and saw CTR rise from about 2% to 6–7% (disputed).
- Use a daily budget, not a lifetime budget.
- Typical plan: 16 credits/day for 7–10 days. The minimum is 10/day; 12/day if broke.
- 1 credit ≈ $1 by card ≈ 263–285 Robux. Buying Robux to fund ads costs about twice as much.
- A staged plan: 12/day for 3 days to fix funnels, 16/day for 7 days, then 3 more days if needed.
- Set the genre in ad settings. A missing genre gave under 2% clickthrough rate (CTR); setting it gave 6%.
- Aim for 3%+ CTR; 4.5–6%+ makes HEP fast. Judge thumbnails after 50k–100k impressions (owner-recommended).
- 16+-only CTR sometimes runs lower and often jumps after unlock (disputed).
- Without ads, reaching HEP is unlikely unless TikTok, search trends or a community carry the game.
- A TikTok with ~50k likes reportedly delivered 250 HEP on release (low confidence).
