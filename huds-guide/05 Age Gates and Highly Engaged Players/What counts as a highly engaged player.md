# What counts as a highly engaged player

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[How the 16+ default lock works]] · Next: [[Checklist to unlock all ages]]

- Majority definition: an age-checked 16+ player who played your game long enough and spent Robux on Roblox in the past ~60 days.
- The spending does not have to be in your game.
- Other reported windows range from 30 days to 90 days. Some add account age or tenure (low confidence).
- Playtime threshold guesses range from about 5 to 30 minutes (low confidence). Players with 20–70 minute sessions sometimes still did not count.
- HEP is not visits. A game can have 10k visits and few HEP.
- HEP is not concurrent users (CCU). 250 HEP means 250 qualifying players over the window, not at once.
- The count is a rolling window, not cumulative. It can fall as players age out, for example 240 to 215 or 105 to 45.
- Whether an unlocked game can lose all-ages status over time is unknown; one report says too few monthly users reverts it (low confidence).
- Most HEP arrive from home recs after the algorithm picks the game up, not directly from ads.
- Home-rec and search players count far more often than ad players. Players from search after a TikTok trend count well.
- Many ad visitors never spend Robux, so they never count.
- A chatbot claim that HEP drops are "bot removals" was invented and should be ignored.

### Typical conversion from visits

| Traffic | HEP gained | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 200 visits | 7 | Low |
| 2k visits | 22 to a full 250 (Engagement-only run) | Low |
| 400 visits from Engagement ads | 60 | Low |
| 4.5k–5k visits | 12–65 | Medium |
| ~10k visits | 65–100 | Medium |
| 13k–17k visits | 65–80 (Plays-heavy traffic) | Medium |
| 28k–31k plays | 250 | Medium |
| 20k–50k visits | 500 in the 500 era | High |

- Spread is huge: one game needed 40k visits for 200 HEP, another 3k visits to finish the bar.
