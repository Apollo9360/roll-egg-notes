# Common problems and fixes

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[What happens to CCU at unlock]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Age Gates)]]

### Kids' games

- Kid-targeted games struggle because ads reach only 16+ until unlock, so early stats look bad.
- Some kids' games run Engagement ads to find eligible 16+ players.
- A kids' game now needs the expedite fee or a design that 16+ players also enjoy.
- Many "16+" players are kids on a parent's ID. High CTR but few plays usually means under-16s clicking.
- The owner sees 16+ players as underserved and spending more. Older audiences out-earn kids per CCU.

### Monetization before unlock

- Purchases are low during the 16+-only phase. Average revenue per paying user (ARPPU) of 26–30 Robux at ~15 CCU is normal.
- Some games still earn 17k–30k Robux a week at 25–45 CCU while gated.

### Stuck counters, labels and warnings

- Treat a stuck counter as lag first. Wait for the "last updated" refresh.
- An "All ages" label below the threshold can be a visual bug. Check the game page.
- After the "Manage audience" change, some all-ages games reverted to 16+ and needed the questionnaire redone.
- "Publishing tier at risk" means the next publish could lose expanded reach. It has been buggy.
- The claim that any update loses all-ages reach is disputed. Most say it happens only if Plus lapses.

### Other gotchas

- Do not enable beta mode while waiting for unlock. A game in beta cannot reach home recs.
- Reusing an old experience that already has HEP is generally considered fine. The questionnaire still catches content changes (disputed).
- Do not sell a game cheaply before clearing HEP and running ads.
- Do not pay outsiders for ads or the fee in advance.
- A ".scr" "HEP button script" being passed around is malware.
- Under-16 developers cannot add social links, and ads need a parent's card.
