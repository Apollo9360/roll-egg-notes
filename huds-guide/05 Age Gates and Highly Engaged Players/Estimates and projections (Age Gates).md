# Estimates and projections (Age Gates)

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Common problems and fixes]] · Next: [[Common misconceptions]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported data, not reported facts.

| Estimate | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Ad cost to 250 HEP, decent game | ~$30–100 (30–100 credits) | Reported ~$0.2–1 per HEP on good runs |
| Ad cost to 250 HEP, weak game | ~$150–300, or never | Poor runs at $1–4+ per HEP |
| Ad time to 250 HEP at 16/day | ~3–7 days, plus 1–2 days of counter lag | Reported 2–7 days on good runs |
| Ad cost to 100 HEP (November) | ~$15–40 | Per-HEP cost scaled; early HEP accrues slower before home recs |
| Ad time to 100 HEP | ~1.5–4 days | Interpolated between 65 HEP in 2 days and 250 in 3–7 days |
| Visits needed for 250 HEP | ~5k–25k | Halving 500-era figures of 20k–50k; 10k visits gave 65–100 |
| Expedite fee value | ~$175–190 at DevEx, ~$500–600 bought | DevEx ~$3.5–3.8 per 1,000 Robux |
| Expedite time saved | ~3–10 days | Hours to 2 days paid versus 3–7 days plus review by ads |
| Expedite route total spend | 50,000 Robux locked plus ~$50–150 of Plays ads | Ads are still needed for data and the algorithm |
| Normal route fees | 1,000 Robux (~$3.5–10) or ~2 months of Plus | Refundable fee or subscription |
| Unlock CCU change | ~2–4x spike in about half of cases; often reverts | Tally of reported outcomes |

- Rule of thumb: pay the fee when a few days of reach are worth more than tying up 50,000 Robux for 90 days.
- Rule of thumb: if 100 credits of Plays have not delivered 250 HEP, fix the game rather than paying to skip.
- If the November drop holds, the ad route should cost roughly 40% of today's.
