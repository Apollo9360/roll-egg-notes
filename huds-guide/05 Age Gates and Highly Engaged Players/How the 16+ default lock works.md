# How the 16+ default lock works

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Previous: [[Threshold and fee history]] · Next: [[What counts as a highly engaged player]]

- Every newly published game defaults to 16+ reach. Under-16 players can only play games rated for them.
- The server owner and many others see the change as a response to lawsuits against Roblox.
- While a game is 16+, it is hidden from under-16 users everywhere, including home recommendations ("home recs") and ads.
- Ads serve only to eligible 16+ users while the game is 16+, whatever the ad targeting says.
- 16+ players can still join with a direct link.
- Publishing to 16+ is free with an age-checked account. Publishing privately needs no age check.
- The threshold is per game, not per group or account. Every new game starts from 0.
- At launch there was a ~90-day window to reach the threshold, or expanded reach was deactivated.
- A game can reach home recs and "the algorithm" while still 16+ (owner-recommended). One game had ~100 home recs at 150 HEP.
- 16+ games only reach the home pages of 16+ users.
- A kid-targeted game rarely gets a real algorithm push until it clears HEP.
- Being stuck at 16+ also weakens signals such as likes, since kids who favorite the game cannot play it.
- Explainer on ads, HEP and the home page: [YouTube](https://youtu.be/m7015JSIrpw).
