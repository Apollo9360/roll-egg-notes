# Current rules at a glance (October 2026)

Up: [[Age Gates and Highly Engaged Players]] · Next: [[Threshold and fee history]]

| Item | Current state | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Default reach for new games | 16+ only (age-checked players) until the game qualifies | High |
| HEP threshold | 250 highly engaged players, counted per game | High |
| Expedite fee | 50,000 Robux, skips the HEP threshold entirely | High |
| Expedite refund | After roughly 90 days, if the game stays in good standing; exact conditions reported vary | Medium |
| Normal-route publishing fee | 1,000 Robux in the Reach tab after reaching 250, or Roblox Plus held 2+ consecutive months | High |
| Account requirements | ID or age verification and two-step verification (2FA) on the owner account | High |
| Content requirement | Approved Maturity & Compliance Questionnaire rating the game suitable for under-16s | High |
| Next change | Drop to 100 HEP announced for November; not in effect yet | Medium |
| 25,000 Robux fee | Rumor only, unverified | Low |

- Two routes exist. Normal route: reach 250 HEP, then pay 1,000 Robux or hold Plus. Expedite route: pay 50,000 Robux and skip the threshold.
- Either route still needs ID verification, 2FA and a passed questionnaire.
- Whether the expedite route also needs the 1,000 Robux fee or Plus is unclear (low confidence that it does not).
- Official docs: [Kids and Select publishing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select).
