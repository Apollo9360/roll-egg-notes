# Age Gates & Highly Engaged Players

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

As of October 12, 2026, a new game stays 16+ until it reaches 250 highly engaged players (HEP). A refundable 50,000 Robux expedite fee skips that threshold. Most developers reach 250 with about $30–100 of ads in 3–7 days; the server owner pays the fee for trend games because speed matters.
