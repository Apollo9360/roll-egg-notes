# PTR (Play-Through Rate)

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Share of home-recommendation impressions that become a play. Roblox median ~1.07%, 90th percentile ~2.13%. Driven by thumbnail, icon and title; the main gate to home-rec exposure.
