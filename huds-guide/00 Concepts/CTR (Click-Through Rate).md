# CTR (Click-Through Rate)

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Share of ad or thumbnail impressions that get clicked. In ads, under ~2% means replace the creative; 3–5% is good; 5–8%+ is great. Judge after ~10k impressions.
