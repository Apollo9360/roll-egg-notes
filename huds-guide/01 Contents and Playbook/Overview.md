# Overview

Up: [[Contents and Playbook]] · Next: [[Launch playbook]]

Both channels agree on the core method: fix onboarding and retention, buy data with a Plays ad campaign at about 16 credits/day for 7–14 days, test up to 25 thumbnails, and let home recommendations take over. This guide cross-checks that advice across two channels of the server.

| Owner posts (in #general-chat) | May 20 – Oct 12, 2026 | 8,761 messages | All substantive posts, read with surrounding context |

How to read it:

- **Confidence.** Tables carry a Confidence column. High means corroborated in both channels, by many independent reports, or taught by the owner and not contradicted. Medium means several reports in one channel. Low means single or conflicting reports.
- **Markers in bullets.** "(low confidence)" and "(disputed)" flag weak or contested points; unmarked bullets are High or Medium. "(owner-recommended)" marks practices the server owner explicitly teaches.
- **Estimates.** Most tabs end with an "Estimates and projections" section. Those figures are interpolated or extrapolated from reported data, each with its basis stated. Treat them as planning numbers, not facts.
- **Platform rules move fast.** The age gate changed three times in five months. Check the Roblox Creator Hub before acting on any policy item.
