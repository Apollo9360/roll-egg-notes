# Quick reference

Up: [[Contents and Playbook]] · Previous: [[Launch playbook]]

These are the most-used numbers as of October 12, 2026. Full ranges, Roblox's own percentile benchmarks and the estimates behind them are in the [[Benchmarks and Typical Results|Benchmarks]] tab.

| Item | Figure | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| HEP threshold for all-ages reach | 250 (500 until ~Aug 19); 100 announced for November | High |
| Expedite fee (skips the threshold) | 50,000 Robux, refundable after ~90 days (100,000 until ~mid-September) | High |
| Normal-route fee after 250 HEP | 1,000 Robux, or Roblox Plus held 2+ months | High |
| Estimated cost to 250 HEP with ads | Strong game 30–60 credits; average 85–125; weak 250+ | Estimate |
| Ad budget | 16 credits/day (daily budget) for 7–14 days, Plays objective, broad targeting | High |
| Credit price | 1 USD = 1 credit; about 255–285 Robux per credit | High |
| Learning phase / metric lag | Ignore first 24–48 h; data lags about 2 days | High |
| Ad CTR | Under 2% cut; 2–3% OK; 3–5% good; 5–8%+ great | High |
| Sample before judging a thumbnail | About 10k impressions | High |
| Cost per play | 0.003–0.010 credits typical; stop a thumbnail above ~0.008 | High |
| Home PTR (play-through rate) | Roblox median 1.07%, 90th percentile 2.13% | High |
| Average playtime | Median 11.6 min, 90th 25 min; aim 15–25 | High |
| D1 retention | Median 7.3%, 90th 12.0%; ad-only D1 runs about half of home-rec D1 | High |
| D7 retention | Median 0.65%, 90th 1.98% | Medium |
| Bounce rate (first 60 s) | Under ~15% target; 20–25% is bad | Medium |
| Payer conversion / ARPPU | Median 0.43% / 70 Robux; 90th 2.63% / 316 Robux | High |
| Home recs start | About 3–10 days into ads with good stats | High |
| Stop ads when | Home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions | High |
| Revenue per 1k CCU | ~70k Robux/day mixed audience; ~245k Western; ~3.5k Southeast Asia | Estimate |
| DevEx rate | About $0.0038 per Robux standard; ~$0.0054 for US 18+ funds | Medium |
| DevEx tax | US withholding of up to 24–30% from Nov 1 without tax info on file | Medium |
