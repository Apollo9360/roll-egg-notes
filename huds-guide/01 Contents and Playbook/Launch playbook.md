# Launch playbook

Up: [[Contents and Playbook]] · Previous: [[Overview]] · Next: [[Quick reference]]

This is the launch sequence both channels converge on as of October 2026. Detail for each step is in the topic tabs listed under Contents.

1. **Build the first 5 minutes before anything else.**
    - Within 30–60 seconds a new player must know where to go and how to progress. Use arrows, beams and highlights; kids don't read.
    - Spawn players at the first objective and make early earning steps take seconds. A short cutscene on first load is a proven bounce-rate fix (one tycoon went from ~21% to ~14%) (owner-recommended).
    - Let players see each other's progress; visible "social flex" is a strong retention lever (owner-recommended).
    - Track every tutorial step as a funnel and fix any step losing more than ~2% of players.
    - Targets: tutorial completion 80%+, first-60-second bounce under ~15%, 5-minute retention 50–70%.
2. **Set up the account and the game.**
    - ID-verify the owner and turn on 2FA (two-step verification). Complete the maturity questionnaire honestly and set the game's genre.
    - New games start locked to 16+.
3. **Decide how to clear the age gate.**
    - Normal route: reach 250 highly engaged players (HEP), then pay the 1,000 Robux fee or hold Roblox Plus. Average games take about 5–8 days and 85–125 credits of ads.
    - Expedite route: pay the refundable 50,000 Robux fee (about 195 credits' worth) to skip the threshold. It is mainly worth it for trend games where speed matters (owner-recommended for those).
    - A drop to 100 HEP is announced for November.
4. **Make up to 25 thumbnails.**
    - Show the core fantasy and real gameplay: bright, 2–3 large subjects, high contrast, little or no text.
    - AI images built from competitor references routinely beat commissioned art. Only 5 go on the game page; ads rotate up to 25.
5. **Buy credits in USD and launch a Plays campaign.**
    - 1 USD = 1 credit, versus about 255–285 Robux per credit. Never buy Robux to buy ads.
    - Daily budget about 16 credits for 7–14 days, broad targeting, no pausing. Choose Plays, not the "recommended" Engagement objective. A small parallel Engagement campaign (about 8/day) until 250 HEP is a common add-on (disputed).
6. **Read the data, not the CCU.**
    - Ignore the first 24–48 hours of learning. Judge thumbnails after about 10k impressions; cut anything under ~2% CTR.
    - Ad players show roughly half the D1 and lower playtime of home-rec players, so judge D1 against that.
    - Update the game daily or every few days during the run, mainly onboarding and funnel fixes (owner-recommended).
7. **Hand off to home recommendations.**
    - First home recs usually arrive 3–10 days into ads. Stop or taper ads once home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions.
    - If there are no home recs after about 2 weeks, fix the game instead of spending more.
8. **Read the spikes correctly.**
    - Spikes followed by crashes are Roblox testing the game. The next, bigger test depends on D1, playtime and monetization beating the last one.
    - The algorithm weights long-term retention (play days, D1–D28) and monetization most; it changes often and silently.
9. **Monetize for ARPDAU (Robux per daily user), not CCU.**
    - Pair a cheap first purchase (3–49 Robux) with 1,000–9,999 Robux items for big spenders, plus offline-earnings and 2x products.
    - Audience region swings revenue by 10–50x at the same CCU; turn on regional pricing with care.
10. **Keep shipping.** Weekly meaningful updates sustain D7 and play days. If stats are still weak after about 2 weeks of ads, change the game, not the budget.
