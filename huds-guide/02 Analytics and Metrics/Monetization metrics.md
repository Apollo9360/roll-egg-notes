# Monetization metrics

Up: [[Analytics and Metrics]] · Previous: [[Retention, playtime and sessions]] · Next: [[Highly engaged players (HEP) and audience reach]]

- ARPDAU equals PCR × ARPPU, so daily revenue equals DAU × PCR × ARPPU.
- Watch ARPDAU over ARPPU. A 1–3 Robux deal drags ARPPU down while raising conversion.
- PCR is not an algorithm signal and is easily gamed. Some call 7-day Robux spent in home-rec stats the most accurate monetization stat.
- High ARPPU with very low PCR is a conversion problem. Add a cheap item many will buy, and make passes visible.
- High conversion with low ARPPU, or the reverse, both underperform average conversion with decent ARPPU.
- Split ARPDAU and DAU by country. An apparent 0.01 ARPDAU was fixed this way, and it separates a monetization problem from a demographic shift.
- A flood of low-paying regions can cut ARPDAU to about 0.1 and kill ad profitability.
- A game at 0.1 ARPDAU with sales high relative to creator rewards can be better monetized than a 1.0 game living on rewards.

| ARPDAU | Reading | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| About 0.1 | Low; often a low-paying region mix | Medium |
| About 0.25 | Weak but not terrible | Medium |
| About 0.7 | Reported as below average | Low |
| 2 | Seen from a single whale; not real | Medium |
| 5 | Exceptional; seen on low-CCU games | Medium |

- CCU is a vanity metric. Monetization and ARPDAU decide earnings and a game's sale value (owner-recommended).
- 4,000 players spending 1,000 Robux in about 12 h is roughly 0.25 ARPDAU.
- Ads Manager earnings and ARPPU are unreliable because one buyer swings them. Watch CPP and Home Rec Signals instead.
- Pre-launch purchases inflate PCR. A single developer purchase can show 100% PCR that lingers in averages.
- PCR can differ widely between views on a new game, for example 15% on the 7-day average versus 2% daily.
- Private servers count toward ARPPU, ARPDAU, paying users and PCR.
- The 30% platform cut is taken on every sale up front, not at DevEx.
- Whether "Hourly Robux spent" is before or after that cut is unconfirmed (low confidence).
- Robux arriving without in-game purchases are most likely engagement-based creator rewards.
- Creator rewards matter less at scale; one developer called them almost negligible at 40k CCU (low confidence).
- Gamepass sales history goes back only 90 days; lifetime sales per pass are not visible.
- In one studio's game, about 15% of players still active at D30 were payers (low confidence).
