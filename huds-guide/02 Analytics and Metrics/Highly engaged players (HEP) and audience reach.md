# Highly engaged players (HEP) and audience reach

Up: [[Analytics and Metrics]] · Previous: [[Monetization metrics]] · Next: [[Where to find things]]

- HEP counts players who joined, met a playtime threshold and spent Robux on any experience in the past 60 days.
- The playtime threshold is reportedly about 5 min. ID verification, Plus and bans may also matter (low confidence).
- HEP is not visits. You can have 10k visits and 0 HEP, and 50k-visit games have stayed short.
- For example, 200+ players with 15 min playtime counted as only about 90 HEP.
- Many ad players do not count because they have not spent Robux. Young or free-to-play audiences fill the bar slowly.
- HEP is a rolling 60-day total, not cumulative. A minority say 3 months (low confidence).
- Counts can fall, for example 105 to 45 in a day, or wobble 330, 320, 335 during an ad run.
- AI-chatbot explanations that HEP drops come from "bot removal" are made up.
- HEP counts once per day in UTC. Far from UTC, you may see progress 1–2 days late.

| Period | Rule | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Late May to about August 19, 2026 | 500 HEP to unlock all-ages reach | High |
| From about August 19, 2026 (current) | 250 HEP, then the 1,000 Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus | High |
| Until about mid-September 2026 | Expedited review skipping HEP cost 100,000 Robux | High |
| Current | Expedited review costs 50,000 Robux, refundable after roughly 90 days; conditions reported vary | High |
| November 2026 (announced, not in effect) | Threshold drops to 100 HEP | Medium |
| Rumor | 25,000 Robux expedited fee | Low |

- See the [[Benchmarks and Typical Results|Benchmarks]] tab for full unlock details.
- A "Publishing tier at risk" warning appears when HEP drops. You lose that audience reach at the next publish.
- An "All ages" label far below the threshold is a visual bug. Check the maturity label on the game page.
- Even dead games without ads usually show 5–10 people in Reach. 0 HEP after a day of ads is usually just delay.
- HEP counts usually appear about 2 days after ads start.
- Check HEP on Overview > Audience reach (shows 16+ until unlocked), under the game description, or in settings.
- On October 12, 2026 the Creator Hub rework added a "Manage audience" page. Many users could not find the HEP chart afterwards.
