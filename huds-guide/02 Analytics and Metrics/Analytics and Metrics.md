# Analytics & Metrics

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

The home-recommendation algorithm reads PTR (play-through rate, packaging quality) and first-play bounce rate (onboarding quality) first, then play days and playtime. Read them in the Daily view filtered to home-rec traffic. Wait for roughly 10k+ visits or 500+ DAU (daily active users) and 2–3 days of settled data.
