# Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - PTR and QPTR

Up: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate]] · Previous: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - CTR]] · Next: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - Bounce rate]]

- PTR and QPTR come only from home-rec (and search) impressions. Ad plays never produce them.
- If a QPTR or PTR number exists at all, Roblox is already showing you on home recs.
- 0.0% PTR or QPTR, or a 1st–2nd percentile reading, before home recs start is normal.
- PTR depends on thumbnail, icon, title, description and trailer, not onboarding.
- Qualified-play length is disputed. Most reports say about 5 min; a minority say 2–3 min.
- A thumbnail is penalized on QPTR only when a clicker leaves before the qualified mark.
- Low QPTR with fine PTR or CTR is usually a high bounce rate.
- A better thumbnail can raise CTR and PTR but does not fix QPTR, which depends on onboarding.
- If PTR and bounce rate are fine, low QPTR is not a problem.
- PTR changes slowly after a thumbnail swap, while other stats update within minutes.
- PTR can swing day to day while QPTR stays steadier. One game's PTR fell from 4.7% to 0.9% overnight while QPTR held at 2.2%.

| Ad CTR versus home-rec rate | Observed | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| QPTR below ad CTR | Typically 3–10x lower, such as 6.5% CTR with 1.1% QPTR or 4% CTR with 0.49% QPTR | High |
| PTR versus ad CTR | Varies widely; about 6x lower in one game, higher (3% CTR, 8% PTR) in another | Low |
| PTR versus QPTR | PTR about 2x QPTR | Low |

- Ad CTR hints at your potential PTR but often fails to translate. The owner blames audience mismatch and says to test thumbnails on home recs.
- PTR and QPTR fall as home-rec impressions scale, for example from 17k to 50k a day.
- One tycoon game's QPTR fell from about 2.1% in week one to about 1.5% at 2k CCU.
- Worry only if PTR drops by about 70% after an impression jump.
- Strong games usually have PTR around 5% or less; 3–5% is good.
- About 1% QPTR in Simulation or Tycoon is poor, though it reached the 90th percentile in one incremental game.
- Do not confuse a PTR percentile (for example 90th falling to 60th) with the PTR percentage.
- A claimed 7% QPTR on hundreds of millions of impressions was widely doubted as implausible.
- Home-rec viewers form a rotating but overlapping pool. Skips accumulate, so QPTR decays; some refresh thumbnails every 1–2 weeks (low confidence).
