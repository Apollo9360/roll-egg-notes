# Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - Bounce rate

Up: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate]] · Previous: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - PTR and QPTR]] · Next: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - Clicks versus plays]]

- Bounce rate is the share of first-time players who leave within about 60 s. It is a negative stat: you want it low and below benchmark.
- The dashboard has two buckets: under 60 s and 61–180 s.
- A high under-60 s bounce means the first 180 s are not streamlined. A poor 61–180 s bucket marks a dead zone to fix immediately.
- A high bounce rate reduces home exposure. Roblox likely uses it to catch clickbait and weak onboarding.
- Bounce is roughly the inverse of tutorial completion. A 60 s tutorial that 75% complete implies about 25% bounce.

| Bounce reading | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 15% or lower | Owner target; depends on genre | High |
| 0–9% | Home-rec benchmark range after a September 21 benchmark change | Low |
| 1–3% | Aggressive target some aim for | Low |
| 16% (0–60 s) and 11% (61–180 s) | Reported on an incremental game | Medium |
| 15–25% with 21 min home-rec playtime | Reported; long playtime does not cancel high bounce | Low |

- Small samples swing hugely. Readings of 9% (0–60 s) and 45% (61–180 s) came from only 11 home-rec plays.
- At about 25 qualified plays a day, percentiles near the 60th and 95th are common noise.
- Use the bounce-rate-by-age-group breakdown. 0% in an age group can mean nobody from that group plays.
- Some home-rec views do not show bounce rate at all.
