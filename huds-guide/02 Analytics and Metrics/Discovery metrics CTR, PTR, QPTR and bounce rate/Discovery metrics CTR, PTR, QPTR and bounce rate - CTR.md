# Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - CTR

Up: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate]] · Next: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - PTR and QPTR]]

- CTR exists only while ads run. Home-rec CTR has no official benchmark; PTR does.
- CTR falls naturally as impressions grow.
- Ad spend sets a fixed impression budget. Roblox decides which thumbnail gets most of it.
- Compare thumbnails by CPP or plays per credit, not CTR alone. A lower-CTR thumbnail with 3x the plays for the same spend wins.
- High CTR does not guarantee good PTR or QPTR. A baseplate with a great thumbnail can get clicks but no qualified plays.
- High CTR with high CPP means the thumbnail over-promises and people leave on the game page. Replace it.
- Do not optimize purely for CTR. It pulls in lower-intent players with shorter sessions.
