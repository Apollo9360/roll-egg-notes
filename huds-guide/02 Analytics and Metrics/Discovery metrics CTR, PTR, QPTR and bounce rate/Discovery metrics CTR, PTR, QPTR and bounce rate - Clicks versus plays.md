# Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - Clicks versus plays

Up: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate]] · Previous: [[Discovery metrics CTR, PTR, QPTR and bounce rate - Bounce rate]]

- Clicks-to-plays should be about 50% (owner-recommended). 988 clicks with 530 visits is typical.
- Clicks far above plays means the detail page (first image, name or description) turns people away.
- Problem cases have ranged from about 2% (1,200 clicks, 23 plays) to 12%.
- Fix it by making the description concise and accurate to the game.
- First rule out delayed play stats, which can lag 1–2 days, and check you are not confusing clicks with impressions.
