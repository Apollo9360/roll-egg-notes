# Likes and external estimates

Up: [[Known bugs, external tools and performance stats]] · Previous: [[Known bugs, external tools and performance stats - Platform bugs]] · Next: [[Custom and API analytics]]

- Most developers say like ratio does not feed discovery; it is feedback that may sway players. A minority still treat a bad ratio as a warning sign (disputed).
- Like ratio usually improves over time, for example from 60% at 50 ratings to 96% later.
- Favorites are visible but likes are not, so most games ask players to "like and join group". Favorites reportedly do not boost analytics.
- A [player liked games API thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-player-liked-games-api/3392567) exists, but others say like status cannot be checked in scripts.
- Revenue estimator sites multiply DAU by an assumed average monetization. They are roughly right only for average games.
- creatorexchange.io revenue is very inaccurate. Use it for keyword research, not revenue (owner-recommended).
- Reported errors run both ways. One game earning 60k Robux a day showed 12k; a 3k CCU game showed 44k.
- Rotrends is useful for research but can badly undercount CCU and playtime.
- Roblox does not share per-player playtime with third parties, so outside sites cannot calculate it exactly.
- Do not give analytics access to owners of data-aggregation sites (low confidence).
- Creator-exchange sites show other games' trends. Higher lows every day is a healthy pattern.
