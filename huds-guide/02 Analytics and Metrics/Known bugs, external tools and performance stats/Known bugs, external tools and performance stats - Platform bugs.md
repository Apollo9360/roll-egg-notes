# Known bugs, external tools and performance stats - Platform bugs

Up: [[Known bugs, external tools and performance stats]] · Next: [[Likes and external estimates]]

- Single-day 0% retention between normal days (for example 12% and 20%) is a dashboard bug.
- D1 and D7 tiles can freeze or show junk such as "0.01%" for days after release.
- Platform-side incidents have included analytics delays, thumbnail upload errors and stuck "all ages" labels.
- Other outages stopped retention and engagement data, broke custom events, and delayed benchmarks by about 7 h.
- Analytics and Ads Manager were slow or broken repeatedly in late September and early October 2026.
- Panels showing different numbers for the same stat are common visual bugs, including the D1/D7 graph versus the cohort chart.
- Badge "% owned" stats can be bugged on new games.
- "Not eligible" means fewer than 100 eligible plays in the last 30 days. Whether the window is really about 30 min is disputed.
- When a game goes public after weeks private, analytics show zero-playtime days for the private period. The effect is unknown.
