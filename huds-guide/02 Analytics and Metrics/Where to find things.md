# Where to find things

Up: [[Analytics and Metrics]] · Previous: [[Highly engaged players (HEP) and audience reach]] · Next: [[Daily view vs 7-day average, and sample size]]

| What | Where | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Analytics on a phone or browser | Game page > three dots (top right) > View Analytics | High |
| PTR, QPTR, bounce rate, signal weights | Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations, scroll down | High |
| Home-rec benchmark and competing games beating yours for impressions | Acquisition > Home Recs, scroll to the bottom | High |
| Home-rec D1 | Overview, scroll down | High |
| Impressions CTR and Detail Page CTR | Table at the bottom of the Acquisition page | Medium |
| CTR by source | Acquisition instead of Overview | Medium |
| Traffic sources | Acquisition chart lines: total, friends, other, search, home recommendation | High |
| Onboarding conversion | Acquisition > New Users funnel > click Acquisition again | Low |
| First-session retention | Analytics > Retention | High |
| Benchmarks, D1, D7, playtime | Overview; Analytics > Engagement | High |
| Average session time | Analytics > Engagement, scroll down | Medium |
| Cumulative playtime | Engagement > Total playtime hours, switch to cumulative | Low |
| Peak and intraday CCU | Monitoring > Performance; pick 7 days and half-hour interval | High |
| HEP progress | Overview > Audience reach; Manage audience page | High |
| ARPDAU | Monetization > Overview, scroll to the bottom | Medium |
| Revenue, last 72 h | Monetization | Medium |
| PCR by country | Monetization breakdown | Medium |
| Player feedback | Analytics, Feedback section; written feedback under Audience | Medium |
| Funnels | Analytics > Engagement > Funnels; filter to any period | High |
| Per-thumbnail ad stats | Ads Manager > click the campaign name; click the CTR header to sort | High |
| Per-thumbnail organic impressions and QPTR | Configure Place > Thumbnails; Thumbnail Performance view | Medium |

- Home-rec impressions can show in thumbnail personalization days before the Home Recs tab fills.
- "Other" in impressions by source seems to include some home-rec traffic.
- Some home-rec plays in Acquisition mean you are partly, not fully, on the home page.
- Search traffic is not home-rec traffic.
- To drill in, click a metric box such as average playtime, then "Explore this metric".
- Peak CCU may be empty on brand-new games. It also lags, for example showing 60 when the real peak was 90.
- The "similar experiences your players play" list can be missing even at about 300 CCU (unexplained).
- The Overview page stats matter most, but dashboard stats are blended across all traffic sources.

To diagnose a declining game:

1. Open New Players.
2. Turn off the benchmark.
3. Split by source over the last year.
4. Compare home recs with other join types.
