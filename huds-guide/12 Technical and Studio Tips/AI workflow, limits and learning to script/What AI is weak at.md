# What AI is weak at

Up: [[AI workflow, limits and learning to script]] · Previous: [[AI workflow, limits and learning to script - Workflow tips]] · Next: [[AI workflow, limits and learning to script - Learning to script]]

- AI can't "see" UI; it needs MCP to edit properties. It is better at UI functionality than looks.
- AI is bad at optimization, balancing, taste, ideas and fun.
- No AI could do R15 animations as of early August 2026.
- Building with AI is disputed. Some build maps with Claude and props via Blender MCP; others say AI maps, models and UI are unoptimized.
- Large language models (LLMs) can't generate meshes directly; use Tripo, Meshy, [Hunyuan 3D](https://3d.hunyuanglobal.com/studio) (20 free models a day) or Astra, then optimize.
- Meshy on sale offered 10,000 credits for $25, roughly 500 untextured or 285 textured models.
- Roblox previewed [segmented 3D model generation](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/early-preview-generate-3d-models-segmented-into-multiple-parts/4678458), producing models split into parts.
- Generating a sub-2k-tri mesh with Studio's AI, then refining in Blender and adding studs by hand, beat Blender MCP generation for one modeler.
- By October 2026 AI handled modeling and building well with specific instructions; VFX, animation and UI still needed humans or heavy iteration.
- Whether AI now out-models human modelers is disputed: one 3-year modeler said Opus 5.5 beats him, others say skilled modelers still win on quality and performance.
- Whether manual scripting is obsolete is disputed; some developers found AI-made code limited their games.
