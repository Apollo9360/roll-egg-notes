# AI workflow, limits and learning to script - Learning to script

Up: [[AI workflow, limits and learning to script]] · Previous: [[What AI is weak at]]

- Start with fundamentals from beginner series such as BrawlDev's, then build your own systems from API docs and the DevForum.
- Use AI as a tutor: tell it to direct your thinking without writing code. One learner gained more in a day than in two weeks of tutorials.
- Level up with modules, object-oriented programming (OOP), types, and your own utility modules or plugins.
- Even with AI, learn basic Luau, client/server architecture, debugging, data structures and DataStore saving to direct and fix AI code.
- A common skill ladder: basic scripter, then AI alone, then AI plus basic scripter, then advanced scripter, then advanced scripter plus AI.
- AI-built games have reached 1k–40k CCU, but understanding Studio and architecture is what gets past ~1k CCU and a month of lifespan.
- Vibe-coded codebases become hard to maintain at 10k CCU; a simple brainrot game shouldn't need 50k lines.
