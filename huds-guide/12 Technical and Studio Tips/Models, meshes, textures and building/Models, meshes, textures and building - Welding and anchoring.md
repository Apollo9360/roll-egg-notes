# Models, meshes, textures and building - Welding and anchoring

Up: [[Models, meshes, textures and building]] · Previous: [[Models, meshes, textures and building - Unions and collisions]] · Next: [[Models, meshes, textures and building - Texture asset IDs]]

- Weld multi-mesh models with Moon Animator's Easy Weld, Massless, or HingeConstraints for turrets.
- Models can't be anchored directly; Alt-select the parts or tell an AI or Model Context Protocol (MCP) agent to anchor everything.
