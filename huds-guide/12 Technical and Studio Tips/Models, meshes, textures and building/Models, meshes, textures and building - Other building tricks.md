# Models, meshes, textures and building - Other building tricks

Up: [[Models, meshes, textures and building]] · Previous: [[Models, meshes, textures and building - Texture asset IDs]]

- Colorful Ambient plus ColorCorrection gives a stylized look; Future lighting gives smooth following lights.
- Cartoony or simulator lighting templates from YouTube give a consistent look.
- Odd shadows come from sun position or time of day, not CastShadow.
- Squishy objects: skinned meshes plus bones (modelers) or EditableMesh (scripters and AI). EditableMesh texturing is hard and laggy.
- Models that closely copy another game's look can draw a copyright (DMCA) takedown (owner-recommended).
- 3D item icons: the [Photobooth plugin](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/82716202460157/Photobooth) bulk-renders with strokes and glows; Multi-Photobooth exports via OBJ for PNGs.
