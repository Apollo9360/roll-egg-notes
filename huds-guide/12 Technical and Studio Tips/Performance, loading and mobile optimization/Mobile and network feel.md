# Mobile and network feel

Up: [[Performance, loading and mobile optimization]] · Previous: [[Memory, rendering and network]]

- Use Roblox's default joystick rather than a custom one (owner-recommended).
- Fix lag spikes at normal settings rather than defaulting to a performance mode.
- High latency in shooters or player-versus-player (PvP) games makes hits feel missed.
- Laggy replicated movement usually comes from client/server WalkSpeed desync or a non-standard character size.
- Server Authority fixes that desync and stops speed hackers, but adds input lag. Testers of one game found movement jagged, with players stuck on jumps or flung at high ping.
- Further reading: [Roblox performance docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/performance-optimization), [optimization video](https://youtu.be/lmLmAZ2UL5M), plus [video 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OCUZKJJR-TE), [video 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VDO_amtWfDw), [video 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YWyJHwynM1M).
