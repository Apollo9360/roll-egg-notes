# Live-ops teams and partners

Up: [[Updates and live ops (Game Design)]] · Previous: [[Updates and live ops - Release hygiene]]

- A large game's live team: one scripter for admin abuse, two for updates, one or two per event. Admin abuse takes up to 80% of team time.
- Hiring is the biggest risk. An unvetted new developer rushing an update killed a 20k-CCU game (owner-recommended lesson).
- Vet hires, talk to references, and give equity for full buy-in. Hire specialists through Twitter or Discord.
- Do not hand live ops to an inexperienced coder; one mistake halved D1 from 20% to 10% and CCU from 20k to 11k.
- Live ops separates a game that sells for thousands from one that sells for millions.
- Partner revenue shares range from 5–10% offers to 50% asks; 15–30% is typical. Judge studios by their live-ops record.
- Some partners do minimal work (new skins weekly, thumbnail swaps) for large shares (disputed).
- Delegating makes sense for games under 3–5k CCU you want to leave. One developer ran a 2k game's live ops for a month with a $20 AI plan.
