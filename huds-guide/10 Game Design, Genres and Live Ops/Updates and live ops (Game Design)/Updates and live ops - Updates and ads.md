# Updates and live ops - Updates and ads

Up: [[Updates and live ops (Game Design)]] · Previous: [[Updates and live ops - Cadence]] · Next: [[Updates and live ops - Release hygiene]]

1. Release the update, refresh thumbnails, and announce it in the group and on Discord.
2. Wait about 2 days, then decide on ads.

- The core growth loop: ads, check stats, update, check the algorithm, repeat until home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions. Do not turn off ads (owner-recommended).
- During ads, update every day or two on data, fix bugs immediately, and test new thumbnails daily.
- A minority wait for stats to settle between updates. Starting ads about 10 hours before an update gives clean stats (low confidence).
- A small ad burst alongside a big update is common.
- Test fixes carefully during ad runs; a buggy update sharply cut one game's playtime from 16.3 minutes.
- Rerunning ads with fresh thumbnails before a big update is fine; the learning phase restarts.
- Pausing ads for a big update can reset home-rec progress, and home recs may not recover immediately. Keep ads running.
- After a big update, data lags 1–2 days, and D7 improvements show about a week later.
