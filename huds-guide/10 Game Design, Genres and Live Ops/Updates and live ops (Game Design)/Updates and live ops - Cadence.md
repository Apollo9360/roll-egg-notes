# Updates and live ops - Cadence

Up: [[Updates and live ops (Game Design)]] · Next: [[Updates and live ops - Updates and ads]]

- Roblox games are not passive income. Update weekly with changes that genuinely improve the game.
- Live ops (live operations) means fixing and updating after release to lift benchmarks until home recommendations take over.
- Target one specific KPI (key performance indicator) per update, usually the weakest stat.
- The growth recipe behind one hit: read data daily, form hypotheses, ship iterative updates. In 1–2 weeks D1 rose from 8% to 14% and ARPDAU from 0.6 to 2.2 (owner-recommended).
- Ship the first update about 3 days to a week after launch. CCU may dip first, then climb.
- Most recommend weekly updates plus weekly events for D7. Biweekly updates bring players back; near-daily patches help while the algorithm pushes a game.
- Updating too fast can also hurt. Wait for new stats before judging an update (owner-recommended).
- Release the next update when players are about 90% through current content.
- Hold some content (a World 2 or final stage) for early updates rather than launching everything.
- Cadence depends on genre. Mature content-heavy games update every few months; steal and RNG games cannot skip updates.
- A sample weekly cycle: plan Saturday–Sunday, build Monday–Wednesday, polish and test Thursday, ship Friday.
- Update days bring 2–3x revenue; a tempting new developer product plus trade tokens reportedly multiplied it about 5x.
- Updates show visible jumps: +100 CCU per update on small games, +3–5k per gameplay update on a large +1 game.
- Shrinking update spikes (5k, then 4.8k) signal decay.
- Neglect kills games: 2k fell to 250, 8k to 2k, and a 5k game lost it all after 2 weeks without updates.
- Weak benchmarks are not always fatal; a sound idea can often be saved by live ops.
- Avoid big updates until you have a big playerbase. During ads, make funnel and quality fixes.
- Do not schedule updates against big games' events; a weekly update clashing with a major Halloween event hurt CCU.
