# Game Design, Genres & Live Ops

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Hits pair a strong, kid-legible core fantasy with one simple proven loop plus a twist, shipped early in a trend and polished for mobile. After launch, weekly updates and weekly admin abuse events hold Day-7 retention (D7). Admin abuse typically spikes concurrent users (CCU) 2–4x, while neglect or one bad update can cut CCU by 75% or more.
