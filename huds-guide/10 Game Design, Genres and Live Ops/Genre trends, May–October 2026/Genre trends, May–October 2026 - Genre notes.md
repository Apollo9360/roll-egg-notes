# Genre trends, May–October 2026 - Genre notes

Up: [[Genre trends, May–October 2026]]

- Brainrot's audience is largely Indonesian. Developers mostly buy model packs and reuse them.
- Whether the algorithm disfavors brainrot is disputed. One side says it only punishes bad stats; another cites an ~80% flop rate (disputed).
- Brainrot games tend to last 1–2 months and need far more CCU than incrementals for the same revenue.
- Brainrot ARPDAU (average revenue per daily active user) is reported near 0.1 Robux.
- Eggs and animals are now seen as brainrot reskins aimed at the same audience.
- Kids have mostly picked their main +1 games; breaking in needs a strong mutation. Whether +1 is saturated is disputed: some say any +1 hits 1k, others cap it at 500–1k (disputed).
- Steal-an-X went from hot in August to bloated by October, and reaching the 250 highly engaged player (HEP) threshold took longer.
- Steal an Egg took the gym-class tag loop and made it Roblox; it blends +1 speed, Steal a Brainrot and escape-tsunami.
- Puzzle games are rising but hard to monetize; escape rooms, quiz games and obby tycoons earn more than expected.
- Soccer, brainrot and anime are crowded niches.
- Endless co-op games rarely hold Day-28 retention (D28) without constant updates or strong replayability.
- Survival is wide open with a good team, story and world, and makes strong YouTube content. Horror shares the audience and has been a top-5 genre for years (owner-recommended).
- Roleplay (RP) and survival earn for a long time but are harder to crack; RP is all core fantasy. Teen-focused fighting RP filled one gap.
- PvPvE (player versus player versus environment) extraction may be next: a safe base, loot runs, NPC (non-player character) and player threats, low punishment. Make PvP add value for regular players too.
- FPS (first-person shooter) is competitive and hard to innovate but earns well; it needs unique mechanics. Instant-kill games chart above 10k because shooting rewards within a second.
- Tower defense pays very well but needs updates at least every 2 weeks. Players churn at session ends.
- Simulators and Steal an Egg-style tycoons earn short-term money. RPG and open-world games earn long-term but need long development.
- Gambling Robux-bought currency is a terms of service (TOS) risk; case-opening games reached 40k then died.
