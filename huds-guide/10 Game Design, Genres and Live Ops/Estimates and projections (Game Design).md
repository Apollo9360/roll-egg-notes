# Estimates and projections (Game Design)

Up: [[Game Design, Genres and Live Ops]] · Previous: [[Events and admin abuse]] · Next: [[Game examples referenced]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported data, not reported facts.

| Estimate | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Admin abuse lifts CCU to about 2.5–4x the daily average for its 20–60 minutes | Reported 1.5–2k to 6k, 1–2k to 4k, 15k to 40k, 10–20k to 30k | Medium |
| About 0.3–1% of event "interested" users show up concurrently | 9 of 1.3k (0.7%); ~200 of 59k (0.3%) | Low |
| An incremental earns about 5–10x more per CCU than a trend simulator | 40 incremental CCU likened to 200 sim CCU; 1k incremental likened to a 10k game | Medium |
| ARPDAU spans about 20x across genres: ~0.1 for brainrot to ~2.2 for a tuned money game | Reported brainrot ~0.1; one game rose from 0.6 to 2.2 | Medium |
| A weekly update cadence adds about 4–8 high-revenue days a month | Update days at 2–3x revenue; one or two update days a week | Low |
| Chance a new +1 game exceeds 1k CCU is about 1–2%; a "steal a" game under 0.5% | 60 of ~4,400; fewer than 15 of 4,000+ | Medium |
| Copying a 5k-CCU game likely yields about 500–5k | Interpolated between 1k copies yielding ~100 and 10k copies yielding 1–10k | Low |
| A 20–40 game portfolio at 50–300 CCU each totals about 1k–12k combined CCU | Reported per-game range times game count | Medium |
| Obby ads cost about $0.005 per peak CCU versus about $0.12 for a simulator | $30 for 6k CCU; $300 for 2.5k CCU | Low |
| One reported ad efficiency of ~$0.20 per HEP would put the 250-HEP threshold near $50 and a future 100-HEP threshold near $20 | One ASMR game hit 250 HEP from $50 of ads; single data point | Low |
| A 10k-CCU trend game is likely under 5k within 2–3 months | Peaks typically fall below 50% within months | Medium |
| Live-ops share midpoint about 22% (typical band 15–30%) | Reported offers from 5% to asks of 50% | Medium |
