# Game examples referenced

Up: [[Game Design, Genres and Live Ops]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Game Design)]]

| Game | Genre or pattern | Why it is cited |
|---|---|---|
| Steal an Egg | Steal-an-X tycoon | Gym-class tag loop made Roblox; theme packaging beat near-identical steal games; peak claims range widely |
| Grow a Garden | Grow and farming | Offline growth; inflation handled with social late-game loops |
| Sell Lemons | Incremental | 100k–200k+ CCU with about 112 buttons; three reset layers |
| Hack a Business | Plot-placement money | Oil Empire loop with a selling twist; daily data-driven updates lifted D1 and ARPDAU |
| Clean the Squishies | Cleaning and ASMR | 30k CCU as first of its kind; genre fell under 1k within a month |
| +1 Mine per Click | +1 incremental | 30k CCU; item drops plus selling are the core |
| Secure the Airport | Survival | Brought survival to an airport fantasy dominated by roleplay |
| Animal Hospital | Co-op mascot horror | About 500 to 500k CCU via TikTok, pacing and story |
| My Giant Sandwich | Simulator | Small-in-a-giant-kitchen fantasy; green objectives; likely female reach |
| Huss Valley | TikTok trend | 40k to 100k CCU; social rounds with spectating |
| Demonology | Horror | About 5k CCU by merging two horror playerbases |
| ASMR Soap Carving | ASMR | 250 HEP from $50 of ads; novel but lacked progression |
| Rivals | FPS | Evergreen; about $10k USD needed to compete |
