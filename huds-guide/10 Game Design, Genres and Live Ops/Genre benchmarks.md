# Genre benchmarks

Up: [[Game Design, Genres and Live Ops]] · Previous: [[Genre trends, May–October 2026]] · Next: [[Saturation, clones and reskins]]

| Genre | Typical CCU outcome | Key condition | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pure incremental | Usually under 1–2k; rarely past 5k; none seen past 10k | Revenue per CCU far higher; skews older; relies on home recs, weak on ads | High |
| +1 speed or escape | Copies 500–5k; hits 15k–300k | About 60 of ~4,400 +1 games exceeded 1k CCU | High |
| Steal-an-X | Originals into the hundreds of thousands; clones 100–500, rarely 60k | Fewer than 15 of 4,000+ "steal a" games above 1k | Medium |
| Brainrot | Top games ~20–37k; many 1–5k | Very low ARPDAU; 1–2 month lifespan | Medium |
| Clean-the-X and ASMR | 10–50k at peak | Can fall under 1k within a month; ~80% female | Medium |
| Tower defense | High earnings | Needs updates every 2 weeks | Medium |
| FPS and duels | Ceiling ~10–15k | About 15 FPS games above 1k | Medium |
| Survival and co-op horror | 20k+ if well designed; roguelites passed 1M | Needs team, story and world | Medium |
| Linear horror | 100–1k, rarely 5k | Story-driven ones go higher | Low |
| Hyper-realistic | ~500–1k | Kids prefer stylized | Low |
| Obby | Cheap to push to several thousand | Monetizes poorly | Medium |
| Custom-character games | Rarely above ~10k | Players want their avatar | Low |
| Idle ball games | Ceiling ~2k | Multiplayer raises it | Low |
| Limited-item guessing | Rarely above 1k | Needs a widely known item set | Low |

- Peak CCU typically falls below 50% within months; 99% of 10k-CCU games will not hold 10k for 3 months.
- Trend games can hit 60k and die within about 2 weeks. Anything under 5k made in the last year tends to die within a month.
- Recent long-lived evergreens (2+ years): 99 Nights, Rivals, Forsaken, Fisch, Sol's RNG. Games holding 30k+ for years have strong communities.
- Old games can hold 300–1.3k CCU for years without updates (Tower Battles, Grass Cutting Incremental).
- "Steady beats spiky": 4k CCU for 3 years beats 30k for 3 weeks. Optimize average CCU, not peak.
- About 70% of home-page games are tycoon or idle. Top 10 games (August): 3 survival, 3 simulator, 1 each RPG, roleplay, shooter, strategy.
