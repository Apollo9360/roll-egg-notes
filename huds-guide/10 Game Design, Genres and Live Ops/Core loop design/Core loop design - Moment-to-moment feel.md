# Core loop design - Moment-to-moment feel

Up: [[Core loop design]] · Previous: [[Core loop design - Structure]] · Next: [[Core loop design - Layout and format]]

- Juice multiplies a good loop; it does not replace one. SFX are underrated, and music matters for atmospheric games.
- Simulators at 10k+ CCU often have plain mechanics but win on effects and progression jumps timed like a slot machine.
- Decide what players should feel at key moments, then refine around session time and retention.
- A smooth spin or reveal interface can carry a game alone. Reveals need anticipation: preload models and use a scale tween.
- RNG egg reveals: raise luck with distance after the egg breaks, then play a satisfying roll and collection animation.
- Generic RNG gets repetitive after 5–7 minutes; make outcomes less predictable.
- Show pet and egg openings in-world, not in a GUI (graphical user interface).
- Make better items progressively cooler: size tiers (big, huge, titanic), erratic bounce or split effects. Color plus particles is not enough.
- Announce rare spawns with a notification and a temporary teleport button.
- Juice: SFX and popups on hits, SFX on claiming money, ASMR-style walking or painting sounds, finished pixels turning green.
- Breaking feels good with repeated impact sounds, then a break animation with SFX.
- ASMR games are very popular, and ASMR touches boost other genres.
- Collect coins by walking through them, not clicking buttons. Make this obvious; players often try clicking.
- Use the native Roblox backpack. Kids know it and top games use it.
- Cleaning games: fade the transparency of stain decals rather than swapping dirty and clean models.
- Highlight every objective (one hit used green). Show the player's best item on their plot.
