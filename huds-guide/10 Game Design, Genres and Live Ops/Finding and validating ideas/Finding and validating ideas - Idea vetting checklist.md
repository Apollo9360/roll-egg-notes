# Finding and validating ideas - Idea vetting checklist

Up: [[Finding and validating ideas]] · Previous: [[Finding and validating ideas - Market research]] · Next: [[Finding and validating ideas - Validation and shipping]]

- Is it monetizable, understood in seconds, and repeatable 50 times without boredom?
- Is it fast to build, easy to juice with SFX (sound effects) and VFX (visual effects), and clippable for TikTok?
- Is the target age clear, could D1, D7 and D30 retention be green, and would you play it all day?
