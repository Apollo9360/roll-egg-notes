# Finding and validating ideas - Market research

Up: [[Finding and validating ideas]] · Previous: [[Finding and validating ideas - Idea sources]] · Next: [[Finding and validating ideas - Idea vetting checklist]]

- Search Roblox and trend trackers (RoTrends, creator exchange) for keywords to gauge demand and find weak results. RoTrends revenue figures are estimates and can be about half the real number.
- Shortlist fast-growing games around 500 CCU, then check for YouTube and TikTok traction. One such shortlist of 10 all grew past 10k.
- Search your theme keyword and study every competitor, even at 0 CCU.
- Check a genre's CCU ceiling before attacking it; chess totals about 5k CCU. Small-cap genres without innovation do not grow.
- Thirty games at 1k CCU in a genre signals demand, not saturation. Execution decides who gets the players.
- If the closest rival only reaches about 1k, demand may be fading.
- Demonology reached about 5k CCU by merging two horror playerbases: one high-quality but pay-to-win, one free-to-play but rarely updated.
- Do not mix themes with different audiences (anime plus noob incremental; a Minecraft-mod theme plus +1 speed backflip).
