# Finding and validating ideas - Idea sources

Up: [[Finding and validating ideas]] · Previous: [[Finding and validating ideas - Fantasy first]] · Next: [[Finding and validating ideas - Market research]]

- Combine two proven ideas into one loop and add a twist; trend hopping plus a twist is the current meta.
- Find a fandom and ask ChatGPT for gaps in the market for it (owner-recommended).
- Look for underserved audiences and niches with many visits but little CCU; existing games are not serving them.
- Find untapped fantasies (such as firefighting) on [creatorexchange.io](https://creatorexchange.io) and combine them with trending games.
- Borrow proven fantasies from YouTube: a "dig for sea shells" video with 58M views is readable, relatable and has collection built in.
- Turning MrBeast-style videos into games is common knowledge now. One recreation took 12 hours and went from 50 to 600 CCU.
- Turn a satisfying real-life activity from TikTok into Roblox form and simplify it until a very young child understands it.
- Copy Steam hits fast, but adapt them to Roblox; being on Steam does not make a hit. Arcane: Tidy Up the Library became a "clean the library" hit.
- Port mobile idle and 2D web incrementals. Sell Lemons follows AdVenture Capitalist.
- Revive old mobile, flash or decade-old games kids never played; anything copied onto Roblox feels new.
- Look at flash games and other platforms' modes (hot potato, Fall Guys bomb mode). Play every rival and note round length and player count.
- Find older games in your genre with little competition that still average 1k+ CCU, then refine them with new systems.
- Find a flaw in a game you like and fix it. Mine similar games' communities and YouTubers' playthroughs for what players focus on.
- Do the opposite of what everyone else is doing (owner-recommended).
- Play Roblox actively, especially 10–100 CCU games, and ask why people play them. Play all the top games in a genre before entering it.
- Prefer popular themes (mining, plants, combat, army) over dull ones (clouds, business, food, ovens).
