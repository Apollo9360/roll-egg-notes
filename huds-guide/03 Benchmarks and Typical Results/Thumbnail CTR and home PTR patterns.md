# Thumbnail CTR and home PTR patterns

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Impressions, cost per play and click-to-play]] · Next: [[Engagement quota (HEP) cost]]

| Situation | Typical effect | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail iteration | 20–60 thumbnails per game to find winners | 25 tested with only 2 above 2.5%; ~20 to reach 3%, ~40 more until all were 6%+ | High |
| Learning phase | CTR usually climbs, sometimes settles down | 1.2–2.7% rising to 3–5%; 20%+ on ~1k impressions settling at ~9% | High |
| Same creative, new campaign | Large swings | 3.5% to 4.5% to 5.8% on reruns; 3.95% versus 1.25% | High |
| Setting the campaign genre | Large gain | CTR under 2% to 6%; tripled in another case | Medium |
| Narrow audience targeting | Higher CTR, often worse players | 3% to 7.5% on male shooter players, but in-game stats collapsed | Medium |
| Name change | Moderate to large gain | 1.5% to 5.9%; 1.7% to 3%; title tests on recs usually differ only 0.1–0.2% | Medium |
| New thumbnail set | Moderate to large gain | 1.5% to 4.4%; 2–2.7% to 4.3–4.9% | High |
| Copying another game's style | Unreliable | A current hit's style gave only 1.8% | Medium |
| AI thumbnails | Now competitive | 3–7% common; 4.8% on 240k impressions from a prompt workflow | High |
| In-game footage | Can work well | One developer always gets ~10% | Low |
| Winner over time | Decays with heavy use | A winner fell to ~1% after 30M impressions | Medium |
| Plays versus engagement goal | Plays goal usually shows higher CTR | 6% versus 1.8%; 6.2% versus 2.5% | Medium |

- High ad CTR does not predict retention or home PTR. Thumbnails at 9–14% fronted games with almost no players.
- Ad CTR versus home PTR on the same art varies widely. Reports: 2% to 3.8%; 2.5% to 12.7%; 4% to 0.75%; 6% to 1%; 13.6% to 1.8%.
- CTR is inflated before all-ages reach (up to ~15%) and during a trend.

### Home PTR and QPTR by scale

| CCU or impression level | Typical PTR / QPTR | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| First home recs (under ~10k impressions/day) | 3–8% | 7–7.5% on ~7.5k impressions; 8% on the first 1k | Medium |
| Under ~150 CCU | 1–3.5% | 0.4% stuck across many thumbnails | High |
| 500–5k CCU | ~1–3% | 1.06% at 600 CCU; 2% at 2k; ~5% on 1M impressions for a strong game | High |
| Very large scale | 1.5–3.5% | 1.61% on 127M impressions; ~3.5% at 50k CCU | Medium |
| Growing impressions | QPTR roughly halves or worse | 7% at 10M to 1.5% at 500M; 9% to 3% after 20M in a week; 3.5% to 0.7% in a month | High |

- PTR is the bottleneck in most stuck games. Games with every other stat green died at 0.4–0.5% PTR.
- Audience shifts move PTR: one game fell from the 90th to the 40th percentile while other stats rose from the 40th to the 80th.
- 3–5% QPTR did not save a game with weak retention.
