# Impressions, cost per play and click-to-play

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Ad spend and early CCU outcomes]] · Next: [[Thumbnail CTR and home PTR patterns]]

| Campaign goal or setup | Typical result | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plays goal, default targeting | 8–15k impressions per credit; $16 buys ~150k | 1k to 33k per credit; 89 credits bought 874k; 122 credits bought 1.16M | High |
| Plays goal, narrow targeting | ~6k impressions per credit | Targeted CPP of 0.005–0.008 is common | Medium |
| Engagement goal | 1/3 to 1/10 the impressions of plays | $30 bought 90k versus 300k on plays; 38 credits bought 23k | High |
| Plays per dollar | 100–200 in good runs | 11 credits gave 1,453; $47 gave 2.6k; bad runs under 10 per $ ($70 for 420) | High |
| Visits goal | Similar to plays, often worse | $20 at 2% CTR gave ~300 visits | Low |
| Click to play | 20–40% typical | 22.5k to 8k; 11k to 2k; 7k to 1k; 5.2k to 266; 1,500 to 5 | High |
| CPP over a campaign | Starts high, then settles | 0.061 to 0.009; 0.014 to 0.010 at 3% CTR | High |
| High-CPP warning signs | Restart the creative | 0.030 at 2.6% CTR; 0.040 at 6% CTR; 0.079 at 0.7% CTR; 0.128 at 1.45% CTR | High |

- Cost per 1,000 impressions (CPM) is usually about $0.07–0.25. A claim of ~$2 per 1,000 does not fit the reported totals.
- Cost per click stays fairly stable at about 0.004–0.009 credits. CPP mostly depends on how many clickers press Play.
- Roblox appears to charge more per impression for high-CTR creatives. One test: $20 bought 250k impressions at 1% CTR; $40 bought 175k at 2.5%.
- So CTR and CPP do not move together. A 0.9% ad beat a 5% ad on CPP, and a 1.7% ad beat a 4% one.
- Impressions per dollar vary up to ~800% between runs. A second run with the same money can get 1/10 the impressions.
- A larger budget can buy fewer impressions: $56 bought 119k where $22 had bought 345k.
- Low click-to-play signals a broken funnel, slow load or misleading thumbnail.
- A campaign that is clearly overpriced should be stopped and restarted. Relaunching the same thumbnails once moved CTR from 1% to 3–4.5%.
- Standard CPM is spend ÷ impressions × 1,000.
