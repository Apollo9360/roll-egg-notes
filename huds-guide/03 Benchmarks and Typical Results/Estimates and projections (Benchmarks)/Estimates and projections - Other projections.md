# Estimates and projections - Other projections

Up: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]] · Previous: [[CTR to cost per play]]

- Estimate: telling 2% from 3% CTR needs ~2k impressions per thumbnail. 3% versus 3.5% needs ~10k, and a 0.25-point gap ~40k (basis: 95% binomial confidence).
- Estimate: all-ages unlock moves a game at 30 CCU to about 60–180 within days (basis: typical 2–6x). A weak game may gain nothing.
- Estimate: sale offers run from about 2 weeks of revenue (studio lowballs) to 6 months (strong trend games). A game making 50k R$/day (~$175/day) would draw $2.5–30k (basis: reported 2-week and 6-month valuations).
