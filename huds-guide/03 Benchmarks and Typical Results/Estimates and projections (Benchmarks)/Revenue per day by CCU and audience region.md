# Revenue per day by CCU and audience region

Up: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]] · Next: [[Cost and time to reach the HEP quota]]

Estimate: daily Robux ≈ CCU × 70 × ARPDAU (basis: 70 DAU per CCU fits ~20 min daily playtime; reported range 50–100). Rows at 250 and 2.5k CCU are interpolated.

| Average CCU | SE or South Asia (ARPDAU ~0.05) | Brazil or Russia heavy (~0.35) | Mixed global (~1) | US, UK, Western (~3.5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | ~200 R$ | ~1.2k R$ | ~3.5k R$ | ~12k R$ |
| 100 | ~350 R$ | ~2.5k R$ | ~7k R$ | ~25k R$ |
| 250 (interpolated) | ~900 R$ | ~6k R$ | ~17k R$ | ~60k R$ |
| 500 | ~1.8k R$ | ~12k R$ | ~35k R$ | ~120k R$ |
| 1k | ~3.5k R$ | ~25k R$ | ~70k R$ | ~245k R$ |
| 2.5k (interpolated) | ~9k R$ | ~60k R$ | ~175k R$ | ~600k R$ |
| 5k | ~18k R$ | ~120k R$ | ~350k R$ | ~1.2M R$ |
| 10k | ~35k R$ | ~245k R$ | ~700k R$ | ~2.4M R$ |

- Estimate: multiply Robux by ~$0.0035 for DevEx cash (basis: 1M R$ ≈ $3.5–4k). A mixed-audience 1k-CCU game is then ~$245/day, or ~$7k/month.
- These line up with reports: 1k Indonesian CCU at 3–4k/day; 1k mixed CCU at 45–100k/day; 10k CCU at ~1M/day.
- Estimate: shorter playtime (10 min) roughly doubles DAU per CCU; longer playtime (40 min) halves it.
