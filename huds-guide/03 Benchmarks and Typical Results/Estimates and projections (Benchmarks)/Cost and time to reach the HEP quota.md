# Cost and time to reach the HEP quota

Up: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]] · Previous: [[Revenue per day by CCU and audience region]] · Next: [[Ad budget to first home recs]]

Basis: reported ~4–8 HEP per credit for strong games, 2–3 for average and 0.5–1 for weak, at ~255 Robux per credit. Days assume 16 credits/day.

| Game quality | Credits to 250 HEP | Days to 250 | Credits to 100 HEP (from November) | Days to 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strong | 30–60 (~8–15k R$) | 2–3 | 13–25 (~3–6k R$) | ~1 |
| Average | 85–125 (~22–32k R$) | 5–8 | 35–50 (~9–13k R$) | 2–3 |
| Weak | 250–500 (~65–130k R$) | 16–30+ | 100–200 (~25–50k R$) | 6–12 |

- Estimate: the 50k-Robux fee equals about 195 credits of ads (basis: ~255 R$ per credit). Ads are cheaper for strong and average games, and they also bring players and data.
- Estimate: the fee only saves money for weak games, which usually fade after all-ages anyway.
- Estimate: at a 100 threshold, the fee's value drops further, since an average game would need only ~$35–50 of ads.
- Estimate: visits needed are about 10–40k for 250 HEP and 4–15k for 100 (basis: ~1 HEP per 40–150 visits).
