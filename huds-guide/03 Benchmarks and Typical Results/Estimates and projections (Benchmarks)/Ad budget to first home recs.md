# Ad budget to first home recs

Up: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]] · Previous: [[Cost and time to reach the HEP quota]] · Next: [[CTR to cost per play]]

Basis: reported first-rec days for each spend level, interpolated between data points.

| Daily budget (credits) | Days to first recs, good stats | Days to first recs, average stats | Typical total spend |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 5–10 | Often never | 30–50 credits |
| 10 | 4–7 | 7–14 | 50–100 credits |
| 16 | 3–5 | 6–10 | 60–160 credits |
| 20–30 | 3–4 | 5–7 | 80–200 credits |
| 40 | 2–3 | 4–6 | 100–240 credits |

- Estimate: average CCU during ads is about 1–2 CCU per daily credit for a decent game (basis: 16/day giving 20–30 CCU; 10/day giving 10–15).
