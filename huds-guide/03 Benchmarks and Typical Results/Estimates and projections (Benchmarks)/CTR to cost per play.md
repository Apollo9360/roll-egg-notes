# CTR to cost per play

Up: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]] · Previous: [[Ad budget to first home recs]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections - Other projections]]

Estimate: CPP ≈ cost per impression ÷ (CTR × click-to-play). Basis: ~10k impressions per credit at typical CTR. Real gains above ~4% CTR are smaller, because high-CTR creatives get fewer impressions per credit.

| Ad CTR | Cost per click (credits) | CPP at 30% click-to-play | CPP at 60% click-to-play | Plays per 16 credits (30% to 60%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1% | 0.010 | 0.033 | 0.017 | ~480–960 |
| 2% | 0.005 | 0.017 | 0.008 | ~960–1,900 |
| 3% | 0.0033 | 0.011 | 0.0056 | ~1,400–2,900 |
| 4% | 0.0025 | 0.0083 | 0.0042 | ~1,900–3,800 |
| 5% | 0.0020 | 0.0067 | 0.0033 | ~2,400–4,800 |
| 8% | 0.0013 | 0.0042 | 0.0021 | ~3,800–7,700 |

- Check: a reported 2.33% CTR with 41% click-to-play gave CPP 0.010; the formula gives ~0.0105.
- Estimate: raising click-to-play from 30% to 60% halves CPP, the same as doubling CTR. Fix load time and the detail page first.
