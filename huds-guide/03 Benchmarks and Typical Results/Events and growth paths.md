# Events and growth paths

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Engagement quota (HEP) cost]] · Next: [[Spike-and-drop patterns]]

| Event | Typical effect | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| All-ages unlock | CCU usually multiplies 2–6x within days; outliers 10–30x | 90 to 250 instantly; 35 to 500 overnight; 150 to 520; 100 to 3k; no change or drops (130 to 50) | High |
| All-ages spike on a weak game | Fades within minutes | 90 to 115, then 6 within 7 minutes | Medium |
| Home-rec start | Impressions roughly double daily for several days | 5k, 7k, 11k, 20k, 30k, 35k, 50k by day; 37 to 2k plays over 7 days | High |
| Rec ramp over weeks | Can reach very large volumes | 21M impressions in 5 days took one game from ~200 to ~5k CCU | Medium |
| Algorithm test burst | Short spike, then fall back | 500 to 2k to 200 over days; 1.7k to 60 in 2 hours | High |
| Content update | Lifts and often holds CCU | 300 to 500; 100 to 800 overnight after one change; +3–5k per update on a large game | High |
| Admin abuse event | One-day spike, then baseline | 300–500 to 500–800; one event drew ~30k players | Medium |
| Weekly and daily cycle | Large swings by time zone and weekday | 100–200 weekdays versus 300–500 weekends; a daily drop at 00:00 UTC | High |

### Growth paths

| Path | Typical result | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ads 5–10 days, then recs and all-ages | Hundreds to low thousands of CCU | The owner's simulator-tycoon: first 100 CCU on day 9, 2k by week 4, 7.8k peak | High |
| Recs without ads | Slow start, then fast | 5 to 670 CCU in 10 days with no ads; 20 to 600 to 3k over two weeks | High |
| Gave up after poor ads | Recs can arrive later anyway | 600+ CCU from recs 3 weeks later; a month idle at 20 CCU, then 400–500 on reaching the quota | Medium |
| Novel trend game | Very large peaks | 500–1k to 175k in 3 weeks; 70k on a first game; 100 to 3k after unlock | Medium |
| Cheap ads on trend games | Occasional breakouts | 70k CCU on ~30 credits; 10k on 6 credits | Low |
| Cross-promotion | Pickup without ad spend | ~4k DAU in 2 weeks from a large partner game | Medium |
| Off-platform video | Can carry a game | 2 YouTube videos gave 300–500 CCU; 2M TikTok views raised D1 from 7% to 20% | Medium |

- A game-page video or trailer coincided with a 4x CCU jump in one case and an all-time high in another.
- Big off-platform audiences often convert poorly. A 300k-member group gets only ~3,000 impressions per announcement.
- A jump with no ad or impression change is often bots or algorithm tests.
