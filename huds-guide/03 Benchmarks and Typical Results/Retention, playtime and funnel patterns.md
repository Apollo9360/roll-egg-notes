# Retention, playtime and funnel patterns

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Spike-and-drop patterns]] · Next: [[Revenue by CCU and monetization]]

### Playtime

- Ad players play less: 6–8 min on ads versus 14–27 min on recs in several games. The owner treats 6 min from ads as acceptable.
- One early counterexample: 23 min on ads versus 14 on first recs.
- Playtime drifts down as audiences widen (24 to 16 min in 2 days; 40 to 16 over half a year).
- Fixes can raise playtime fast. One game went from 3.7 to 16 min in 2 weeks of daily funnel work. Another went from 19 seconds to 13 min after a tutorial remake and cutscene.
- Removing lobby music raised one game's playtime by 50%. Bug fixes alone add 1–2 min.
- High playtime does not guarantee retention or recs. Games at 35–40 min still had 0.4–8% D1 and no traction.
- A sudden playtime drop usually signals a bug.

### Retention

- D1 roughly doubles once a game reaches home recs (owner). Reports: 2% to 11%; 5% to 13%; 7–8% to 13%; 1% to 9%.
- Balancing fixes took one game from 4% to 18% D1. Acting on player feedback raised another from 3–4% to 8–9%.
- Raising D1 from 8% to 11% tripled one game's CCU.
- Even 18% D1 and 30+ min left one game at 100–200 CCU because PTR was the bottleneck.
- Daily rewards alone did not fix D1 of ~4% and D7 of 1.1%.

### Tutorial and first minutes

- A tutorial with 16 steps converted 33%. Cut to 12 steps, it converted 58%.
- Onboarding fixes moved conversion from 37% to 69% in one game and 45% to 75% in another (via visuals, not the tutorial).
- 50–60% of players can leave before a core action (first pickaxe, a mobile swing button).
- Completion under 15% is abnormal and signals a broken flow.
- High completion can hide a later wall: only 1/4 of players reached a match in one lobby game.
- A typical exit curve: 23% leave before 1 min, 50% by about 2.5 min, 90% before 10 min.

### Likes, favorites and mobile

- Rewards for a second session or group join pushed one like ratio to 98%. A join prompt with a perk gave 200–2,000 joins per 10k visits.
- Favorites can far exceed likes, especially for unique games.
- 50–80% of players are on mobile; +1 games skew almost entirely to mobile. Console can reach 12–30% on some games.
