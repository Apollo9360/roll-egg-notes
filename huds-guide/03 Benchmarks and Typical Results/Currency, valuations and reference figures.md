# Currency, valuations and reference figures

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]]

### Credits, Robux and cash

- 1 ad credit costs about 250–280 R$ or about $1. Examples: 16 credits for just under 5k R$; 70 credits for 18k R$; 50 credits for ~14k R$.
- Buying credits with Robux effectively costs DevEx value.
- DevEx pays about $0.0035–0.0038 per Robux: 1M R$ ≈ $3.5–4k. Some report a higher rate for 18+ earnings.
- 16 credits/day for a week costs about 28k R$.

### Game sales and investor deals

| Deal type | Typical terms | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad funding for equity | Small budgets for 4–40% | $500 of ads for 15%; 40 credits/day for 10 days for 15%; 40k R$ for 4% | High |
| Studio buyout offers | Often ~2 weeks of revenue | $12k for the owner's 7.8k-peak game; $3k for 2.5k CCU; $5.5k for 1.6k CCU | High |
| Partial sale of a live game | ~2–6 months of revenue | 30% of a 100-CCU game for $5k; 50% of a 200-CCU game for $5k | Medium |
| Strong trend game | ~6 months of revenue | A $250k valuation on ~500k R$/day | Medium |
| Pre-revenue games | Rarely valued highly | $20k for 20% was called wild by the owner | Medium |

- Selling cheaply can backfire. Games bought cheap later made $50k+ or millions of Robux.
- The owner advises buying only if you will keep running the game.

### Reference figures

- Revenue rank thresholds: top 1,000 earns ~~50–100k R$/day (~~$400/day); top 200 earns ~400–600k R$/day; top 100 earns ~1.25M R$/day.
- The median CCU of top-200 earners is ~12.6k, and 44.5% are below 10k CCU.
- Roughly 1,000–1,200 games have 1k+ CCU. The top 0.1% of games sit near 500 CCU.
- The median developer earns ~$1.5k/year. Roblox reported $1.7B in DevEx over the past 12 months and $5B+ since 2013.
- Trend leaders dwarf followers: late clones of a trend often max out near 12 CCU.
- Many hits followed long losses: one developer lost ~1.2M R$ over 28 games; another burned ~650k over 6 games before a $20k/month hit.
- The owner's first games flopped; the fourth did decent. Three later games in a row passed $30k/month.
