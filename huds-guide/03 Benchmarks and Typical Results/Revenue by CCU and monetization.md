# Revenue by CCU and monetization

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Retention, playtime and funnel patterns]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Benchmarks)]]

Revenue per CCU varies more than 100x between games. Audience region, genre and monetization depth matter more than CCU.

| CCU level | Typical Robux/day | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 30 CCU | 1–5k | Incrementals 2–5k at 10 CCU; donation-only games far less | Medium |
| 30–100 CCU | 3–15k | 20k at 50 CCU and 4 ARPDAU; 40k at 60 CCU and 10 ARPDAU | Medium |
| 100–300 CCU | 5–30k | 300 R$ for a tower game; 100–230k/day for strong niche games (disputed) | High |
| ~500 CCU | 15–50k | 1–1.5k with a young audience; 120k at 18 ARPDAU; 200k for an incremental | High |
| ~1k CCU | 30–100k | 2–5k with poor demographics; 300k–1M with US adults | High |
| 2–5k CCU | 50–400k | 1k with an Indian audience; 1–1.5M at 5.4k CCU with 450 ARPPU | High |
| ~10k CCU | 100k–1.5M | Under 30k for a weak game; ~1M typical for decent monetization | Medium |
| 25–50k CCU | 0.5–6M | 80–110k/hour at ~25k; 200–250k/hour at ~50k | Medium |

- Monthly USD is often quoted as 5–10x CCU. It holds roughly to ~20k CCU, then slows.
- The owner's large games reportedly made ~$118–120k/month at 30–40k CCU (about 3x CCU).
- 1k CCU is often quoted at $5–10k/month. 500 consistent CCU with average monetization is about $4–5k/month.
- Incrementals, anime fighters and pay-to-win niches earn most per CCU. Obbies, parkour and +1 games earn least: an obby with a 4k peak and 6M visits made only 160k in total.
- Tower and young-audience games often earn little. A 10k-CCU tower game "makes nothing usually."
- Creator rewards from Roblox Plus sometimes match or exceed product sales on small games.

### Audience region (reported)

| Audience | Typical ARPDAU | Example | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Southeast or South Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, India) | 0.01–0.1 | 1k CCU made 3–4k R$/day; 15k CCU made under $15k/month | High |
| Brazil, Russia, East Asia heavy | 0.2–0.5 | A 3k-CCU game sat at 0.5 ARPDAU; PCR fell from 1% to 0.16% as East Asia grew | High |
| Mixed global | 0.5–2 | 2 ARPDAU at 1k CCU is "very good money" | Medium |
| US, UK, Western Europe majority | 2–5+ | 4–5 in Western countries; US adults made ~1M R$/day at 1k CCU | Medium |

- Revenue can rise 5x for a few hours when US players come online.
- A shift from DE, UK and US to BR and ID players dropped revenue per visit from ~0.9 to ~0.2.

### Monetization changes and effects

| Change | Typical effect | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copying a roll-then-buy RNG mechanic | Large gain | Nearly tripled revenue in the owner's game | Medium |
| Pricing and product update | Large gain | 30 to ~2k R$/day in a week; 6k/week to 30k in under 24 hours | Medium |
| Whale sinks for high-PCR, low-ARPPU games | Raises ARPPU (owner-recommended) | 4% PCR with very low ARPPU was the trigger | Medium |
| Adding a 9 R$ pack | PCR up, ARPPU down | PCR ~3% while ARPPU went red | Medium |
| Turning on regional pricing | Can cut revenue | 7k/day to under 1k at 600 CCU | Low |
| Paid access (25–30 R$) | Very high PCR | 95% conversion and ~20 ARPDAU at 5 CCU; one reached 800–1k CCU | Low |
| Targeting good-demographic countries | Large PCR gain | 0.12% to 0.76% | Medium |
