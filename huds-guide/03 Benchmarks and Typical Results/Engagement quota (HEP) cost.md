# Engagement quota (HEP) cost

Up: [[Benchmarks and Typical Results]] · Previous: [[Thumbnail CTR and home PTR patterns]] · Next: [[Events and growth paths]]

Current rule (October 12): reaching 250 HEP unlocks all-ages reach, after the 1,000-Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus. A 50,000-Robux expedited review skips the quota. It is reported as refundable after roughly 90 days, with varying conditions. Approval usually follows within about 48 hours.

History: 500 HEP at launch in late May; 250 from about August 19. The expedited fee was 100k until about mid-September, then 50k. A drop to 100 HEP in November is announced but not yet in effect.

| Spend or setup | Typical HEP | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong game, 250 era | 250 in 2–3 days for under $50 | 250 in 12 hours; 250 in 2 days on 30 credits; plays at 15/day reached 250 in 3 days | High |
| Average game, 250 era | 250 in 5–8 days for 80–125 credits | 88 credits in a week; ~100 credits gave 240; "always about 80 credits" | High |
| Weak game, 250 era | ~1 credit per HEP or worse | $60 gave 105; 75 credits gave 70; 160 credits gave 105; $30 gave 15 | High |
| 500 era, typical | 500 in 3–7 days for ~70–200 credits | Owner estimate ~$100; 20k Robux each for two games; $200 in another case | High |
| Plays ads per day | ~20–30 HEP/day at 16/day | 3–5/day on weak games; ~100/day on strong ones | Medium |
| Engagement ads per day | ~30–60 HEP/day at 12–16/day | $30 gave 114 in 2 days; 12/day for 5 days reached 250 | Medium |
| Per visit | ~1 HEP per 40–150 visits | 10k visits gave 65; 13k gave 80; 2k gave 22; outliers of 1 per 7 to 1 per 400 | Medium |

- Engagement versus plays for HEP is disputed. Most weaker games fill faster on engagement (50/day versus 3/day in one test). Others saw plays win (25–30/day versus 7).
- A common compromise is a main plays campaign plus a small engagement campaign. One such pair added ~100 HEP/day.
- Long playtime alone does not guarantee pace. A game at 43 min average took over 10 days at 16/day.
- The dashboard lags about a day behind the true count.
- Home recs can start before the quota. One game had ~100 home recs at 150 HEP.
- The owner's game without the fee took 11 days to reach 500. A later game with the fee paid was approved in 48 hours.
- Reaching the quota does not guarantee growth. Several games reached 250 and stayed near 20–60 CCU.
