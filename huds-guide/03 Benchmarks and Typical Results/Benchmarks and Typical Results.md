# Benchmarks & Typical Results

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

A $16/day ad campaign usually holds only 10–40 concurrent users (CCU). The real levers are ad click-through rate (CTR) of 3%+, home play-through above Roblox's ~1.1% median, and 250 highly engaged players (HEP) for roughly 80–125 ad credits. Revenue then scales with CCU and region: about 70k Robux/day per 1k CCU for a mixed audience, only 3–4k for Southeast Asia.
