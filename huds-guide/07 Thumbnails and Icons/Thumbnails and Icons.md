# Thumbnails & Icons

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Make 15–25 clearly different thumbnails and test them in ads. Keep those at 3%+ click-through rate (CTR), ideally 4%+, after about 10k impressions each; the server owner prefers 50–100k for a final call. Promote winners to the 5 home slots only if they honestly show the core loop. Ad CTR usually, but not always, carries over to home play-through rate (PTR).
