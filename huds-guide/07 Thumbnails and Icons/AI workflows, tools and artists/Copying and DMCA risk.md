# Copying and DMCA risk

Up: [[AI workflows, tools and artists]] · Previous: [[AI vs GFX artists and pricing]]

- Many developers remix competitor thumbnails with AI. References from high-CCU games roughly guarantee 2%+ CTR.
- Unique thumbnails often beat straight copies; copying top games' style gave only 1.8% in one test.
- Near-identical copies can be taken down under the DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act). One game lost its thumbnail, and Roblox rejected 4 counter-notices.
- An exact AI reproduction can still be taken down. Copying only layout or style is generally safe.
- Owners are more likely to file over copied thumbnails and icons than UI icons.
- Anime characters and other franchises carry copyright risk. Copying another game's art directly is risky.
