# AI workflows, tools and artists - Named workflows

Up: [[AI workflows, tools and artists]] · Previous: [[AI workflows, tools and artists - Core method]] · Next: [[AI workflows, tools and artists - Tools]]

1. Owner-video method: give the owner's [thumbnail video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=az7em1IoBhA) transcript to Claude, have it write game-specific prompts, then generate in Gemini with in-game screenshots. Reported 4.8% CTR on 240k impressions.
2. Rough layout in Canva or Gemini, final render in RoThumbs (a widely watched tutorial's method).
3. Reskin: send a successful thumbnail plus your asset images and tell the AI exactly what to swap.
4. Studio scene: stage the scene with your own assets in about 5 minutes, then have AI render it cartoony.
5. Claude builds Studio mock-ups with in-game models, then writes Gemini prompts for about 10 variants.
6. Blender rigs plus a Studio background, GPT cel-shaded art, Krita outlines, Gemini bloom (about 2.5 hours).
7. Google Flow to generate, ChatGPT to refine, details added by hand.
8. Magnific ($40/month) with Nano Banana 2, driven by Claude through the Magnific MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. Reportedly averages 3–5% CTR.
9. Feed ChatGPT a 7% thumbnail and ask for 25 prompt variants; 20 reached over 6% in one report.
10. Claude analyzes the top 50 games in your genre, then writes prompts; Codex or ChatGPT generates 20–50 variations.

- The owner's [thumbnail walkthrough](https://www.loom.com/share/d189d4c1d91f400e878eecb6560eed27) covers his critique process.
- Reference sources: the top 50 games, front-page and Recommended games, successful Creator Exchange games, and current trends.
