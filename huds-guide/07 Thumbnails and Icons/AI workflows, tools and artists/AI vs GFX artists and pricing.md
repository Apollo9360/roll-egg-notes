# AI vs GFX artists and pricing

Up: [[AI workflows, tools and artists]] · Previous: [[AI workflows, tools and artists - Tools]] · Next: [[Copying and DMCA risk]]

| Result type | Reported range | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| AI thumbnails, typical | 3–8% CTR | High |
| AI thumbnails, best cases | 10–16% CTR on large samples | Medium |
| Commissioned thumbnails, typical | 0.8–2% CTR | High |
| Commissioned thumbnails, good artist | 3–7% CTR | Medium |
| Studio-only or hand-drawn by the developer | 0.2–5.4%; usually low | Low |

- Most experienced developers now find AI matches or beats hired artists at a tiny fraction of the cost.
- The owner hired 12+ GFX artists; his best is a side-gig solo developer, not a big-name studio.
- Kids mostly cannot tell, and many top thumbnails are AI. "Obvious AI" thumbnails have reached 8–16% CTR.
- Counterpoints: some test 50–70+ AI thumbnails and never pass about 1.2% QPTR; the concept or title was the limit.
- Older players spot AI more easily, and shiny AI thumbnails can attract dislike farming.
- At higher CCU, some move to professional GFX so the game looks higher quality.
- Alternative: buy about 5 from a good artist, then iterate on the best with AI.

| Source | Price | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| AI generation, self-made | About 1–5 cents per image, or $5–40/month | High |
| Budget marketplaces (RoDevs, Hidden Devs, Fiverr) | About $7–30 per thumbnail; often low CTR | Medium |
| Mid-range artists | About $45–150 per thumbnail, or 2k–15k Robux | Medium |
| High-end artists | $150–200+, or 20k–75k Robux per thumbnail | Medium |
| Icon plus thumbnail bundle | About $60–125 | Low |
| Full-time studio designer | About $1.5k–10k per month | Low |

- Some buyers seek refundable or CTR-guaranteed commissions; nobody reported a reliable source.
- Others being able to screenshot your thumbnail is no reason to avoid a good one.
