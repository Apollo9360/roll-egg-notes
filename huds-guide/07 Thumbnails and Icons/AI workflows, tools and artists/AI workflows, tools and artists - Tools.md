# AI workflows, tools and artists - Tools

Up: [[AI workflows, tools and artists]] · Previous: [[AI workflows, tools and artists - Named workflows]] · Next: [[AI vs GFX artists and pricing]]

| Tool | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT / GPT Image 2 | Most used; strong for thumbnails and icons | High |
| Gemini | Free plan covers about 50 thumbnails; 2–7% CTR reported | High |
| Nano Banana 2 | Underlies most wrapper tools; mixed thumbnail quality | Medium |
| [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) | Free, 4 images at a time | Medium |
| Codex | $20 plan has a high image limit; good for bulk variants | Medium |
| RoThumbs | Trained on Roblox thumbnails; about $15–16/month, about 16 cents per thumbnail; the owner uses it | Medium |
| Magnific | About $40/month unlimited; used with Nano Banana 2 | Low |
| Vizzbees, ForgeGUI | Tokens buyable with Robux; the owner called ForgeGUI awful and output can look cheap | Low |
| Renderbux, Thumby | Minor alternatives; RoThumbs preferred over Thumby | Low |
| Canva, Figma, Photopea, Krita | Layouts, text, faces, outlines, badges | High |

- Opinion on wrappers shifted. In July 2026 RoThumbs was widely recommended; by September many called wrappers middlemen reselling Nano Banana 2.
- Roblox is reportedly adding AI thumbnail generation to Ads Manager (low confidence).
- Services promising guaranteed 16–19% CTR are dubious promotions; ignore them.
