# Designing for kids and age gates

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[Composition, contrast and text]] · Next: [[Honesty vs clickbait]]

- Kids click bright, catchy colors, big or rainbow text and a cool fantasy item. Bland thumbnails flop.
- For a very young audience, a bright, clickbait-style AI thumbnail easily gets high CTR. A/B test it against the others.
- Use a bacon-hair avatar so players relate. Avoid mangled or overly AI-looking bacon hairs.
- The common grin face is called "pal face" and is easy to find online.
- Use only the bacon hair rather than mixing in other hair styles (owner-recommended).
- Small avatar tweaks matter; adding glasses to the bacon hair moved CTR by about a point in one test.
- For mixed-gender audiences, run a boy set and a girl set in parallel, such as a girl avatar with a pink, cute item.
- A princess-tower ad aimed at girls reportedly reached about 15% CTR (low confidence).
- Avoid shirtless characters. A shirtless avatar lifting a building was removed by Roblox as sexual content.
- IP-trend thumbnails attract a very young audience who may bounce and wreck stats.
- Pixel art is rare and not well liked on Roblox, though kids may appreciate a unique style (disputed).
- A widely known anime character can lift CTR but carries copyright risk.

### Age gates and CTR

- Games below the HEP threshold are 16+ only. The threshold is 250 HEP (500 at launch in late May, 250 since about Aug 19).
- A drop to 100 HEP in November is announced but not yet in effect.
- The expedited-review fee that skips the threshold is 50,000 Robux (100,000 until about mid-September). It is refundable after about 90 days, with varying conditions.
- After reaching 250, the normal route still needs the 1,000 Robux reach fee or Roblox Plus.
- 16+-only ads tend to show lower CTR than all-ages ads, and some see CTR jump after unlock (low confidence).
- High CTR with few plays on a kid-styled 16+ game suggests under-16 users click but cannot join.
- Targeting ads at 18+ raised CTR from 2% to 6–7% for one 16+ game (disputed). The owner reportedly advised against bothering, and ineligible users may not see ads at all.
- Some suggest 16+-friendly art until you clear the threshold, then shifting toward kids. Others test eye-catching, cash-grab variants anyway and let the algorithm choose.
- Older audiences spot AI more easily. For 13+ or 16+ games, avoid an obvious AI look.
