# Managing home and page thumbnails

Up: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup]] · Next: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup - Game page video]]

- Home Page thumbnails and Experience Detail Page thumbnails are separate. Upload to both; the same image is fine.
- Make sure your best ad thumbnail is actually enabled in the game's thumbnail list.
- Put your highest-CTR thumbnail first on the experience page.
- How many to run on home is disputed. Many fill all 5; others run 1–4 to keep metrics readable. The owner suggested starting with 1 under about 1k daily active users (DAU).
- Test new thumbnails in ads first. Adding 4 new 1.5% QPTR thumbnails beside a 5% one tanked CCU in one case.
- Swap out the lowest-QPTR home thumbnail. Replace fast if QPTR starts bad; watch 1–2 days if it starts good then declines.
- Roblox may push most home impressions to one thumbnail, sometimes the weaker one (low confidence).
- Keep thumbnail personalization on even under 1k DAU, despite Roblox's warning (low confidence).
- A strong new thumbnail often produces a visible CCU spike, though small games also fluctuate on their own.
- A poor like ratio lowers clicks and clicks-to-plays; lifetime likes never reset, and dislike-bombing cut one game's CTR from 3% to 1%.
- Click-to-play conversion on the detail page is reported at about 60–85%.
- [qptr.io](https://qptr.io) previews your thumbnail in a home-page grid but gives no data. Pls Donate stands out most there.
- [Big Bang Vision](https://big-bang-vision.vercel.app/) is another free preview tool (desktop only).
- A cheap check: mock up the home row in qptr.io, then ask ChatGPT which thumbnail stands out most to kids.
- [rebloxed.com](https://rebloxed.com) rates thumbnails and generates icons; its accuracy is unknown (low confidence).
