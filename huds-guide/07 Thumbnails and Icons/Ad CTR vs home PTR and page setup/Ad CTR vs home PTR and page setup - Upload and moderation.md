# Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup - Upload and moderation

Up: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup]] · Previous: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup - Game page video]]

- Thumbnails normally appear within 5 minutes, or about 30 minutes when awaiting approval.
- To skip the delay, upload the image to an ImageLabel in Studio about 15 minutes earlier, then swap it in (low confidence).
- If missing after 40 hours, re-upload one at a time.
- Upload errors (jpeg/png, 300kb–5mb) in early July 2026 were a Roblox bug.
- ChatGPT exports sometimes fail to upload; re-capturing the image fixed one case.
- Icons can look blurry at 512x512; check them at real size.
