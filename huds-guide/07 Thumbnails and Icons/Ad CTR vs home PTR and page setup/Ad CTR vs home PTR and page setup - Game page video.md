# Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup - Game page video

Up: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup]] · Previous: [[Managing home and page thumbnails]] · Next: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup - Upload and moderation]]

- Roblox now embeds videos directly on the game page; YouTube links are being removed from detail pages.
- Go straight to gameplay. Hook in the first 2–3 seconds with the most exciting moment, then show the basics.
- Change scene every 2–4 seconds and alternate fast and slow parts.
- Show cause and effect (buy animal, it produces babies), not isolated steps.
- A recorded onboarding beats a confusing montage.
- Whether video lifts PTR is disputed. Some saw better PTR and bounce. Others note it does not appear on home recommendations.
- One game's CCU rose from about 850 to 1.2k within 15 minutes of removing its trailer (low confidence).
