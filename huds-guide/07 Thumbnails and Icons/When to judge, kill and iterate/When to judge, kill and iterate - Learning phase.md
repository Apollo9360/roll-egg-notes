# When to judge, kill and iterate - Learning phase

Up: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]] · Previous: [[Impression and spend thresholds]] · Next: [[When to judge, kill and iterate - Kill or keep]]

- The first 24 hours of ads are a learning phase while Roblox finds the audience. Wait out learning, plus about 12 hours.
- CTR direction after learning is disputed. Many saw CTR rise over 3–7 days, for example 2–3% to 7%. Many others saw it fall.
- Learning usually moves CTR by about ±1%. A drop from 3% to 2.3% is normal.
- A good thumbnail usually shows about 3% even during learning.
- Low CTR rarely fixes itself. A 1% reading at 1k impressions will not become 5%.
- Restarting a campaign often lifts CTR and impressions per dollar; second campaigns usually beat the first, sometimes by about 50%.
- A thumbnail at 4–5% can decay after about 2 weeks; replace it before it fades.
