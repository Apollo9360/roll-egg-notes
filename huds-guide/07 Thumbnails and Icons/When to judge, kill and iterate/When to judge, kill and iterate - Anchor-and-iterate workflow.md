# When to judge, kill and iterate - Anchor-and-iterate workflow

Up: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]] · Previous: [[When to judge, kill and iterate - Kill or keep]]

1. Generate a batch of 15–25+ distinct concepts.
2. Run them all and pause clear losers after their threshold.
3. Compare winners and losers to learn what your audience likes.
4. Once a thumbnail holds high CTR after about 5k impressions, make about 10 variations of it.
5. Vary details: monster, outlines, pose, color, avatar accessories. Details decide results.
6. Also try a few different layouts, plus no-text and enlarged-subject versions of top performers.
7. Swap out the worst performer every cycle and keep testing. The owner tests almost daily.

- Iterating raised CTR from 2.8% to 6% over 14 days for one developer; another went from 3% to 16% over 12 AI remixes.
- Variants of the best thumbnail often work, but iterations sometimes fail to beat the original.
- The "sloppiest" quick thumbnail is often the top performer; polish does not predict CTR.
