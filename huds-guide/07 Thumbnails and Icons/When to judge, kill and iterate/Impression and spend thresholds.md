# Impression and spend thresholds

Up: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]] · Next: [[When to judge, kill and iterate - Learning phase]]

| Threshold | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1k impressions or under 2 credits | Meaningless, even at 7–20% CTR | High |
| About 1k impressions | Earliest quick cut for anything far under 3% | Medium |
| About 5–10k impressions per thumbnail | Most common judging point; PTR is fairly firm by 5k | High |
| About 20–25k impressions | Reliable; some thumbnails make big comebacks before this | Medium |
| About 50–100k impressions | Owner's rule: 100k usually tells you; 50k if you accept more risk | High |
| At least 24 hours per thumbnail | Owner-recommended minimum; many wait 2–3 days | High |
| About $1–3 or 2–3 credits per creative | Common minimum before cutting; switch early if near 1% by $3 | Medium |

- Small-sample spikes usually fall back; a 17% reading at 2,000 impressions dropped to 2%.
- A 20% early CTR tends to average out far lower.
- The same thumbnail can swing between campaigns, for example 1% in one and 8% in the next.
- A 7% test-ad CTR can become 2% in a larger real campaign.
- Ad CTR on small samples is unreliable overall.
