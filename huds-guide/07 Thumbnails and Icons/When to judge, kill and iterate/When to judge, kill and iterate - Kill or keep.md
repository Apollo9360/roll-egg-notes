# When to judge, kill and iterate - Kill or keep

Up: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]] · Previous: [[When to judge, kill and iterate - Learning phase]] · Next: [[When to judge, kill and iterate - Anchor-and-iterate workflow]]

- Whether Roblox auto-allocates impressions within a campaign is disputed. Many see it favor some creatives and starve others. Documentation cited by one developer says sponsored creatives are distributed evenly.
- Either way, disable weak creatives manually rather than waiting for the system.
- Allocation is not CTR alone. Ads optimize for plays, so a 2% creative may get more impressions than a 10% one.
- Kill rules in use:
    - Under 3% after 1k impressions or after day 1: stop it.
    - Under about 2% after the third day of ads: keep testing new ones (owner-recommended).
    - Under 3% after about $1 of spend, or after 3–5 days: remove it.
    - Several creatives sitting at 1% just waste money.
- Keep 3–4% creatives and drop 2% ones. Winners change between campaigns, so keep all decent ones.
- Exception: a low-CTR creative that brings a high-quality audience may be worth keeping.
- If CTR is bad, do not stop ads. Add new thumbnails, switch off weak ones, and check the title.
- Replacing poor thumbnails does not hurt good ones.
