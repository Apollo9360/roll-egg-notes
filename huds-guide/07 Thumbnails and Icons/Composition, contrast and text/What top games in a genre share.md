# What top games in a genre share

Up: [[Composition, contrast and text]] · Previous: [[Composition, contrast and text - Composition]] · Next: [[Text vs no text]]

An analysis of the top 50 thumbnails in the egg-stealing genre showed clear patterns:

| Pattern | Share of top 50 |
|---|---|
| Bright scene: blue sky, green baseplate or desert sand | 45 of 50; none used dark space |
| Only 2–3 huge subjects on a plain background | 44 of 50 |
| Default bacon-hair avatar with a smug grin | 33 of 50 |
| Threat on the left, player with the prize on the right | 32 of 50 |
| Player carrying a prize as big as themselves, often overhead | 25 of 50 |
| A slogan or sentence | Only 3 of 50 |

- The leader showed only the item name in rainbow letters, income per second in green, and a red curved arrow at the item.
- The developer's own dark-galaxy thumbnails with 6–10 subjects lost; the blue-sky variant performed best.
- A similar critique for a heist game: strip the background to green ground and blue sky (owner-recommended).
- Then show only a big gray bank, a blue cop and a bacon hair carrying the loot.
