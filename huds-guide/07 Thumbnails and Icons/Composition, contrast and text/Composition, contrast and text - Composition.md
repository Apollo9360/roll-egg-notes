# Composition, contrast and text - Composition

Up: [[Composition, contrast and text]] · Next: [[What top games in a genre share]]

- Use one big, clear focal subject on a simple background, with high contrast between them.
- A proven recipe: one focal point, one trending or meme element, and colors that stand out from neighboring thumbnails.
- Remove clutter: trees, clouds, sun beams, busy skies, extra characters. Busy thumbnails are a top critique (owner-recommended).
- Think mobile: a subject that blends into a similar-colored background disappears on small screens.
- Make the character bigger than surrounding objects and move the main subject closer.
- Characters that are too small make a recognizable IP (intellectual property) unrecognizable.
- Highlight the key actor, for example the thief in red. Avoid too many colors.
- Use the rule of thirds and a low camera angle for power shots.
- Add a thin white stroke inside a black stroke so subjects pop. Remove color tint from the character.
- White backgrounds are uncommon and stand out. Add black outlines to subjects on white for mobile visibility.
- Bright, simple scenes win. Dark thumbnails and dark-space backgrounds underperform, and dark subjects read as a black blob.
- An overly bright background stops the subject popping. Prefer high contrast over all-bright colors.
- Show emotion. Expressive characters help CTR and qualified play-through rate (QPTR).
- Head close-ups, a consistent cartoon style and harmonized colors help.
- Pure eye candy with no readable subject scored about 1.1% CTR.
- Avoid characters moving the wrong direction, mouse cursors, and big logos.
- Do not just screenshot your Studio window. Stage and polish the scene.
- Upscale if low quality shows at thumbnail size. Check it on your own home page.
- Check on console, mobile and PC, in dark and light mode.
- Plain or unexpected designs sometimes outperform. Simpler designs unlike typical Roblox thumbnails reached about 10% CTR.
- Upload ad creatives and thumbnails at 1920x1080, 16:9. Other ratios get stretched.
