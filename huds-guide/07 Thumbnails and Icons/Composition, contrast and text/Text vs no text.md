# Text vs no text

Up: [[Composition, contrast and text]] · Previous: [[What top games in a genre share]] · Next: [[Composition, contrast and text - Proven formats]]

- Text is common but optional. Many high-CTR thumbnails have no text, so test no-text versions of winners.
- Young kids often cannot read well, so story-telling images beat sentences.
- If you use text, keep it to a few words, outlined or cartoon style, large, with strong color contrast.
- Short labels work: "1 ft" vs "676 ft," or "1 damage" vs "999 damage," sell a before/after.
- A "YOU" label over the player character helps.
- Headline text should match the game title, but do not put the title itself or your logo on the thumbnail.
- Thin fonts are unreadable. Common fonts:
    - Fredoka One, used in almost every game.
    - Montserrat Extra Bold.
    - BuilderSans Bold.
    - Comic Sans.
- Adding a trending meme phrase as text raised CTR from 2.4% to 4.2% in one test (low confidence).
- Putting the Robux logo on a thumbnail reportedly got a game banned in the past (low confidence).
