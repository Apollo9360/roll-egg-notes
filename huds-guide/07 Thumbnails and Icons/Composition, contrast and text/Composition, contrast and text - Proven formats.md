# Composition, contrast and text - Proven formats

Up: [[Composition, contrast and text]] · Previous: [[Text vs no text]]

- Before/after often performs best.
    - Exaggerate the contrast so viewers feel something; do not just use screenshots.
    - Both sides must look clearly different, with the same character.
- First-person and point-of-view (POV) shots.
- "Vs." layouts and evolution sequences, such as noob to strong for anime games.
- "Day 1 vs Day 100" or "Day 1 vs Day 7," with a simple font.
- Memes and joke thumbnails: a "who is this" meme reached about 4%; a first joke thumbnail had 5x the CTR of earlier ones.
- Trending topics: in one test, only thumbnails using current hot topics reached top CTR.
- Copying the composition of a highly viewed YouTube thumbnail worked well.
- In-game footage styled well reached about 10% CTR in one report (low confidence).
