# Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]] · Next: [[Icons]]

- The owner reports that a winning ad thumbnail usually also does well on home recommendations.
- Exceptions are common enough to check separately:
    - 4% ad CTR became 0.49% QPTR as the real thumbnail.
    - 5% ad CTR sat beside 1.6% QPTR.
    - A 1.5% ad thumbnail beat a 4% one on home recommendations.
- Judge home-page thumbnail stats while ads are not running. You cannot see thumbnail CTR without ads.
- Reported QPTR levels: about 3–5% is strong; about 1% is weak. Some aim for about 5% PTR after 100k impressions.
- To improve PTR, work on thumbnails and title first (owner-recommended).
- If PTR and first-user engagement are already good, adding more thumbnails can still squeeze out gains (owner-recommended).
- Raising QPTR from 1.2% to 2.5% was projected to triple revenue for a small game (low confidence).
