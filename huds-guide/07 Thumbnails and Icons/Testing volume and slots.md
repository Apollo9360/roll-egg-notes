# Testing volume and slots

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[Honesty vs clickbait]] · Next: [[When to judge, kill and iterate]]

- Ads are the thumbnail testing lab. The owner runs campaigns mainly to test thumbnails, at times $100 per day.
- By the time home recommendations unlock, you already have proven high-CTR thumbnails.
- Ad campaigns hold up to 25 thumbnails. The game page and home recommendations hold only 5.
- Personalization data updates slowly, so a small ad campaign is the fastest test.

| Recommendation | Confidence |
|---|---|
| Prepare 20–25 thumbnails per campaign (owner-recommended) | High |
| At least 10; 15–25 gives more chances of a winner | High |
| Have about 3 ready at launch and 6–10 across the first campaign | Medium |
| After testing, keep the best 3–10 and rerun them in a fresh campaign | Medium |
| Add about 2 new thumbnails a week, even without ads | Medium |
| Make up to 5 new thumbnails a day until one passes 5% | Low |
| Run only 2–3 creatives at about 16 credits a day to concentrate data | Low (disputed) |

- Experienced developers expect about 2–5 of 25 thumbnails to pass 5% CTR.
- About 15 AI thumbnails usually yield one at 7–9% in reports.
- One developer put 1.9k ad credits on only 5–6 thumbnails and failed; a bigger roster fixed it.
- A minority say fewer thumbnails per campaign gives better CPP (disputed).
- Thumbnail testing is plain A/B testing with no secret method.
- Start with truly different concepts. Three useful hypotheses are gameplay fantasy, character emotion, and reward or aspiration.
- Most say near-identical copies waste slots. A minority use color or character variants to fill slots and learn which detail wins.
- The same creative in two simultaneous campaigns can cannibalize itself. Pause the weaker campaign and push the winner.
- At 25/25 slots, remove or pause creatives before adding new ones.
- Cheap tests:
    - At about 800 CCU, swap the worst creative in a live campaign.
    - Or run the minimum spend for 2 days and pause each creative at about 10k impressions.
- Very low daily spend can inflate CTR. About 16 credits a day gives a more realistic test.
- Home-recommendation tests are limited by impressions. With about 10k daily impressions, 25 thumbnails take too long to read (owner-recommended).
- One untested idea: make thumbnails first and only build games whose thumbnail reaches 3–5% CTR.
