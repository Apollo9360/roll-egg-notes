# Icons

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[Ad CTR vs home PTR and page setup]] · Next: [[Game names and titles]]

- Most experienced developers say icons matter much less than thumbnails. Home recommendations and ads both use thumbnails.
- The icon is the square tile in Continue Playing and "recently played"; it matters mainly once you are on the charts.
- A minority argue the icon drives the click or matters more for retention.
- Roblox reports no separate icon CTR and has no native icon A/B test.
- Use your best thumbnail as the icon, or a less cluttered version if it is busy (owner-recommended).
- Preview at Roblox page size. Use bigger text and a bigger subject; fill the icon with the character.
- Make characters the main focus; icons without one rarely do well.
- For number icons, use short text such as "73M."
- Keep logos readable; stylized text can be misread as other characters or words.
- Icon variants can be polled with your community; the owner picked a new icon this way.
- Updates and "earns offline" badges on icons are common in top games.
- Rotate icons with updates so players notice, but keep a proven, iconic icon. Some only change it above 200k monthly active users (MAU) (low confidence).
- Before events, some automate countdown prefixes on the icon and game name, from 12 hours down to "NOW."
- Roblox also overlays an upcoming event on your icon a few hours before it starts.
- Hourglass or "update soon" badges are hand-edited. Use Photopea or the [flayodev icon maker](https://flayodev.com/icon-maker/).
- Text on icons, and clickbait icons vs branded logos, remain unresolved debates.

### AI icons

- Ask for 2D drawings. ChatGPT is good for vector and simulator-style icons.
- Feed the AI icons from successful similar games and ask it to replicate them with your changes.
- Or make a rough icon, then have AI polish it alongside a popular game's icon.
- For RNG dice icons, give a reference GFX pack style plus a basic cube.
- Codex image generation and Claude plus Gemini have also made good icons.

### Item and UI icons

- The Photo Booth plugin ($5) makes clean item icons. [PixelForge 3D Icon Studio](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/103129928198990/PixelForge-3D-Icon-Studio) is another option.
- Free route:
    1. Screenshot the item in good light.
    2. Remove the background with remove.bg.
    3. Add stroke and color correction in Photopea.
- Give Gemini about 10 style examples plus the item and a color for consistent item icons.
- Screenshot an icon from another game and ask ChatGPT or Claude for the icon alone with no background.
- Codex is reported to beat Claude at UI icons.
- Robux, Premium and Verified glyphs are available as text: utf8.char(0xE002), 0xE001 and 0xE000.
- Free pack: [gvesster free icon pack](https://gvesster.itch.io/free-icon-pack).
