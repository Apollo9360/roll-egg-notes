# Game names and titles

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[Icons]] · Next: [[AI workflows, tools and artists]]

- The title is underrated and drives roughly half of ad CTR (owner-recommended). Packaging means title plus thumbnails plus demand.
- If the thumbnail seems good but CTR is bad, it is probably the title. Retitle and add a new thumbnail together to repackage.
- Fix the name before over-optimizing the art.
- Renames swing CTR and CCU sharply:
    - Deleting one word ("Fusion Core Incremental" to "Core Incremental") took CTR from 1.4% to 3.6%.
    - "Goofy Ponies" to "Grow a Horse" doubled CTR.
    - "Draw a Brainrot" to "Draw your Brainrot" took a game from about 400 to 10k CCU.
    - A rename alone took another game from 2% to 4.5% CTR.
- Renames can backfire: "Pets Adventure" to "Pets Fight!" lost about 110 average CCU.
- A rename can raise CTR yet lower plays, so watch both.
- A thumbnail that wins under one title may lose under another; retest after renaming.
- Name the game after what the player does. Use verb-based, kid-readable names: "Make a Cloud," not "(NEW!) Cloud Factory."
- "Your" usually performs better in titles (owner-recommended).
- Avoid weak verbs: "Fight the Slimes" is weaker than "Hatch a Slime Army." "Own a ___" is weak; "Kill the" beats "Defeat the."
- Research keywords on [Creator Exchange](https://creatorexchange.io) by searching a verb and studying the games that use it (owner-recommended).
- Use recognizable nouns and proven interests: "Bean Bag" over "Hacky Sack," "Hacking" over "Matrix."
- Avoid developer jargon such as "Incremental," which young kids do not understand.
- Put "RNG" in the title for RNG games. Adding "+1 Power" to an anime game's name improved PTR.
- For PvP (player versus player) games, use an identity name like "Super Strikers." Skip "soccer" if the ball is clear in the thumbnail.
- Generic titles hurt PTR (owner-recommended example: "Build a Base and Steal").
- Clearer titles trade away curiosity; the owner dropped an overdone word like "pets" and let the thumbnail show it.
- Money symbols such as "$$" in titles get the most clicks, though the owner dislikes needing them.
- When "Grow a ___" is saturated, try "Raise a ___." Drop "(NEW!)" tags.
- Concrete beats abstract: "Escape Gravity For Eggs" beat "+1 Gravity."
- Defending concepts tend to lower CTR because defending is less appealing than stealing.
