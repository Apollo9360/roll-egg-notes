# Honesty vs clickbait

Up: [[Thumbnails and Icons]] · Previous: [[Designing for kids and age gates]] · Next: [[Testing volume and slots]]

- PTR matters more than CTR. High CTR is worthless if most clickers leave immediately.
- Clickbait is the reason QPTR was introduced.
- High CTR with few plays or high CPP signals an expectation gap; players feel scammed.
- Flashy thumbnails can raise CTR yet tank playtime and CPP. Switching to core-loop thumbnails gave 0.009 CPP at 3–4% CTR in one case.
- Some deliberately avoid 10%+ thumbnails because 5%+ CTR often comes with a low play rate (low confidence).
- A 2–3% CTR can be healthy. If first-session metrics are good, clickers are genuinely interested and nothing needs fixing.
- A 2.5% thumbnail bringing longer-playing players can beat a 5% one.
- Typical failure patterns:
    - A top-CTR thumbnail with very low PTR; replacing it made PTR climb.
    - A misleading 4% thumbnail hurt playtime and was removed.
    - A detail-page thumbnail that did not match the ad's promise raised CPP; matching it lowered CPP.
    - A famous character shown on a thumbnail but barely present in the game gave a terrible bounce rate.
    - A 3D-looking thumbnail for a 2D game risks high bounce; lean into the 2D style.
- Deliver the promise in the first minute. A mismatch makes players leave the lobby in about 30 seconds.
- Make the feature shown on your top thumbnail visible and reachable from the first join.
- Keep the game-page thumbnail very similar to the ad thumbnail players clicked.
- A title that does not match the thumbnail or game is an underrated killer of PTR and bounce rate.
- Pose real game objects so the image does not read as clickbait.
- Adding a featured character as a rare in-game find, so it can honestly appear on thumbnails, is accepted practice.
- Fake "UPDATE" overlays when nothing changed ruin first impressions and retention.
- For bad home-recommendation QPTR, thumbnails may not be distinctive enough. Try a weird, viral-video-style one within the terms of service (TOS).
- Commissioning new thumbnails is only worth it if QPTR needs fixing.
