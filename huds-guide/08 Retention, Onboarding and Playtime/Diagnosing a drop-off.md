# Diagnosing a drop-off

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[Multiplayer, PvP and empty servers]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Retention)]]

1. Locate the drop: first step, mid-tutorial, right after the tutorial, minute 5–6, or between sessions.
2. Split by device; check phone first, then console.
3. Watch a fresh first-time player silently, then read funnels.
4. Fix the biggest of the top 3 drop-offs, wait a few days, and remeasure.

- A big early drop with strong D1 points to onboarding, not the core loop.
- Raising the share of players who stay past 5 minutes tends to lift D1 naturally.
- Whether low D1 is mostly onboarding's fault or the core loop's is debated (disputed).
- Leaving right after the tutorial means no hook; a tutorial cannot save a bad game.
- Mobile leaves right after the tutorial may mean the right audience has not arrived yet, before the HEP threshold.
- Quitting at minute 5–6 usually comes from effort, such as hunting an unhighlighted sell NPC, and unrewarding upgrades.
- Common reasons players leave: the game misses its fantasy, onboarding fails, music is missing, or mechanics feel unimportant.
- Session length varies hugely at low CCU, so do not over-read early averages.

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leaves at first tutorial step | Lag or long loading | Stream worlds, preload only HUD, staged loading | Medium |
| Players stand around, leave within 1 min | Confusion, no clear target | Spawn at objective, beams, darken-and-highlight | High |
| Bounce at 60–180s | No goal | State a clear goal and what currency buys | Medium |
| High bounce, title mismatch | Gameplay does not match name or icon | Short opening cutscene, deliver the promise at once | High |
| Large drop at one funnel step | Bug, missing instruction, or console or mobile input gap | Test on each device, state the action explicitly | High |
| Low tutorial completion | Too long, too much text, overlapping UI | Core loop only, 15–30s, about 5 words per step | High |
| Leaves right after tutorial | No hook or weak core loop | Polish core mechanic, tease progression | High |
| Quits at minute 5–6 | Effortful loop, unrewarding upgrades | Highlight NPCs, juicier upgrades, micro loops | Medium |
| Average playtime under 4 min | Broken onboarding | Rebuild the first minute | High |
| Bad PTR and bounce, game seems fine | Flow forces leaving, such as group join | Remove exit-requiring prompts | Medium |
| Good stats, bad PTR | Thumbnail problem | Fix thumbnails, not gameplay | Medium |
| Low playtime, other stats good | Mobile lag or balance | Test on phones, rebalance early pacing | Medium |
| Average playtime | Average core loop | Improve the loop; playtime rewards will not fix it | High |
| High playtime, low D1 or D7 | Too little content, game finished on day 1 | Add content depth and long-term goals | Medium |
| Good D1, D7 near 1% | Mid or late game too hard or boring | Fix the progression curve | Medium |
| Low D1 despite daily and offline rewards | Weak onboarding or unfun loop | Polish, rebalance, or move on | High |
| Low D7 | Game finishes too early, no updates | Content depth, weekly updates, events, strong day-7 reward | High |
| Solo joiners leave PvP or 2-player games | Empty servers | Bots, solo mode, smaller servers, friend boost | High |
| Rage-quit after first death | Newcomers outmatched | Extra lives, easy early wins, instant requeue | Medium |
| All stats under 20th percentile after a week of ads | Weak concept | Move on to a new game | Medium |
