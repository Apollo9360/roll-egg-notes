# D1 and D7 levers

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[Core loop, progression and content depth]] · Next: [[Favorites, likes and group prompts]]

- Top games usually have at least 2 of these features, since Roblox now rewards retention more.
- Playtime is a progression problem; D1 is a reason-to-return problem.
- D7 comes mainly from attachment to the core loop, more content and deeper progression, not from rewards.

| Feature | Main effect | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily login rewards | D7 more than D1 | Escalate; make day 2 and day 7 very strong | High |
| Streak and comeback rewards | Return visits | Common in top games | Medium |
| Offline earnings | D1 and D7 | Welcome-back screen showing earnings; can let players collect and leave | High |
| Daily and weekly quests | D1 and D7 | Week-long quests with a strong day-7 reward | High |
| Timed free rewards (chest, spin wheel) | Return visits and playtime | Hourly-reset spins also used | High |
| Limited-time events, admin abuse events | Retention and D7 | Keep at least 1 active or upcoming | High |
| Weekly updates and live ops | D7 | One game fell from 5k to 3.5k CCU after a week without updates | High |
| Collection index, achievements | D7 and long-term goals | Reward each completed set | High |
| Long-term progression | D7 | The game must take longer to finish | High |
| Timers that finish later | D1 | Tasks almost done at session end, or upgrades that complete over time | Medium |
| Shared restock shop | Appointment returns | Grow a Garden restocks every 5 minutes for all players | Medium |
| Co-op or friend reward | D7 and CCU | One co-op game had poor D1 but 98th-percentile D7 | Medium |
| Favorites prompt | Return visits | Mostly helps D7 and D30 | Medium |
