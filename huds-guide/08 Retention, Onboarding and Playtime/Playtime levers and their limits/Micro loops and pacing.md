# Micro loops and pacing

Up: [[Playtime levers and their limits]] · Previous: [[Playtime levers and their limits - Levers]] · Next: [[Satisfying feedback and sound]]

- Micro reward loops are needed; gating every upgrade behind rebirths makes play repetitive.
- Build visual value progression, such as single coin, then 3 coins, then a pouch; inflated numbers feel bigger.
- Balance with a simulation, an AI (artificial intelligence) script or Desmos plots; include rebirth formulas to see interactions.
- 20 minutes to beat world 1 is fine before adding worlds; some target 1–2 hours to max world 1.
- QoL (quality of life) trade-off: omitting "equip best" or using long unbox animations inflates early playtime, but QoL matters more at scale.
- Save hotbar order across sessions; losing it annoys players.
