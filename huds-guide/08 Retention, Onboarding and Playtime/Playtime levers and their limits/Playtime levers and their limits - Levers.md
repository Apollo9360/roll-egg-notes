# Playtime levers and their limits - Levers

Up: [[Playtime levers and their limits]] · Previous: [[Playtime levers and their limits - Genre playtime benchmarks]] · Next: [[Micro loops and pacing]]

- Show timed playtime rewards at 5, 10, 15 and up to 50 minutes in the UI (owner-recommended).
- Early playtime rewards with luck potions add dopamine; scaling rewards give a reason to stay.
- A free autoclicker or ticket system unlocked by playtime keeps players online.
- In-game short-video feeds lifted playtime in several games, by up to 44% in one report (disputed).
- Such feeds can grow a 1k-CCU game to 3–4k, but the core loop must earn the first 1k.
- Roblox later restricted reward-driven media feeds for Kids and Select audiences.
- Free private servers help playtime and retention a lot, especially for players avoiding PvP (low confidence).
- AFK (away from keyboard) worlds or AFK bonuses reportedly boost playtime a lot (low confidence).
- Auto-roll suits roll-only games, but not games where AFK rolling makes no sense (owner-recommended).
- To avoid the 20-minute idle kick, reset a timer on input and teleport idle players a minute before it.
- Run in-game events every 5–15 minutes, such as 1 of 20 random RNG events, or a boss every 30 minutes.
- Prefer interactive events, such as a frozen pond to drill through, over passive mutations; add a "Next event starts in" timer.
- When sessions drop from 10–15 to 2–7 minutes, give the core tool on join, speed up the early game and add rebirths.
