# Satisfying feedback and sound

Up: [[Playtime levers and their limits]] · Previous: [[Micro loops and pacing]]

- Dopamine means feeling rewarded: more and nicer VFX, SFX and UI, plus more and faster rewards.
- Make unsatisfying chores juicier, or automatable by a buyable NPC.
- Tween FOV (field of view) in during a roll and out on the result; FOV changes also add speed to slow games.
- An RNG roll should feel like a roll, not too quick; the roll is a major retention feature.
- Very rare rolls, such as 1 in 3k, should get a big visual, like Slime RNG's fall-from-sky effect.
- Show merges level by level with accelerating animation, VFX and SFX; show the "pop" only for undiscovered items.
- Replace text-only catch screens with viewport reveals, such as a silhouette fade-in plus zoom.
- A single too-loud sound cut playtime by 20% in one A/B test; avoid harsh high-pitched SFX.
