# Playtime levers and their limits - Genre playtime benchmarks

Up: [[Playtime levers and their limits]] · Next: [[Playtime levers and their limits - Levers]]

| Genre | Typical average playtime | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Tower defense | About 80 min | Medium |
| Pet simulators | 90+ min | Low |
| RNG, tycoon and simulator | 20+ min target | Medium |
| Incrementals | About 3x obbies; longest among casual genres | Low |
| Big games overall | About 15 min | Low |
| Steal-an-Egg style | About 14 min | Low |
| +1 games | About 11 min | Low |
| Survival | 90th percentile about 9 min (disputed; others cite survival for peak playtime) | Low |
| Obbies and platformers | Lowest bar of all genres | Medium |

- Compare against the real genre; a "tycoon" that is really a simulator should use simulator benchmarks.
- Incrementals give longer playtime, while RNG games give longer retention (low confidence).
