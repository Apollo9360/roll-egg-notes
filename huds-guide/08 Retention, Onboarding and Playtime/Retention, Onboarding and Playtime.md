# Retention, Onboarding & Playtime

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Make the tutorial the core loop only, visual rather than text, and about 15–30 seconds long, aiming for 75–80%+ completion. Then hold first-5-minute retention near 60%, D1 (day-1 retention) at 10–15%+ and average playtime at 15–20+ minutes; reward systems only amplify a game players already like.
