# Multiplayer, PvP and empty servers

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[Mobile, console and performance]] · Next: [[Diagnosing a drop-off]]

### Cold start and empty servers

- Empty servers kill playtime; one game fell from 9 to 2 minutes when players were alone.
- Low CCU deters new joiners, who move to bigger games; keep ads on until home recommendations hold the game.
- Games needing 2+ players are very hard to start; never make a game strictly 2-player.
- Two-player obbies lose most players at checkpoint 1, because solo joiners have no partner.
- A queue system needs about 500 CCU, and a hangout game about 50 sustained CCU.
- Fixes include matchmaking, solo modes, more early ad spend, and AI bots for PvP (player versus player).
- Fill empty slots with NPCs or bots, such as max server size minus real players; opinions on impact vary.
- Fake players risk bans, especially while chasing the HEP requirement.
- Lower max server size, such as 16–22, so small games feel full.
- Fully isolated single-player games usually do worse; solo progression with social interaction is fine.
- Requiring VC (voice chat) causes quick bounces; only about 23% of players had it in one game.

### Friend invites

- Friend-invite boosts work because people invite friends for the rewards.
- Add a small, always-visible friend boost shown as inactive when no friend is present; it costs nothing.
- Reward co-play when a friend is in the server (owner-recommended).
- Rewarding "invite 3 friends" may beat rewarding likes (low confidence).
- Roblox has disabled some SocialService friend APIs that used to help retention.

### Rounds, lobbies and queues

- Round-based games work if rounds are short and players can spectate, vote or edit loadouts.
- Let lobby players join rounds in progress, or guide them with a beam.
- Auto-equip the weapon, shorten intermission and map vote, and warn about 15 seconds before map parts disappear.
- Keep about a 30-second intermission so players see the lobby.
- Waiting 2 minutes in a lobby queue loses kids; fill waits with a lobby obby or fling minigame.
- Round-based shooters often lose about 25% at the results screen or lobby teleport and about 20% in the queue.
- Keep players in the same server between rounds and show XP and unlock progress.
- A revive mechanic helps round-based games (low confidence).
- Infinite games with a weak lobby show poor D7–D28.

### New players vs veterans

- Newcomers get crushed by experienced players; consider pro lobbies, skill-based matchmaking and simpler mechanics.
- Match new joiners against weaker veterans so they rarely lose their first match.
- Players often leave after one death if they feel they cannot win.
- Give newcomers something positive before death: easier early wins, bots, spectating or tutorial protection.
- Move PvP tutorials to a separate target-practice area.
- Give 3 lives instead of 1, and let losers instantly join another round.
- Players who finish an onboarding bot match feel done; give them a reason to fight real players.
- Scale boss health or rewards to server count so a solo player is not stuck for 30 minutes.
