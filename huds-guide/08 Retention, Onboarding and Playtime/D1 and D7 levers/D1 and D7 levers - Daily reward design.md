# D1 and D7 levers - Daily reward design

Up: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Next: [[D1 and D7 levers - Offline earnings]]

- Escalate rewards toward a big day-7 reward instead of repeating the same reward.
- Give something powerful on day 2, not only on day 7.
- Labelling day 2 as an "OP REWARD" raised D1 by about 2%; strong rewards on days 2, 3–5 and 7 are common.
- Make a day-7 reward fully exclusive, and tell players it is exclusive.
- Show a countdown warning so players know when the next reward unlocks.
- For non-tycoon games, use daily and playtime rewards rather than offline earnings.
