# D1 and D7 levers - Offline earnings

Up: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Previous: [[D1 and D7 levers - Daily reward design]] · Next: [[Quests, index and badges]]

- Offline earnings and quests improve D1; state offline income in the game description.
- Top games show a popup or sound on Esc saying the game earns offline.
- Grow a Garden popularized offline rewards and most games copied them.
- Offline earnings can backfire: players log in for 5 seconds, collect and leave.
