# Social, events and community

Up: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Previous: [[Quests, index and badges]] · Next: [[Limits of reward systems (disputed)]]

- Socialization drives FOMO (fear of missing out), competition and playtime, then D1 and D7.
- Add a top progress bar showing every player's map position, and place the leaderboard prominently.
- A lobby lets players breathe, and leaderboards create status; a round game with no lobby has a problem (owner-recommended).
- Admin abuse events bring players back after a week and lift weekend CCU.
- Announce admin abuse 30–60 minutes ahead in the title and icon; it works poorly at about 30 CCU.
- Retarget ads at returning users and show a "what's new" panel on join after a meaningful update.
- Make a Discord for your game at any size, so you can talk to players (owner-recommended).
