# Limits of reward systems (disputed)

Up: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Previous: [[Social, events and community]]

- Most experienced developers say rewards do not help if players do not like the game.
- None of the top 10 CCU games reportedly have daily rewards, though some 50k-CCU games use them.
- By October 2026, daily and offline rewards hooked players less than before, since every game has them.
- D1 around 3–4% despite daily rewards, offline earnings and quests means weak onboarding or an unfun core loop.
- Remedy: polish the core mechanic, balance rewards, and know when to move on.
