# Quests, index and badges

Up: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Previous: [[D1 and D7 levers - Offline earnings]] · Next: [[Social, events and community]]

- Notify quest completion, then require a manual "Claim" with SFX and VFX; it beats auto-claim.
- Add an index with rewards per completed set, such as a full legendary pet set.
- Interactive badges granting +5% income, such as for completing the index, extend D7 in brainrot and "spin a X" games.
- Customization builds attachment; saveable custom bunkers raised one game's D1 by 37% and D7 by 6%.
- A base-decoration focus aimed at a female audience took another game from 1k to 25k+ CCU.
