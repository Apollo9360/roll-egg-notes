# Estimates and projections (Retention)

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[Diagnosing a drop-off]]

- Estimate: CCU ≈ DAU (daily active users) × average minutes ÷ 1,440; 1,000 DAU at 15 minutes holds about 10 CCU (basis: arithmetic).
- Estimate: raising average playtime from 6 to 9 minutes lifts CCU about 50% at equal traffic (basis: linear scaling).
- Estimate: a 5-step tutorial at 95% per step completes about 77%; at 90% per step, about 59% (basis: compounded step pass rates).
- Estimate: a 15-step tutorial at 95% per step completes only about 46%, which explains why cutting to 3 steps helped (basis: same compounding).
- Estimate: telling 70% from 80% tutorial completion needs about 300 new players per variant (basis: two-proportion test, 95% confidence, 80% power).
- Estimate: telling 8% from 12% D1 needs about 900 new players per variant, often several days of ads (basis: same test).
- Estimate: 1,000 new ad players a day return about 50 at 5% D1 and 150 at 15% (basis: reported D1 ranges).
- Estimate: 10 hours of content lasts about 10 days for an engaged player at an hour a day (basis: reported 8–20 hour targets).
- Estimate: if the developer reaches a milestone in 3 minutes, plan for about 6 minutes for new players (basis: reported 2x new-player slowdown).
- Estimate: the announced 100-HEP threshold needs 60% fewer HEPs, so time to all-ages falls about 2.5x (basis: 250 to 100 ratio).
