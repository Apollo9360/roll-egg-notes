# HEP threshold and early-life stats

Up: [[Benchmarks and priorities]]

- Stats often jump after a kid-targeted game crosses the highly engaged player (HEP) threshold and under-16 players arrive.
- One keyboard game went from 13 to 27 minutes average playtime after unlocking all ages.
- The HEP threshold for all-ages reach is 250 (500 at the late-May launch, 250 since about August 19).
- Reaching it still needs the 1,000 Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus; a 50,000 Robux expedited review skips it.
- A drop to 100 HEP in November is announced but not yet in effect.
- HEP roughly corresponds to about 6 minutes of playtime per player (low confidence).
- Whether a game can lose HEP status later is unknown, even to experienced developers.
- Early stats are unreliable: one game sat near the 50th percentile for a week, then hit 90th–99th percentile at 18k CCU.
- First-day-of-ads D1 is often inflated and then drops; playtime can also fall after a few days with no changes.
- Expect about 15 days per cycle of hypothesis, update and measurement once ads start.
