# Playtime levers and their limits

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[Favorites, likes and group prompts]] · Next: [[Mobile, console and performance]]

- Playtime rewards never fix bad average playtime; they only boost good playtime.
- Average playtime reflects the core loop; an average loop gives average playtime.
- Low playtime usually comes from a bad tutorial, bad UI, slow early progression, bad economy or little content.
- If playtime is low, fix onboarding and the game; if it is high, add content.
- When playtime is already about 20 minutes, mid-term gameplay is the hook; only improve onboarding.
- High playtime with no return visits is still a failure, often signalling too little content.
- Nobody plays an obby for an hour, even from the algorithm; focus on retention there.
