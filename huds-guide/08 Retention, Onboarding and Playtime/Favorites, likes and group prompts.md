# Favorites, likes and group prompts

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Previous: [[D1 and D7 levers]] · Next: [[Playtime levers and their limits]]

- Prompt a favorite at a milestone, such as stage 25, or right after a purchase; both draw many favorites.
- Show a favorite prompt after 90 seconds to 15 minutes of play, never right on join; one prompt at 90 seconds added about 30 favorites per hour.
- Pair the favorite prompt with a join-group prompt; one game gained 600k group members from 10M visits.
- Early-dying players downvote while players who stay rarely upvote; add a like reminder.
- 60–70% likes at the start of ads is common and usually recovers; big games often sit under 60%.
- Likes are an effect, not a cause; playtime and retention matter far more than like ratio.
- Reward liking and group joins; boost the reward temporarily after a bug hurts the ratio.
- The Roblox API cannot verify likes, so a like requirement is soft; only favorites can be checked.
- Community like goals work, such as server-wide coins every 50 likes or admin abuse at 1,000 likes.
- Like-for-reward is technically against the TOS (terms of service) but widely ignored.
- Free rewards for favorite, group join and like reached a 97% like ratio over about 300 votes.
- Asking your own Discord to like the game can backfire; one attempt drew far more dislikes than likes.
