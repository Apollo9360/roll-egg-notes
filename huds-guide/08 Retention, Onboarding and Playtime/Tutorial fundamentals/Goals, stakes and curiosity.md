# Goals, stakes and curiosity

Up: [[Tutorial fundamentals]] · Previous: [[Length and step count]] · Next: [[Skip buttons and cutscenes]]

- Give a clear goal; teaching mechanics without a goal causes bounces at 60–180 seconds.
- Say what currency is for, such as "buy better cases", and show shops early instead of hiding them far away.
- Tease what progress unlocks; hiding later biomes leaves players asking why they are doing the core action.
- Remind the player of a longer-term goal during the first session to lengthen it.
- Emotional attachment is critical; add stakes such as "stop the robber" or "save the grandma".
- Sell the fantasy in short reasons, such as "Place your ice cream to build your tower".
- Music that sells the fantasy matters; silence or mismatched music is a common reason players leave.
- A simple starter choice, such as picking 2 of 4 starter pets, creates early ownership.
