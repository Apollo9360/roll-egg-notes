# Length and step count

Up: [[Tutorial fundamentals]] · Next: [[Goals, stakes and curiosity]]

- Consensus shortened over time: by August 2026, 7–9 minute tutorials were rejected and 15–30 seconds became the common target.
- Aim for 15–30 seconds and under 1 minute; chop 3 trees instead of 10.
- The best trending tutorials, such as Steal a Brainrot's, run about 30 seconds.
- Keep step count low; more than 5 steps tends to fail, and one game gained a lot of engagement cutting 15 steps to 3.
- Keep the first stage very short and never make players wait a minute on step 1.
- Cut tutorial timers to about 3–10 seconds; a 5-minute first grow time is far too long.
- An incremental reveal of mechanics across 3–5 minutes can still perform if each step is short and rewarding.
