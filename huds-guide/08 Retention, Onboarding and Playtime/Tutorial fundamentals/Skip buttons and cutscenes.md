# Skip buttons and cutscenes

Up: [[Tutorial fundamentals]] · Previous: [[Goals, stakes and curiosity]]

- By late July 2026, consensus was no "skip tutorial" button; players skip by accident, get lost and leave.
- A skip option only makes sense for tutorials longer than 3–5 minutes, which should be shortened anyway.
- A short opening cutscene of about 15 seconds, played on load and then followed by the tutorial, lowers bounce rate (owner-recommended).
- The owner tested this on two games and calls it the current onboarding meta for tycoons and simulators.
- Cutscenes are probably less suited to +1 games, and incremental games often have little to showcase.
- Use the cutscene to fix a title-to-gameplay mismatch, such as a clickbait name the gameplay does not obviously deliver.
- Keep it short and engaging; a 2-minute cutscene plus a long tutorial was blamed for one brainrot game's failure.
- Onboarding cutscenes and trailers are also reported to lift PTR.
- An interactive start beats a static cutscene (disputed).
