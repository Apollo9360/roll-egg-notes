# Benchmarks and priorities

Up: [[Retention, Onboarding and Playtime]] · Next: [[Tutorial fundamentals]]

| Metric | Healthy target | Warning level | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tutorial completion | 75–85%; 70% acceptable for complex games | Under 50% is weak; 10–30% usually means a bug | 50–70% is the most common real-world range | High |
| Bounce (first-time players leaving within 3 min) | About one third | About half is typical, not good | Ad traffic bounces far more than home-recommendation traffic | Medium |
| First-5-minute retention (new users) | 60–65% (owner-recommended); 65–70% on algorithm traffic | Below 60% signals an onboarding problem; 18% is awful | One report accepts 35%+ on ads and 50%+ on home recommendations | High |
| D1 | 10–15%; 19–20% is excellent; 8% is roughly the floor for success | 3–5% means weak onboarding or core loop | Ad cohorts often show only ~5%; 90th-percentile D1 tends to succeed | High |
| D7 (day-7 retention) | About 5–6% is solid (low confidence) | About 1% even with a big day-7 reward | Needs 7+ days of continuous ads to read | Medium |
| Average playtime | 15–20 min for most genres; RNG, tycoon and simulator games 20+ min | Under 4 min means broken onboarding; 5–7 min is bad | 6 min on day-1 ads is weak; 9 min is better (owner-recommended) | High |
| Launch content | About 8–10+ hours for incrementals; at least a reason to return on day 2 | Beatable in 30 min or less kills D1 | See content depth below | High |
| Percentile stats during ads | 50th–60th percentile is typical | Under ~20th percentile after a full week of ads means move on | Stats usually improve once home recommendations start | Medium |

- Some reports in the help channel cited D1 of 50–70%; most data points put real D1 far lower, so treat those figures as a different metric (disputed).
- Metric priority: playtime and retention first, then PTR (play-through rate), then revenue.
- Playtime is the first stat to fix, since the others tend to follow once it rises.
- Fix metrics in funnel order: first 30 seconds and bounce, first minute, 15 minutes, D1, then D7.
- Do not chase D28 while D1, D3, D7, bounce and PTR are still poor.
- Fix one stat per update, so you can tell what moved it.
- When retention and playtime are good but PTR is bad, fix thumbnails rather than gameplay.
- A bad D1 is the best single sign a game is finished, but only read it with a consistent player base.
- Improve anything below the 50th percentile, but a bad concept cannot be saved; know when to move on.
- Stats do not improve on their own; gather feedback and improve daily while ads run.
