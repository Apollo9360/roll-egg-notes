# Known bugs and errors

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Common mistakes]]

| Error or symptom | Fix or status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| "This game is not yet eligible for ads. Review the moderation, approval, and age guideline status" | Complete the maturity questionnaire and moderation; Roblox assigned a team ([devforum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-saying-this-game-is-not-yet-eligible-for-ads-review-the-moderation-approval-and-age-guideline-status/4831955)) | High |
| "To create a sponsored campaign, you must first create an eligible experience" | Make the game public; sometimes persists after that and the questionnaire | Medium |
| Can't start a campaign | Open the experience from Creator Hub's Create tab and use Ads Manager at the bottom | Medium |
| Stuck "In review" or "scheduled" for hours | Recreate the campaign under a different name; backlogs clear within a day | High |
| Campaign shows "inactive" or "moderated" but still spends | Recurring; no known fix | Medium |
| Campaign removed without reason, game gone from recently played | Appeal; unresolved | Low |
| Ads won't start for many users | Platform outage; wait | Medium |
| Vague campaign rejections | AI art is not the cause; check thumbnails and in-game names | Low |
| Can't disable thumbnails, or paused ones still served | Intermittent; one bad thumbnail spent half a day's credits | Medium |
| Thumbnails marked "Moderated" inside a campaign | Replace them; one rejection can block the campaign | Low |
| Editing fails or uploads return "request failed" | Intermittent; retry later | Medium |
| "Reporting may be temporarily unavailable" or "data might be delayed" | Platform reporting outage; numbers catch up later | High |
| Impossible stats (200% CTR, clicks far above plays, totals not matching per-thumbnail sums) | Reporting lag; compare with the game's own visit count | High |
| Plays frozen while spend accrues, or spend shown above real spend | Intermittent | Medium |
| Only 10 of the active thumbnails shown | Unresolved | Low |
| Credits deducted with no running campaign, or while paused | Check auto-reload and old promoted communities | Medium |
| Personal credits not usable on a group game | Expected since September 20; buy credits from the group | High |
| Investor can't see the experience despite permissions | Unresolved; recheck Roles manager permissions | Low |
| 16 credits spent at once, or only $0.07 in 3 hours | Delivery glitch or normal burst pacing | Medium |
| Can't re-enable ads after a short pause | Unresolved | Low |
| Rewarded video ads blocked as "restricted content" | Requires an approved maturity questionnaire; unresolved | Low |
