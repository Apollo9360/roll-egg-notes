# Ads & Ad Credits

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Run a Plays-objective campaign on a daily budget of about 16 credits for at least 7 days, ideally 10–14, with broad default targeting. Buy credits in USD (1 USD = 1 credit, versus about 263 Robux per credit) and stop when home-recommendation impressions overtake ad impressions.
