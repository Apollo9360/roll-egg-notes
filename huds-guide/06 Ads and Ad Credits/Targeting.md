# Targeting

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Reading results CTR, CPP and impressions]] · Next: [[Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing]]

- Use broad targeting (owner-recommended). The ads system likely optimizes on quality signals beyond CTR, much as broad targeting now wins on Meta.
- Roblox ads do not work like Meta ads; fancy setups only pay off for games with already-excellent stats (owner).
- Choose "All players" for a new game; "New players" only once you have a playerbase.
- Until the all-ages unlock, ads serve only 16+ even with "All players" selected. Including younger ages wastes nothing.
- One game's CTR rose from about 8% at 16+ to about 11.5% after the all-ages unlock.
- Mature games reach the HEP threshold via normal ads more easily than kid-targeted games. +1-per-click games depend on the all-ages unlock.
- Some target 18+ or only high-spending countries while gated, for better revenue per paying user (disputed). Older audiences click ads less.
- Some 16+ campaigns still reach younger players or unverified accounts (low confidence).
- A hangout game drawing only young kids can add a campaign targeting 18–24.
- Set the game's own genre (High). A campaign genre filter is disputed: it raised CTR to 3–4% in some tests but cut estimated reach by about 300M.
- Narrow filters choke delivery: a simulation filter on a 17+ campaign got 5 impressions in an hour.
- Many developers exclude Russia, reporting bot traffic that fakes clicks and playtime.
- A few advertise only to North America, Western Europe and Australia on mobile for better monetization (low confidence).
- Southeast Asian players rarely spend. A Japan-only test gave high CTR and low CPP, then drifted to Southeast Asia.
- If 50% of players come from one country, target it and translate creative text.
- A second 12/day campaign aimed at high-spending countries raised payer conversion from 0.12% to 0.76% in one case.
- Ads deliver mostly mobile players (96% in one case). Run all devices, then stop sponsoring devices that get few home recommendations.
- Ads reach players in both search and home, which is why they beat Roblox Moments.
- Time of day and launch day matter little; the system learns when players are online. Avoiding a Sunday launch is a minority tip.
