# Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing - Pausing and restarting

Up: [[Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing]] · Previous: [[One campaign or several]]

- Never turn ads off if you can afford it (owner-recommended); toggling distorts stats and resets learning.
- A brief bug-fix pause is fine if 7–10-day stats are good. Pausing has made a game vanish from players' Continue lists.
- Stopping ads usually drops CCU for a day or two; a 2-day stop can kill early momentum.
- Cancelling wastes nothing: unspent credits stay in your balance. Expired campaigns can't be resumed.
- Pausing overnight is pointless, since ads run across time zones. Morning-only day-parting worked for one developer (low confidence).
- To change budget, edit the existing campaign rather than restarting it.
