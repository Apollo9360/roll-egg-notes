# Thumbnail and title testing

Up: [[Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing]] · Next: [[One campaign or several]]

1. Make 5+ fully different concepts, each with a few iterations. Ten strong concepts beat 25 random ones on a small budget.
2. Run them in a low-budget test campaign (about 5 credits/day, or 1.5–2 credits per thumbnail) on Plays.
3. If none passes 3.5–4% CTR after about 10 credits, stop and redesign.
4. Start a new campaign with the winners, 5 fresh concepts and 1–2 previous best as anchors. A second batch averaged about 1% higher CTR.
5. Once proven, move the winners into the full-budget main campaign.

- A creative at 5% inside a mixed campaign hit 10% running alone, so solo runs can reveal more.
- Title testing: make about 3 duplicate places (empty baseplates that teleport to the game) with identical thumbnails and different titles.
    - Run one Plays campaign per title for a day or less, so the algorithm ignores them.
    - Use only 1–2 thumbnails per title and normalize by impressions; campaign audiences differ.
