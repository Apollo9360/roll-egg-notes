# One campaign or several

Up: [[Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing]] · Previous: [[Thumbnail and title testing]] · Next: [[Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing - Pausing and restarting]]

- Most run one campaign, since each extra campaign pays its own learning phase. One $32 campaign beats two $16 ones.
- Keep a campaign that knows your niche: disable losers and add variants of winners until full.
- Start a new campaign when replacing all thumbnails, after a title change or big revamp, or when CTR is low.
- Duplicated campaigns give very different CTRs for the same creatives (5–6% to 2%, or 1% to 4–5%), because audiences differ.
- Anchor method: copy the best creative into a new campaign with fresh ones and keep the original running.
- To force spend onto other creatives, put them in a second campaign and lower the main budget.
- Two campaigns also suit Plays plus Engagement splits or geo-targeted tests.
- Running 2–3 campaigns with the same thumbnail to pull more launch players is a minority trick (low confidence).
