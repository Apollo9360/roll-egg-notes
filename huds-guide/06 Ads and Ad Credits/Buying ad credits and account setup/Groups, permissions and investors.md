# Groups, permissions and investors

Up: [[Buying ad credits and account setup]] · Previous: [[Card payments, billing and age]]

- Set ad permissions in the group's new Roles manager, not the legacy one.
- Since September 20, 2026, credits for a group game must be bought from the group. Personal-account credits no longer work on group games.
- A common setup: link ads to the group and make an 18+ member the "advertiser" with their card.
- An investor needs Creator Hub ad permissions in your group, not Studio access.
- Investors who fund ads usually want a percentage of the game; the share depends on the amount. A 20–40k Robux credit stake is a typical serious offer.
- For small budgets (under about $100), work and fund ads yourself rather than seek investors (owner-recommended).
- Ad-credit investors advertise in developer communities. Follow each server's self-promotion rules.
- A private game won't appear in Ads Manager. Restart Ads Manager or check the owning group.
