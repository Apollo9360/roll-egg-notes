# Card payments, billing and age

Up: [[Buying ad credits and account setup]] · Next: [[Groups, permissions and investors]]

- Card payment requires 18+. Under-18 accounts are redirected to Robux conversion ([devforum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-cant-18s-directly-purchase-ad-credits-using-card/3836308)).
- Under-18 options: a parent's verified account with ad permissions in your group, or paying via Robux. Using alts carries policy and DevEx risk.
- Some countries can only pay via Robux conversion, even on 18+ accounts.
- "Can't buy ad credits" or "$1.00 hold declined / failed to authenticate card" means the bank declined Roblox's $1 authorization hold.
    - Fixes: retry, call the bank, swap cards (sometimes several), use a fintech card such as Revolut, or pay with Robux. PayPal on the same card does not help.
    - Banks sometimes block Roblox after repeated daily ad charges.
- USD billing charges the card in steps: $5 first, then $15, $25 and $50 thresholds, and at month end. Thresholds rise over time toward about $100.
- Select the card in the campaign's payment-method section. You cannot switch a running campaign from credits to card.
- Check auto-reload settings. Unexpected charges of $25–65 with no active campaign have been reported.
- Unspent credits stay in your balance when a campaign ends. Credits already spent are not refunded.
