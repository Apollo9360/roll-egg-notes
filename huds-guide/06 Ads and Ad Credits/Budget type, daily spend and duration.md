# Budget type, daily spend and duration

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement]] · Next: [[Standard launch campaign recipe]]

### Daily vs lifetime

- Use a daily budget (owner-recommended). The owner says nobody serious runs lifetime budgets.
- A lifetime budget can burn the credits within about 1 hour, leaving 23 hours without players.
- A minority argue lifetime lets the system test evenly for 24 hours, then favor winners (low confidence).
- A daily budget is not a hard cap: $20/day can spend $30 in 3 days, and an $8 budget showed $9.37.
- Spend arrives in bursts, sometimes a third of the day's budget at once. Changing the budget mid-campaign seems harmless.

### Spend and duration

| Daily budget | Duration | Status | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 credits | 7 days (about $112) | Most-repeated default (owner-recommended) | High |
| 16 credits | 10–14 days | Preferred for a brand-new game if affordable (owner-recommended) | High |
| 16 credits | 3 days minimum | Stats take about 3 days to show | High |
| 12–16 credits | 5–14 days | Accepted range; 12/day for 5–7 days is "fine" per the owner | High |
| 10 credits | 7–14 days | Works for good games; data is thinner | Medium |
| 5–8 credits | 7–14 days | Works for some incremental or solo games; others say it takes months | Low |
| 20–32 credits | 1–2 weeks | Multiplayer and party games | High |
| up to 100 credits | Short | PvP duel games without bots, per the owner | Medium |
| 40 credits | 7–10 days | Reaches home recommendations faster, not more surely | Medium |
| over 50 credits | Any | Not worth it for most games (owner) | High |

- The Ads Manager may enforce a minimum of about 10 credits/day. Others report 4–5/day campaigns running (disputed).
- Suggested totals: $100–200 is typical; $50–100 unlocked HEP for many; about 150 credits per game; $300–400 for a strong launch.
- The owner calls $20–30 total "not nearly enough" and 30k Robux (about 114 credits) "barely" enough.
- Low spend gives too small a sample to judge CTR, find bugs or reflect real stats.
- Very low budgets at 1–5 CCU can hurt, because players won't stay in near-empty servers.

### Longer beats bigger

- With a fixed budget, spread it over more days. 5/day for 6 days beats 16/day for 2; 10/day for 5 beats 16/day for 3.
- Examples: $50 as 5/day for 10 days; $100 as 16/day for 6 days or 10/day for 10; $120 as 16/day for 7–8 days.
- Why 7 days: day 1 goes to learning, day-7 retention (D7) needs 7 days, and day-1 retention (D1) and playtime stabilize over the week.
- Week one establishes 7-day stats; week two reinforces them so you stay in home recommendations.
- Bursts ruin 7-day stats. 100 credits in one day, $200 in 12 hours, or 20k Robux on one Saturday gives a spike that disappears.
- More spend buys speed, not outcome. A game that would plateau at 100 CCU plateaus there at 16/day or 100/day.
- Heavy spend does not save a bad game (owner). 95/day for 4 days and 150/day for a week both failed in reports.
- Heavy spend suits a big game shipping a huge update; the owner pushed one to the front page with about $150/day for 10 days.
- There is no ideal total. Spend until home-recommendation impressions are consistent.
