# Other paid channels and history

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Ad players, home recommendations and when to stop]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Ads)]]

- Ads seldom pay for themselves during the run. Aim to recover at least 10% of spend; strong games report about 50%.
- Return varies wildly: $84 of ads fed a 160k Robux merge tycoon, while $500 of ads earned 900 Robux.
- Roblox ad formats include sponsored experiences, search ads and premium home placements.
- Official Roblox ad tutorials: [video 1](https://youtu.be/D_YmdVZxIZw) and [video 2](https://youtu.be/CxGL_iQCOiA).
- The [Ads Manager API on Open Cloud](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/test-ads-manager-api-now-on-open-cloud/4766543) allows scripted campaign management.
- Paid YouTuber segments cost about $3k–10k. Results are poor in reports: $4k bought 89k visits, and a 40-creator push still flopped (low confidence).
- A cutscene on first load is worth more than a paid game trailer (owner).
- Leaderboard prize competitions, where developers fund a Robux pool for top players, are a niche alternative (low confidence).
- Rewarded video ads in your own game reportedly unlock at about 2,000 visits plus ID verification, and pay far less than game passes (low confidence).
- Roblox will charge cost-per-thousand-impression (CPM) fees on creator brand deals from 2027 ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-sets-cpm-based-fees-for-creator-brand-deals-starting-in-2027/)):

| Region | CPM fee | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| United States | $1.50 | Medium |
| UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Nordics | $0.75 | Medium |
| Western Europe, Japan, South Korea | $0.20 | Medium |
| Rest of world | $0.05 | Medium |
| Any region after 28 days live | $0.10 flat | Medium |

- Before 2026, ads were far cheaper per player: 20 credits over 2 days could bring 1k CCU, and 5 credits hundreds.
- The 16+ gate and HEP threshold sharply reduced what small budgets achieve.
