# Buying ad credits and account setup

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Next: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement]]

| Payment route | Rate | Notes | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| USD by card in Ads Manager | 1 USD = 1 credit | Requires an 18+ ID-verified account; the community default | High |
| Robux conversion | about 263 Robux per credit; quotes range 236–285 | 16 credits is about 4,200 Robux; 112 credits about 30k; 100k Robux about 380 credits | High |
| Robux bought at retail (500 for $5) | about 2–3x the cost of USD | Never buy Robux just to buy credits | High |
| Robux already earned | roughly par with USD | Conversion uses the Developer Exchange (DevEx) value of about $0.0038/Robux, so 263 Robux is about $1 | High |

- Pay with Robux your games earned rather than cashing out and rebuying (owner-recommended). Converting skips DevEx taxes and fees.
- Spending earned Robux on ads instead of a small DevEx payout is common practice among reinvesting developers.
- Convert Robux at [Ads Manager billing](https://create.roblox.com/advertise/billing/paymentsettings) via "reload balance".
- Robux gift cards cannot buy ad credits directly. Robux earned by games stays pending for weeks before it can be spent.
- From about September 1, conversion reportedly spends 18+-qualified Robux first at the 18+ DevEx rate, which changed the quoted rate for some.
- Third-party Robux sellers and credit middlemen exist, but overcharging and account risk are reported (low confidence on value).
- Some suspect Robux-funded campaigns perform worse than USD-funded ones (low confidence). Most say a credit buys the same impressions either way.
- Ad spend can count as a deductible business cost. Some regions add sales tax to credit purchases (low confidence).
