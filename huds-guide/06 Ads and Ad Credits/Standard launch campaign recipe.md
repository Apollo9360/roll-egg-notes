# Standard launch campaign recipe

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Budget type, daily spend and duration]] · Next: [[Learning phase, review and metric lag]]

1. Playtest with friends, cousins or testers first. Aim for at least the 50th percentile before paying for traffic (owner-recommended).
2. Make the game public, complete the maturity questionnaire and set its genre (ads appear to follow genre interest).
3. Buy credits with USD by card, or convert earned Robux. Budget at least $100–200.
4. Prepare as many distinct thumbnail creatives as you can, ideally up to 25 (see the [[Thumbnails and Icons|Thumbnails]] tab).
5. Create the campaign from the experience's Creator Hub page, in the Ads Manager section at the bottom.
6. Choose Plays, not the "recommended" Engagement default.
7. Set a daily budget of about 16 credits, ending 7–14 days out.
8. Leave targeting broad: all players, ages, devices and countries.
9. Schedule the start at least 30 minutes ahead for review.
10. On day 1, read only CTR and session time while the 24–48-hour learning phase runs.
11. After learning, disable clearly bad creatives with enough impressions. Add variants of winners.
12. After 3–4 days, check D1, playtime and CPP. If bad, stop, fix the game and relaunch.
13. Keep updating the game throughout the run, mainly onboarding and funnel fixes (owner-recommended).
14. If good, run without pausing until home-recommendation impressions exceed ad impressions.
15. Taper (for example 14, 12, then 10/day) or stop once home recommendations sustain CCU.

### Staged alternative

1. Run 12 credits/day for 3 days to get first data and fix funnel steps.
2. Run 16 credits/day for 7 days with only small funnel updates.
3. If HEP or home recommendations still haven't arrived, run 16/day for 3 more days.
4. If the game hasn't reached all-ages within about 6–7 days of ads, stop spending and rework it.

### Recommended settings

| Setting | Value | Why | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Objective | Plays | More impressions and data per dollar | High |
| Budget | Daily, 16 credits (12–20) | Lifetime can burn out in an hour; below 10 is risky | High |
| Multiplayer budget | 20–32 credits/day | Keeps lobbies filled | High |
| Duration | 7 days minimum, 10–14 ideal | Needs a full D7 cycle | High |
| Audience | All players | New players only once you have a playerbase | High |
| Age | All ages | Roblox serves only players who can join | High |
| Countries | All, optionally excluding Russia | Russian traffic widely reported as bots | Medium |
| Devices | All | PC-only suits only PC-oriented games | Medium |
| Genre filter | None, or your own genre | Raises CTR in some reports but cuts reach | Low |
| Creatives | Up to 25 (owner-recommended) | Roblox pushes the best ones | Medium |
| Campaigns | 1 | Each campaign pays its own learning phase | Medium |
| Start time | Any | Ads run across time zones | High |
