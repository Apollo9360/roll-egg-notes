# Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement - Common compromise

Up: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement]] · Previous: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement - The arguments]]

- Run Plays first. Add Engagement only if Plays stats are good but HEP still isn't reached.
- The most-repeated split is 16 Plays + 8 Engagement credits per day. Kill Engagement at 250 HEP, then phase out Plays after home recommendations arrive.
- Other daily splits seen (Plays + Engagement): 10 + 5, 10 + 6, 12 + 8, 16 + 5, 16 + 10, 25 + 10.
- Another variant runs Engagement for only the first 2–3 days, the rest on Plays.
- Plays keep servers populated so engagement players don't bounce off empty servers, which matters most for round-based games.
- Tiered rule: a low-effort or first game gets Plays; a decent game gets both; a serious or kid-focused game may start with Engagement.
