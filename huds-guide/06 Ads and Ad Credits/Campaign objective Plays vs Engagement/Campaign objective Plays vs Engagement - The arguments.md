# Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement - The arguments

Up: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement]] · Previous: [[How the advice changed in 2026]] · Next: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement - Common compromise]]

- Most experienced developers, and the owner, recommend Plays: more data per dollar, and if that data is bad, the game is the problem.
- Good games on Plays typically reach 250 HEP in 2–5 days at 16/day. Weaker games report 100–115 HEP after 9 days, or none.
- Plays-only HEP rates vary widely: about 5–25 HEP per day at 16/day, or roughly 1–4 HEP per credit.
- One test logged 7 HEP/day on Engagement versus 25/day on Plays.
- Engagement without a genre set gave 0.1% CTR and 0 plays from 31 clicks in one case.
- A vocal minority favor Engagement, citing reports of:
    - 110 HEP after one day, or 250 HEP at about 2,000 visits.
    - 75 HEP in 2 days, or 20+ HEP per day.
    - Weak or one-day games still reaching 250–500 HEP, while a 30/day Plays game got 35.
    - 125 HEP from about $30, or 30–80 engaged players per 5 credits.
    - About 42 minutes of playtime versus 7 on Plays, and more purchasers.
- At 5/day, Plays took about 2 weeks to reach the threshold in reports.
- Some say Engagement only suits established games with 500–1k CCU (low confidence).
- Engagement is sometimes suggested instead of the 50k expedited fee. In July the owner instead recommended paying the fee (then 100k) and running Plays.
