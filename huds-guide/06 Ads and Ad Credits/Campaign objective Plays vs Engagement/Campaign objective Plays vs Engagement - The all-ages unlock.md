# Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement - The all-ages unlock

Up: [[Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement]] · Next: [[How the advice changed in 2026]]

- New games are shown only to 16+ players until they unlock all-ages reach.
- Normal route: reach 250 highly engaged players, then pay the 1,000 Robux publishing/reach fee or hold Roblox Plus.
- Expedited route: pay 50,000 Robux for expedited review and skip the HEP requirement. It is reportedly refundable after about 90 days; conditions reported vary.
- History: 500 HEP at launch in late May; 250 HEP from about August 19. The expedited fee was 100,000 Robux until about mid-September, then 50,000.
- A drop to 100 HEP in November is announced and widely reported but not yet in effect. A 25,000 Robux fee is an unverified rumor.
- Some report the 1,000 Robux fee or Plus is also needed before any ads can run (low confidence).
