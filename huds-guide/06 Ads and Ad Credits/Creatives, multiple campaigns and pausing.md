# Creatives, multiple campaigns and pausing

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Targeting]] · Next: [[Testing, beta and multiplayer games]]

- You must make your own image creatives (essentially thumbnails); video isn't supported. See the [[Thumbnails and Icons|Thumbnails]] tab.
- A campaign holds up to 25 creatives. Published creatives can only be disabled, not removed.
- Add creatives via the three dots, then Edit, or click the campaign title to open the sidebar. Click into a campaign for per-creative stats.
- Keep 25 creatives in the campaign at all times for "more shots on goal" (owner-recommended). Bad ones get little spend anyway.
- Turn off bad creatives only after learning ends. Don't disable everything at once; keep your best performers running.
- Some argue never to pause creatives manually, since a slow starter can become the winner (disputed).
- Roblox front-runs 1–2 creatives with most of the spend. One case gave 500k impressions to 7 of 25 creatives.
- A high-CTR creative can still get little spend if the system judges its players poor.
- Cut thresholds: under 0.5–1% is hopeless; under 2% stop; under 3% after 25k impressions.
- Keep your all-time winner running as its CTR decays, while testing new ones.
- Ads can A/B test user-interface choices such as button color.
