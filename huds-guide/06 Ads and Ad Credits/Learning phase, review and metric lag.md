# Learning phase, review and metric lag

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Standard launch campaign recipe]] · Next: [[Reading results CTR, CPP and impressions]]

- Review ("In review") normally takes 10–60 minutes. Backlogs stretch it to 3–24 hours on some days.
- Creatives added mid-campaign also wait for review, sometimes about 8 hours.
- If a campaign is stuck past its start time, stop it and recreate it under a different name. For a scheduled start, wait a few hours first.
- Gambling-style names in the game, such as "gambler" or "jackpot" abilities, held one review for 12 hours until renamed (low confidence).
- One rejected thumbnail can get the whole campaign moderated (low confidence).
- Learning lasts 24–48 hours, sometimes about 3 days. Roblox spreads impressions, costs more per play and learns who clicks.
- After learning, ads go to more relevant players, with fewer bots and very young kids.
- Learning does not transfer between campaigns. Ending a campaign starts over.
- Adding or changing creatives resets learning; the owner says that is acceptable. Add new creatives all at once.
- Spend starts unevenly: a handful of impressions and about $0.01 after 2 hours is common, as is a large first burst.
- CTR can rise after learning (1.2% to 4.2%) or start high and fall (4.7% to 3.8%). Both are normal.
- A new campaign at about 1% CTR in its first hours should be left to run (owner-recommended). Don't cut sub-2% creatives during learning.
- Stats can take 20+ minutes to appear. Plays and CPP lag clicks, so compute early CPP from visits directly.
- Higher CTR with worse CPP and fewer impressions is often just lag. Wait 48 hours after the campaign ends for final numbers.
- CCU usually starts rising after about 2 days. Ad stats become trustworthy after 24 hours to 3 days.
