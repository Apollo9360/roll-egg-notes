# Estimates and projections (Ads)

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Other paid channels and history]] · Next: [[Common mistakes]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported data, not reported facts.

- Estimate: a standard 16/day run costs about 29.5k Robux for 7 days, 42k for 10 and 59k for 14 (basis: about 263 Robux per credit).
- Estimate: the 50,000 Robux expedited fee equals about 190 credits, or about 12 days of ads at 16/day (basis: 263 Robux per credit). The old 100k fee equalled about 380.
- Estimate: reaching 250 HEP with Plays costs about $65–80 for a good game and $150–300 for an average one (basis: 250 in 4–5 days at 16/day; 1–4 HEP per credit).
- Estimate: at a future 100 HEP threshold, Plays would cost about $25–100, roughly 2–6 days at 16/day (basis: same HEP-per-credit range, scaled linearly).
- Estimate: reaching 250 HEP with Engagement costs about $35–150 when it works (basis: 120 HEP from 40 credits; 250 in 2 days on about $17/day).
- Estimate: 16 credits/day buys about 65k–145k impressions and 1,600–8,000 plays per day (basis: 4k–9k impressions per credit; CPP 0.002–0.01).
- Estimate: ad CCU scales at roughly 1.5–3 CCU per daily credit, so 10/day gives about 15–30 (interpolated between reported 8/day and 16/day figures).
- Estimate: time to home recommendations at 16/day is about 4–5 days for top-decile stats and 10–11 days for average stats (basis: reported 4–5 days versus 1.5 weeks).
- Estimate: 40/day reaches home recommendations in about 3–4 days, spending 120–160 credits, roughly the same total as 16/day for 10 days (basis: reported day 3–4 at 40/day).
- Estimate: detecting a CTR difference needs about 1k impressions per thumbnail for 2% vs 4%, about 4k for 2% vs 3% and about 17k for 2.5% vs 3% (basis: standard two-proportion test, 95% confidence, 80% power).
- Estimate: a 25-thumbnail test needs about 40–50 credits (basis: reported 1.5–2 credits per thumbnail, with uneven spend).
- Estimate: break-even while running 16/day needs about 4,200 Robux per day of game revenue (basis: 16 credits × 263 Robux). Reported 10–50% recovery implies about 400–2,100 Robux/day back.
- Estimate: after ads stop with home recommendations, retained CCU is roughly 5–30% of ad-phase CCU (basis: reports of 50–80 falling to 15–20, and of about 5%).
