# Reading results CTR, CPP and impressions

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Learning phase, review and metric lag]] · Next: [[Targeting]]

- Judge on cost efficiency (CPP, cost per thousand impressions, cost per click), not CTR alone.
- 8k impressions per dollar at 4% CTR beats 1k impressions per dollar at 10% CTR.
- Roblox may keep funding a lower-CTR thumbnail because its CPP is better. Keep it.
- Higher CTR often brings fewer impressions; low CTR can bring more, cheaper impressions (unverified).
- Lower CTR means paying roughly 4x more for the same players. High CPP at about 1% CTR means change the thumbnails.
- At 3.5–4%+ CTR, impressions normally surge, up to about 20k per hour.
- Typical delivery is about 4k–9k impressions per credit; 4k per dollar is considered low.
- Ads deliver in waves, so several CCU spikes per day is normal. Only spend sitting at 0 for long is a concern.
- A bad like-to-dislike ratio after a bug can raise CPP.
- [CPP calculator](https://bloxg.com/tools/cost-per-player-calculator).

| CPP (credits per play) | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| about 0.001–0.002 | Ideal | High |
| under 0.005 | Good target | Medium |
| 0.005–0.008 | Expected for geo-targeted ads | Low |
| 0.01 or under | Good | High |
| 0.015–0.045 | High; improve thumbnails | Medium |
| 0.06–0.1 | Bad | Medium |
| about 0.3 | Typical of Engagement-only campaigns | Medium |

### Sample size

- 2,000–3,000 impressions is noise for close calls. CTR needs 5k+ impressions and still swings under 10k–15k.
- Clearly bad thumbnails can be cut after about 1k–2k impressions; close calls need about 10k each, or 100–150 clicks.
- Call a winner only on a large lift, about 50% (2% vs 3%+). 2.5% vs 3% is noise (owner).
- A 1-credit test is unreliable; give it at least a day at 16–20. Don't judge a thumbnail on under about $1–2 of spend.
- Wait 3–4 days, or 1k–10k visits, before judging game stats unless CTR collapses.

### Impressions-per-credit anomalies (2026)

| Period | Reported anomaly | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-June | Low impressions per dollar, attributed to learning | Low |
| Late July | Fewer impressions per credit; big spenders blamed for higher prices | Medium |
| Mid-August | Impressions cut to a tenth and spend up 10x overnight; support said to bid more | Low |
| Late August | Suspected change in ad rates | Low |
| Mid-September | Impressions collapsed even at 4.7–5.5% CTR; $3 spent all day on a 16/day plan | Medium |
| Late September | Reporting outage banner; about 4x fewer impressions per credit; spend jumps | High |
| October | Uneven spikes; fewer impressions on weekends; about 10x fewer on some accounts | Medium |

- Ads are an auction with auto-bidding. CPP and impressions vary with day of week and competition.
- Weekends may cost more per play (owner guess, several reports).
- First check for changed targeting, device or audience settings.
- Otherwise start a new campaign; restarts sometimes return to normal. Pausing and resuming does not.
- During a platform-wide slump with limited money, consider promoting on TikTok and YouTube until Roblox fixes it.
