# Campaign objective Plays vs Engagement

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Buying ad credits and account setup]] · Next: [[Budget type, daily spend and duration]]

| Aspect | Plays | Engagement | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buys | Raw traffic (visits, new players) | Players likely to become highly engaged players (HEP), often recent Robux spenders | High |
| Impressions per credit | Baseline | About 10–20x fewer | High |
| Cost per play (CPP) | Usually 0.002–0.01 credits | Around 0.3 credits in reports | Medium |
| Learning phase | About a day | Longer; may take 24 hours to start | Medium |
| Early concurrent users (CCU) | 10+ in the first hour is common | Often 0 for hours; about 50 visits/day | Medium |
| Player quality | Many short sessions | Longer playtime and more Robux spend per player | Medium |
| Best for | New games, data gathering, most cases | Finishing the HEP threshold when Plays stats are already good | High |

- Roblox pre-selects Engagement as "recommended" and describes it as best for reaching eligibility. Many beginners pick it by accident.
- The objective cannot be changed after publishing; make a new campaign. Running both needs two campaigns.
- Engagement plays are often reported late, so spend can overshoot what the dashboard showed.
- Once a game is all-ages, Engagement may no longer be offered (low confidence).
- The Earnings objective is locked for many accounts, possibly by region. Large games reportedly run revenue-optimized ads that pay for themselves.
