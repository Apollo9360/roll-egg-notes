# Common mistakes

Up: [[Ads and Ad Credits]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Ads)]] · Next: [[Known bugs and errors]]

- Accepting the "recommended" Engagement objective, or a lifetime budget, on a first campaign.
- Buying Robux at retail to convert into credits, which costs 2–3x more than paying in USD.
- Advertising an unpolished prototype, or optimizing thumbnails while D1 and playtime are bad.
- Expecting more spend to fix bad stats; continuing to spend just donates credits to Roblox.
- Front-loading: running 1–3 days or one-day bursts, toggling campaigns, or pausing at the first bad hour.
- Judging on 2,000 impressions, under $1 of spend, day-1 CPP or ad-phase CCU.
- Launching an update and ads together. Push the update, wait about 2 days, then advertise if CCU barely moves.
- Splitting a small budget across many campaigns, so none exits learning.
- Narrow genre or region filters, and underfunded multiplayer lobbies.
- Expecting ad players to monetize, or putting most of your savings into one game's first week.
- Paying the 50,000 Robux expedited fee as a small developer without good stats (disputed). Most reach 250 HEP via Plays ads instead.
- Leaving auto-reload on after a campaign ends.
