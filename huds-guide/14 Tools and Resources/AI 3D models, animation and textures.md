# AI 3D models, animation and textures

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Previous: [[AI coding and agent tools]] · Next: [[Studio plugins]]

| Tool | What it's for | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Meshy](https://www.meshy.ai/workspace) | AI mesh plus texture | Low quality but cheap; preferred over Claude for models. [Referral](https://www.meshy.ai/referral) gives a friend 1 month Pro for €1 and you €20 credit. | High |
| Tripo (Tripo3D) | Image-to-3D | Fine for detailed models that are not game-ready. | Medium |
| [Hunyuan 3D](https://3d.hunyuanglobal.com/studio) | Free AI mesh plus texture | Disputed: one report rates it above Meshy, others say results look obviously AI-made. | Low |
| Roblox mesh generation | Built-in AI meshes | Disputed: the owner calls it free and decent; the help channel calls it weak. A [segmented-parts preview](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/early-preview-generate-3d-models-segmented-into-multiple-parts/4678458) is in testing. | Medium |
| Mesh2Motion | Auto-rigging with preset animations | Useful for pets. | Low |
| [Default R15 animation IDs](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/list-of-the-default-r15-animation-ids/140425) | Stock animations | Reference list. | High |
| Blender | Modeling, renders, normals, PBR (physically based rendering) baking | Standard. Statistics overlay shows face counts. | High |

- Avoid AI modeling except for simple styles; good human modelers still beat it.
- [Blueprint-bench](https://andonlabs.com/evals/blueprint-bench-2) benchmarks 3D AI models; [nocapmocap](https://www.nocapmocap.com/) offers AI motion capture (low confidence).
