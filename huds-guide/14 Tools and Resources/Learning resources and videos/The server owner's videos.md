# The server owner's videos

Up: [[Learning resources and videos]] · Next: [[Other videos and channels]]

- Beginners are routinely told to watch all of them; most-cited topics are thumbnails, the under-16 restriction, keeping a game alive, and blue vs red ocean.
- The owner's favorite is the blue-ocean video on why no one plays your game.
- Following them reportedly lifted one game's stats to the 60th percentile or higher.
- A common top tip: optimize stats until all are green and above average.
- [Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@TizzyRBLX12); [Algorithm video](https://youtu.be/B3R1ybI3yzs): the algorithm weighs D2–7 and D8–28 retention, reportedly predicting D8–28 from D1.

- [Full playbook video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnsIL4zPCQg); [lean teams and project management](https://youtu.be/7tYPvXYlif8); [coaching video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NvN3NNpCRrg).
- [Gym-class games loop](https://youtu.be/ne-uS98wClw); [thumbnail walkthrough (Loom)](https://www.loom.com/share/d189d4c1d91f400e878eecb6560eed27); [one developer's approach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dUmh6GAvJ-E).
- Other titles: keeping a $131k/month game alive (live ops), improving stats fast, and monetization explained simply.
- Ads: run 16 credits/day for at least 3 days, until home-recommendation impressions surpass ad impressions.
