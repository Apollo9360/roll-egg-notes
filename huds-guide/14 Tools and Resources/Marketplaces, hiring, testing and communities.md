# Marketplaces, hiring, testing and communities

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Previous: [[Roblox official pages and docs]] · Next: [[Learning resources and videos]]

| Tool | What it's for | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hidden Devs ([discord.gg/hd](https://discord.gg/hd)) | Hiring, commissions, testers, scripter rate checks | Most-named hiring server; thumbnails about $10. Fine for one-off commissions; scams common. Avoid long-term deals and "investors" there. | High |
| RoDevs ([discord.gg/rodevs](https://discord.gg/rodevs)) | Hiring and commissions | Thumbnails about $7; builders often underpriced. | High |
| RSC, Roblox Studio Community ([discord.gg/robloxstudio](https://discord.gg/robloxstudio)) | Hiring, posting for work | Scripters should post a portfolio here and on Hidden Devs. | Medium |
| Twitter/X | Hiring, investors, rigged pets, spotting games early, contract templates | Some rate it above Hidden Devs. | Medium |
| QA Central ([discord.gg/qac](https://discord.gg/qac)) | Free or paid playtests, private sessions | Considered the best QA (quality assurance) server. | Medium |
| BuiltByBit, itch.io | UI kits, icons, brainrot models, character, anime and sports packs | Cheap, instant download. Skip BuiltByBit sellers with no reviews. | High |
| Fiverr | Thumbnails and commissions | Riddled with scammers. | Medium |
| creatorexchange.io, Rocquire | Game sale marketplaces and valuation reference | The owner has heard good things about Creator Exchange. | Medium |
| [Simple Bricks](https://simplebricks.com.au/games) | Buys, invests in or publishes games | Owns Clean the Leaves. Mass-sends acquisition requests; mostly considered legit. Takes about 50% for marketing. Verify links before sharing analytics. | Medium |
| [INDG / Indigo](https://www.indg.vc/#team) | Scouts and buys games | Buys 50% stakes and helps with PTR. Verify scouts via the team page. Deals close, but some call offers lowball. | Medium |
| Looped.gg, Nordic Studios, Misfits, DoBig | Acquisition and live ops studios | Nordic focuses on live ops. Little feedback. | Low |
| BloxPodium | Dev-funded weekly leaderboard competitions to bring in players | New; little feedback. | Low |
| Server help, help-your-peers and resources channels | Peer help, feedback, testers, curated links | Owner-recommended: help-your-peers for feedback; free community tools belong in resources. Hiring posts do not belong in help. | High |
