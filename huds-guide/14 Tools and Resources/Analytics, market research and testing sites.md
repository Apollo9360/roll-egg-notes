# Analytics, market research and testing sites

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Next: [[Thumbnail and AI image tools]]

| Tool | What it's for | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [qptr.io](https://qptr.io/) | Previews a thumbnail or icon in a mock home-page grid among high-CTR (click-through rate) thumbnails | Free and owner-approved. Gives no data, only shows whether yours stands out. Pls Donate stands out most, so compete with it. | High |
| [RoTrends](https://rotrends.com) | Trending and "top moving" games; other games' CCU (concurrent users), session length and revenue | Good for checking whether a competitor's CCU is stable, average playtime by genre, and how many games in a genre exceed 1k CCU. Revenue estimates are inaccurate and some games are undercounted. Hides games under 100k visits from top moving. A late-August closure report proved wrong; still used in October. | High |
| [creatorexchange.io](https://creatorexchange.io/feed) | Game stats, fantasies, title keyword research, thumbnail generator; also a game sale marketplace | Paid since late May; revenue data needs a pro plan of about $50. Good for finding untapped fantasies. Revenue estimates are very inaccurate, e.g. 50k Robux/day for a 6.5k CCU game was questioned. | High |
| RoAnalytics | Market research and idea validation | Usually paired with RoTrends before investing in a game. | Medium |
| RoMonitor, Rolimons, Roblox Charts | CCU history and chart positions | Common for tracking competitors over time. | Medium |
| [rebloxed.com](https://rebloxed.com/), Rotinder | Thumbnail rating and testing; rebloxed also turns thumbnails into icons | Accuracy unknown. | Low |
| GameAnalytics | External analytics | Avoids the Creator Dashboard's 24–48 hour delay. | Medium |
| Discord webhooks | Real-time logs of joins, leaves and session length | Another dashboard-delay workaround; AI can write the script. | Medium |
| [bloxg CPP calculator](https://bloxg.com/tools/cost-per-player-calculator) | Cost per player (CPP) for ad campaigns | Early on, click-to-play lags, so compute CPP from visits. | Medium |
| [Owner's game-prediction spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/14_Y-gfSULdGb02kbBAyml6M3-p309XV_Xn7r5qxmxT4/edit) | Notes on whether games would blow up and why | Useful for training your own judgement. | Medium |

- No public revenue estimator for Roblox games is accurate.
- QPTR means qualified play-through rate: the play-through rate (PTR) on home recommendations.
- Custom analytics plus an in-game feedback system early on beats waiting for dashboard data (low confidence).
