# Audio and music

| Tool | What it's for | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Creator Store audio](https://create.roblox.com/store/audio) | Purchase and hit SFX (sound effects) | Free but slow to search. Toolbox UI SFX are low quality. | Medium |
| [GameChestAudio](https://elements.envato.com/user/GameChestAudio), [Cute UI interact pack](https://epicstockmedia.com/product/cute-ui-interact-sound-effects-pack/) | Paid UI SFX | Recommended; the Epic Stock Media pack has previews. | Medium |
| Splice | SFX and music samples | About $5/month subscription. | Low |
| [DevForum 142-sound kit](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/massive-sound-kit-with-142-sounds-all-categorized-taking-suggestions-for-new-sound-categories/1617141) | Categorized free SFX | Recommended. | Medium |
| Pixabay, [freesound.org](https://freesound.org) | Free SFX | Edit with Audacity. | Medium |
| Suno AI, NCS (YouTube) | Background music | AI-generated and free options. | Medium |
