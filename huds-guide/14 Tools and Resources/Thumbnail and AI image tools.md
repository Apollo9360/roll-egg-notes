# Thumbnail and AI image tools

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Previous: [[Analytics, market research and testing sites]] · Next: [[AI coding and agent tools]]

| Tool | What it's for | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| RoThumbs (Rothumbz) | AI thumbnail generator trained on Roblox thumbnails | Most common choice. About 1 credit per thumbnail; 3 free credits; Pro about $13–15/month for 100 credits. A credit costs about 1/5 of a commissioned thumbnail. Reported CTRs of 4% over 2M impressions and 6%. Needs a Studio-built reference plus a style reference. Critics call it GPT image plus a prompt. | High |
| VizzBees ([vizzbees.com](https://vizzbees.com/)) | Swaps characters or settings on a screenshot | About $20/month, 2 free credits, card required. Widely seen as an overpriced GPT Image 2 wrapper. The owner called its outreach self-promotion disguised as help. | High |
| Magnific | Unlimited Nano Banana 2 images | About $40/month. Strong when Claude drives it through the Magnific MCP. | Medium |
| Nano Banana 2 / Gemini | AI image generation and edits | Free tier usable. Split opinions on thumbnails; some rate it above gpt-image-1. Good for UI drafts, not pixel-perfect. Makes reference images for 3D models. | High |
| ChatGPT / GPT Image 2 / Codex | Thumbnails and icons from composition references | Works after prompt iteration. Codex image generation is well regarded. Pairs well with Photopea. | High |
| [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) | Free AI images | Usable free option. | Medium |
| ForgeGUI | AI GUI and thumbnails | Considered bad, and the owner agrees: a wrapper over Gemini, Claude and GPT. One user still got a decent CTR. | High |
| PixelForge ([3D Icon Studio](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/103129928198990/PixelForge-3D-Icon-Studio)) | Thumbnail and icon renders in Studio | About $5; Blender is the free alternative. | Low |
| Canva | Mockups, layouts, exports | Standard for layout drafts; fallback for Figma exports. | High |
| Photopea, paint.net | Free image editors | Thumbnails or icons in about 15 minutes; outlined icons in 2–3 minutes in paint.net. | Medium |
| remove.bg, unwatermark.ai | Background and watermark removal | remove.bg is core to the free icon route. | Medium |
| Blender | Scene renders | Render in Blender, then composite in RoThumbs. | Medium |
| Fiverr | Commissioned thumbnails | Riddled with scammers. About $10 for 2 thumbnails plus 2 icons gave very low CTR. Good artists charge around £20 for 3–4. | Medium |

1. Mock the layout in Canva or Figma with in-game screenshots and [ui-resources.com](https://ui-resources.com/) assets.
2. Generate the final art with an AI tool, without text.
3. Add text, strokes and color correction in Figma or Photopea.
4. Check standout on qptr.io before uploading.

- One reported workflow: Claude on the Magnific MCP writes prompts from niche reference thumbnails, averaging 3–5% CTR per game.
- Free icon route: screenshot the item, remove the background, add a stroke in Photopea.
- Do not reuse the same ChatGPT prompt in RoThumbs, or you get the same thumbnail.
- Claude has no native image generation; use Gemini or ChatGPT.
- Most experienced developers see paid thumbnail sites as wrappers around Nano Banana 2 or GPT image, charging about 200% more.
- Watch free trials on paid thumbnail subscriptions; cancel before billing.
- Tools in development scrape Roblox charts daily for references and aim to predict CTR (low confidence).
