# Roblox official pages and docs

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Previous: [[Audio and music]] · Next: [[Marketplaces, hiring, testing and communities]]

The highly engaged player (HEP) threshold for all-ages reach is 250 as of October 12, 2026. Expedited review skips it for 50,000 Robux, refundable after roughly 90 days. The normal route still needs the 1,000 Robux fee or Roblox Plus after reaching 250.
