# Reference games and design inspiration

Up: [[Tools and Resources]] · Previous: [[Learning resources and videos]]

| Game | Pattern it illustrates | Community verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| [My Giant Sandwich](https://www.roblox.com/games/139546619723000), [My Animal Farm](https://www.roblox.com/games/116854324683100), [My Hungry Giant](https://www.roblox.com/games/112345685575988/My-Hungry-Giant) | Simple "my giant X" growth loops | Praised by the owner; clones followed quickly. | High |
| [Hellhole](https://www.roblox.com/games/84568117169118), [Dig Clean](https://www.roblox.com/games/83038462357724/Dig-Clean), [Jump for Animals](https://www.roblox.com/games/126870639873289/Jump-for-Animals) | Clear fantasies with tight loops | Owner-cited examples. | Medium |
| [+1 Skip a Lucky Block](https://www.roblox.com/games/103337226613298/1-Skip-A-Lucky-Block) | "+1 per second" twist on a trending genre | Owner-cited example. | Medium |
| [Sink a Boat](https://www.roblox.com/games/119212238836915), [GearBound](https://www.roblox.com/games/13753835236/GearBound) | Physics-destruction core hypothesis | Owner's own game and a predecessor sharing its idea. | Medium |
| [Escape With Loot](https://www.roblox.com/games/110398021993835/Escape-With-Loot) | Adapting a hit PC game (extraction shooter) to Roblox | Owner has a stake. | Medium |
| [Catch Airport Criminals](https://www.roblox.com/games/102905846904488/Catch-Airport-Criminals) | Fast build with AI and free models | Owner's test of a lean pipeline; mirrors an underserved Steam niche. | Medium |

- Study a game's loop, onboarding and shop rather than copying its theme.
- A twist on a saturated genre beats a direct clone (owner-recommended).
