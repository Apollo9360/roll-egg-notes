# Search vs home vs sorts

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Similar games and saturation]] · Next: [[Audience, regions and the 16+ gate]]

- Appearing in search is not the same as home recs.
- Check Analytics → Acquisition → Home Recommendations to see algorithmic traffic.
- The acquisition chart has separate lines for total, friends, search, home recommendation and other.
- The "People also join" section on your game page shows whether the algorithm placed you in the right category.
- PTR and QPTR come only from home-rec impressions. Ad and search plays do not count.
- A PTR of 0.00 during ads means the game is not in the algorithm yet.
- Zero qualified plays right after first home recs means data is still refreshing, or the first few hundred impressions missed.
- PTR is always lower than ad CTR. A 6.5% ad-CTR thumbnail has shown about 1.1% QPTR.
- A rough sim and tycoon 50th-percentile QPTR is about 1.2% (low confidence).
- Too many games with the same or similar name make search downgrade yours.
- The PTR and bounce-rate split applies to the Recommended For You sort.
- Up and Coming placement reportedly boosts the algorithm (low confidence).
- Charts can be restricted alongside Home and Search in a reduced-exposure notice.
- Release day of the week does not matter.

### Search and off-platform traffic

- Traffic is what reaches home recs, and it does not have to come from ads. The algorithm needs seed data.
- About 300 Discord visits before release have produced about 500 home impressions.
- Search alone has carried games to 1k–2k CCU, for example trend names kids search for.
- One trend game reached 500 HEP and about 250 CCU purely from search.
- Targeting keywords from games with 250M+ visits, rather than under 2M, has made a game pop off (low confidence).
- Searching off a big game's name can boost D1 and playtime. In a one-game genre, it can backfire as players expect a mod.
- Social traffic is probably the highest intent, but hard to track because it mixes into search.
- One viral TikTok cannot sustain thousands of CCU, but can feed the algorithm the stats it needs.
- Players arriving by search after TikTok count as HEP more often.
- Link and off-platform visits are reportedly about 20x less effective than ad or home traffic (low confidence).
- YouTube promotion can send the wrong audience unless the creator is huge. Big creators rarely cover games under 5–10k CCU.
- Playing your own game, asking friends or using bots does not help algorithm stats.

### Seven-day averaging

- Roblox calculates impressions from 7-day average stats. Bad 7-day averages mean fewer impressions.
- The dashboard defaults to a 7-day moving average. For new games, switch to Daily or set smoothing to None under Explore.
- Your own Studio and tester sessions inflate the 7-day view early on. PCR has read 15% on 7-day vs 2% daily.
- Stats right after first home recs look too high, then settle within a couple of days.
