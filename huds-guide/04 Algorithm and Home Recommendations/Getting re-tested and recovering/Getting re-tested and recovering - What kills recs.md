# Getting re-tested and recovering - What kills recs

Up: [[Getting re-tested and recovering]] · Previous: [[Getting re-tested and recovering - Fixing PTR]] · Next: [[Changes that are safe]]

- Breaking the game during a surge. A broken tutorial raises bounce rate and stops recs; a broken update has cut CCU by about 70%.
- A single glitch has dropped a game from 20k to 1.5k CCU within 2 weeks.
- Updating without a soft shutdown, kicking everyone at once, has wrecked a 2k CCU game's momentum.
- Recovery after a breakage is possible if stats recover.
- Privatizing to fix a bug has dropped algorithm CCU from about 30 to 5. Leave the game public and ship the fix fast.
- Going private shortly after launch, then public again with the same ads, gives noticeably worse results.
- Pausing ads even one day hurts D1 while ads still carry the game.
- Starting ads on a game already growing from home recs has crashed revenue and retention.
- A moderation warning while on recs has shifted the audience and turned stats red overnight (low confidence).
- Using another game's character in an icon has been blamed for about 2 months without recs (low confidence).
- If benchmarks are awful, such as 1st-percentile D1, Roblox will not push the game.
- A drop after switching to a "better" thumbnail is more often a game issue than the thumbnail (low confidence).
- Impressions sometimes keep falling for 3+ days after a big update, even with ads restarted (unexplained).
