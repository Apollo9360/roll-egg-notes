# Changes that are safe

Up: [[Getting re-tested and recovering]] · Previous: [[Getting re-tested and recovering - What kills recs]]

- Adding or removing thumbnails does not reset home recs.
- Keeping a winning thumbnail while swapping others does not hurt it.
- Roblox decides home impressions per thumbnail and rotates to others as they perform.
- Renaming after recs start is fine only if the new name is better. A rename has revived a flopped game.
- Names cannot be A/B tested in campaigns.
- Moving ownership from a personal account to a group does not affect search or recs.
- Changing a game's concept hurts its home recs but does not get it banned.
- Claims that dropping to 0 CCU, re-uploading a thumbnail or changing category resets learning are unconfirmed.
