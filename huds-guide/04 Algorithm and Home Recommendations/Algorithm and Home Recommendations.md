# Algorithm & Home Recommendations

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Once a game is in the algorithm, home recommendations (home recs) supply 90% or more of its players; ads at about 12–16 credits/day for 7–14 days mainly generate the data that earns them. First home recs usually arrive after 4–7 days of ads, once play-through rate (PTR), first-play bounce rate and early retention beat roughly the 50th percentile of similar games.
