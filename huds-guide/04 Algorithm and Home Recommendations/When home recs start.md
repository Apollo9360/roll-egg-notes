# When home recs start

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Why ads come first]] · Next: [[Test bursts, spikes and crashes]]

| Situation | Typical time to first home recs | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steady ads with decent stats | 4–7 days | 1 day to about 2 weeks | High |
| Meaningful home-rec volume ("hitting the algorithm") | About 2 weeks from first ad | 6–14 days; some 3–4 weeks | High |
| Very strong stats or 30+ credits/day | Under a week | 1–5 days | Medium |
| Weaker games | 10–14 days | Up to 3 weeks | Medium |
| Story horror games | About a week | Gets in easily | Low |
| 1k CCU even with 90th-percentile stats | About 3 weeks | Roblox rolls out slowly | Low |
| After ads end | 3–4 days later | Up to 1–4 weeks later | Medium |

- A typical pattern: first recs about 4 days into ads, then about 7 more days before they arrive in volume.
- Some developers have always needed 3–4 weeks to really hit the algorithm, while some games skyrocket.
- Home recs start once enough stats exist, not randomly.
- If the D1 seen during ads holds, home recs usually follow within the next week.
- Home recs drip in slowly at first, often 10–20 impressions a day.
- A few hundred home impressions is basically no algorithm yet. Real entry shows as a spike where home impressions dominate.
- A small home-rec test is about 200k impressions a week; a real push can reach 20M.
- Home recs can begin before the HEP gate is cleared, and while the game is still 16+.
- Home recs can arrive with stats below the 50th percentile, but they rarely continue past about 2 days.
- Good stats do not guarantee recs. All-green games stuck at 10–20 CCU are common, usually because of PTR or appeal.
- Once the game appears under the Home Recommendations acquisition source, it is in the algorithm.
- Before the engaged-player gate, some games got recs a week after release without ads. Now about 2 weeks of ads is typical.

### When to give up

- Recs within half a week signal a likely winner. About a week signals a middling game.
- No home recs after about 2 weeks of steady ads usually means a flop.
- About 21 days at 16 credits/day with no algorithm entry suggests a fundamental flaw. Scrap or rework it.
- Some games sat at 25–50 CCU until day 13–14, then jumped past 200. Give it the full 2 weeks.
- You usually know a game has flopped about a week after home recs start.
- By about 500k visits a game has been in the algorithm long enough to predict its trajectory.
