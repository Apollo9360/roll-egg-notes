# Algorithm changes and shadowban claims

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Beta Mode]] · Next: [[Estimates and projections (Algorithm)]]

Roblox changes the algorithm often and mostly silently. The direction since spring 2026 is consistent: away from clickbait, templates and short-session games, toward long-term retention and, later, monetization.

| Date (2026) | Change or report | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Before 2026 | CTR-only ranking was exploited by misleading thumbnails; Roblox moved to QPTR with a 5-minute FTUE window | Medium |
| Early 2025 era | Algorithm was predictable and D1-heavy; good QPTR alone pushed games | Medium |
| March–April | Changes hit brainrot and farm-style games, even some with 90th-percentile stats | Medium |
| Late May | 16+ visibility gate launched with a 500 HEP threshold | High |
| June 15 | Recommended For You update: long-term retention (days 2–7 and 8–28), predictive D28 scoring, QPTR split into PTR and first-play bounce rate, co-play rewarded | High |
| June 15–19 | Many games swung sharply; obby and short-session games fell; Roblox webinar explained the update on June 19 | High |
| June–July | Templates, clickbait thumbnails and "test" or age-test games stopped working | High |
| After July 4 | Some games' impressions dropped sharply while some dead games got boosts | Low |
| Around July 10 | Engaged-player requirement reportedly relaxed | Low |
| About July 30 | Community saw the D28 and D30 emphasis take effect; brainrot games built for the 7-day model lost recs | High |
| August 3 | Home recs cut abruptly for "clean a ___" playtime-farm games, many falling from about 7k to 600 CCU | High |
| August 6 | [Boost your discovery](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042) post: retention and monetization emphasized; PTR not mentioned | High |
| Mid-August | Spike pushed to D7 cohorts; mainly high-PCR games kept it | Low |
| August 19–20 | HEP threshold dropped from 500 to 250 | High |
| About August 22 | Games with poor D30 reportedly recommended less | Low |
| August 24 | Roblox reportedly running algorithm tests, explaining big swings | Low |
| September 5 | Roblox outage and data issue; large games lost CCU and small games swung | High |
| Mid-September | Expedited-review fee cut from 100,000 to 50,000 Robux | High |
| September 20–23 | New games' home impressions dropped sharply; many reported dips | Medium |
| September 25 onward | Recs yo-yoing; suspected weekly silent changes and a heavy revamp | Medium |
| Week of September 28 | Home-rec impressions reported plummeting within minutes | Low |
| November (announced) | HEP threshold expected to drop to 100; not yet in effect | High |

- The June update favors high-quality, unique games and hurts templates and clones (owner-recommended reading).
- Many games dropped after June 15, then recovered or grew 10x. Let things stabilize before reacting.
- The August 6 change likely followed top-earning games such as Gakuran: retention plus monetization.
- With silent changes, the only reliable response is making a good game and improving stats (owner-recommended).
- Front-page games may churn again as D28 metrics reach them.
- The new system makes about 500 CCU easier to hold and 10k+ harder to reach, while unique games hit 20k more often (low confidence).
- Brainrot is not formally shadowbanned. It lost recs because of good PTR but weak long-term stats (disputed).
- Few brainrot games launched in July gained traction after the changes; similar treatment is expected for egg-game waves.
- Rumors that "brainrot" in the title lowers visits are unverified.
- One-shot AI-built games reportedly vanish from recs within a day.
- Troll tower games have been marked undiscoverable with ads cut off and no message.
- Crypto-themed games are unlikely to be pushed (low confidence).
- Shadowbans from friends' Continue rows, Discord dislike-bombing or repeated buggy launches are claimed but unproven.
- Legacy games may receive a set share of impressions, explaining sudden revivals (low confidence).
