# What the algorithm ranks on - Playtime and monetization

Up: [[What the algorithm ranks on]] · Previous: [[Retention and the 28-day window]] · Next: [[Entry gates and position]]

- Above about 12 minutes average, retention tends to follow. About 8–10 minutes is often cited as the entry level.
- Mid stats still get some recs; games averaging 5–10 minutes have reached the homepage briefly.
- Some genres hold recs with short sessions; a tower obby sits in recs at about 2 minutes average.
- After August 6, solid retention without solid monetization reportedly earns a weaker push (low confidence).
- With good playtime but very low conversion, fixing conversion first has unlocked bigger pushes (low confidence).
- A free-to-play game needs other stats near the 90th percentile to compensate (low confidence).
- High-RPV (revenue per visit) games, such as Blade Ball at 15+ RPV, appear to keep long-term recs.
- Claims that high PCR with ARPPU under the 50th percentile blocks pushes are unproven (disputed).
- Many advise ignoring revenue stats until home recs start, then fixing them.
- A claimed 70% like-ratio trigger is unsupported.
