# Entry gates and position

Up: [[What the algorithm ranks on]] · Previous: [[What the algorithm ranks on - Playtime and monetization]]

- A common mental model treats ranking as a series of doors. Door 1 checks playtime and session time; door 2 checks retention.
- Failing a door stops a game from advancing.
- A minority think it is one combined evaluation rather than separate gates (disputed).
- Roblox keeps an internal rating for search and home recs. Reliable stats, with both D1 and D7 high, raise it.
- Beating roughly the 50th genre percentile gets a game in. Other stats raise its position within home recs.
- There is no fixed threshold. Roblox continuously tests games and sets distribution relative to competing games.
- Retention shape matters more than the D1 peak:
    - About 20% D1 with poor D7 gives a small spike.
    - About 15% D1 with good D7 gives a bigger spike.
    - When retention does not hold, the game is removed from recs.
- Roblox has a fixed number of daily eyeballs. It shows them to games that yield the highest return.
