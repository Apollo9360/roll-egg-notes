# Retention and the 28-day window

Up: [[What the algorithm ranks on]] · Next: [[What the algorithm ranks on - Playtime and monetization]]

- The June 15 update values long-term retention over days 2–7 and days 8–28. See the [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575).
- Roblox does not wait 28 days. A predictive model scores D28 outcomes early from D1 behavior such as playtime and session frequency.
- A good 7-day game still gets recs; games strong at 30 days are prioritized.
- Some believe the big push waits for real 30-day data, or that games get dropped when D28 is calculated (low confidence).
- Once retention stats exist, you must sustain them or drop out of recs. Stable D1 avoids recs yo-yoing.
- D1 below the 50th percentile looks bad to the algorithm.
- Whether D1 or D7 matters more is debated. Most recent reports lean toward D7 and longer windows.
- Sticky loops, steady content and weekly updates favor long-term retention (owner-recommended).
- Obby-style, short-session and playtime-farm games lost the most from the D28 emphasis.
