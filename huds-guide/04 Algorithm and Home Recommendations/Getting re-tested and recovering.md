# Getting re-tested and recovering

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Test bursts, spikes and crashes]] · Next: [[When to stop ads]]

- To earn a bigger test, beat the stats of the previous test. Home recs pick up when retention and playtime improve.
- Changes take about 2 days to affect the algorithm.
- The algorithm uses a 7-day rolling window, so old bad stats stop mattering once the game is fixed.
- The model keeps learning; old stats do not permanently stain a game.
- Dormant games can re-enter testing after new plays from ads, Discord, TikTok, YouTube, direct links or friends.
- A revamp plus ads works, but returning players carry their bad first impression.
- A bought dead game has been revived with an update for a 10x return.
- If home recs fall with no new content, fix stats. If they are not fixed within a few days, move on.
- Stagnating in recs for over a week usually means the game itself needs improving.
- To grow from home recs toward 1k CCU, ship frequent patches and mini updates based on educated guesses (owner-recommended).
- Updates matter more as CCU grows. Biweekly updates bring players back.
- Without updates, benchmark stats can decay, for example playtime falling from 24 to 16 minutes over 3 weeks.
- Several games blew up after about 12 days of poor stats, including Defend Your Treehouse.
- Old, un-updated games sometimes spike without explanation, for example 200 to 4k CCU overnight.
- Late home-rec spikes happen, such as 15k impressions a month after ads stopped.
