# What does not work

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Algorithm)]]

- Spending more to buy home recs. Budget speeds entry, but retention and PTR decide the outcome.
- Some argue 5 credits/day with good stats beats big budgets; most still run 12–16/day for 7–14 days.
- Many short campaigns. Learned audiences do not carry over between campaigns.
- Turning ads off before home impressions catch up with ad impressions.
- Waiting for organic growth with no seed traffic. Expect almost no impressions.
- Cloning a leader 1:1, reposting a used template or copying its thumbnail. The top game is shown first.
- Faking your genre to dodge benchmarks.
- Privatizing to patch bugs, or relaunching after going private.
- Judging early stats on the 7-day average.
- Panic-replacing thumbnails based on a handful of qualified plays.
- Skipping the dedicated home-rec thumbnail slot.
- Clickbait thumbnails. High CTR with high bounce rate is now punished.
- Chasing like ratio; it does not affect ranking.
- Hunting for a "small window" to exploit; testing is continuous.
- Paying the 50,000 Robux expedited-review fee purely for discovery. A good game reaches the gate through ads.
- Relying on one very good stat to carry a game (low confidence).
- Re-uploading a game to refresh the algorithm after a bugged test (low confidence).
- Reviving a 0-CCU game from years earlier with event subscribers is untested.
