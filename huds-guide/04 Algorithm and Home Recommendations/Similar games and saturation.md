# Similar games and saturation

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[When to stop ads]] · Next: [[Search vs home vs sorts]]

- Past a point, a game is compared against similar experiences, not the whole genre. Beat their stats to keep impressions.
- The benchmark is experiences with similar players. A Steal a Leaf game competes with Steal a Car and Steal an Egg.
- The algorithm acts like a shelf. If competitors improve, your CCU falls even with unchanged stats.
- It is a winner-takes-all economy. The 99th-percentile games in a genre take most of the recs.
- If similar games already serve those players, Roblox has little reason to push yours, even with good stats.
- Bigger games in a niche are shown first. Unless you lead the genre, your packaging must be clearly distinct.
- You cannot overtake a dominant game in an established niche. Find an unsaturated niche or a real twist.
- Execution plus demand plus low supply is the strongest combination (owner-recommended).
- Demand is channeled, not created. Low demand with high supply is playing on hard mode.
- A 1:1 copy is assumed to perform far worse than the original (blue ocean vs red ocean thinking).
- Reposted templates rarely hit the algorithm now, because players have already played many like them.
- One 1:1 clone still reached 400 CCU two days after release, so exceptions exist.
- A near-identical name and mechanic to a large game typically stalls around 10 CCU. Move on.
- Copies are fine if quality is equal or higher and there is a twist.
- Mass-producing copies, such as 50 +1 games, earned no recs despite good stats.
- Templates are not detected as reused content.
- Copy-pasted places, 1:1 metadata matches or near-identical icons can trigger reduced exposure on Home and Search.
- Duplicate metadata mainly matters for active duplicates; privated duplicates have not hurt a large game.
- Reduced exposure across Home, Search and Charts can also arrive months after publishing with no reason given.
- Roblox's [low-quality content notice](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improving-user-experience-by-reducing-exposure-of-low-quality-content/3299107) targets low-effort copies, not trend games with a real twist.
- Every game is capped by its fantasy. The same stats can yield 50x the CCU with broader appeal.
- Outfit and shopping games inherently get very low QPTR and rarely reach the homepage.
- Players in the +1 genre get recommended other +1 games regardless of concept.
- Do not fake or blank your genre to dodge weak benchmarks. Home recs will target the wrong players.
- Genre can only be changed once every 3 months. Forgetting to set it has stalled recs for about a week.
- Repurposing an old place for a different genre confuses targeting (disputed).
- Sharing a name with a small game released 1–2 months earlier is considered safe.
