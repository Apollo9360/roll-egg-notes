# Estimates and projections (Algorithm)

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Algorithm changes and shadowban claims]] · Next: [[What does not work]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported ranges, not reported facts. They assume 1 ad credit is about 1 US dollar.

- Estimate: ad cost to first home recs is about $50–110 (basis: 12–16 credits/day for 4–7 days).
- Estimate: ad cost to real algorithm entry is about $170–225 (basis: 12–16 credits/day for about 14 days).
- Estimate: the flop cutoff costs about $250–340 (basis: 12–16 credits/day for 21 days with no entry).
- Estimate: 32 credits/day for 5–7 days costs $160–225, about the same total as 16/day for 2 weeks, but reaches recs sooner.
- Estimate: the 50,000 Robux expedited fee is worth about $190 at the developer exchange rate (basis: about $0.0038 per Robux). That is comparable to a full 2-week ad run.
- Estimate: after the November drop to 100 HEP, reaching the gate should take roughly 40% of today's engaged-player count and time (basis: 100 ÷ 250, assuming a steady HEP rate).
- Estimate: home-rec CCU ≈ hourly home impressions × PTR × average session minutes ÷ 60 (basis: steady-state arrivals times time in game).
    - At 10k hourly impressions, 2% PTR and 20-minute sessions, that gives about 65 CCU.
    - The reported 300 CCU on 10k hourly impressions implies about 9% PTR at 20 minutes, which fits the "strong play-through" reading.
- Estimate: a 200k-impression test week at 1.2% PTR yields about 2,400 home-rec plays; at 2% it yields about 4,000.
- Estimate: at a real push of 20M weekly impressions, each 0.1-point PTR change is about 20,000 plays a week (basis: 20M × 0.001).
- Estimate: about 20–25k home impressions per thumbnail are needed to tell 1.2% from 1.5% PTR reliably (basis: two-proportion test at 95% confidence, 80% power). This matches the 25k-impression stop heuristic.
- Estimate: about 2,500 impressions per thumbnail can separate 1% from 2% PTR (basis: same test, larger gap).
- Estimate: home-rec traffic is worth about 5 times ad traffic per impression (basis: 10k home ≈ 50k ad impressions).
- Estimate: a sustained all-ages unlock typically lands near 1–2x prior CCU after the 3–4x first-day spike fades (basis: reports from -50% to 6x, most showing little lasting change).
- Estimate: a game reaching about 50 CCU on home recs has passed roughly 25k–40k home impressions (interpolated between 10+ CCU and 40k impressions with 50 home players).
