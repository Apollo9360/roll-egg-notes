# Audience, regions and the 16+ gate

Up: [[Algorithm and Home Recommendations]] · Previous: [[Search vs home vs sorts]] · Next: [[Beta Mode]]

- Roblox tests the game in different countries and audiences and pushes harder where it succeeds.
- You cannot force a richer demographic. The algorithm serves whoever likes the game most.
- Home recs can shift the mix, for example from Germany and the UK to Brazil and Indonesia, which lowers ARPDAU.
- When a game is pushed to a better-fitting demographic, D1 can rise quickly.
- Publisher location likely has negligible effect (low confidence).
- Ideal country mix: the top two are high-monetization tier-1 countries, the third drives CCU.
- About 40% tier-1 audience is good shape. A Brazil-heavy game needs about 300–400 CCU to earn comparably.
- Brazil still has value through its large mobile player base.
- Blocking countries via Regional Access is untested. Most advise leaving it alone.
- No India or Indonesia players suggests an underpopulated game. Indonesian players favor brainrot games.
- Monster and horror themes bring a poorly monetizing demographic; anime themes monetize better.
- The core audience has aged with the platform, likely now mostly 12+.
- Mature-audience games may retain better, which the algorithm rewards (low confidence).
- Home recs go to everyone in the game's maturity label, regardless of the ad's 13+ or 16+ targeting.
- Games restricted to 13+ reportedly lose heavily in the algorithm (low confidence).
- After the June 2026 change, home recs sometimes reached the wrong audience. PTR collapsed and "not interested" rose 3–5x.

### HEP gate as it affects discovery

- New games are visible only to verified 16+ users, even in ads, until they reach the HEP threshold.
- The threshold was 500 HEP at launch in late May 2026 and 250 from about August 19 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/highly-engaged-player-threshold-drops-to-250/4820164)).
- A drop to 100 HEP in November 2026 is announced and widely reported, but not yet in effect.
- After reaching 250 HEP, the normal route still needs the 1,000 Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus.
- An expedited review skips the HEP requirement for 50,000 Robux. It was 100,000 until about mid-September.
- The fee is reportedly refundable after roughly 90 days; reported conditions vary.
- A 25,000 Robux fee is an unverified rumor.
- 16+ games can hit the algorithm, but recs reach only 16+ users and CTR is generally lower.
- A youth-targeted game has not really had an algorithm chance before clearing the gate.
- Unlocking all ages usually brings a 3–4x CCU and impression spike for about a day.
- Average stats usually stay similar after unlock; one game's playtime rose from 7.5 to 9.5 minutes.
- Sustained results vary from about 6x growth to a drop of over half. Many saw little lasting change.
- Clearing HEP is separate from truly entering the algorithm. Some games stayed near 20 CCU after reaching 250.
- Cash-grab games draw "16+" players who are mostly young kids on a parent's ID.
- A game built for real 16+ players has reached nearly 1k CCU from ads alone.
- See the separate gates tab for full requirements.
