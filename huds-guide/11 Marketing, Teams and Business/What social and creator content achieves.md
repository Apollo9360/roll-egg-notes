# What social and creator content achieves

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Off-platform marketing versus ads]] · Next: [[Short-form video playbook]]

DAU is daily active users.

| Channel | Typical outcome | Range seen | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viral TikTok (2–3M views) | Pushes the game into home recommendations | 200 to 1.5k CCU; 3M day-1 views to ~2k CCU; one game 100–150 to a 6k peak | High |
| Ordinary TikTok posts | Little or no traffic | Most developers get 100–200 views per post | High |
| High-like TikTok that fails | Few players despite likes | 250k–700k likes left games at 500–2k CCU | High |
| Pre-launch TikTok rollout | Strong launch day | About 2k CCU on day 1 | Medium |
| Short video with pinned link | Can carry a launch | 400k–600k views gave about 1.4–1.5k CCU | Medium |
| YouTuber pickup | Large but often brief spike | A few hundred CCU to 10k+ after a ~350k-average-view creator | Medium |
| YouTube Shorts on own channel | Steady views, weak clicks | 30k+ views when formatted well; high impressions, tiny click-through | Medium |
| TikTok Live with gift-to-game effects | Viewer-driven spikes | A keyboard-escape game hit its first 10k CCU using TikFinity | Medium |
| Small streamer promotion | Modest boost | About 200 CCU | Low |
| Cross-promotion from your own game | Strong seeding without ads | A 100K+ DAU game seeded a new game to 4,000 DAU | Medium |
| Free limited UGC (user-generated content) giveaway | Sustained traffic | A 1,000-stock item (~100k Robux) held 700–2.5k CCU for a month | Low |
| Facebook | Occasionally grows a game | One reported case | Low |

- Viral games often ride TikTok trends, such as Grow a Garden-style games and the locust trend.
- A large murder-mystery hit is attributed to YouTube plus a dedicated community.
- An anime fighting game's popularity is attributed to social media and word of mouth.
- Non-English YouTubers with big followings still boost a game when they play it.
- A creator boost to a weak game brings players briefly, then it flops anyway.
- A novelty campaign brought 60k visits but no revenue (low confidence).
