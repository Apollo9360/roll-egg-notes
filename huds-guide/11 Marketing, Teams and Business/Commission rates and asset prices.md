# Commission rates and asset prices

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Building a team and finding talent]] · Next: [[Revenue shares, fixed pay and AI]]

Robux prices convert at roughly $3.8 per 1,000 Robux at the DevEx rate.

| Role | Typical range | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Scripter, small game | $250–800 per project; about 40k Robux for a simple game | High |
| Scripter, complex or top-tier | 150k+ Robux; about $2.5k; $30–40 per hour | Medium |
| Full-time developer | $3k–10k per month | Medium |
| Builder, map | $100–400 per map; 5k–15k Robux; about $1k for a large simulator map | Medium |
| UI artist, per frame | $5–30 typical; $60 common for basic frames; $100–125 for top quality | High |
| UI for a whole simple game | About $200–400 | Medium |
| Pre-made UI kit | $10–20 for about 8 frames | High |
| Thumbnail artist | 2k–10k Robux; about $40 typical | Medium |
| Icon graphics (GFX) artist | About 7k Robux with VFX, outline and arrow | Low |
| VFX artist | $10–20 per effect; about 5k Robux per emitter; some charge 30k Robux minimum | Medium |
| Animator | 200–2k Robux per animation; about $500 for a 72-animation pack | Medium |
| 3D model, simple | $10–20 per model; 4k–6k Robux | High |
| 3D model, character or custom | $50–200; 45k Robux for high quality | Medium |
| Model packs | $10–50; 40k–50k Robux for 200–640 rigged animals | Medium |
| Pets and eggs set | About $200+ | Low |
| Sound designer | About $300 | Low |
| Template | About $500 from a legitimate seller | Low |
| Game review or consultation | About $200 per session | Medium |
| Discord management | 10k–20k Robux | Low |
| Floor | Under about 500 Robux per task gets ghosted | Medium |

- Price commissions as a base fee plus a set amount per extra task.
- If a commission runs more than 25% over the agreed time, move on.
- Builders are quoted by area, e.g. 80x80 studs; 1 baseplate square is 4x4 studs.
- The [Studs UI Pack](https://builtbybit.com/resources/studs-ui-pack-2.119154/) costs about $15. A pre-made kit beats building UI yourself.
- UI artists can add depth with a duplicate layer offset -4x/-4y behind the main frame.
- Most-requested model commissions are low poly: trees, chests and potions.
- BuiltByBit sells cheap brainrot, anime, soccer and pet packs. Skip sellers with no reviews.
- Buy templates only from reputable sellers. Random templates are often stolen (DMCA, Digital Millennium Copyright Act, risk) or backdoored.
- Outsource repetitive asset work, such as 100 fish models, or generate it with Blender plus AI.
- Top freelancers earn well: a busy UI artist reported about 1M Robux a month; scripting for others can pay about $8k a month.
