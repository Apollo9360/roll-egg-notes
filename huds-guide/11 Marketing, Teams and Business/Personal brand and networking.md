# Personal brand and networking

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Discord and Roblox group communities]] · Next: [[Groups for ownership, payouts and selling]]

- A YouTube or Twitter presence is the strongest inbound funnel for hiring, investors and deal flow (owner-recommended).
- Before having a channel, the server owner was ghosted and scammed in hiring. Afterwards, developers reached out first.
- Post what you learn, even if you look inexperienced (owner-recommended).
- Twitter/X is a separate Roblox developer world worth joining. Many top developers stay quiet and just lurk.
- Basic copywriting helps titles. When second to market, make a bolder or more specific claim than the leader.
- A personal brand needs consistency: the same name, avatar and look on every platform.
- Cold DMs work very rarely, roughly 1 in 1,000.
- Pay for education through coaching or consulting. Some top developers sell one-on-one calls.
- Business-focused dev communities grow because few servers cover game creation as a business.
