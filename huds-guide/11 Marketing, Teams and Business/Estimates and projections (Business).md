# Estimates and projections (Business)

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Scams and safe deals]]

All figures below are estimates derived from reported data, not reported facts.

### Revenue and valuation

- Estimate: games earn about 10–40 Robux a day per CCU, with outliers from 5 to 130 (basis: eight reported CCU and daily-revenue pairs).
- Estimate: a 250 CCU game earns about 2.5k–10k Robux a day, or $10–40 at DevEx (basis: interpolation of the per-CCU rate).
- Estimate: a revenue-multiple valuation is about daily Robux × 90–100 × $0.0038 (basis: 3-month and 100x-daily rules).
    - 10k Robux a day gives about $3.4k–3.8k; 25k Robux a day gives about $8.5k–9.5k.
- Estimate: growing games sell for 2–5x that revenue figure (basis: a 4k Robux/day game sold for $3.5k, about 2.5x).
- Estimate: implied sale value runs about $20–100 per CCU for small games (basis: $5k at 50 CCU; ~$20k at 1k CCU).
    - Interpolated: about $8k–15k at 250 CCU and $10k–25k at 500 CCU.
    - At 25k CCU, about $40 per CCU; the owner's $1k-per-CCU floor sits 10–50x above market.

### Investor terms

- Estimate: $150–500 of ads for 15–25% implies a valuation of only $1k–3.3k (basis: investment ÷ share).
    - That is below typical sale values once a game has 50+ CCU, so ad-for-equity is costly for games with traction.
- Estimate: an investor paying $500 for 15% breaks even after about $3.3k of developer revenue, about 87 days at 10k Robux a day.
- Estimate: a 20%-until-2.5x deal on $500 ends after about $6.25k of developer revenue (basis: $1,250 ÷ 20%).
- Estimate: 16 credits a day for 14 days totals about 224 credits for 10% (basis: reported deal terms).

### Budgets and commissions

- Estimate: a lean AI-first game costs about $400–600 (basis: AI $100–150, ads $200, contingency $100, UI and model packs ~$70).
- Estimate: a fully commissioned small team costs about $2k before ads (basis: builder $400, UI $300, modeler $270, animator/VFX $400, scripter $500–800).
- Estimate: custom UI for 15–30 frames costs about $300–900 at $20–30 a frame.
- Estimate: a busy UI artist at 60 frames × 15k Robux grosses about 900k Robux, roughly $3.4k a month at DevEx.

### Off-platform conversion

- Estimate: viral videos convert at best to about 0.5–2 CCU per 1,000 views (basis: 2M views to +1.3k; 3M to ~2k; 600k to ~1.4k).
    - A typical 1M-view video likely adds a few hundred CCU or less, given many high-view failures.
- Estimate: a 50k-like TikTok implies roughly 500k views (basis: about 1 like per 10 views on viral posts).
- Estimate: Discord members convert to launch CCU at 0.004–0.3 (basis: 13k to 47; 100k to 30k).
- Estimate: group announcements reach about 1% of members, so 100k members reach about 1k players.

### All-ages threshold

- Estimate: the 50,000 Robux expedited fee is worth about $190 at DevEx, often less than a week of ads (basis: $0.0038 per Robux).
    - The fee is reportedly refundable after about 90 days; conditions vary.
- Estimate: at the announced 100 HEP threshold, social-only reach needs about 4k subscribers or 200 loyal fans.
    - Basis: linear scaling from 10k subscribers and 500 fans at 250 HEP.
- The 1,000 Robux publishing fee or Roblox Plus is still required on the normal route after reaching 250 HEP.
