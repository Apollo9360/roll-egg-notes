# Building a team and finding talent

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Groups for ownership, payouts and selling]] · Next: [[Commission rates and asset prices]]

- Lean teams work best (owner-recommended). A typical core is a producer, one programmer and a builder who can model, with VFX contracted.
- Salaried long-term teams are hard and risky. Running any team is one of the hardest things on the platform.
- Hire T-shaped people: deep in one skill and broad elsewhere.
- Mindset beats raw skill. Drop developers with a fixed mindset.
- Hire for proactive, self-managing, communicative and creative people.
- A paid trial task is the best filter, because it shows hunger (owner-recommended).
- Hire fast, fire faster. Set standards immediately and use contracts to hold people accountable.
- One bad team member can stall a whole release.
- Check references before partnering.
- Order of hiring: builds, models and UI first, scripter last. Placeholders break when the hierarchy changes.
- Models are often the bottleneck when everyone is hired at once.
- Every top game has a real scripter. Do not go cheap on scripters, and hire programmers rather than pure AI users.
- Game design is the highest-paid skill. A good PM (project manager) also does design and gives real direction.
- Credit every developer in the game.

### Where to hire

- Hiring is a volume game. Build an inbound funnel through YouTube and Twitter (owner-recommended).
- Hiring servers: [HiddenDevs](https://discord.gg/hd), [RoDevs](https://discord.gg/rodevs) and [Roblox Studio](https://discord.gg/robloxstudio). Talent Hub, Upwork and GameTank are also used.
- HiddenDevs and Talent Hub suit one-off commissions and testers. Both are full of beginners, scams and lowball posts.
- Getting ripped off on a big hiring server is described as a rite of passage. Avoid long-term deals there.
- Fiverr is riddled with scammers.
- Connections are the best way to hire. Friends of friends and creators' bios in dev communities work well.
- Underpriced talent can be found in hiring-server building chats.
- Some rate Eastern European and Middle Eastern developers highly.
- Hiring, scouting and buying games in this Discord are banned.
- Specify scope precisely. A VFX-only commission delivers VFX without the scripting to trigger it.
- Live-ops developers can be poached from successful games in a similar genre.

### Testers

- [QA Central](https://discord.gg/qac) organizes free or paid playtests and is the most recommended option.
- Testers cost about 10–100 Robux each; hosting a playtest costs about 1k Robux.
- Pay testers by bug severity, and direct them to likely weak areas.

### Getting hired yourself

- Build a portfolio with past work and a small code sample. Post it in Roblox Studio Community and HiddenDevs.
- Modelers should show renders or videos of models in action, not neutral grey renders, and a site with contact info.
- Scripting jobs increasingly require having worked on a game that did well.
- Take small projects, accept lower prices at first, and post about yourself on Discord, Reddit, X and the forum.
- Beginners can offer free or paid intern work to an experienced developer to learn (owner-recommended).
- An example portfolio: [UjRobloxPortfolio](https://ujsain.github.io/UjRobloxPortfolio/).
- Commissions are possible from age 12 and can fund ads. Take 25–50% upfront.
- Take a percentage instead of a flat fee when you believe in the game. Flat-fee scripts have later earned millions.
