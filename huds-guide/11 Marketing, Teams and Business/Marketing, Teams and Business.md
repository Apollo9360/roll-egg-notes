# Marketing, Teams & Business

Up: [[Hud's Guide To Roblox Success]]

Roblox ads remain the main growth channel. A single 2–3M-view TikTok can lift a game to about 1.5–2k CCU (concurrent users), but most videos convert poorly. Small games (50–700 CCU) typically draw $2k–5k for about 30%, most unsolicited offers are lowballs, and selling early is the most common regret.
