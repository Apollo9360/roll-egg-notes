# Groups for ownership, payouts and selling

Up: [[Marketing, Teams and Business]] · Previous: [[Personal brand and networking]] · Next: [[Building a team and finding talent]]

- Release under a group, not your profile. Creating a group costs about 100 Robux.
- Groups let you pay people through group payouts and send announcements.
- New members wait before receiving payouts: usually 10–14 days, sometimes 3 days, up to a month on a new alt.
- Payouts that do not divide evenly stay in the group. Many teams calculate revenue and pay monthly.
- Use a separate group per game. Games at 2–4k+ CCU especially should have their own group.
    - When selling, the whole group transfers, so you avoid handing over other games.
    - A group ban or restriction takes down every game in it.
- Withdraw accumulated group Robux before selling a group.
- Multi-person teams use a shared holder account. Solo developers should keep an alt in case of moderation.
- Paying contractors through a gamepass in your own game is common but disputed. A September ban wave reportedly hit senders and receivers.
- When paying in Robux, covering the 30% marketplace fee is standard.
- Paying a real developer abroad is fine. Having a US person cash out through DevEx (Developer Exchange) for you is illegal.
- Selling Robux or group funds for USD off-platform is widely seen as suspicious and risky.
- Holding large Robux balances carries false-ban risk.
