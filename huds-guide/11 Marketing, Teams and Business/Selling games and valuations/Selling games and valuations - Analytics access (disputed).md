# Selling games and valuations - Analytics access (disputed)

Up: [[Selling games and valuations]] · Previous: [[Acquisition studios and scouts]]

- Reviewing stats before an offer is industry standard. Studios ask you to rank an analytics account in your group.
- Fake buyers sometimes only want your data. Check reputation, Twitter presence, past work and mutual contacts.
- Some advise screenshots instead of a group rank. Others consider read-only access safe.
