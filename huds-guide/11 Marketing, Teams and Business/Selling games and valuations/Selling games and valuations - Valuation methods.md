# Selling games and valuations - Valuation methods

Up: [[Selling games and valuations]] · Next: [[Acquisition studios and scouts]]

- Most offers are a multiple of recent revenue. Expect at least 2–3 months of revenue.
- Simulators and low-poly games are valued near 100x daily revenue, or about 1 month when they die fast.
- One studio formula: average developer-share revenue over 3 months, times 8 (low confidence).
- A 9-month payback is bad for sellers, because the algorithm could change twice in that span.
- Others estimate genre ARPDAU times expected players.
- The server owner half-jokingly sets a floor of $1k per CCU and urges sellers to raise asking prices.

| Game state | Typical offer or value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 CCU with potential | About $1k; studios occasionally buy | Low |
| 20–50 CCU | $1.2k–5k; up to ~$15k with top-percentile stats | Medium |
| 100–150 CCU | $3.5k–18k; about $1.5k–3.5k for 30% | Medium |
| 200–300 CCU | $2k–13k; 50% for about $5k; one $35k valuation | Medium |
| 500–700 CCU | $5k–25k; $50k claimed | Low |
| About 1k CCU | About $20k typical; lowballs $1k–11k; $70k with 50-minute playtime | High |
| 1.5k–2k CCU | $10k–20k | Medium |
| About 5k CCU peak | About $50k | Low |
| 10k CCU with good monetization | $250k–500k | Low |
| About 25k CCU | $250k–1.5M | Medium |
| 100k–300k CCU | $5M–10M+ | Low |
| About $5k a month revenue | At least $10k–15k | Medium |
