# Acquisition studios and scouts

Up: [[Selling games and valuations]] · Previous: [[Selling games and valuations - Valuation methods]] · Next: [[Selling games and valuations - Analytics access (disputed)]]

- About 90–95% of acquisition studios are not worth it (owner-recommended view). Many hold only tiny brainrot games.
- Studios spray offers, prey on young developers, buy broken one-hit games and rarely help game design.
- Scouts DM within hours of first ads, or as low as 5–20 CCU. Many are paid per submission and per sale.
- A popular game gets about 10 scout DMs a day, roughly 90% lowballs.
- They lowball by claiming the stats are weak and the game will die. Some copy an easy game instead.
- Ask a studio to show a high-CCU game it owns in your genre. Get the CEO to confirm a deal and verify their Discord UID (user ID).
- Named buyers: FRG, Misfits, Indigo, DoBig, Basket, Looped, Empire Studio, Vantrex, Spong and Xolbor.
- Indigo ([indg.vc](https://www.indg.vc/#team)) is legitimate but known for lowballing. Its site has a page to verify scouts.
- Indigo often offers about $1.5k plus PTR thumbnails for 30%, and sometimes does one update then leaves.
- Misfits reportedly targets games with 5k+ CCU potential. One acquisition supplied only AI thumbnails.
- SimpleBricks (simplebricks.com.au) is real and sends many offers, sometimes asking 50% for promotion.
- Developers praised Tencells Studio and Twin Atlas for useful evaluations (low confidence).
