# Estimates and projections (Debates)

Up: [[Debates, Myths and FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ]] · Next: [[Community, tools and practices]]

All figures below are derived estimates, not reported facts. DevEx is taken as about $0.0038 per Robux.

### Reaching the HEP threshold

- Estimate: reaching 250 HEP costs about $50–130 in Plays ads (basis: 16 credits/day for the reported 3–8 days).
- Estimate: HEP accrue at roughly 30–80 per day on 16 credits/day (basis: reports of 65 in 2 days and 250 in 3 days).
- Estimate: a 100 threshold would cost about $25–55 over 3–5 days (basis: 1.5–3.5 days of accrual plus counter lag).
- Estimate: the 50k fee equals about $190 in earned Robux or $500 in retail Robux (basis: DevEx and retail rates).
- Estimate: if refunded, the fee's real cost is about 3 months of locked capital, not $190 (basis: ~90-day refund).

### Ads to recommendations

- Estimate: first home recommendations cost about $50–160 at 16/day (basis: typical arrival on day 3–10).
- Estimate: a full launch campaign costs $112–224 (basis: 16 credits/day for 7–14 days).
- Estimate: at a CPP of $0.003–0.008, 16 credits buy about 2,000–5,300 plays per day (basis: CPP target range).
- Estimate: average ad CCU is about daily plays times average minutes, divided by 1,440. At 3,000 plays and 10 minutes, that is ~21 CCU, matching reported 5–50.

### Judging CTR

| CTR difference to detect | Impressions per thumbnail (estimate) |
|---|---|
| 2% vs 3% | About 2,000 |
| 3% vs 4% | About 2,700 |
| 3% vs 3.5% | About 10,500 |
| Home PTR 1% vs 1.5% | About 4,000 |

Basis: a two-standard-error gap between two proportions. This supports the reported 5k–10k rule; learning-phase allocation adds more noise.

### Revenue by CCU and region

Estimate method: DAU ≈ CCU × 1,440 ÷ average daily playtime in minutes. Revenue ≈ DAU × ARPDAU (Robux per daily user). At 20 minutes, 100 CCU is about 7,200 DAU.

| CCU | Low-spend audience, ~20 R$/CCU/day | Typical, ~100 R$/CCU/day | Strong Tier-1 or older audience, ~200 R$/CCU/day | Typical, USD per month at DevEx |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 2k | 10k | 20k | ~$1,100 |
| 250 (interpolated) | 5k | 25k | 50k | ~$2,900 |
| 500 | 10k | 50k | 100k | ~$5,700 |
| 1,000 | 20k | 100k | 200k | ~$11,400 |

- Basis: per-CCU rules of 5–200 Robux/day and reported points such as 100 CCU at 2k–30k/day and 500 CCU at 20k–120k/day.
- Low-spend audiences include India, the Philippines, Indonesia and much of Brazil. US and 18+ players sit at the top.
- Estimate: about 33k Robux/day is needed for ~$3.8k/month at DevEx, roughly 330 CCU at typical rates.

### All-ages unlock and sale price

- Estimate: a game with good stats might see 1.5–3x CCU after the all-ages unlock (low confidence; basis: reports ranging from drops to 10x).
- Estimate: a sale price runs about 2–3.3 months of revenue (basis: the 2-month rule and the 100x-daily rule).
- Estimate: a 500-CCU game at typical rates sells for about $11k–19k (basis: ~$5.7k/month at DevEx). This matches a reported $15k+ for 600 CCU.
