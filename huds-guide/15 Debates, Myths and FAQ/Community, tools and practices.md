# Community, tools and practices

Up: [[Debates, Myths and FAQ]] · Previous: [[Estimates and projections (Debates)]] · Next: [[Open questions (Debates)]]

### Learning resources and etiquette

- The server owner's videos are cited as required viewing, especially on thumbnails, the under-16 restriction and keeping a game alive.
- The owner answered directly mainly until mid-July 2026; later advice is relayed by coaches and helpers.
- Several regular helpers have run games of 1k–7k CCU, one near 20M visits.
- Live game-review seminars run about 90 minutes on US Eastern evenings. Games are picked by wheel spin and may be recorded.
- From September, a $5/month tier funds coaches and mods; the free server and videos stay free.
- The owner does not offer free DM consults; paid reviews run about $200 per hour.
- Coverage centers on simulators; PvP growth resources are scarce.
- No self-promotion, hiring, selling or scouting posts; scouting DMs bring a ban. Use RoDevs or HiddenDevs.

### AI tools

- Claude Max costs $100 (5x usage) or $200 (20x usage); some heavy users say only 20x is worth it.
- On the $20 Codex plan, a common split is high reasoning for features and light for fixes.
- For hard updates, write implementation-ready product requirement documents (PRDs) with a top model, with code on GitHub.
- AI floods the platform with clones, so ideas, taste and speed matter more.

### Design, business and motivation

- Successful developers often reinvest most income into ads to hold recommendations.
- Expect many failures: developers report 5–30+ flops before a hit. Failures are data (owner-recommended).
- Consistency beats bursts of effort. Rereading positive feedback helps with burnout.
