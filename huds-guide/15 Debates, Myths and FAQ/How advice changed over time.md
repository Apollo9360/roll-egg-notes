# How advice changed over time

Up: [[Debates, Myths and FAQ]] · Previous: [[Myths corrected]] · Next: [[Debates]]

| Topic | Earlier view | Later view |
|---|---|---|
| HEP threshold | 500 players (late May to about 19 August 2026) | 250 since. A drop to 100 in November is announced, not yet in effect. |
| Expedite fee to skip HEP | 100k Robux until about mid-September | 50k Robux, refunded after about 90 days; reported conditions vary. A 25k figure is an unverified rumor. |
| Paying the fee | Pay if Robux sits idle; one owner game was approved in ~48 hours versus 11 days organically | After the 250 cut, most advise ads; the owner still pays for speed on trend games |
| Ad objective | Early July: Engagement to hit the threshold, or about 1:2 with Plays | Plays from mid-July; Engagement cost ~10x more per play by October. Plays plus a 2:1 Engagement side campaign became common. |
| Thumbnails per campaign | 10+ in June, about 25 in July | 15–25 rotated in September; 4–10 at $16/day in October |
| Impressions before judging a thumbnail | About 40k–100k (June) | 5k–10k or $5–10 (August to October) |
| Minimum good ad CTR | 2.5–3% (June) | 3% (July–August); October answers put the average near 1.5–2% |
| Main algorithm stat | Mid-July: QPTR, then retention, then playtime | From late July: thumbnails judged by CTR and PTR, FTUE by QPTR and bounce |
| When to stop ads | Home-rec plays about equal to ad plays (July) | Home-rec impressions about 2x ad impressions, or 30–50+ stable home-rec CCU with a tapered budget |
