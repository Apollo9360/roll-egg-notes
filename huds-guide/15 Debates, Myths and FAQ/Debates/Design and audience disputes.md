# Design and audience disputes

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[How likely is early success]]

| Dispute | Sides | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| UI polish | Kids tolerate rough UI, as simple games like Sell Lemons show; polish matters for older players | Polish matters more for older audiences | Low |
| Unique game or proven formula | A unique game flopped while +1 brainrot sims thrive; others favor copy plus twist | A proven loop with a clear twist | Medium |
| Saturated genres | Saturation proves demand; reposted saturated games rarely get pushed | Enter only with a visible improvement | Low |
| Single-player or multiplayer | The owner prefers multiplayer on a social platform; multiplayer-only games starve early | Multiplayer-capable, but playable solo or with bots | Medium |
| Young or older audience | Kids dominate; the owner calls 16+ players underserved | Serve both when possible | Medium |
| Trend game or long-term game | Trend games fade in weeks; long-term games compound | Use trend profits to fund a long-term project (owner-recommended) | Medium |
| Social media and the algorithm | Only stats matter; social traffic raised D1 from 7% to 20% in one case | Social traffic helps seed and qualify players | Medium |
| Puzzle level skips | Skips suit kids; skips kill hardcore puzzle communities | Sell hints or cosmetics instead | Low |
| Incrementals | About balancing, or about gameplay | Depends on pure incremental versus +1 hybrid | Low |
| Brazil as a market | Large audience; low monetization, such as 0.08% payer conversion | Pair with Tier-1 ads or managed pricing | Low |
| Publisher game-jam splits | A 75% publisher share is fair without a hit, or exploitative | Fair only if you lack distribution | Low |
| Acquisition offers | Viewing stats first is standard; lowball offers are common | Verify and hold good games | Medium |

- **Unresolved claims:**
    - QPTR norms may be genre-relative; outfit games run lower (low confidence).
    - 70% conversion being normal for two-player obbies (disputed).
    - Session time by segment being a combined figure (disputed).
    - Hourly Robux spent being the post-cut amount (low confidence).
