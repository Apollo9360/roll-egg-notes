# Can a game reach the algorithm without ads

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[How soon home recommendations arrive]] · Next: [[Does ad CTR predict home performance]]

- **No:** Without traffic Roblox has no data, so ads are near-required for new games.
- **Yes:** The owner says any traffic works. Short-form video and organic discovery seeded several hits.
- **Verdict:** Traffic is required, not ads specifically. Ads are the reliable route; virality is the exception. Confidence: High.
