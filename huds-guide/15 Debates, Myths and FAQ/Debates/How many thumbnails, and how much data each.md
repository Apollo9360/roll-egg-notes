# How many thumbnails, and how much data each

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Disable weak thumbnails or let Roblox allocate]] · Next: [[Paid graphics vs AI thumbnails]]

- **Many:** The owner keeps all 25 slots full and judges at 50k–100k impressions.
- **Few:** 4–10 strong creatives at $16/day, judged at 5k–25k impressions each.
- **Verdict:** Match the count to budget. At 16/day, 5–10 creatives let each reach about 10k impressions in days. Confidence: Medium.
