# How likely is early success

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Does updating a game drop all-ages reach]] · Next: [[Design and audience disputes]]

- **Hard:** Most report 5–13 flops first; one developer spent 650k Robux across 13 games.
- **Likely:** The owner calls 1k CCU very achievable; one developer's first game peaked at 70k CCU.
- **Verdict:** Expect several flops. Fast shipping and stat reading shorten the path. Confidence: Medium.
