# How soon home recommendations arrive

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Stop or keep ads once home recommendations arrive]] · Next: [[Can a game reach the algorithm without ads]]

- **Views:** Reports range from day 1–5 to 3–4 weeks. The owner cites 3–4 days, then about a week more to hit hard.
- **Verdict:** First recommendations usually come on day 3–10 at 16/day, sometimes days after ads stop. None after about 2 weeks means fix the game. Confidence: High.
