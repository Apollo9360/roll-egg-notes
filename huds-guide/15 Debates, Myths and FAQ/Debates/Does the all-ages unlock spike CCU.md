# Does the all-ages unlock spike CCU

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Monetization vs engagement in the algorithm]] · Next: [[Pay the expedite fee or advertise]]

- **Yes:** Reported jumps include 60–70 to 200, 90 to 250 and 40 to 1k CCU.
- **No:** Many saw no change, and some lost up to two-thirds of CCU for hours or a day.
- **Verdict:** Expect a new-audience impression test, not a guaranteed lift. Gains stick only with good retention. Confidence: Medium.
