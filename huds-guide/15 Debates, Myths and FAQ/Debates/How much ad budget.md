# How much ad budget

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Beta mode and ads before launch]] · Next: [[Stop or keep ads once home recommendations arrive]]

- **Low:** 5–12/day works for data, single-player games or tight budgets with strong CTR.
- **Standard:** 16/day for 7–14 days, never under 3 days (owner-recommended). $20–30 total is not enough.
- **High:** $40/day reached the algorithm in 3–4 days. The owner caps around $50/day, except PvP at about $100/day.
- **Budget type:** Daily over lifetime (owner-recommended); lifetime budgets occasionally worked.
- **Verdict:** About 16 credits/day for 7–10+ days, $100–250 in total. More spend speeds data but never fixes stats. Confidence: High.
