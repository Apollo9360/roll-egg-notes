# Do ads or home recommendations fill the HEP bar

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Plays vs Engagement ad objective]] · Next: [[Beta mode and ads before launch]]

- **Ads:** Plays ads alone reached 250 in 3–7 days for many games.
- **Home recommendations:** Many say the bar fills fast once the game hits recommendations.
- **Verdict:** Both contribute. Ads seed the count, and good games often finish it through early recommendations. Confidence: Medium.
