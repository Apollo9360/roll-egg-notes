# Plays vs Engagement ad objective

Up: [[Debates]] · Next: [[Do ads or home recommendations fill the HEP bar]]

- **Engagement:** Built for HEP and cheaper per HEP for some, but it reports late and gets few impressions.
- **Plays:** 15–20x more plays, faster learning and about 10x cheaper per play by October. The owner calls Engagement not worth it.
- **Middle path:** From September, many ran about 16 Plays plus 8 Engagement credits/day and killed Engagement at 250.
- **Verdict:** Make Plays the main campaign. A small Engagement side campaign is optional for the last stretch to 250. Confidence: High for Plays-first, Low for Engagement's added value.
