# AI-made UI, art and code

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Paid graphics vs AI thumbnails]] · Next: [[Does like ratio matter]]

- **For AI:** Most developers use AI to code, and the owner endorses it. AI-assisted games have reached 3k–20k CCU.
- **Against AI:** AI-designed UI looks generic, and AI is weak at maps, models, animation and visual effects (VFX). One-prompt games die within a day.
- **Verdict:** AI-assisted development is mainstream and produces hits. Real design work, about 100+ iterative prompts and reference-driven UI are still needed. Confidence: High.
