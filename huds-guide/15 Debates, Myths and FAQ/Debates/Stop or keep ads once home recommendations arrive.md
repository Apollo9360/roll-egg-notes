# Stop or keep ads once home recommendations arrive

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[How much ad budget]] · Next: [[How soon home recommendations arrive]]

- **Stop:** Some games only got recommendations after ads stopped.
- **Keep:** Run until recommendations alone hold 50–150 CCU. Multiplayer games may need ads to fill servers.
- **Verdict:** Taper once home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions, the owner's rule. Whether to stop at around 2k CCU stays unresolved. Confidence: High.
