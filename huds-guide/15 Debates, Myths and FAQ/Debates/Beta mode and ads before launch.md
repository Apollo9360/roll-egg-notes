# Beta mode and ads before launch

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Do ads or home recommendations fill the HEP bar]] · Next: [[How much ad budget]]

- **For:** Fix stats with ad players in beta, since home-rec stats weigh more.
- **Against:** Beta only hides the game. The owner advises playtesters instead and warns against advertising an unpolished loop.
- **Verdict:** Skip beta mode. Polish with live playtests, then run public ads. Confidence: Medium.
