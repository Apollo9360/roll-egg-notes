# Pay the expedite fee or advertise

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Does the all-ages unlock spike CCU]] · Next: [[Daily and playtime rewards]]

- **Pay:** Faster, especially for trend games, and refunded after about 90 days (owner-recommended for speed).
- **Advertise:** Ads reach 250 in days for about $50–130 and also buy algorithm data. The refund ties up Robux for 3 months.
- **Verdict:** Advertise by default. Pay with idle earned Robux when speed matters, such as trend or kid-targeted games. Confidence: Medium.
