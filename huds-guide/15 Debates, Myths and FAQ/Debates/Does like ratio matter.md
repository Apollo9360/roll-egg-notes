# Does like ratio matter

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[AI-made UI, art and code]] · Next: [[Monetization vs engagement in the algorithm]]

- **No:** Likes are feedback, not an algorithm input.
- **Maybe:** A poor ratio may make browsing players scroll past. A claim that Roblox pushes games at 70% likes is unverified.
- **Verdict:** Not a direct input; very low ratios may cost clicks. Confidence: High.
