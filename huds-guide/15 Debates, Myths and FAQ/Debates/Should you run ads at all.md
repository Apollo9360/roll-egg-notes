# Should you run ads at all

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Daily stats vs 7-day average]] · Next: [[Broad or narrow ad targeting]]

- **Against:** Ads ruin stats.
- **For:** Ads feed the algorithm data.
- **Verdict:** Run ads on a polished game. Ad-traffic stats look worse than home-rec stats, which is expected. Confidence: High.
