# Monetization vs engagement in the algorithm

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Does like ratio matter]] · Next: [[Does the all-ages unlock spike CCU]]

- **Monetization:** Top-monetizing games get long-lived recommendations.
- **Engagement:** PTR, bounce, play days and playtime come first.
- **Verdict:** Engagement first, citing official documentation. Monetization matters once players stay. Confidence: High.
