# Does ad CTR predict home performance

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Can a game reach the algorithm without ads]] · Next: [[Disable weak thumbnails or let Roblox allocate]]

- **Yes:** The owner says a high-CTR ad thumbnail is usually a good home thumbnail.
- **No:** Cases include 4% ad CTR against 0.49% QPTR, and 5–7% on ads against ~1% on home.
- **Verdict:** Absolute numbers don't carry over; home PTR runs several times lower. Rankings usually carry over, with occasional audience mismatch. Confidence: Medium.
