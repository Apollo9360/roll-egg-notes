# Disable weak thumbnails or let Roblox allocate

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Does ad CTR predict home performance]] · Next: [[How many thumbnails, and how much data each]]

- **Allocate:** Roblox shifts budget to the best creative automatically.
- **Disable:** Turn off clear losers.
- **Verdict:** Wait out 24–48 hours of learning, then disable creatives under about 2–3% CTR with meaningful impressions. Confidence: High.
