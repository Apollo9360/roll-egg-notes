# Broad or narrow ad targeting

Up: [[Debates]] · Previous: [[Should you run ads at all]] · Next: [[Does updating a game drop all-ages reach]]

- **Narrow:** Some saw CTR rise from 2% to 6% with 18+ targeting.
- **Broad:** The owner warns higher CTR can come with worse fit. Genre targeting shrank one audience estimate by 300M.
- **Verdict:** Target everyone. Add a separate 18–24 campaign only for games built for older players. Confidence: Medium.
