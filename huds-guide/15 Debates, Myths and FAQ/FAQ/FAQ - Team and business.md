# FAQ - Team and business

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Monetization]] · Next: [[FAQ - Technical]]

**Where do I hire?** HiddenDevs, RoDevs, Talent Hub, Twitter/X and YouTube; BuiltByBit for asset packs. Use a paid trial task, references and contracts. Hire fast, fire faster (owner-recommended).

**Group or profile?** A group lets you pay team members, sell more easily and limit ban risk. Use a separate group per major game, even solo.

**Main or alt account?** Use a separate owner account for the group and Robux. Upload risky assets from an alt.

**Sell or take investment?** About 95% of acquisition studios aren't worth it; hold good games. Raise D1 and monetization, then sell near the top. Get paid first and use a lawyer for big deals.

**How do I price a game for sale?** Value follows ARPDAU. Rules range from about 2 months of revenue to about 100x daily revenue. A 600-CCU game was estimated at $15k or more.

**How do I spot scams?** Expect mass scout DMs, lowball offers such as $200 for a 1k-CCU game, and fake test-payment schemes. Verify before sharing analytics.

**How do I find partners or investors?** Build a personal brand on YouTube or Twitter so developers come to you. Cold DMs rarely work. Avoid DM "investors" asking for a percentage to run ads.
