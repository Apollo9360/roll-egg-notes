# FAQ - Monetization

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Analytics]] · Next: [[FAQ - Team and business]]

**Good ARPPU but low conversion?** Average revenue per paying user (ARPPU) is fine, but too few buy. Add a cheap, visible, useful product.

**Low ARPDAU with real home-rec players?** Make products solve in-game problems. Place them in high-traffic spots, add rotating offers and keep best sellers visible.

**Enable regional pricing?** Yes, especially outside the US and UK. For low-spending demographics, use managed pricing, 25–50% cuts or a Tier-1-country ad campaign.

**What does a game at X CCU earn?** About 100 Robux/day per CCU with decent monetization; the observed range is 5–200. See Estimates and projections.

**When do payouts arrive?** New groups are locked about 31 days. Pass and product Robux pend 5–7 days. Creator Rewards go to the owning group in about 60 days. DevEx takes under a week.

**Do experience-level splits get Creator Rewards?** Yes, but leftovers go to the group owner and commissions aren't auto-split. Group-level splits are preferred.
