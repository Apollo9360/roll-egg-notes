# FAQ - Algorithm

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Thumbnails]] · Next: [[FAQ - Age gates]]

**How do I know I hit the algorithm?** Home-recommendation impressions and plays appear, first as a trickle, then tests of thousands.

**Which stat matters most?** QPTR (thumbnail plus onboarding), then retention, then playtime above ~12 minutes.

**Why is QPTR 0.00 after days of ads?** No home-rec impressions yet.

**Home recommendations dropped or spiked and crashed.** Normal rubber-banding while the algorithm finds your audience. Wait about a week; hitting hard can take 3–4 weeks.

**CCU spiked at the all-ages unlock, then crashed.** A test spike. Beat the previous D1 and playtime for the next push.

**Why does CCU swing daily?** Time zones and school hours. Spikes after 8pm US Eastern and on weekends are normal. D1 dips every Sunday.

**How do I go from first recommendations to 1k CCU?** Make educated guesses from data and ship frequent mini updates.
