# FAQ - Thumbnails

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Ads]] · Next: [[FAQ - Algorithm]]

**How many thumbnails?** 3–5 on the game page. For ads, 5–10 at $16/day, up to 25 at higher spend.

**What's a good ad CTR?**

| CTR | Verdict | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1% | Extremely bad | High |
| Under 2% | Turn the creative off | High |
| 2–2.5% | Average; normal for non-trend games | Medium |
| 3–4% | Good minimum target; the owner calls 3.8% excellent | High |
| 5–7% | Strong; needed for multiplayer games | Medium |
| 8–10% | Exceptional; mostly towers or narrow targeting | Medium |

Genre shifts these: towers reach 7–10%, incrementals sit near 4%.

**When can I judge or replace a thumbnail?** After 24+ hours of learning and about 5k–10k impressions or $5–8 each. Cut under 3% at ~1k impressions only if clearly bad. At 50k–100k impressions with 1–2% CTR, it is dead.

**How do I improve CTR and PTR?** Fill slots, pause losers, iterate on winners and show the core loop clearly. Make the genre obvious; one game rose from under 2% to 6%. Low QPTR also points to title and genre demand.

**How do I avoid a generic AI look?** Use AI with good references, then add text and faces manually in Figma. Copy styles from top games in your genre. Test on [qptr.io](https://qptr.io).

**Uploaded a thumbnail but the default still shows?** Upload it on the Experience Detail Page.

**Should I change the title or icon after hitting the algorithm?** Risky above roughly 100–1k CCU. Revert if stats drop.
