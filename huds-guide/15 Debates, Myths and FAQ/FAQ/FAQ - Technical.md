# FAQ - Technical

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Team and business]] · Next: [[FAQ - Other]]

**How do I store huge simulator numbers?** A big-number module (mantissa plus exponent). IntValue caps at about 9.22 quintillion.

**How do I make UI work on phone and PC?** Scale not Offset, UIAspectRatioConstraint, anchor points, scaled strokes and device-emulator tests. Handle portrait and landscape. Most games see more phone and tablet players than PC.

**How do I import Figma UI?** RoImport (free), Figblox, GPT conversion, or exported frame images with ScaleType Fit.

**How do I connect Claude to Studio?** Studio AI Assistant, three dots, Model Context Protocol (MCP), then Quick Connect.

**How do I build an "X items exist" counter?** MemoryStore for the live count, flushed periodically to a DataStore.

**Does uploading a dev place over the main place wipe data?** No. DataStores persist.

**How do I balance a simulator or incremental?** A spreadsheet with an exponential cost curve and a zero-to-endgame simulation. Playtest repeatedly.

**What's the best AI?** Opus 5.5 was most praised; Sonnet 5.5 was called nearly as good at half the price. Codex/GPT Astra is cheaper with good modeling.
