# FAQ - Analytics

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Age gates]] · Next: [[FAQ - Monetization]]

**Why aren't stats or benchmarks showing?** Ads and analytics lag 1–3 days. Benchmarks need about 100 DAU and appear after 1–14 days.

**Where are bounce rate, PTR and QPTR?** Analytics, then Acquisition, then Home Recommendations. D1, D7 and benchmark colors are on Overview.

**What do the terms mean?** PTR is the share of home-rec viewers who play. Bounce rate is the share leaving within 60 seconds. Playtime is daily total; session time is one session.

**When are D1 and D7 reliable?** After about a week with 500+ DAU, ideally from home-rec players. A player returning on day 7 counts toward D7.

**What are good targets?**

| Metric | Target | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Tutorial completion | 80–85%+ good; 50–80% normal depending on length | Medium |
| Average playtime | 12–15+ min; 20+ for random number generator (RNG), tycoon and simulator games | Medium |
| Day-one playtime on ads | 2–7 min common; 9+ min minimum, 12–13 min good | High |
| Still playing after 5 min | 35%+ good, 50%+ strong, 60–65% target, 60–90% for top games | High |
| First-session retention on ads | 40% OK, 50% decent, 70% great | High |
| First-session retention on recommendations | 65–70% | Medium |
| D1 on ads | 5–7% typical, 15% excellent; roughly doubles on recommendations | High |
| D1 on recommendations | 10–20%+; one claim of 50%+ is disputed | Medium |
| Bounce rate | Below genre benchmark; one estimate puts the ideal at 1–3% | Low |

**How do I fix players quitting in the tutorial?**

1. Set up funnels and find the biggest step-to-step drop; check step two first.
2. Spawn players at the first objective; one case cut first-step drop-off from 22% to 12%.
3. Auto-start the tutorial and auto-equip the first item. Show no popups before loading ends.
4. Make early earning steps take about 3 seconds; ask players to collect before they buy.
5. Teach the 3-step core loop with arrows, highlights and minimal text. Explain why, not how.
6. Hide secondary UI and whitelist actions per step. Make losing impossible during the tutorial.
7. Replace the loading screen with a short cutscene; one tycoon cut bounce from ~21% to ~14%.
8. Remove non-gameplay and repetitive steps, and fix mobile UI size.

**Is a long tutorial OK?** A 5–10 minute onboarding works if it feels like playing; mature audiences tolerate more. Forced 3-minute teleports lost most players by 5 minutes. Completion alone isn't enough; 100% completion still came with 4-minute playtime.

**How do I improve D1?** Fix onboarding drop-off and core-loop fun first. Add clear goals, offline earnings with a welcome-back popup, a strong day-2 reward, and fast bug fixes.

**How do I improve D7 and play days?** Deepen progression: traits, merge machines, indexes with rewards and more content. Add co-play rewards and consumable products.

**How do I improve playtime?** Check first-5-minute retention, which is usually onboarding. Add rewards, satisfying feedback and clear goals. Supporting console helps, since console players play long.
