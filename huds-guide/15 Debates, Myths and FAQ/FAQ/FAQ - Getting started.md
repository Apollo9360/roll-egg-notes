# FAQ - Getting started

Up: [[FAQ]] · Next: [[FAQ - Ads]]

**How do I get first players without money?** Realistically, pay for ads. Social virality is rare. Some sell about 10% of a game to fund ads (low confidence).

**Release now or keep polishing?** Get live feedback from friends or kids first. Then release with a bug-report button, start ads, and ship the first update quickly.

**Should I release if a similar game exists?** Yes, if yours is equal or better with a twist. A much better game beats a same-named small one. A clone stuck near 10 CCU should move on.

**Where do I find game ideas?** Start from a fantasy and look for gaps. Mine YouTube viral concepts, TikTok trends, Steam games and fandom gaps. Remix proven loops and serve underserved audiences.

**How do I build game-design intuition?** Play many games, including every top game in your genre. Log predictions in a spreadsheet and check which blow up. Launch and fail repeatedly.

**Should I buy templates?** Only from reputable sellers; random ones risk backdoors or copyright takedowns. Template use itself isn't detected.

**Is a Roblox game passive income?** No. Weekly meaningful live-ops updates are expected.

**Do I need a new place to reset stats after testing?** No. Use a new DataStore key to reset player data. Republishing to refresh the algorithm has no consensus.

**Where do I find testers?** Voice chat with players, QA Central ([discord.gg/qac](https://discord.gg/qac)), live game-review seminars, peer-help channels or Hidden Devs.

**Where do I get sound effects?** Toolbox, Pixabay, GameChestAudio and DevForum sound kits.

**Is learning scripting still worth it with AI?** Yes, at least the basics, to understand and fix AI code.

**Should I work on several games at once?** Solo developers should build one at a time (owner-recommended).

**When should I give up on a game?** After 7–14 days of ads with bad stats and no home recommendations. Check funnels first.
