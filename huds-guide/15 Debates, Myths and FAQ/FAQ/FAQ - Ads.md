# FAQ - Ads

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Getting started]] · Next: [[FAQ - Thumbnails]]

**How much to spend, and for how long?** About 16 credits/day (~$16) on a daily budget and Plays objective, for 7–14 days. Budget $100–250 in total. Multiplayer games need 20–30/day; with ~$40, run 5/day for 8 days.

**Robux or USD?** USD in Ads Manager if you are 18+ with ID verification: 1 credit equals $1. Use Robux only if earned, at about 236–285 per credit.

**Under 18 or card declined?** Convert earned Robux, or use a verified adult account with campaign permissions. For declines, retry, call the bank, switch cards or use Revolut.

**Is the 1k fee needed to run ads?** Some report that ads need the 1k fee or Plus (low confidence). Missing ID verification can also mean 0 impressions.

**Should I target a demographic?** Generally no; target everyone.

**How long does ad review take?** Usually 1–4 hours, up to about 20. During late-August 2026 delays, scheduling ahead or recreating the campaign helped.

**Why can't I resume my ad?** It probably expired. Create a new campaign; unspent credits remain.

**My game doesn't show in Ads Manager.** Make it public and restart Ads Manager. For group games, select the group.

**How do I let an investor run ads?** Give Creator Hub or group ad permissions only, not Studio access.

**When can I trust ad stats?** After the ~24-hour learning phase; give it 2–3 days. CPP lags over 24 hours.

**What CCU should 16 credits/day bring?** About 5–50 CCU, with occasional 100–300 spikes. Near 0–5 CCU after several days points to a game or thumbnail problem.

**Lots of clicks but few plays?** Plays lag clicks by up to about 3 days. If it persists, the thumbnail over-promises or players bounce on the page. Fix the first thumbnail, title and description.

**What's a good CPP?** $0.01 or less; ideally $0.003–0.008. Good CTR with bad CPP can mean too small a budget or a competitive genre.

**No purchases in the first hours of ads?** Normal. Ad players convert poorly, and 8–24 hours is too early to judge.

**Run ads with an update?** Release the update, wait about 2 days, then run ads with new thumbnails.

**When do I stop ads?** When home-rec impressions exceed ad impressions, ideally 2x, or recommendations hold 50+ CCU. Taper rather than cut. Expect CCU to drop when ads stop.
