# FAQ - Age gates

Up: [[FAQ]] · Previous: [[FAQ - Algorithm]] · Next: [[FAQ - Analytics]]

**Why is my game stuck at 16+?** All of these are required:

1. 250 HEP (500 before about 19 August 2026; 100 announced for November), or the 50k Robux expedite fee.
2. ID verification and the owner's age check.
3. Two-factor authentication.
4. The maturity questionnaire.
5. The 1k publishing fee in the Reach tab, waived with 2+ months of Roblox Plus.

**Where is my HEP count?** Under the game description, in settings, or on the Manage audience page. It lags 1–2 days.

**How long after hitting 250 until all ages?** Usually 24–48 hours, up to 3 days. No extra click is needed beyond the fee or Plus.

**How long and how much to reach 250 HEP?** Usually 3–7 days at 8–16 credits/day, often $50–130. Fast games took 2–3 days and $30–80; some never pass 250 after 160 credits.

**Can I reuse an old experience that already has HEP?** Considered fine for genuine reuse. Funnels carry over; HEP transfer is unconfirmed.

**Other under-16 limits?** Under-16 developers can't add social links and need a parent's card or ID for ads.
