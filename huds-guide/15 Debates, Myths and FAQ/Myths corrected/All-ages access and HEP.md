# All-ages access and HEP

Up: [[Myths corrected]] · Next: [[Myths corrected - Ads]]

| Myth | Reality | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Paying the 1,000 Robux fee unlocks under-16 players | Publishing to 16+ is free. The 1k fee, waived with 2+ months of Roblox Plus, is paid after reaching 250 HEP and does not replace it. | High |
| You must pay a big fee to publish or reach kids | The expedite fee (50k Robux, 100k until about mid-September) is an optional HEP skip. Most games reach HEP through Plays ads. | High |
| Paying the expedite fee puts a game on the algorithm | It only lifts the all-ages gate early. Stats still decide recommendations. | High |
| Roblox removed the HEP requirement | It was cut from 500 to 250 around 19 August 2026, not removed. | High |
| The threshold is already 100 HEP | A drop to 100 in November is announced but not yet in effect. A 25k expedite fee is an unverified rumor. | High |
| The threshold means 250 concurrent users (CCU) or visits | It counts qualifying players accumulated over time. Visits needed ranged from about 3k to 40k. | High |
| HEP are simply 16+ players | They are verified 16+ accounts that also spent Robux recently. Targeting 16+ in ads does not change the count. | High |
| Enough playtime makes a player count | Recent Roblox spending is required; long-session non-spenders often don't count. The window is usually cited as about 60 days. | Medium |
| A qualifying player must spend in your game | Spending anywhere on Roblox counts. | Medium |
| HEP met on one game covers the whole group | HEP is counted per game. | Medium |
| HEP drops mean Roblox is removing bots | Players age out of the window after about 60 days. | Medium |
| A good game can't reach the threshold | Most decent games reach 250 within 2–7 days of ads. Young-audience games struggle more. | High |
| Fee refunds need 100 CCU | Reported conditions are about 100 HEP and no bans or warnings. The expedite fee returns after about 90 days; whether the 1k fee is refundable is disputed. | Medium |
| No home recommendations arrive before the threshold | Some games ran 100–300 CCU from recommendations while still 16+. | Medium |
| Hitting the threshold needs a manual application | The page shows eligible and review takes 1–3 days. Only the 1k fee or Plus is needed. | Medium |
| All-ages showing below the threshold is real | It is a visual bug. | Low |
| All ages will fix bad 16+ stats | Usually not. A broader audience exposes the same weaknesses. | High |
| You need Roblox Premium to publish | Plus or Premium only waives the 1k fee. | High |
