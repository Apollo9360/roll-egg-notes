# Myths corrected - Thumbnails and AI

Up: [[Myths corrected]] · Previous: [[Myths corrected - Ads]] · Next: [[Myths corrected - Algorithm and stats]]

| Myth | Reality | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Ad CTR equals home-page performance | 4–10% ad CTR often becomes 0.5–1% play-through rate (PTR) or QPTR on home; one case ran about 6x lower. | High |
| Thumbnail changes fix QPTR | Thumbnails drive CTR and PTR. QPTR and bounce also depend on the first-time user experience (FTUE). | Medium |
| Thumbnails are the problem when playtime is 5 minutes | Check genre benchmarks first. | Medium |
| Copying a trending game's thumbnail gets the same reach | Without unique packaging, the top game is shown first. | Medium |
| Icons strongly affect CTR | Barely. Use the winning thumbnail as the icon (owner-recommended). | High |
| Polished AI thumbnails always beat quick ones | Not necessarily; test both. | Low |
| Claude can make thumbnails | It has no native image generation. Use Gemini or ChatGPT. | Medium |
| Top thumbnails are fully hand-made | A common workflow is a rough Canva layout, then an AI generator such as Rothumbs. | Medium |
| AI use itself makes games disliked | Criticism targets low-effort, microtransaction-stuffed games. AI art, AI code and free models are not inherently bad. | Medium |
| Figma locks most features behind a subscription | Figma is free. Paywall prompts usually mean the wrong file type. | Medium |
