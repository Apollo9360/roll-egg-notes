# Myths corrected - Revenue and CCU

Up: [[Myths corrected]] · Previous: [[Myths corrected - Algorithm and stats]] · Next: [[Building, tools and career]]

| Myth | Reality | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| High CCU means high revenue | Demographics, genre and monetization dominate. A 100-CCU game can out-earn a 1k-CCU game. | High |
| You need 1k CCU to earn | Revenue starts at 1 CCU. Many call a sustained 300–800 CCU a living. | Medium |
| Revenue tracking sites show real revenue | They multiply DAU by a constant average revenue per daily user (ARPDAU) and can be off by about 2x. | Medium |
| 100k Robux equals $1,000 | At DevEx it is about $380. Viral posts often use the retail price of about $10 per 1,000. | High |
| DevEx takes 70% | US withholding of up to 24–30% applies to US-player revenue, depending on tax forms. | Low |
| Group withdrawals open after 2 weeks | The first payout takes about 1 month. | Medium |
| A genre can't make money | Monetization is case by case; improve it before blaming the genre. | Low |
| 100k Robux of ads buys 10k CCU | Ads only kickstart recommendations; a bad game fails at any budget. | High |
| No game gets big without ads | Several hits grew from organic or social traffic, up to a 200k-CCU game. | Medium |
| Roblox margins match other businesses | The owner contrasts e-commerce near 10% margin with Roblox near 90%, before DevEx conversion. | Medium |
